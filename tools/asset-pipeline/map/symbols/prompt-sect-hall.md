The attached sheet shows a set of small map symbols in one style (style G): bold simplified silhouettes in a warm off-white with a dark brown outline and a soft drop shadow, flat with one light shade.

Paint 4 new map symbols in exactly that style, in an exact grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, evenly spaced, all about the same size (each about 30 % of the canvas width across), on flat pure chroma green (#00FF00) with clear green space between them. Every symbol must read clearly when shown very small (about 40 pixels). No text, letters, characters, seals, banners with writing or numbers anywhere.

Each is a different take on one subject: a sect hall, the old seat of a cultivation sect that has moved its main seat elsewhere, now its outer hall or court. It must read as smaller and plainer than the minor sect symbol (a single three-tier pagoda with a courtyard wall) and more important than the town symbol (a cluster of tiled houses). (1) a single-storey hall with a sweeping double roof behind a gate arch; (2) a two-tier pavilion with a courtyard wall and a small gate; (3) a gatehouse with a hall roof behind it and one tall pine; (4) a hall on a raised stone terrace with steps and two lanterns.
