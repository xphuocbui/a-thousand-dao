Meridian Map rework · hand-drawn pilot region

Metal's land, drawn by hand on the original map's layout: roads run out from the start like spokes and around the tree like rings, and the clusters sit in the spaces between them. Nothing is generated. The start opens into a twin-hub web, as every PoE 2 start does. Each band then holds a group of clusters that reach each other: a three-lane bridge, a wheel and a gourd; a crown, a four-leaf lattice and a shared zigzag; then a sword tomb, a summit and an anvil; and past a fourth ring, a blade pinwheel, a furnace square, two peaks and the Metal apex. Small clusters are PoE 2's figures rather than crescents: kites, L and V bends, zigzags, crosses, a star, hearts, hourglasses, eyes, a sword, an arrow, a hexagon and a cash coin; roads have fewer nodes, and most road nodes carry two or three clusters, one of them big; clusters grow toward the rim. Roads join every cluster at its nearest node. The start is at the bottom and the rim at the top. Hover any node for what it gives and how many points it costs.
