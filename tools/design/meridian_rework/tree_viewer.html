Themes

Reading the map

  • Starts. Your spiritual root picks one: five element starts round the Taiji (Chaos starts at its heart), Ice inside Water's snowflake, Wind on Water and Wood's outer ring, and Light, Darkness and Thunder on their islands.
  • Roads (gold, with diamond travel nodes) pay talents: Metal physique, Water comprehension, Fire soul strength, Earth Dao heart, Wood fortune; the ring and dantian roads pay a talent of your choice.
  • Islands join by bridges: Light to Fire, Thunder to the Wind start, Darkness to Water (straight in, square to the rim). The shadow road runs outside the rim from Darkness to Earth, past the Shadow Shrine (Ám Từ), a waystation on the east side.
  • Sealed clusters (outlined) sit beyond the rim and in a few clearings inside. Points alone cannot open them: an event, an item, a cultivation technique or a story moment unseals each one. Silhouette hides what they hold until you find them; Visible shows it.
  • Keystones (red, haloed, in an eight-point frame) end every approach that leads to them; each land has one beside its start. Each start sits in a seal.
  • Quiet fields are clearings round one landmark; set pieces are composed groups such as the Alchemist's Hall or the Beast Den; the socket (a diamond with a small gem) in a great ring marks a future cluster mastery (Tinh Thông).
  • Plan a build. Click acupoints to spend points along the cheapest path from your start; click an allocated one to refund it and anything cut off beyond it. Clear starts again; Copy build link shares it. Find rings every acupoint whose name, cluster or theme matches.
  • Two builds for open point 1 (the budget): the full build (about 8,670 acupoints) and the lean build (about 6,400).
