Passive Tree Survey

The Path of Exile 1 and Path of Exile 2 passive trees, redrawn from their published data, beside today's Kinh Mạch Đồ. Pick a start, then hover any node to see the shortest path to it and what it costs in points. Click a node to measure from there instead.

What makes the PoE trees work

Shape

A ring of starts around a hub, each in its own colour

PoE 1 puts six class starts on a ring at a third of the radius, 60° apart, with the Scion in the centre. PoE 2 pulls its six shared starts in to a tight inner ring (about a tenth of the radius). Each start owns a region (strength, dexterity, intelligence and their blends), and the space between two neighbours is a hybrid zone: about 61–67 % of all nodes are within 3 points of being closer to a second start.

Full, round, and richer toward the rim

Both trees are as wide as they are tall and fill 63–71 % of the plate, with no empty bands. Density grows outward: the inner 40 % of the radius holds about a tenth of the nodes, and most notables and keystones sit in the outer half (keystones average 0.68 of the radius).

Geometric clusters on orbits, drawn as arcs

Every node sits on one of a few concentric orbits around its cluster's centre, so clusters come in a small set of recognisable forms: wheels you pass through, half-rings, short spokes. 61 % of PoE 1's edges are arcs along those orbits, which gives it the round, hand-drawn look. PoE 2 uses more straight spokes and stars (29 % arcs).

Hand-shaped, never symmetric

Rotating either tree by 60° lines up only 35–39 % of nodes, and a left-right mirror about half. Each region was drawn for its own themes, so every side of the map reads differently, even though density is even around the circle.

Connection

Highways and pockets

About 60–65 % of nodes are plain links in a line (two neighbours), and they form long travel roads between crossroads of 5 to 9 roads (starts, jewel sockets, wheel centres). Notables live in pockets off those roads: 79 % of PoE 1's notable clusters (64 % in PoE 2) have a single way in, and cost only 2.7 (PoE 2: 3.4) small nodes each.

Keystones are always a deliberate trip

Every keystone in both trees is a dead-end tip with one link. You never pass through one on the way somewhere else, so taking it is always a choice.

Loops give alternate routes

Both trees carry about 16 independent loops per 100 nodes, mostly wheels that you enter at one point and leave at another. A path can collect a cluster's value on its way through instead of backtracking.

Travel is never worthless

PoE 1's travel nodes give +10 to an attribute, and PoE 2's let you pick +5 to any attribute. Roads pay for requirements, so the cost of a long trip is softened rather than dead.

Pathing

Small diameter, huge breadth

From any start, the farthest node is only 30–33 points away in PoE 1 (38–42 in PoE 2), yet a finished build allocates only about 3–6 % of the tree. You can reach anything; the choice is which few percent to take and which roads to share between goals.

Payoff arrives early and keeps coming

The nearest keystone is 6–8 points from a PoE 1 start (9–13 in PoE 2), and 34–61 notables sit within 10 points. Inside a cluster, notables are 2–3 steps apart, and clusters are 4–7 steps apart, so a planned path hits a payoff every few points.

Neighbours are close, the far side is a commitment

Adjacent starts are 12 points apart and opposite ones 26–30. Borrowing a neighbour's region is cheap; crossing the map is a build-defining spend.

What PoE 2 changed

Twelve classes share six starts, set much closer to the centre, and the ascendancy sits in the middle. The tree is twice as big (4,115 nodes, 984 notables) but has fewer keystones (33), and they sit further out. Notables come in tight groups of 2–3, then a longer gap (the spacing has peaks at 2–3 and at 6–7 steps). More pockets are dead ends (559 tips against PoE 1's 77), and plain travel nodes are mostly the pick-an-attribute kind.

Where today's Kinh Mạch Đồ differs

  • An empty middle band. Only about 2 % of our nodes sit between half and 85 % of the radius, so the rim clusters float. The map fills 27 % of its plate, against PoE's 63–71 %.
  • Thin payoff. A notable cluster carries 5.8 small nodes per pocket, against 2.7–3.4, and 85 of 193 notables are 8 or more steps from the next one.
  • No real crossroads. Our biggest junction has 4 roads (the centre has 6). PoE's hubs have up to 9.
  • The first keystone is far. Apart from the centre keystone, the nearest one is 22 points away, against 6–13.
  • Some keystones sit on the path. 6 of our 19 keystones have two links, so a build can walk through one.
  • Blob clusters, not orbit forms. Our builder grows each cluster as a blob. Its curves are smoothing, not orbits, so clusters lack PoE's recognisable wheel and arc shapes.
  • One start. Everyone begins in Đan Điền. You've already chosen to let the root pick the start, which moves us toward PoE's start ring.

Same in spirit: about 15 loops per 100 nodes, mostly single-entrance pockets (83 %), and a round outline.

Where the nodes sit, centre to rim

Clusters: what they hold and where they sit

Two kinds of theme

Universal themes are scattered on purpose

Life, crit, mana, auras and attributes appear in 9–12 of 12 directions, and the nearest one is 4–11 points from every start. PoE 2's crit has 48 clusters in all 12 directions, 5–10 points from every start. Every build needs these, so every start gets them nearby. Choose "Where a theme sits" above, then crit or life, to see it.

Identity themes are concentrated

Minions, traps and mines, warcries, flasks, evasion and armour sit in 2–6 directions, owned by 2–5 starts, and can be 17–22 points from the others. They make a start feel like itself, and taking them far from home is a real detour.

Scattered themes rarely sit next to themselves

A crit cluster's nearest neighbour is almost never another crit cluster (17 % in PoE 1), and the same holds for spell, curse and elemental. Concentrated themes run in districts (evasion 81 %, weapons 70 %, minions 65 %). The commonest neighbours pair offence with defence: crit next to life is PoE 2's most common pair.

Shape and flow

Every crossroads is a small menu

In PoE 1, 397 of 422 notable pockets hang directly off a crossroads, and a crossroads usually holds 3–5 pockets of different themes (89 % mixed). You stand on a cheap travel node and choose which door to take.

Mostly one notable at the end of a short spoke

86 % of PoE 1's clusters (72 % of PoE 2's) hold one notable. About two-thirds are straight spokes and one-third are wheels, and 79 % have a single way in. The small nodes leading in carry the cluster's theme 66–71 % of the time, so the walk builds toward the payoff.

Packed tight

Neighbouring clusters are almost always 2 points apart. Leaving one pocket puts you two steps from the next, so the tree reads as one fabric instead of islands.

Masteries (PoE 1)

A mastery turns repeated themes into choices

315 of PoE 1's 422 notable clusters have a mastery at their centre, named for the theme (Life ×14, Mana ×12, Critical ×7). It opens once you hold a notable in that cluster, and you pick one of its effects (277 offer six). Each effect can be taken only once, so the second Life cluster offers a different Life choice. That is what makes repeating a theme around the tree worth it.

What this could mean for Kinh Mạch Đồ

  • Our scatter is mechanical. Each of our 14 themes is copied about once per element sector (5 copies, 5 directions), whatever the theme. Universal and identity themes are spread the same way, and no theme forms a district (same-theme neighbours 9 %).
  • Split our themes in two. Universal: health (Khí Huyết), linh lực, thần thức, ward (Hộ Thể), and a crit-like offence theme. Identity: sword intent (Kiếm Ý), beasts (Ngự Thú), formations (Trận Đạo), talismans (Phù Đạo), alchemy (Đan Đạo), refining (Luyện Khí), fields (Linh Điền). Scatter the first group widely and unevenly, and give the second group districts near the root that fits it.
  • Menus at crossroads. Hang 3–5 short pockets of different themes on each road junction, about 2 points apart, so every fork is a choice.
  • Pair offence with defence. Put a defensive pocket next to most offensive ones, so any route can stay balanced.
  • Cluster mastery (Tinh Thông), later. One mastery per themed cluster, opened by a notable in it, offering a few choices that can each be taken once. It fits the Effects tiers (minor), and it makes repeated universal themes worth taking.

How spread out each theme is

Spread 1.0 means the theme's clusters sit as far apart as clusters in general; lower means grouped. Access is the points from the closest and the farthest start to the theme's nearest cluster. PoE 1's themes come from its mastery names; PoE 2's come from the stat text, so they are approximate.
