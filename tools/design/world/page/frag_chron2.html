The chronicle: 1,200 years of the Thiên Nguyên calendar

200 landmark events in six eras, from the first opening of Phù Tang Cảnh in year 0 to the day every game begins, year 1,200. Scroll the list and the map follows the year. Land is coloured by whoever held it then: the seven great sects, and the eleven that fell (paler). Wild land stays clear. Cities appear as they are founded and turn to ruins when they fall; the plain turns to miasma after 803. Where an event made one of the 28 hidden places, the map shows all its candidate spots, because each game's seed puts it at a different one. Click a name or an item to follow it through the years.
