The 20 regions

Each sect's regions share a theme. Thiên Xu Tông's are stars of the Northern Dipper, like its own name. Lạc Hà Tông's come from Vương Bột's Đằng Vương Các Tự, the same text as its name. Phượng Minh Cung's come from the phoenix odes. Băng Tâm Các's follow its poem and the cold. Thính Vũ Lâu's follow rain and thunder. The last word names the land: Lâm forest, Lĩnh ridge, Sơn mountains, Nguyên plain, Xuyên river land, Mạc desert, Giác cape, Loan bay, Châu land by the water, Đảo island, and Hải, here a sea of pines.

The 22 minor sects

Each minor sect is a vassal of the great sect whose land it sits in: it pays tribute, sends levies and holds sites, never a whole region (Sects & Missions rules 1–7). There are 4 under each tier-1 sect and 2–3 under each tier-2 sect, at least one in every region, and 22 leaves room under the cap of 25 for sects founded in play. Each name comes from a line of classical poetry, and each suffix is humbler than a great sect's: Môn, Phái, Cốc, Trang, Quán, Viện, Trai, Đường, Am, Đài, Viên. Some echo their lord: Củng Tinh Quán's stars bow to the Heavenly Pivot, and Lãng Đào Môn's waves answer Tùng Đào's pines.

Places, roads and arrays

204 places, inside the 150–250 that Travel & Map rule 6 asks for. There are 15 cities, 10 cultivator markets, 40 towns, 70 villages, 16 mines and 24 famous wild sites, on top of the 29 sect seats. Each settlement sits where the land is most livable: grassland and forest, mild and wet enough, low and flat, best of all at a river mouth. Trunk roads join the cities, markets and great seats along the cheapest ground. Every other place takes a branch to the nearest road. Sea lanes hug the coasts, and the 7 teleport arrays sit in each great sect's best-placed city.

Sacred features

The features the generator never makes, laid by hand-tuned rules, plus three the terrain makes. A spirit vein (linh mạch) runs along the ridge under every sect seat: a great vein under each of the 7 great seats and a small one under each of the 22 minor seats. Each great sect also holds one famous wild vein, a new spirit vein place. Six of the seven sit near another sect's border, because veins are what sects fight over. Three sacred summits give Light, and three deep caves give Darkness. One miasma wasteland, Chiết Kích Nguyên, is an old battlefield, set by hand in the scorched badlands where three sects meet. The snowline, storm peaks and high plateaus are read from height, rain and flatness.

What the history leaves on the land, in two kinds. Hidden slots are ground for the written history (#27). Each has 2–3 candidate spots in the wild, and each game's seed picks one, so the same story lies somewhere different and a guide can't give it away. The history names each slot and ties it to a dead cultivator, a vanished sect or a lost blessed land.
