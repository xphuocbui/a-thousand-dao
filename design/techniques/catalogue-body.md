# Techniques: body family (công pháp)

Family `body`: element-less fist, palm and thể tu (body-cultivation) arts. Three body techniques carry all 8 signature arts of the
family: one per suite and one carrier for the two arts with no suite. They name no element. Passives are plain numbers that feed each
engine; W is the lint's price at full mastery.

## Unbroken Flesh Canon (Bất Hoại Nhục Kinh)
Kind: body. Tier 0 (Hoàng), 6 layers. Names: none. Suite `unbroken-flesh-canon`.

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 1 | Marrow Tithe Strike (Tủy Cống Quyền) |
| 3 | Bulwark Knuckle (Thuẫn Quyền) |
| 5 | Last Breath Fist (Tàn Tức Quyền) |

Passives (7.9 W of 8):
- Body shield +6 pp of health (3.1 W)
- Health regeneration, increased +10 % (3.0 W)
- Maximum health +3 % (1.8 W)

Bonuses: none (Hoàng).

Build use: health is the resource. Marrow Tithe pays health and writes Debt, Last Breath and Bulwark spend it. The shield and
regeneration passives make the pay sustainable and add to the shield Bulwark scales on; the extra maximum health makes each tithe a
smaller share. Stack shield size, healing and regeneration around it, and fire all three arts within 2 s to open every top step.

## Hundred Echo Palm Scripture (Bách Hưởng Chưởng Kinh)
Kind: body. Tier 1 (Huyền), 9 layers. Names: none. Suite `hundred-echo-palm-scripture`.

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 1 | Answering Palm (Ứng Hưởng Chưởng) |
| 2 | Twin Shadow Fist (Song Ảnh Quyền) |
| 4 | Rolling Barrage Fist (Cổn Liên Quyền) |

Passives (15.9 W of 16):
- Crit chance +4 pp, palm (2.7 W)
- Art speed +8 %, physical (3.8 W)
- Crit damage +20 pp, palm (4.0 W)
- Damage increased +6 %, palm (4.0 W)
- Art cost -3 %, physical (1.4 W)

Bonuses: Linh lực +10 %.

Build use: a rhythm engine. Art speed and cheaper arts fire more arts per pulse, which fills Tempo and the palm echo; crit chance
feeds the Barrage's crit stacks and the double's speed-up. Stack art speed, crit and cooldown cuts, and a Slowed Time source as the
outside key to the top steps.

## Mountain Bone Body Art (Sơn Cốt Thể Thuật)
Kind: body. Tier 0 (Hoàng), 6 layers. Names: none. Carrier (no suite) for the group-less arts.

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 2 | Mountain Root Stance (Sơn Căn Thế) |
| 5 | Bone-Turning Palm (Chuyển Cốt Chưởng) |

Passives (7.7 W of 8):
- Block +8 rating (3.2 W)
- Body shield +4 pp of health (2.0 W)
- Resistance +40 pts against physical (1.3 W)
- Damage increased +1.5 %, force (1.2 W)

Bonuses: none (Hoàng).

Build use: a defensive base. Block, shield and resistance deepen what the stance gives and feed Bulwark Knuckle's shield routes; the
Palm's Force share gains from the Force damage line, and it counts as a palm art for any echo. Pair it with the Unbroken Flesh Canon
or the Hundred Echo Palm Scripture.
