# Techniques: element-less Force (Chấn) and Chaos (Hỗn Độn)

Family `force-chaos`: 8 signature arts in two suites plus two loose arts, carried by 3 techniques. Each names one base
element (water, earth, metal; changed from Primordial Chaos `hon_don` 2026-11-11 so most roots can slot them; the arts
are element-less). Hollow Tide Scripture is the main
method; the other two are secret arts that ride beside any main. Passive W are from `lint_techniques.py`.

## Hollow Tide Scripture (Không Triều Kinh)

Main method, Earth (Địa, tier 2), 12 layers. Names: `thuy`. Suite `hollow-tide-scripture`.

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 1 | Marrow-Siphon Palm (Tủy Hấp Chưởng) |
| 2 | Hollow Husk Stance (Không Xác Thế) |
| 4 | Hollowing Strike (Khoét Rỗng Kích) |

Passives (23.9 W of 24): force damage +8 % (broad), over-time damage +9 %, status duration +12 % (implied over_time),
force art cost -8 %, linh lực leech 2 %.
Bonuses: linh_luc_pct +12, khi_huyet_pct +10.

Use: a drain build. Qi Disruption burn ticks build Drink and return linh lực; the passives raise tick damage and
lengthen the burn, and the cost cut pays for the Husk stance's 35 % tax. Stack burn sources (basic hits under Husk,
other tick arts) and anything that lowers foe linh lực so Hollowing Strike's dry-foe route is open.

## Ruin Ledger Scripture (Phế Trướng Kinh)

Secret art, Profound (Huyền, tier 1), 9 layers. Names: `tho`. Suite `ruin-ledger-scripture`.

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fraying Thread (Tơ Rã) |
| 3 | Ripening Curse (Thục Chú) |
| 6 | Gathered Ruin Verdict (Tụ Phế Phán) |

Passives (15.6 W of 16): curse art speed +12 %, curse status duration +20 %, chaos damage +6 %, curse art cost -2 %.
Bonuses: tam_ma_resist_pct +10.

Use: a curse-stacking build. Fray rises with every status or stack batch from any art, so pair it with element-stack
or multi-status arts; faster curse arts and longer curses keep four distinct statuses on the foe, the no-payment route
for all three arts. Cast the Thread, then Ripening, then spend on the Verdict.

## Tearing Force Rites (Xé Chấn Nghi)

Secret art, Yellow (Hoàng, tier 0), 6 layers. Names: `kim`. Carrier for the two loose arts (suite: none).

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 1 | Shearing Palm (Cắt Xé Chưởng) |
| 3 | Bloodletting Rite (Phóng Huyết Nghi) |

Passives (7.4 W of 8): force crit chance +5 pp, health leech 10 %, chaos resistance penetration +20.
Bonuses: none (Yellow).

Use: a force-to-chaos crit build. Shearing Palm's crit tear wants crit chance; the leech repays Bloodletting Rite's
health cost. Stack crit and conversion around it, and keep health low enough for the Rite to pay out.
