# Ice (Băng) technique catalogue

Family: ice. Two techniques, one per suite; every ice art has a `group`, so no carrier techniques are needed. Both are built around Ice stacks on the foe: the first (a main method) locks and shatters them, the second (a secret art) freezes timers, bursts shields and turns the stacks into healing. Passives are plain numbers that feed the stack engine (stack size and length, art speed, cost, cold damage); the rules live in the arts. Suite arts all unlock by layer 6.

## Winter Toll Canon (Đông Toán Kinh)

- Kind: main method (tâm pháp). Tier: Profound (Huyền), 9 layers. Names: `bang` (ice).

| Layer | Art unlocked |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rime-Lock Seal (Sương Tỏa Ấn) |
| 2 | Deep Winter Form (Thâm Đông Thế) |
| 4 | Black Ice Glaze (Huyền Băng Ấn) |
| 6 | Shatter Palm (Toái Băng Chưởng) |

Passives (15.8 W of 16):
- Ice-stack duration +12 % (`stack_duration_pct`)
- Ice-stack effect +6 % (`stack_effect_pct`)
- Art cost -9 %, seal arts (`art_cost_pct`)
- Art speed +6 %, seal arts (`art_speed_pct`)
- Damage increased +3 %, cold (`attack_pct`)

Bonuses: linh lực +12 %.

Build use: feeds the cost-lock and cash-in engine. Longer, larger stacks reach the 10 and 15 Ice thresholds of Seal and Palm; cheaper, faster seals fire the three-step pattern sooner. Stack around it with Ice stackers (Sleet Volley, Frost Seal) and cold damage; Frost builds from Seal, Palm and the form pays the thaw break.
