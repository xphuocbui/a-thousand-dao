# Water (Thủy) techniques (công pháp)

Family: water. Three techniques carry the 8 Water signature arts: one per suite plus one carrier for the group-less
Backwash Seal. They exist to give the Water engines (Tide, Slowed Time, echo) a home whose passives feed them:
healing, regeneration, art speed, stack duration, linh lực regen. Passive W is the lint's figure (W = value x price
÷ breadth). The `abilities` count of 4 on the first technique exceeds cong-phap rule 17's "1-3" (the lint notes it).
