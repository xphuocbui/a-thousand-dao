# Metal (Kim) technique catalogue

Three techniques carry the 8 Metal signature arts: one per suite plus one carrier for the group-less Iron Hide Stance.
Passives are plain numbers that feed each suite's engine (Metal stacks and their duration, crit, art speed, curses).
Numbers are first guesses; `design/techniques/catalogue-metal.json` is the data.

## Nine-Temper Scripture (Cửu Luyện Kinh)
Main method (main), Yellow (Hoàng, tier 0, 6 layers), names: kim.

| Layer | Art |
|---|---|
| 1 | Whetstone Pass (Mài Phong Chưởng) |
| 2 | Blood-Quench Blade (Huyết Thối Nhận) |
| 4 | Sheathed Edge (Nhập Sao Nhận) |
| 6 | Shatter-Edge Cleave (Toái Nhận Trảm) |

Passives (7.7 W of 8): +4 crit chance (kim), +5 % art speed (kim), +10 % crit damage (kim), +4 % stack duration (kim).
Bonuses: none (tier 0).

Build use: feeds the Temper engine. Crit chance and art speed make Temper arrive fast and bank Sheathed Edge strikes;
stack duration keeps Metal buff stacks alive for the Cleave route. Stack quick Metal arts, crit and health-cost reduction;
Blood-Quench's low health opens Sheathed Edge step 3.
