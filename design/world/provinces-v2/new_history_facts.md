# New history facts in the provinces-v2 lore (for review)

**Status:** proposal, 2026-11-23 night; checked against canon by an independent reviewer on 2026-11-24 (no hard contradictions; one territory slip, seven dates and five readings fixed in the lore files). The writers who wrote the province and place lore
(`design/world/provinces-v2/lore/`) added these small facts. All are dated before 1200, and as far as the writers
checked, none contradicts `world_history.json` or `world_place_lore.json`. None is in the annals yet. This list is
gathered from the writers' reports; the text itself is in the lore files.

## Tùng Đạo Cung (regions 1, 4, 5)

- **Ruins:**
  - Cựu Châu Trấn (475–749): its pearl beds died in the 748 freeze.
  - Hỏa Tẫn Trấn (410–548): burned before Bạch Hoa was named in 560.
  - Luyện Thiết Trấn (410–540): smelted iron for the Thiết Tỏa chain.
  - Cựu Hà Trấn (150–282): left dry by the river shift that ended Đông Lưu Thành.
  - Tĩnh Thủy Quán (310–702): drowned in the flood of 700.
  - Phá Chu Trấn and Phân Thủy Môn (to 786–788): their survivors joined Lãng Đào Môn in 790.
- **Disasters:**
  - an ice shove, 1033;
  - a landslide, 1104;
  - a rockfall, 1012;
  - an avalanche, 1151;
  - an earthquake, 1134.
- **Small fallen sects:** Bắc Minh Quán, Lãnh Hương Viện, Vọng Triều Các, Khô Mộc Tự, Tuyết Thiền Tự, Lăng Tiêu Các and
  Cô Hạc Quán.
- **Proposed sects:** Sương Tùng Các (1060), Tuần Tùng Đường (1191, after the 1190 wolf caravans) and Ngạo Tuyết Phái
  (1105).

## Thúy Vi Tông (regions 2, 3)

- **Disasters:**
  - an earthquake, 1033;
  - an avalanche, 1019;
  - a flood, 1148;
  - the coughing sickness of 760 emptied Hoàng Bá Trấn.
- **A new Thôn Hải raid:** pirates burned Lam Triều Các in 744.
- **Ruins and forts:**
  - Quan Lan Bảo (to 790): left after the Purge of 781.
  - Vọng Bắc Bảo (672–910).
  - Lô Khẩu Trấn: emptied under Tùng Đào's resin tax from 674.
- **Proposed sects:** Vụ Ẩn Môn (1050) and Hộ Lâm Đường (910).

## Băng Tâm Các (regions 7, 8, 9)

- **Desert Road War:**
  - Lang Yên Bảo (to 726);
  - Lãnh Hương Các (to 724), lost riding to Cô Thành.
- **Dạ Quang:**
  - Huyết Đao Trại, burned by Băng Tâm in 1121;
  - Khuy Tinh Bảo (1100–1189), withdrawn after the 1188 rejection.
- **War of the Frost Coast:** Thoa Lạp Trấn, Sóc Phương Thành and Cố Ngạn Bảo (to 497–498).
- **Other ruins:** Bắc Đình Thành and Lũng Đầu Trại were emptied after Hồ Thiên's eruption in 425.
- **Disasters:** an avalanche in 1012, ice beasts in 1102, and the sea freezing in 655.
- **Proposed sects:** Tầm Kiếm Đường (1162), Trấn Sa Đường (1125) and Lãnh Giang Phái (1140, close in name to the
  fallen Hàn Giang Môn).

## Lạc Hà Tông (regions 10–13)

- **Ruins:**
  - Vọng Phàm Quán: Cô Phàm's watch hall (430–708), kept three years after the fleet was lost. It is not seat V-4.
  - Phiêu Bồng Quán (860–1102).
  - Hoa Ổ Viện: burned in the Petal Shore fighting of 618. It is not seat V-1.
  - Phù Dao Quán (760–1121).
- **Town ruins and their falls:** sand (748), a storm surge (988), a river move (860), a storm (870), a karst collapse
  (912) and spent silver (1040).
- **Customs:** a dusk silence for 803 in four villages.
- **Proposed sects:** Nhàn Vân Quán (905), Nhạn Trận Môn (806, by survivors of Chiết Kích Nguyên), Minh Loan Các (960)
  and Yên Quang Các (1065).

## Thiên Xu Tông (regions 6, 14, 15)

- **Sects:**
  - Bạch Lộc Quán (370–612), which asked for the 380 hunting ban;
  - Khuy Thiên Các (380–650).
- **Disasters:**
  - a drought, 940;
  - an earthquake, 912, which emptied Lục Hợp Thành.
- **Forts and outposts:**
  - Hắc Phong Trại, held by tán tu bandits 880–940;
  - Phá Quân Môn's posts, taken by Thiên Xu's siege of 871–874;
  - Tuần Hải Bảo, held 650–790;
  - outposts tied to the battle of 803.
- **Proposed sects:** Họa Tinh Đường (910), Hoa Cái Đường (1060) and Chân Kim Các (980).

## Phượng Minh Cung (regions 16, 17, 20)

- **Cửu Thiều's fall of 340/341:** five ruins: Minh Cổ Bảo, Trúc Địch Viện, Kích Khánh Đường, Tiêu Sơn Thị and Ngọc
  Quản Trấn. None is the palace itself or near the hidden candidate tiles.
- **Xích Thành:**
  - towns burned in 588 and 590;
  - Lưu Hỏa Các's lower courts covered by lava in 845.
- **Thôn Hải raids:**
  - towns burned in 764, 767 and 771;
  - the shore camp Ẩn Phàm Trại, burned by Phượng Minh's fleet in 780.
- **Disasters:** a fever (812), a landslide (1062) and a storm tide (913).
- **Proposed sects:** Bách Vũ Các (1110), Vọng Triều Đường (790) and Chức Hà Các (1150).

## Thính Vũ Lâu (regions 18, 19)

- **The biggest addition: an eruption in 352** buried Hạ Lôi Thành, a Khải Lôi city about 3 tiles from the Tower.
  Phó Tiêu Tương built on the quiet cone in 380.
- **After the Night Rain Edict (905):** Trúc Ẩn Viện walked north in 906, and the lamp town Thanh Đăng Trấn had emptied by 930.
- **Thunder Vein War:** Phượng Minh burned Thủ Lôi Bảo in 1042, and beasts took Thanh Thạch Bảo in 1041.
- **Pirates:** Thôn Hải burned Lan Tân Trấn in 779.
- **Truce of 805:** the Tower pulled down Vọng Xu Bảo by 806.
- **The 1199 earthquake** also split Sơn Khẩu Trại.
- **Proposed sects:** Vũ Linh Đường (930) and Cốc Vũ Quán (610).
