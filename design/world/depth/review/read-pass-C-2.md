# Fresh read, pass C-2 (depth and tone)

Sample: the six outside groups, plus lac_ha, thinh_vu, phat_y and thai_chi. 453 living people. Read against `_brief-fix-C.md` and the diff from the last commit.

## Verdict (3 lines)
1. The harsher layer works. Deeds are mostly concrete and cost a named person. The demonic group now frightens: 7 Cruel with deeds (cellar child, bricked boy, drunk wells). The best items read as unchanged and intact.
2. Tier B secrets are acts in progress and well varied. The weak spots are mechanical: 23 hand-typed names that do not match the cast, one tie that contradicts a hook (Nghiêm Hồi Hương), and tier C looks written to one template in thinh_vu, thai_chi and phat_y.
3. Counts overshoot or hit targets (see patterns). Fix the 10 items below. None needs a redesign.

## The 10 weakest items

1. **nghiem_hoi_huong** (thai_chi). She "has kept the one answer Lữ Tẫn Chu sent". §9 says Lữ "is still waiting for her brother to answer a letter", hook 1 says he "answers at last", and lu_tan_chu's record says he has not answered. Her upbringing is war_orphan, but she is a flood foundling. Her twin **nghiem_hoac_huong** is street_orphan, and twins must share one.
   - Fix: she hides Lữ Niệm Hà's *outgoing* letters, or the answer is the one still to come (hook 1). Set both twins to street_orphan.
2. **banh_nhi_ho** and **sam_van_nuong** (loose-south). Sầm's secret says Bành thinks the girl is dead and "has sent condolence silver". Bành's backstory says he "has already sent the Hall a lock of her hair and a map". Bành's Cruel trait has no cruel deed: it is a betrayal. His upbringing wild_raised differs from his brother **banh_liep_ho** (village_born).
   - Fix: date the hair-and-map before the grave, so Bành sold her and now mourns for show. Give him a cruelty (a beaten dog or boy). Make the brothers match.
3. **lo_niem_ca** (lac_ha). The cellar captive is vivid. But `tung_dao.md` records no missing raider, and the 1175 toll peace stands. The lac_ha record says the raiders' leader (Nhiếp Thừa Phong) and Lô Tiểu Ca died together on the bridge (N-lac_ha-09), so "which rope-cutter killed his mother" has a known answer.
   - Fix: ask "who held the ropes". Add one tung_dao §9 line or a named C person gone since spring. `hinh_trong_son` lending oil then breaks the peace secretly, which is good.
