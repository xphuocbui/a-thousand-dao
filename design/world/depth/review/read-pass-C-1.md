# Read pass C-1: bang_tam, phuong_minh, thien_xu, tung_dao, coc_vu, hong_dau

Sample: 521 people (all cast files of the six sects), compared with the last commit.

## Verdict

1. The pass works on counts and on tier B. Dark traits now sit at Cruel 22 (4.2 %), Faithless 25 (4.8 %), Callous 24 (4.6 %), Arbitrary 20 (3.8 %), Lazy 26 (5.0 %), Greedy 46 (8.8 %) and Deceitful 51 (9.8 %). Village-born is 36.9 %, flaws 50 (9.6 %), 392 looks added. The tier B wants and secrets are the best part, and Băng Tâm's tier A is untouched (checked field by field: only the dead founder gained an appearance).
2. The tier C deeds are the weak part. 134 traits were swapped and 125 deeds were added, nearly all as one bare closing sentence. Only about 24 name a victim by key, and only about 30 if names without keys are counted. The rest hurt "a junior", "raiders" or "a boy". The registers are narrow: Greedy and Faithless are mostly selling something, Deceitful is "tells X the opposite", Arbitrary is "by mood", Cruel is a maimed junior.
3. Fix three real contradictions (te_vong_quy / lieu_da_thai / te_niem_quy; lo_niem_ca against Tùng Đào; the secrets that repeat a backstory sentence), then break the repeated phrasings in the tier C looks. Nothing is flattened except nhiep_lang_tuyet.

## The 10 weakest items

1. **te_vong_quy / lieu_da_thai / te_niem_quy (hong_dau): the three records contradict.**
   - lieu_da_thai's secret: she drugs Vọng Quy's night tea "so he does not wander the garden and meet his daughter". That says he does not know the girl is his.
   - te_vong_quy's secret: he has found out his daughter is the bean-scatterer and walks the path behind her every night. His wants line says "his daughter gone from the garden before anyone learns whose she is". He knows, and he is awake at night.
   - te_niem_quy's backstory: "no one at the manor has caught her yet". He has.
   - Fix: make the draught keep him from the guests' wing. Let him tip the tea into the trellis. Change Niệm Quy's line to "only her father has caught her, and he has said nothing".

2. **lo_niem_ca (lac_ha.2) against Tùng Đào: Tùng Đào's side does not allow it.**
   - Tùng Đào has no 1199 raid on the Ngư Ca bank. The Ferry War ended with the 1175 toll split (`tung_dao.md` §3 and §8), and no cast file or §9 line has a person missing or taken.
   - The question "which rope-cutter killed my mother" is already answered: N-tung_dao-06, Nhiếp Thừa Phong cut every bridge and died on the last, and everyone knows it. A captive would add nothing.
   - Fix, either of two:
     - Add a §9 line and a tier C name: a Coiled Root Hall patrol scout (an Đàm ferry-hand) did not come back from Ngư Ca last spring, and Nhiếp Cô Tân thinks he drowned.
     - Make Lô's captive one of Nhiếp Thừa Phong's rope crew and change the question to "who gave the order". Then Tùng Đào's own order-and-leaving chain (§9 "The six on the ice") has a mirror.

3. **The secret repeats a backstory sentence: la_tiem_da, mac_kinh_trap, nhiep_minh_tien.**
   - The backstory now says what the secret says: "sells each night's rain twice", "writes to Lệ Tiêu Hồn in a false name", "blames the copyist and rewrites it". A secret should not also be public history.
   - Fix: cut those three backstory sentences. Keep the secret.

4. **nhiep_lang_tuyet (bang_tam) is damaged.**
   - The old Just / Patient / Stubborn woman who walks the line at midnight with warmed stones, and whom Đỗ Sương Nhi pretends not to see, was a tender detail. The new line, "hands the stones only to the sitters she likes", turns it into favouritism, and Arbitrary replaces Just.
   - Fix: restore Just. Put Arbitrary on someone who already has a petty hobby, for example ong_tinh_hai, who pours first for whom he fancies and is already there.
   - Also, ky_han_bach's "two lost toes" repeats quach_lam's "two washers lost toes". Change one to fingers or a split lip.

5. **Deeds with no named victim or cost: ton_han_thach, do_vo_cuu, dai_ho_than, mong_chu_tieu, phan_dong_nhi, ha_cuc_thi, vu_tinh_giai.**
   - The victims are "raiders", "the innkeeper", "a boy", "a pony-boy", "a seal pup", "a dying gardener's daughter" and "the beggars". Nobody to be paid back, so nothing for a later event to use.
   - Example fixes:
     - do_vo_cuu: the innkeeper is Nguyên Hảo Khách's (nguyen_hao_khach) brother, who runs a guest-house.
     - ha_cuc_thi: she refused the one night off a dying gardener asked to sit his daughter's last watch, and he has not looked at her since. Tie him to doan_thai_dau, the girl who is looked at as if known.
     - dai_ho_than: name the fainted boy, using a tier C key from the seat guard.

6. **bac_hai_am against phan_ba_sinh (tung_dao).**
   - bac_hai_am secretly teaches Phan Ba Sinh the Tide Rite's last tide because Nhâm Thu Lãng "is too slow", and means to leave the method to "the faster boy".
   - phan_ba_sinh was swapped to Lazy and "lies on the shingle through half of every midwinter swim". The elder chose the worst swimmer in the Rite.
   - Fix: make the swap Lazy → Reclusive on Ba Sinh, and give the Lazy line to nham_thu_lang's rival. Or write the secret as the elder's mistake ("he thinks the boy lies down to rest the tide; the boy is asleep").

7. **Flaws that do not show, and flaws that are only labels.**
   - Do not show: kha_tieu_dau (homely; the look is chestnut knuckles), uong_tuyet_nhan (unlucky; nothing), mi_kim_bao (slow_witted; "merchant's soft belly"), thach_tieu_lo (slow_witted; "loud voice"), vi_tu_han and phu_suong_nguyen (thin_soul; gentle eyes, blue lips), mong_chu_tieu (restless_heart; only "asked three times").

   - Fix: one concrete tell each. Examples: kha_tieu_dau "a face the dye-stall girls call a boiled turnip"; uong_tuyet_nhan "the third hat this month, lost to the lake"; thach_tieu_lo "counts on her fingers and loses the count at seven".

8. **One-sided cross-sect secrets: thu_ngan_hanh, thieu_lang_ba, tan_mac_bach.**
   - thu_ngan_hanh (phuong_minh) reads Hall of Trust letters aloud to Ngụy Thanh Hàn (bang_tam), who "pays in northern pepper". Ngụy's own wants and secret never mention him, and §9 of neither page has the channel.
   - thieu_lang_ba (tung_dao) flashes a lake mirror and "Băng Tâm's watch answers". Nothing in bang_tam answers.
   - tan_mac_bach (tung_dao) trades malachite to "a Thiên Xu surveyor's apprentice". No such apprentice exists in thien_xu.
   - Fix: add one clause on the other side each time, or name a person who is already there.

9. **The coc_vu cast is one story told five times.** Selling the abbey's goods:
   - thoi_tri_tiet sells the abbey's rain;
   - la_tiem_da sells it twice;
   - khang_vu_ngung sells it and tells the buyers her master's rain runs thin;
   - duong_ha_thao sells the pills' herb;
   - so_loi_duan takes the prior's money.
   - Fix: keep thoi_tri_tiet and la_tiem_da. Give khang_vu_ngung a different betrayal, for example she lets the river rain fail on the night of Lã Tiềm Dạ's call so that her own comes first. Give duong_ha_thao a funny one, for example he counts the herb jars aloud every night and tells the prior he has found a thief, who is himself.

10. **The tier C looks: one frame, across whole files.**
    - The tung_dao frame: every tier C start is "A [adj], [adj] boy/girl/man with X and Y". Across all six files, 226 of 392 new looks start "A ", and 315 of 392 are two-part "X and Y". Many are an occupation's tell (a diver's lungs, a weigher's fingers), which shows the job and not the person.
    - Hong_dau toad-skin gloves: banh_tri_hap, tien_kho_dang and khong_vu_kieu all wear them, with ho_yen_do's buyer a fourth. Keep one.
    - Fix: rewrite about a third as fragments with no "A" and one odd, unreasoned detail ("two different socks, both his", "answers to the wrong name and does not mind").

## The 3 best new items

1. **ha_lang_han (bang_tam, B) with bui_han (C).** She breaks his sword wrist "a little each practice bout, out of sight" so he cannot call her out before she rides for Cô Thành, and Phó Ám Hương "has not looked at the boy's hand". His look is "a wrist wrapped in a hand's width of cloth", and he "glares at Hạ Lăng Hàn". A deed, a victim, a cost, a witness who will not look, and the two records agree.
2. **ta_khac_thach with ta_tieu_bi (thien_xu).** The father secretly eases the Thủ Bi stele back a rope's length a night and tells his son to say nothing to Thiên Cơ's men. The son "tells the Thiên Cơ apprentices where his father hides his measuring book, for a drink", and the book is the only proof the stele ever moved. A dramatic irony that needs no further explanation.
3. **do_suong_nhi (bang_tam, B).** She pays the tea-boy to keep a kettle warm behind the reeds each solstice for sitters who wet themselves, and signs the bill as repairs. Kind, small, funny, an act in progress, and it ties to trac_on_tuyen's steaming palms. A secret that is neither a crime nor a plot.

Honourable mention: ke_chu_sa (phuong_minh), a Callous secret in which she tests a pill substitute a thimble at a time on the outer gate's tea servants and logs their headaches.
