# Brief: content reader (world-depth review, 2026-11-25)

You review authored content for a Godot cultivation (tu tiên) game. It was written overnight by about thirty writer
agents, each told to match one pilot sect (Băng Tâm Các). Your job is to find what is **weak**, not to praise what is
good. Assume nothing is good until you have read it.

## The rubric: the user's own complaint, before the content was written

> "I feel like, the world is a bit non-connected right now. For example, Băng Tâm Các, except for its leader, none of
> its core member actually are thủy or băng linh căn. And their site is not even on a icy region, which makes little
> sense for me. Many things like this, it doesn't feel very deep or well defined. I feel like a lot of the cast member
> lacks a bit of depth as well. And the trait system, for some reason feels very shallow [...] I want a better nicely
> written content. And we are also lacking contents for items, especially the main cast, they should have their own
> item set at least, and their own build."

## Read against it, and also for

- **Pilot-cloning.** The same plot shape, role or arc across sects: a passed-over senior disciple, an unnamed heir, a
  dying elder and one pill, a hostage, a hidden root courted by a demon, the last of a fallen line. The script found a
  succession or heir quarrel in 37 of 50 sect pages. Which are the same story with the names changed?
- **Voice sameness.** Every person written in one register: restrained, secret-keeping, "has never", "for forty years".
  Do people differ in how they are written, not only in what they do?
- **Too virtuous.** Diligent, Brave, Honest and Loyal dominate; Cruel 9, Faithless 10. Where should the world be
  harsher, pettier or funnier?
- **Depth that is not connection.** A backstory that names another person without the tie meaning anything. Ties that
  are only "friend".
- **Sense.** Roots against land and sect, ages against realms and events, history against the chronicle (events are cited
  by id), builds against the person's story (a sword cultivator with no sword, a route that ignores the lineage),
  items against their holder.
- **Craft.** Names in correct Hán-Việt with matching Hán characters; no names or characters from existing novels,
  dramas or games; English that is plain and concrete, not filler.
- **Rulings now in force** (`design/world/depth/DECISIONS-2026-11-25.md`): no terrain, weather, place, time-of-day or
  season conditions in fight rules; no caps inside pieces.

Read `design/world/depth/review/script-findings.md` first. It has counts that point where to look.

## Write

Only `design/world/depth/review/<your-name>.md`:
- **Verdict** in 3-5 lines: how close your slice comes to the user's ask.
- **The 10-20 weakest items**, worst first. Each gets: where it is (file and key or section); what is wrong; **a
  concrete fix** (the rewritten sentence, the new tie, the different arc, the changed root or gear); and a severity of
  must-fix, should-fix or nice-to-have.
- **Patterns** across your slice, with counts.
- **The 3-5 best items**, so the user has a short reading list of what works.

Do not edit any other file. Do not commit. Prefix scratchpad files with your name. Reply in under 200 words: your
verdict and your top 5 findings.
