# Content review: ranked findings (2026-11-25)

Six fresh readers and one statistics script reviewed the night's content (cast, events, builds, items, lineages)
against the user's original complaint. The full reviews, with concrete rewrites for each finding:
- `read-great-1.md`: Băng Tâm, Thúy Vi, Tùng Đào, Lạc Hà
- `read-great-2.md`: Thiên Xu, Thính Vũ, Phượng Minh, and the cross-sect threads
- `read-minor-1.md`: the 25 minor sects of the north and centre
- `read-minor-2.md`: the 18 minor sects of the south
- `read-outside-events.md`: the six outside groups and the new history
- `read-builds.md`: builds, items, sets and lineages
- `script-findings.md`: the counts

**Verdict, in one line:** the world now answers the user's concrete asks. Roots fit land and sect everywhere (all
members pass their gate except Cửu Cao's riders), seats sit on fitting land, every tier A person has a lineage build
and a set, and the outside groups and shared wars really connect the sects. It falls short on **variety**: about six
plot shapes repeat across 50 sects, the voice is one restrained register, the world is too kind, and tier B and C are
thin. A layer of **mechanical errors** also needs a sweep.

## A. Mechanical errors (must fix; a sweep can do them)

1. **Caps survive the no-caps ruling** in at least 40 pieces in about 28 files, including the ruling's own examples
   (`pm-ash-seat` "up to 30 %", `tx-archive-desk` "at most three"), and in `builds/authored/` sources. (all readers)
2. **Phất Y Cốc copies Jin Yong's *Hiệp Khách Hành*:** the Lý Bạch verse cut in rock, an art no one could read, and a
   head surnamed Thạch. This breaks the no-existing-IP rule. (read-minor-2)
3. **cross-sect.md has two facts wrong:** it says Tạ Thiết Trụ lived (he died), and it says Mạnh Lưu Ly doesn't know
   her father (she does). (read-great-2)
4. **Timeline slips:**
   - Tiết Hàn Mai lays the Array in 760, the year she is born.
   - Kỳ Tùng Ẩn's "first century alone" ends at 26.
   - Founders die of "old age" at 710 and 790 under a Nguyên Anh lifespan of 1,000.
   - Lận Viễn Phàm's first boat is dated both 1158 and 1171.
   - A living person is noted "under the ice", and a living son is called "late".
   - Wave 3: one leaked secret (`ninh_ha_vien`), one date clash (`cat_linh_hanh`) and 39 doubled ties.
   (read-great-1, read-outside-events, read-minor-1)
5. **Distances:** a tile is 25 km, but pages write tiles as walks. Porters carry lake water about 200 km "by night";
   graves about 250 km from the seat are swept "every morning". This affects Lễ Tuyền, Thu Phần, Hồng Đậu, Thối Tinh
   and Phần Vũ. (read-minor-2)
6. **Weapon schools without weapons:**
   - 12 sword cultivators have no sword, among them the pilot head Sở Ngọc Hồ (a ward build labelled a sword
     cultivator) and Mạnh Dạ Vũ, whose canon "cuts once" with no blade.
   - 35 of the Tower's 91 swordsmen carry no sword.
   - Tuần Tùng's bow-and-staff school carries neither.
   - The item bases still lack a bow, staff and sabre. (read-builds, read-great-2, read-minor-1)
7. **One named spirit beast for 18 tier A/B beast tamers.** Birds, a horse and others are referenced by sets but have
   no records. (read-builds)
8. **48 tier A routes are leftovers from before the lineages** (placeholder note), 25 of them heads' routes; some heads
   skip their own sect's route. (read-builds)
9. **Cửu Cao breaks R2:** six inner riders hold Wind 26-40, below the core rank's 45. (read-minor-2)
10. **Refiners have no archetype** (a system gap, noted for the design). (read-builds)
