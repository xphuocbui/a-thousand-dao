# Breaking the repeated story shapes: the plan (group B, 2026-11-25)

This plan covers RANKED.md group B (items 11-17) and the Phất Y IP rewrite (A2). A writer applies it **one sect at a
time**: read the sect's block in §4, edit its page (§9, §12, and §1/§3 where named) and the named cast records, then
fix the dependent lines listed under "Keep consistent". The mechanical sweep (group A) is running at the same time.
Where a sweep rename lands first (Thải Chi's herb names, Đan Thừa Củ), use the new key.

Sources: §9 and §12 of all 50 pages, §1 and the §4 taboos of all 50, §2-§8 of Phất Y, the five `read-*.md`,
`script-findings.md`, `cross-sect.md` §1 and the 1150 rows of every page and cast file.

**Rules for every rewrite**
- **A secret is an act in progress.** Someone is doing something now, and it will be found out or finished: selling,
  forging, teaching, paying, drinking or skimming. It is not "X has told no one about Y". Each block below names the act.
- **Change the shape, not the cited facts.** Chronicle ids (C, A, V) are game data and keep their years and text. A
  thread that `cross-sect.md` cites keeps its facts: who did what, and when. The arc around it changes.
- **Do not land on the other flagged engines** either: a ledger that exposes the head (8 pages), a lost top layer of
  the canon (5), "two houses that do not agree" (10), a junior who "has begun to" notice (12 backstories), a Kết Đan
  who refuses the headship (3), a dying witness who wants to tell before the end (Quản Thập Thù, Bàng Lãnh Tôi, Mi Túc
  Thụ, Bồ Tàn Chiếu, Bồ Tọa Tuyết), or "sail past the charts" (11 people in 3 hooks).
- **Harsher.** Each changed sect gets at least one person whose part in the arc is cruel, callous, greedy or petty, and
  whose traits say so (C18 in RANKED.md).
- **A head may still be old and heirless as a fact.** What goes is the §9 bullet and the hook built on it.

### 4.2 The changed sects, one block each

Each block gives:
- **Arc:** what is happening now.
- **People:** who carries it.
- **Fits because:** the trade, land or doctrine it grows from.
- **Act:** the secret in progress.
- **Cut:** what leaves §9 and §12.
- **Keep consistent:** the threads and events that must still hold.

#### bach_vu: the gift stolen from the receiver
- **Arc.** Tạ Thanh Loan means to give the palace head nine red-crested writ birds and ask for the writs. The eggs
  came out of the Wutong aviary, the palace's own. Make it uglier, as the reviewer asks: Ổ Linh Tước killed the Wutong
  hen to take the clutch, which breaks the pavilion's first taboo (killing a bird), and Tạ knows. The arc is the day of
  the gift: it is magnificent, and everyone who touched it is guilty.
- **People.** `ta_thanh_loan`, `o_linh_tuoc`, `nhieu_tieu_yen`, `ta_thanh_thuoc`; palace `vu_ngo_dong`,
  `hoa_trieu_duong`.
- **Fits because.** Bird keepers whose creed is "all birds bow to the phoenix"; a gift of birds is their only road to
  standing.
- **Act.** Nhiêu Tiểu Yến, who feeds the nine, is teaching them to come to Tạ's whistle and not the palace's, so the
  gift will fly home on its first morning (funny, and the palace will not find it so).
- **Cut.** "Seventy years" and hook 3 (the Kết Đan pill clock).
- **Keep consistent.** Vu Ngô Đồng "has not counted his eggs"; A248 (the silent day of 1195); hook 7 (Trúc Thực's rats).

#### binh_thuy: the teacher who teaches a turn wrong
- **Arc.** Replace "The heir" and "The fascicle under the floor" with the reviewer's line. Lương Bạch Tần teaches the
  Still Moon Tide a turn wrong to every heir Bùi names, and that, not his fickleness, is why four of them failed. Sa
  Kình Nhi is the fifth, and she is about to stall at the same turn.
- **People.** `luong_bach_tan`, `bui_phieu_binh`, `sa_kinh_nhi`.
- **Fits because.** A sect of strangers that refuses no one. Lương left her master for Bùi and resents the heirs he
  finds on beaches.
- **Act.** The wrong turn, taught every month.
- **Cut.** The heir bullet. Hook 3 becomes "Sa Kình Nhi stalls where the four stalled".
- **Keep consistent.** The sea-chest ledger (kept); N-binh_thuy-05 and -06; the one shared copy of the Still Moon Tide
  (§7).

#### chan_kim: cornering the square
- **Arc.** Tưởng Ngọc Cân's heavier scale is no kindness. She charges the miners a "good-side fee" to be weighed on her
  side, and the Pivot's assayer across the square has had no custom for two winters. The house is cornering the ore
  market of Ngọc Hành with the Pivot's own miners, and the Ôn toll house wants a share.
- **People.** `tuong_ngoc_can`, `tuong_bach_luyen`, `quan_thap_thu`, `on_song_ngoc`, `on_trong_luong`; Pivot
  `dang_quan_binh`.
- **Fits because.** Two scales on one square since 980, and the house's taboo is "weighing for the house". She weighs
  for herself.
- **Act.** The fee, taken each morning, and the true weight swapped in whenever anyone tests the scale.
- **Cut.** "The succession". "The honest man's lie" becomes: her grandfather knows, and has stopped asking where the
  house's new silver comes from.
- **Keep consistent.**
  - cross-sect §1.7 (`dang_quan_binh` has caught her twice).
  - Phùng Xích Kim's knowledge of Thối Tinh's iron becomes a guess (read-minor-2 item 5).
  - Hook 3 (the miners' side) gains teeth: the cheat paid more, and charged for it.

#### chuc_ha: the dowry is the price

#### coc_vu: the schism of 1180, and the drought that started a war
