# Reader review: minor sects of Thiên Xu, Thính Vũ and Phượng Minh (read-minor-2, 2026-11-25)

Slice: 18 minor sects (thoi_tinh, phat_y, cung_tinh, cuu_cao, chan_kim, hoa_cai, hoa_tinh, xuyen_lam, hong_dau, thu_phan,
vu_linh, coc_vu, truc_thuc, le_tuyen, phan_vu, bach_vu, vong_trieu, chuc_ha). Read: sections 1, 2, 4, 9 and 12 of every
page; all 29 tier A people; about 4 others per sect (one B, one C from the main file, two from each `.5` file: 72 in
all). Counts below are over all 345 people in the slice unless stated.

## Verdict

As cultures, the 18 sects are 18 different peoples. Each has its own trade, dress, food, burial and taboos, and no two
trades are the same. Roots match land and admission everywhere except Cửu Cao. Every tier A has a lineage build and
named gear. The user's complaint is mostly answered.

The weakness is the plot engine in §9 and §12. Half the sects run on one of three stories: the head is the last
holder of the element the re-lean took away; the sect's creed is broken in secret and a junior "has begun to" notice;
the sect is two houses that do not agree. The secrets are mostly kind lies.

The distances are wrong in five pages, because tiles are written as if they were short walks (a tile is 25 km). One
sect, Phất Y, is built on Jin Yong's *Hiệp Khách Hành* (*Ode to Gallantry*). Of the four no-lean sects in the slice,
Chân Kim and Xuyên Lâm are convincing, Vọng Triều is sound, and Thu Phần repeats the user's original complaint.

## The weakest items, worst first

### 1. Distances in tiles are written as walking distances (must-fix)

A tile is about 25 km (Travel & Map; the pages themselves say "67 tiles (some 1,700 km)").
- `le_tuyen.md` §9: "since 1141 the porters carry lake water up to it by night". The lake (193) is 8 tiles, about 200
  km, from the spring (26). The same section says Mai "moved the hall to the lake to make the carrying easy", which
  does not follow. **Fix:** "Since 1141 the Pivot's hundred jars are filled at the Dawn Hall from the lake and carted
  to the Spring House to be sealed. The carters think they carry healers' tinctures." This also gives a real reason
  for the move: the cut wine is brewed where the healers live.
- `thu_phan.md` §1 and §4: the seat is "ten tiles above the graves" (about 250 km), yet "the outer gate sweeps the
  graves every morning" and the Pavilion "goes down every night" in autumn. **Fix:** add a **Sweepers' Lodge** in the
  hamlet on the ruin (221) where the outer gate lives and the autumn chant is sung, and say that the hill is "a week
  up the river". A no-lean sect fits any seat, so moving the seat record to 221 is also free under R4.
- `hong_dau.md` §2: the back garden is "5 tiles" (about 125 km) from the door, by "a garden path". **Fix:** "The
  garden lies three days inland by a cart track that only the manor uses. A guest who drinks the second cup wakes on
  the cart." That is better horror anyway.
- `thoi_tinh.md` §1 says the coal mine is "within a morning's walk"; §2 says it is "nine tiles below" (about 225 km).
  **Fix:** "the coal comes up nine days by mule."
- `phan_vu.md` §2: the treasurer "lives next door", but the Fire Court is 5 tiles (about 125 km) away. **Fix:** "a
  day's ride down the road".

### 2. Phất Y Cốc is built on *Hiệp Khách Hành* (must-fix)

Jin Yong's *Hiệp Khách Hành* is built on Lý Bạch's 俠客行, cut into rock with an art hidden in it that no one could read
for generations until one man did. Its hero is Thạch Phá Thiên (石破天). In `phat_y.md` the same verse is cut into the
cliff (§1, §4). The founder's art sat in a sleeve "for three centuries because no one could read it; he read it"
(`thach_tang_danh` backstory). And the head is surnamed **Thạch**. That is a named novel's premise, its device and its
hero's surname.

**Fix:**
- Rename the head to **Yến Tàng Danh (晏藏名)**.
- Replace the verse with the founder's own saying: "The fee is the whole of the thanks. Brush the sleeve and leave no
  name."
- Change "no one could read it" to "no one had the Wind to open it; he had".
- Keep the grey sleeve, the 2,114 sleeves on the beams and the lost duel. Those parts are original and good.

### 3. The re-lean residue: "the head is the last holder of the root" (must-fix as a pattern)

R4 re-leans dropped an element from 7 sects. In 6 of them the writer turned the dropped element into the head's
private gift, which no heir has:
- Thối Tinh (Fire, the Rite);
- Phất Y (Wind, the Brushed Sleeve Step);
- Củng Tinh (Light, the mirror; hook 7);
- Thu Phần (Darkness);
- Trúc Thực (Fire, the censer);
- Xuyên Lâm (Thunder, which no one at the Gate can teach the head).

This is the user's Băng Tâm complaint ("except for its leader, none of its core member actually are thủy or băng"),
now written in on purpose six times.

**Fix:** keep it where it is the story (Trúc Thực, Thối Tinh, and Phất Y with its cross-sect race against Cửu Cao).
Remove it from the other three:
- **Thu Phần:** make the cave hunters of Thủ Động Trại (852, Darkness 34) a recruiting ground the way Cửu Cao uses
  its winter terrace. Name a **Cave Hall** there under Ôn Tịch Nhai, and give 2-3 named members Darkness 45+ (R1b:
  Darkness land tilts Earth and Water, and the hunters have both).
- **Củng Tinh:** make the Star Lake Mirror an Earth/Metal piece with Light as a bonus, and cut hook 7.
- **Xuyên Lâm:** keep Úc's Thunder as private greed (the mine door), not as a teaching gap.

### 4. The creed broken in secret, with a junior who "has begun to" notice (must-fix as a pattern)

In about 10 of 18 pages, §4 lists a taboo or creed and §9 shows an insider breaking it in secret:
- Thối Tinh sells mountain iron as star iron.
- Chân Kim shaves the house weight.
- Củng Tinh leaves a reading out of the book.
- Cốc Vũ sells rain.
- Lễ Tuyền cuts the wine.
- Vũ Linh hangs a bell where it can hear a private room ("The mistress has broken the last one").
- Chức Hà reels at the vent.
- Họa Tinh's ink comes from a forbidden art.
- Hoa Cái takes the founder's pill.
- Thu Phần's copyist keeps a second copy.

A C-tier witness is attached each time (Mẫn Hắc Châu, Cận Tri Vị "has begun to count", Hướng Cẩn Thư, Trương Hàm
Chước, Phí Đông Canh, Sử Mạn Thanh, Tân Cức Hoa, Đào Lương Khê...).

**Fix:** turn at least four of these into open conflicts that have already happened:
- **Cốc Vũ:** the rain-selling came out in 1180, and the devout half left to found a hermitage on Lôi Trúc Lâm. The
  arc is reunion or rivalry, not discovery.
- **Lễ Tuyền:** the Pivot already knows and takes forty jars a year in fines. The arc is paying it off.
- **Hoa Cái:** the Canopy Pill sits in the open market's records as a failure. The live conflict is the lease (hook 4)
  and the poisoned washers.
- **Họa Tinh:** the box has been opened. The Pivot's archive keeper Sử Ngưng Mặc knows and has chosen to keep the ink.

### 5. One secret, known to everyone (should-fix)

Thiên Ngoại's ore has been "mountain iron since 790". That is known or suspected by Vưu Chước Kim, Bàng Lãnh Tôi,
Hầu Thiết Anh, Mẫn Hắc Châu, Phùng Xích Kim (Chân Kim, "has known for decades"), Sầm Lạc Thiết (Củng Tinh) and Âu
Dương Kiên (Phất Y). The same scene, someone testing the iron, is written four times as hooks: `thoi_tinh` 1,
`phat_y` 7, `cung_tinh` 4 and `chan_kim` 4. A secret that seven people in three sects have kept for decades is no
longer a secret.

**Fix:** keep Vưu, Bàng and Mẫn. Make Phùng Xích Kim's knowledge a guess. Give `cung_tinh` hook 4 a new turn: "Thối
Tinh has been buying Vẫn Tinh discs to salt its ingots, and Sầm Lạc Thiết sold them." Cut `phat_y` hook 7.

### 6. Cửu Cao riders break R2 (should-fix)

`cuu_cao.md` §6 admits riders at "Wind at any purity". R2, approved as written, needs the variant at 45 for inner
disciples and above. Six inner riders sit below it: banh_da_hang Wind 40, tong_phi_thu 36, mo_cao_yen 32,
khong_tuong_vu 30, lo_phi_suong 28, ninh_van_tuoc 26. The innate-Wind ruling (Group 3) covers the **seat** test, not
admission.

**Fix:** raise the six to Wind 45-55 (they are plateau children, and R1b triples Wind there), or rank riders below 45
as outer riders (`ngoai_mon`). If the school should admit at any purity, ask the user for an innate-variant admission
exemption.

### 7. Phần Vũ still treats a ruled question as open (should-fix)

`phan_vu.md` §2 says "Whether the province becomes the palace's ... is a Provinces #48 decision for the user", and
hook 6 branches on it. Group 2 ruled that the lord takes the province, and Phần Vũ keeps its seat inside it as a
vassal.

**Fix:**
- §2: "In 1162 the palace took `prov_phan_vu` with its court (N-phan_vu-0x). The hall keeps its gate and its vents as
  a vassal holding inside its lord's seat province."
- Hook 6: "**The rent on the vents.** The treasurer sets a rent on the sitting places. Hùng pays it, or the levy stops
  guarding the road."

### 8. Caps left inside pieces after the ruling (should-fix)

Group 8 removed caps inside pieces. These remain:
- `thoi_tinh.items.json` Heat the Edge "(at most 5)";
- `hoa_tinh.items.json` Dipper "(at most 7)";
- `xuyen_lam.techniques.json` moving stance "(max 8)";
- `thu_phan.items.json` jade marks "(max 5)";
- `vu_linh.techniques.json` bells "(max 5)";
- `phan_vu.items.json` banner fury "(up to 5)".

**Fix:** turn each cap into a decay or a spend. For example: "each heat step fades after 3 s unless you fire another
Fire art"; "at five jade marks your next seal stuns and spends them". The "at most once a second" rate limits are
internal cooldowns. I left them, but the user should confirm they are allowed.

### 9. "Two houses that do not agree" (should-fix as a pattern)

10 of 18 §9s split the sect into two camps:
- Lễ Tuyền: healers against cellars ("two houses that do not quite agree");
- Hồng Đậu: the door against the garden ("two houses");
- Xuyên Lâm: the staff against the cape;
- Cửu Cao: riders against grooms ("two peoples");
- Vọng Triều: lamps against walls;
- Họa Tinh: selling the ink to all against selling to the Pivot only;
- Củng Tinh: stars against silver;
- Trúc Thực: forcing the grove against a fallow year;
- Vũ Linh: the furnace against the mistress;
- Phất Y: the clerk against the road sword.

Six of them also carry the same "trader against tradition" heir: La Khởi Minh, Nhạc Thuần Nhưỡng, Lạc Tố Bình, Phó
Cương Đẩu, Thành Kim Toa, Lam Khế Ngôn.

**Fix:** keep the binary where it is the sect (Hồng Đậu, Xuyên Lâm, Vọng Triều, Cửu Cao). Replace it in three others
with outside pressure:
- **Họa Tinh:** Thính Vũ forges the Hall's talismans (hook 6 already has deer-glue forgeries; promote it to §9).
- **Củng Tinh:** the Khuy Thiên star is real, and a Lạc Hà astronomer publishes it.
- **Vũ Linh:** the Tower's copper carts stop (hook 5 promoted).

### 10. Thu Phần is ma đạo in name only (should-fix)

Its doctrine is mourning ("We mourn for those no one mourns"). Its top traits are Devout and Calm. Its darkness lives
in hooks. Next to Cốc Vũ, which "thinks the singing in the graves is suffering", the Pavilion is simply right.

**Fix:** make Cốc Vũ right. The Pavilion's canon draws on the resentment it keeps unhardened, so the dead sing because
they are being milked. Lý Hận Bích's "hold them down until jade" is a harvest: grave jade is what is left when the
Pavilion has taken the rest. That gives "two women born in 830" (`thu_phan` hook 7, `coc_vu` hook 3) a real moral
edge, and it fits her Vengeful and Arbitrary traits and her `demonic_path`.

### 11. Secrets are kind lies; the world is too gentle (should-fix)

About 10 of 18 central secrets make the liar more sympathetic:
- the grandfather lies to save his granddaughter;
- the granddaughter shaves the weight so the miners are paid more;
- the prior sells rain to keep the abbess alive;
- the head cuts the wine because he could not say no to the Pivot;
- the sage spared the head and called the duel a draw;
- Ôn keeps every lamp lit out of guilt;
- Úy hides a second grove out of care.

Across 345 people: Cruel 1, Callous 3, Faithless 2, Arbitrary 1. 35 people carry any of Cruel, Callous, Faithless,
Arbitrary, Greedy or Deceitful, and 10 of those are in Hồng Đậu. Bách Vũ and Trúc Thực have none.

**Fix:** make three of these secrets ugly:
- **Chân Kim:** Tưởng Ngọc Cân charges the miners a "good-side fee" for her heavier scale. She is not Robin Hood; she
  is cornering the square.
- **Bách Vũ:** Ổ Linh Tước killed the Wutong hen to take the eggs, which breaks the pavilion's first taboo, and Tạ
  Thanh Loan knows.
- **Vọng Triều:** in 913 Ôn was not with a girl. He was selling the Kim Sa lamp's oil to a wrecker.

### 12. Wood everywhere in the south; builds converge on "outlast" (should-fix)

- 8 of 18 lineages are now Wood-first: Họa Tinh, Hồng Đậu, Vũ Linh, Cốc Vũ, Trúc Thực, Lễ Tuyền, Bách Vũ, Chức Hà.
  Wood/Water appears three times and Wood/Fire three times.
- Thính Vũ (a Metal/Thunder lord) has no Metal or Thunder vassal.
- Phượng Minh (a Fire lord) has one Fire-first vassal (Phần Vũ).
- Tier A tree starts: Wood 10 of 29.
- 12 of 29 tier A build intents are "outlast" builds ("outlast", "standing longer", "let the foe tire", "burn yourself
  out on him").

The land justifies the roots (R1), but fights will feel alike.

**Fix:** keep the roots and vary the plans:
- Mai Lễ Tuyền: "needles at the fever points: she ends a fight by stopping the foe's breath" (control).
- Lam Khế Ngôn: her Debt-Ledger Blade punishes each hit the foe has taken (finisher).
- Úy Thương Điền: "the flood drowns": a burst when her Water stacks break.
- Ninh Trúc Thực: the Sixty-Year Flower as his one-fight all-in (already his art; put it in the intent).

### 13. Bách Vũ is not the youngest vassal (should-fix)

`bach_vu.md` §1 says "The youngest of the palace's vassals" (founded 1110). Chức Hà, a palace vassal, was founded in
1150. **Fix:** "The palace's second-youngest vassal, and the only one founded by people who were not cultivators
first."

- Dương Hảo Vũ 好雨, from Cốc Vũ's Đỗ Phủ line;

**Fix:** where a name repeats the sect name, make it a title taken at headship. For example, Mai Lễ Tuyền: "born Mai
Tố Hà; the school's heads take the spring's name". Rename two or three others outright.

### 15. Ten pages open on a famous line (nice-to-have)

The sources: Lý Bạch (Phất Y), Tô Thức (Xuyên Lâm), Vương Duy (Hồng Đậu), Lý Hạ (Thu Phần), Đỗ Phủ (Cốc Vũ), Kinh

Cốc Vũ "the good rain knows its season"; Thu Phần "the jade"). Give Củng Tinh, Họa Tinh and Cửu Cao a founder's saying
of their own.

**No-lean sects** (4 in the slice):
- **Chân Kim:** the best case. The creed is the reason ("a house that weighs for others has no root of its own"),
  apprentices are tested at the scale, and the roots look like a mixed town (Wood 6, Metal 5, Earth 3, Water 2, Fire
  1).
- **Xuyên Lâm:** convincing. Footwork and a staff need no element; the page argues that the old lean was the head's
  root read as the sect's; the roots follow the road (Metal ridge, Wood bamboo, Water coast).
- **Vọng Triều:** sound. Admission by oath and kin; Water 11, Earth 5, Wood 2, matching its coast.
- **Thu Phần:** weak. It is no-lean only because no Darkness is near, and the result is a ghost sect where only the
  head and one cousin carry the dark root. See item 3 for the fix.

## The best items (a short reading list)

1. **Trúc Thực Viên** (§9, §12; `ninh_truc_thuc`, `uy_thuong_dien`). The grove is forced to seed every year by one
   man's Fire, the rats have bred on it for 150 years, and a second grove is hidden. A head's rare root has a
   world-scale consequence, and every person in the garden pulls on that one thread. Original, and played straight.
2. **Hồng Đậu Trang** (§4, §9; `nghe_hong_dau`, `nghe_xuan_chi`, `te_vong_quy`). The gracious door and the second
   cup, the gardeners who "do not wish to leave", and a daughter who has answered her father's poisons in the market
   for 150 years without his knowing. The one sect in the slice that is properly dark, and it is also funny.
3. **Xuyên Lâm Môn** (§2, §4, hook 3). "A guide never runs", then hook 3: "Nghiêm Tố Tố hears of it in the vaults and
   runs down the road to warn her brother. A guide never runs." The lamps carried through the Tower's lampless forest.
   The best-argued no-lean in the slice.
4. **Vọng Triều Đường** (§9, `on_thu_dang`, `chan_dinh_lan`). Lamps or walls: a public choice with a village's life on
   it, tied to the head's one night in 913. The player can tip it, and both sides are right.
5. **The tier C one-liners** in the `.5` files. Concrete, varied and often funny:
   - `co_tich_dang` lies to the blind founder about the colour of the sky "to make him laugh";
   - `yen_lac_tu` has counted 2,114 sleeves and wants to know which hire took her brother's;
   - `canh_tinh_am`, deafened at a pour, feels the bell lines through her feet;
   - `tan_xuan_duan` is "the laziest novice on the terraces and the best at finding bamboo shoots".
