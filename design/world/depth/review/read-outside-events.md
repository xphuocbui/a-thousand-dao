# Review: the outside groups and the new history (reader `read-outside-events`, 2026-11-25)

Slice: the six outside groups in `people/` (pages whole; all 89 tier A and B people; 24 tier C sampled), and the 273
`N-` events in the sect pages' section 3 and the group pages, read against `assets/data/world_history.json` (688 events).

## Verdict

The outside groups are the most connected writing in the depth pass. Their roots are argued from their birthplaces,
their ties cross sects (Đồ Vô Cữu, Lạc Đan Thanh and Kim Vạn Lý each tie four or more groups together), and the market
folk's web of debts is real game material. They still miss the user's ask in three places. Several stories are told
twice inside this slice (two false-map sellers, three Nam Phố expulsions, two Wind girls from one mine). The demonic
group is written to be pitied. And the new history is mostly lore that stays on its own page: 230 of the 273 `N-` events
are cited by no other group's file, and 16 of them land in the single year 1150.

## The weakest items, worst first

### 1. Nam Phố throws out the same man three times, and the south has two of the same map-seller (must-fix)
- **Where.** `market-folk.cast.json` `trinh_chu_sa`: sent away from Nam Phố by Châu Phi Vân in 1120 "for selling
  unstamped talismans at Hạc Đinh Thị". `loose-south.cast.json` `gian_van_lo`: turned out by Châu Phi Vân in 1168 "for
  selling unstamped talismans at Hạc Đinh Thị" (N-loose_south-04). `demonic.cast.json` `sao_an_lan`: let go by Châu Phi
  Vân in 1097 for darkened ink. On top of that, `loose-north` `gia_chi_lo` (賈指路, "points the way") and `gian_van_lo`
  (簡萬路, "ten thousand roads") are both sellers of false maps who sell maps to Mặc Huyết Sinh's abode.
- **Wrong.** The same charge, the same market, the same hall-mistress, and a duplicate trade with a near-duplicate name.
- **Fix.** Keep Trịnh Chu Sa's charge. Give Giản Vạn Lộ the Ferry War instead: move N-loose_south-04 to 1174 and write
  it as *"Châu Phi Vân turns Giản Vạn Lộ out of Nam Phố Đường for copying the hall's order book of 1173, the one that
  says 'for the reed beds', and selling the copy at Hạc Đinh Thị; he walks south to Thu Trì."* That makes him one of the
  people who can prove N-nam_pho-03, alongside Yến Hồi Độ's slip. Make Giả Chỉ Lộ a guide who sells the *way* to ruins,
  not the paper: *"He walks buyers to the ruins his maps name, and leaves them at the last turn."* That keeps Lâm Dã
  Hạc's frostbite joke and removes the second map trade.

### 4. 1150 is a dumping year (must-fix)
- **Where.** Sixteen new events in 1150 alone: N-coc_vu-04, N-hoa_cai-02, N-hoa_tinh-02, N-hong_dau-04, N-hong_lo-05,

- **Fix.** Make 1150 one year with a cause, and move everything else off it. Rewrite N-coc_vu-04 as the cause of C188:
  *"In the great drought the Tower's rice fails; the abbess of Cốc Vũ holds the Grain Rain rite nine days over Phong
  Đăng, and the Tower marches on Đan Sơn Mạch's cinnabar to pay for grain."* Keep the events that are causally tied to

  N-hong_dau-04 (a war spy, moved to 1153), N-yen_quang-02 (Vi bought the dusk herb because drought killed the

  N-demonic-05 to 1138, N-hermits_sages-09 to 1146 (Kê Thính Đào born 1131), N-market_folk-06 to 1143, Đới Vạn Ti's
  death to 1158. Make the two scripture purchases one copy: Hỗ Tế Xuyên's build note becomes *"bought a copy of the Still
  Moon Tide from Bào Tế Hải, who had his auction scripture copied for the man he builds junks for"*.

### 5. The demonic group is written to be pitied (must-fix)
- **Where.** `demonic.md` §1: "None of them is ma đạo for its own sake." Every living A or B ma đạo has a reason:

- **Fix.** Give the road one predator the reader cannot excuse. Ngư Mộng Tê is the natural one. New backstory line:
  *"In 1193 she did not drink too long. She waited until the raftsman had sold his catch, drank him through the wall
  and took his purse; she cried when Sào found her, and Sào believed the tears."* Traits: Ambitious, Cruel, Deceitful.
  Her tie to Ấn Tiểu Mặc ("worships her") then becomes a threat. Also cut the sentence in §1, or turn it round: *"Most
  of them have a reason. The ones who do not are the reason the Compact hunts the rest."*

### 8. Every outside group wants to sail off the map, in three separate hooks (should-fix)

## The best items (a short reading list)

1. **Đồ Liệt Cơ and Đồ Vô Cữu** (`loose-south` `do_liet_co` secret, `thien_xu.2` `do_vo_cuu`). She cut the rope with
   her brother on it, which is why he lived to become the Pivot's hound. One secret ties the Pivot, the demonic road,
   the Night Rain Market and a bandit camp together.
2. **Hám Tàn Chung and Tòng Bích Linh** (`demonic`). A soul pedlar who keeps an honest ledger with one entry missing
   (his mother's breath), and a clerk who frees one soul a year and writes it down as spoiled. The voice is distinct and
   the ledger is a hook that reaches Lạc Đan Thanh and sect elders.
3. **The paper web** (`market-folk` §8: Kim Vạn Lý, Từ Bỉnh Quyền, Quản Phục Thu, Quản Thận Ngôn). Lost bets used as
   bribes, a lender who will not call, a granddaughter buying small debts to call them on one day, a brother who has
   come to like the man he spies on. The economy turned into people.
4. **The bought rain of 1171** (N-clans-08, `luc_thuong_lam` secret, Cốc Vũ's prior's second receipt book). A clan head
   pays for rain on his own bank, three tenant children die, and a receipt is waiting to be found. This is the
   harshness the rest of the world needs.
5. **Events told from several pages.** The pass at Đan Sơn Mạch (N-thinh_vu-08, N-phuong_minh-11, N-loose_south-02)
   and the Ferry War (N-lac_ha-08/09, N-lang_dao-03/04, N-nam_pho-03/04, N-loose_south-03/05/06, N-thai_chi-04,
   N-tung_dao-06) are one event seen from each side, each witness holding a piece. This is the model the other 230
   events should follow.
