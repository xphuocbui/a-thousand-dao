# Reader review: Thiên Xu, Thính Vũ, Phượng Minh and the cross-sect threads (`read-great-2`, 2026-11-25)

Read in full: `sects/thien_xu.md`, `sects/thinh_vu.md`, `sects/phuong_minh.md`, `cross-sect.md`; every tier A person in the
three sects' cast files (32); a spread sample of tier B (36) and tier C (30) across the `.cast`, `.2`, `.3` and `.4`
files; the three `items.json` files (set and item rules). Script counts below are over all three sects' cast files.

## Verdict

These three sects come close to what the user asked for. Roots follow the land: the Pivot's plateau feeds Earth to the
outer gate and its mines feed Metal to the halls, the Tower moved to the Thunder ridge, and the palace moved to the fire
vent. The admission rules then turn those roots into stories. The tier A cast is the strongest I have seen: each has a
secret with a cost, a want and a build that mostly fits. The Đan Sơn Mạch rear guard ties all three great sects together
in a way that matters. They fall short in three places:
- They repeat the pilot's shapes: no named heir, a child with an odd root stuck at the gate, a vassal's child held as
  surety, a sealed paper. Each repeat is well written, but they are still repeats.
- The cross-sect page contradicts the sect pages on two facts, and it leaves out the best Pivot-Palace thread.
- The items still carry caps after the ruling against them, and the Tower is a sword sect where a third of its swordsmen
  (35 of 91), the head among them, carry no sword.

## The weakest items, worst first

1. **cross-sect.md §1.5, Tạ Thiết Trụ "burned beside him and lived".** He is dead in `thinh_vu.2.cast.json` ("held it
   for nine days and burned with it"). N-phuong_minh-09 and N-thinh_vu-07 both have him die. The cross-sect page is
   meant to be the reference for the threads, and here it is wrong.
   **Fix:** "the fort's keeper `ta_thiet_tru` (thinh_vu) burned with him, and the Tower named its new guardhouse on Thần
   Cổ Phong for him (N-thinh_vu-07)." **must-fix**

2. **cross-sect.md §1.3, Mạnh Lưu Ly "is his and `ky_van_tinh`'s daughter and does not know it, nor that he ordered her
   mother's death".** This is the opposite of her own `secret` (she found the letters in 1157 and knows both things), of
   thinh_vu §9 and of thinh_vu hook 2. That hook only works because she knows and he does not know she knows.
   **Fix:** "`manh_luu_ly` is his and `ky_van_tinh`'s daughter; she has known it, and who ordered the rear guard to hold,
   since 1157, and he does not know she knows." **must-fix**

3. **Caps inside pieces, after Group 8 ruled them out.** Eight set and item rules still have one:
   - `tx-breaker-harness` ("at most 5")
   - `tx-envoy-credentials` ("at most 7")
   - `tx-archive-desk` ("at most three at once"; this is the ruling's own "3 wards" example)
   - `tvl-gate-drum` ("up to 5")
   - `pm-ash-seat` ("up to 30 %"; this is the ruling's "30 % store")
   - `pm-nest-array` ("up to five")
   - `pm-wutong-court` ("spend up to five")
   - `pm-wutong-twig-ring` ("at most 2 a pulse")

   The sect pages repeat three of them (thien_xu §10). **Fix**, with a closing cost or a decay in place of each cap:
   - *Called Thrust:* "each full second in which you strike nothing lays one Earth stack on the foe you face; the waited
     thrust spends them all, and breaks the foe's ward outright if it spends five or more."
   - *Words Before Needles:* "each attack you evade loads one needle; each second you strike, one needle falls out."
   - *The Door Stays Shut:* "each seal-ward loses a tenth of its value each second."
   - *Censer:* "banks 0.5 % of your health each second, and the bank leaks 2 % of itself each second you deal no Fire."
   - *Gate Drums:* drop "up to 5".
   - *Nest threads:* "each new thread costs 1 % linh lực."
   - *Birds Fight First:* "spends them all."
   - *Twig ring:* drop the pulse limit and let the stacks expire after 4 s.

   **must-fix**

4. **The Tower is a sword sect without swords.** 35 of Thính Vũ's 91 `kiem_tu` builds carry no `phi_kiem`. The head is
   one of them: his gear is the umbrella (base `thuan`), the seal and a drum, while his build note says he "cuts once".
   Others are Tưởng Lậu Thanh, Thạch Liên Hoàn, Bộc Thủ Chung, Âu Tiểu Linh and the lamp-room C's. This is the brief's
   own example of "a sword cultivator with no sword".
   **Fix:** make the Tương Phi Tán a `phi_kiem`: "its spotted-bamboo spine sheathes a blade as thin as a rib; the
   umbrella's rule stands while it is open, and the blade is drawn for the one cut." Give each Tower `kiem_tu` a grade-1
   `phi_kiem` in place of the bell or lamp. People who really fight with a lamp, bell or rod become `phap_tu` (the
   copyists and lamp-keepers) or `the_tu` (the barley carriers and gate guards). **must-fix**

5. **The heir story, three times (pilot-cloning).** All three pages carry a head who "has named no heir" (thien_xu §9,
   thinh_vu §9, phuong_minh §9). Each has a favoured young candidate against an elder-backed one, and cross-sect §1.3
   turns this into a world thread ("Every great sect has a head who has not named an heir"). Only the Tower's version is
   built into the setting: two summits of the lineage, and one root that can climb both.
   **Fix:** keep the Tower's. Turn the Pivot's into a Confirmation story: "Doãn Ngọc Hành named Doãn Hi Hòa heir aloud in
   1190. Mâu Tri Viễn's sealed letter says four of the seven great sects would refuse to confirm her." The quarrel is
   then the Pivot's peace system against its head, not one more open heirship. Drop succession from Phượng Minh: its head
   is 250 with three quarters of her life ahead of her. Give the §9 bullet to the Phần Vũ province ruling (DECISIONS
   Group 2). Cut cross-sect §1.3 down to the two quarrels that reach across walls with a consequence: the Pivot's
   Confirmation and Phần Vũ's ward. **must-fix**

6. **The best Pivot-Palace thread is missing from both the Pivot's page and the cross-sect page.**
   - Hoa Triêu Dương's secret is that she played the Pivot's head eight movements of the Shao the night before he
     entered the war on her side. The Pivot destroyed a whole sect for that music (C005). It is a casus belli waiting to
     happen.
   - `cross-sect.md` never mentions the Shao. `thien_xu.md` has no line on it.
   - Doãn's own record ties her only as "friend: his ally of the Cinnabar Vein War".

   **Fix:**
   - *Doãn's `secret`, add:* "Since a night at the ridge palace in 1154 he cannot hear the dusk bell without counting
     eight strokes. He decided for the war the next morning, and has never asked himself why."
   - *Same record, add:* that is why he lets Bách Vong Ngôn's zither go (he "knows and says nothing").
   - *cross-sect §1.5, add a line:* "Hoa owes him, and does not know whether she bought him with the Shao (C097, C182);
     the Pivot's music taboo (C005) makes it a war if it comes out."
   - *thien_xu §9:* add a bullet. **should-fix**

7. **The count, the decoy and the spy do not meet.**
   - *thien_xu.md:* §8 and hook 5 have the Pivot afraid it is "counting a decoy" that Lệ Tiêu Hồn shows Phòng Bách Kế.
   - *thinh_vu.md and the records:* Nhiếp Vô Thanh has sent Doãn Ngọc Hành *true* numbers through the clerk Tào Mặc
     Toán for thirty years, and Doãn's record ties both people.
   - *What is missing:* `thien_xu.md` never names Tào or Nhiếp. So the Pivot's page does not know its head has the real
     count, and that is the obvious reason he refuses Ngũ's writ.

   **Fix, thien_xu §9 bullet:** "The head refuses the writ because Thính Vũ's own spymaster writes to him through the
   Tower's stores clerk Tào Mặc Toán, and her numbers are half of Phòng Bách Kế's. Ngũ does not know the letters exist.
   Mâu suspects them." Hook 5 becomes "which count is true, and who is the decoy for". **should-fix**

8. **A child with an odd root stuck at the gate, in every sect (pilot-cloning).**
   - *Odd-root children:* eight. Mặc Kinh Trập (the Pivot's Thunder girl), Doãn Thường Hi (five short at the beam),
     Nhiêu Hạ Lôi, Âu Tiểu Linh and Tống Lôi Nhi (the Tower's Thunder girls), Chúc Hiểu Nghi (the palace's only Light),
     Kha Thanh Miêu (pure Wood) and Ngạc Thiểm Hồng (the palace's Thunder boy).
   - *Old failures:* two near-identical. Hạ Hầu Thạch has tried the Level Beam "eleven times in eighty years", and Miêu
     Trúc Sinh "sat it for seventy years".
   - *Courted across a border:* three Thunder children (Mặc, Ngạc, Nhiêu).

   **Fix:** keep Nhiêu Hạ Lôi, who carries the R2 problem best, and Chúc Hiểu Nghi, whose Light is tied to the pipes. Make
   Ngạc Thiểm Hồng the Tower's informer at the vein fort: Chử Trì Âm teaches him thunder in exchange for the fort's watch
   rota. That gives the palace a traitor rather than a fourth odd-root child. Give Hạ Hầu Thạch a different end: "passed
   the beam in 1199 with Uất Trì Thu's trick, and Chử Bình Quân has not decided whether to say so." **should-fix**

9. **A vassal's child held as surety, twice.** Úc Đả Diệp (Xuyên Lâm to the Tower, 1170) and Hùng Diễm Nhi (Phần Vũ to the
   palace, 1162, with a second surety Hùng Liệt Nha) have the same shape: the head's kin is sent against a promise,
   breaks a rule at night, and writes letters home.
   **Fix:** keep Úc as the hostage, which drives the Hà Châu isle hook. Make Hùng Diễm Nhi the one who asked to go: "she
   asked to be sent in 1162, to get out of Tào Tấn Kiên's reach, and the vents at night are where she is learning the
   arts she will need to take her father's hall from him." **should-fix**

10. **Traits that contradict the person (too virtuous).** Across the three sects' 1,032 trait slots, Diligent has 81,
    Gregarious 69, Brave 66, Honest 66 and Loyal 62; Cruel has 2 and Faithless 1. The worst cases are tier A people whose
    traits deny their own secret:
    - *Doãn Ngọc Hành* is **Honest**, but he sealed the evidence and judged his own sect's case. **Fix:** Arbitrary,
      Cautious, Just.
    - *Hoa Triêu Dương* is **Forgiving**, but she keeps a dead man's sword as a bargaining piece, and her palace is
      "condescending". No tier A in Phượng Minh is Arrogant. **Fix:** Proud (or Arrogant), Ambitious, Brave.
    - *Quan Vũ Thanh* is Loyal and **Gregarious**, but she has hidden peace terms from her head for fifteen years.
      **Fix:** Loyal, Patient, Deceitful.

    **should-fix**

11. **Cố Dạ Lan and Mặc Kinh Trập: a broken tie.** Cố's tie calls Mặc "the Pivot smith she offered a forge". Mặc is a

## The best items (a short reading list)

1. **The Red Plum at Phượng Minh.** Read Nhiếp Tẫn Sa's record, phuong_minh §10 and C-tier Bối Tàng Ngôn. Bối has dusted
   the sword for seventy years and enters it in the inventory as "one sword, plum guard, not ours". Sử Hoành Dực spoke
   for hiding it and cannot bear it said, because the Tower keeps his wife's fan the same way. It is a moral mirror that
   ties the palace to two other sects.
2. **The rear guard of Đan Sơn Mạch** (thinh_vu §9, N-thinh_vu-08, N-phuong_minh-10/-11, Kỷ Vãn Tình, Chử Trì Âm,
   Phương Đoạn Vũ). One order binds three great sects: a dead mother, a daughter who knows, a prisoner who stayed, a
   survivor who owes his life to a Pivot deserter without knowing it, and peace terms carried in a sleeve. This is the
   thread that really ties the world together.
3. **Hoa Triêu Dương's Shao** (her secret, Liên Thiều Nghi's and Chúc Hiểu Nghi's). The best latent war in the slice.
   Fix item 6 so the Pivot's side carries it too.
4. **The 1188 ruling at the Pivot** (Doãn, Thích Minh Thư, Phương Trực Ngôn, Sử Ngưng Mặc). An institution breaks its own
   law to keep its land, and each official knows a different piece of it.
5. **The Tower's seat move and the Bell Test** (thinh_vu §2 and §6, Nhiêu Hạ Lôi). The user's complaint (roots against
   land) is answered by making the rule itself the story: a Thunder sect whose gate tests only Metal.
