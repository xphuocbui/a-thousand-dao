# Reader: read-minor-1 (minor sects of Băng Tâm, Thúy Vi, Tùng Đào, Lạc Hà)

Slice: 25 minor sects, 485 people (439 alive), 38 tier A. Read: sections 1, 2, 4, 9 and 12 of every page; every tier A;
4 others per sect (1 B, 1 C, 2 from `.5`). Counts below come from a script over all 485, not only the sample.

## Verdict

This slice comes close on the user's two concrete complaints. Roots follow the land on every page, and every tier A
has their own named gear and a build that fits the story. Sects are tied to each other through shared events: the
1173 Ferry War joins four sects, Chiết Kích Nguyên joins four more, and several Băng Tâm vassals come in mirrored pairs.
It falls short on depth in a less obvious way. The 25 sects are 25 different trades but only about six plots. Most
pages run the same engines: a head who lied about one decision and one person who knows, an heir question, a ledger
that would expose it, a lost top layer of the canon. The wave-3 people (`.5`) mostly take sides in the tier A quarrel.
They are not people in their own right.

## The weakest items, worst first

1. **Every head carries the same secret.** *Must-fix.* In 11 of the 25 heads, the secret is that the head lied about
   one founding decision, one subordinate knows, and the truth would bring down the sect's rule:
   - Lê Hoa: Thái and the vow
   - Hồng Lô: Đồng sold Viên's contract, and Tần knows
   - Vụ Ẩn: the founder's grave, and Chúc Minh Lộ saw
   - Hộ Lâm: the Grandmother Fir, and Mi knows
   - Lãng Đào: the toll was his greed
   - Minh Loan: Yến made Hoa pour, and Ngụy knows
   - Ngạo Tuyết: the root fruit
   - Thải Chi: she treated the raiders
   - Tuần Tùng: the cub
   - Nhạn Trận: the man who ran
   - Lãnh Giang: Bồ's stone

   Read side by side, they are one story. **Fix:** in about a third of the sects, make the flaw public, so the sect
   argues about it openly. Mục Vân's three stones shows how well that works. Three rewrites:
   - **Tuần Tùng:** everyone on the road knows a cub was in the column. The quarrel is that Hứa won't name the wagon,
     and Diêm Lạc Nhạn calls that protecting a paying customer.
   - **Thải Chi:** the treated raiders are known on the river. Giang Cửu Khúc buys her pills anyway, and his ferrymen
     spit at the landing.
   - **Minh Loan:** drop the "she made him pour" secret. Give Yến an appetite instead: she sells war bells to Hình
     Trọng Sơn's party behind her lord's back, because the eaves need bronze.
2. **The heir question is on nearly every page.** *Must-fix.* Section 9 raises an heir, successor or headship question
   in 21 of 25 pages, and Lê Hoa's walk of 1202, Nhàn Vân ("Mễ is the obvious head") and Minh Loan (the pendants) make
   it 24. Eight heads are Trúc Cơ, and five of them have a "the hall needs a Golden Core" arc: Vụ Ẩn, Ngạo Tuyết, Tuần
   Tùng, Sương Tùng, Yên Quang. **Fix:** cut the heir thread from at least ten pages and give each a different engine:
   - **Thiên Bảo:** drop "the heir" (the 1209 loan is the engine).
   - **Nhàn Vân:** drop Mễ as heir; Mễ's grievance is the tribute raise.
   - **Minh Loan:** drop the pendant question.
   - **Mục Vân:** drop "no heir named"; the horse is the clock.
   - **Sương Tùng:** drop "Nhạc expected the headship".
   - **Thải Chi:** drop the heir; use the orphans' labour (item 9).
   - **Vạn Hác:** keep the twelfth movement, drop Khương "expected the succession".
   - **Hồng Lô:** keep "who wears both" and drop Tần's "wants to be named heir".
   - **Trạc Anh:** drop the heir quarrel and keep the whale.
   - **Nhạn Trận:** keep the war-or-peace split, drop "If he died now, Đổng would be head".
3. **A ledger exposes the head in 8 pages.** *Must-fix.*
   - Hồng Lô: the 1150 ash ledger
   - Hàm Nguyệt: the second ledger
   - Lãng Đào: the toll books, which Thương Bạch Phàm "has started reading"
   - Bình Thủy: the ledger in the sea-chest
   - Hộ Lâm: the tally keeper and the missing log
   - Vạn Hác: Cù's ledger of zither weights
   - Yên Quang: hook 2, Lạc Đan Thanh's ledger from 1150
   - Minh Loan: the note ledger that catches seconds

   **Fix:** keep Hàm Nguyệt's, which is the whole sect, and Bình Thủy's, which is the region's Chiết Kích plot.
   Replace the others with people:
   - **Lãng Đào:** "Lữ Tẫn Chu has never answered the spring letters because the Lạc Hà elder who raised him kept the
     receipts for ten years of a toll the pines never set, and showed them to the boy on his fifteenth birthday." The
     son's silence then means something, and the wife no longer has to "start reading".
   - **Hồng Lô:** Sở Ngọc Hồ holds the bill of sale with Đồng's seal on it and spends it once a decade for cheap ash.
4. **The lost top layer of the canon comes back five times.** *Should-fix.*
   - Lãnh Giang: the ninth layer needs Ice
   - Vụ Ẩn: the ninth layer of the Haze Walk
   - Bình Thủy: the tenth to twelfth layers under the Tide Hall's floor
   - Vạn Hác: the twelfth movement
   - Vân Khởi: the nine layers of fifteen

   Sương Tùng's box, "the canon written down", is the same idea. **Fix:** keep Lãnh Giang's: it turns the land rule
   (R1) into plot and is the best use of the rules in the slice. Change the others:
   - **Bình Thủy:** "Lương teaches the Still Moon Tide a turn wrong to every heir Bùi names, and that, not his
     fickleness, is why four of them failed." This explains the Fickle trait and makes the lover tie bite.
   - **Vụ Ẩn:** Luyện Tố Y's price for her brother's debts becomes the Mist-Lock Lantern's key, so that Tùng Đào's
     carts can pass under the mist unasked.
5. **The weapon schools have no weapons.** *Must-fix.* Tuần Tùng is "a body school of staff and bow", but none of its
   16 members below the head carries a bow or a staff: their gear is `thuan`, `giap`, `cham` and `phu`. The base list
   in `assets/data/items_artifacts.json` has no bow, staff or sabre. The sabre halls (Dạ Quang, Trấn Sa) carry
   `phi_kiem`, and 17 `kiem_tu` in the slice carry no blade at all. Seven are Trấn Sa wave-3 people, for example
   `la_doan_cuong` (`phu`) and `ninh_ha_vien` (`thuan`). Tầm Kiếm's dress rule is "a sword", yet `cat_linh_hanh`
   carries `giap` and `lac_tam_chan` carries `kinh`. **Fix:**
   - Add `cung` (bow), `con` (staff) and `dao` (sabre) to the item-pass wishlist.
   - Until then, give Tuần Tùng's wardens the shared named piece (a plain grade-1 `ttg-warden-bow`).
   - Give every `kiem_tu` at least one `phi_kiem`.
6. **Two whale hunts and a whale-child.** *Should-fix.* Trạc Anh's Old Ivory, the white whale that took the founder and
   that the harpoon captain is obsessed with, reads as Moby-Dick. Hồng Lô's hook 3 is the same scene: Nghiêu Vọng Kình
   swears revenge on the spirit whale that took his great-grandfather and "takes both boats out, against the head's
   order". Trạc Anh's hook 1 reads "Ngạc takes the boats out without the head's word". Bình Thủy adds a girl found
   beside a whale. **Fix:**
   - **Trạc Anh:** keep the whale, but the white whale is a cow, and the captain wants her calf for the Thiên Bảo
     buyer, not revenge. Đoàn's wish to kill it himself becomes guilt: in 1080 he cut the founder's line.
   - **Hồng Lô:** Nghiêu's vow becomes raising his great-grandfather's boat from the warm reef off Kình Cốt point,
     where the vent keeps the wreck from ice and the hull is full of fire jade.
7. **Wave 3 adds people who take sides, not people with lives.** *Should-fix.* The `.5` backstories mostly restate the
   main quarrel from a seat at the table:
   - `han_thanh_dai`: "agrees with the prior… has said so to the Reader's face"
   - `dam_thiem_dang`: "he says what his master says"
   - `thieu_hoa_tam`: "agrees with Nhạc… says so less politely"
   - `chung_thiet_tuyen`: "he says she should be heir"
   - `thieu_tung_lam`, `tu_tien_suong`, `luong_hoa_tuyen`, `phuc_thuy_hang`, `trinh_luc_phan`

   **Fix:** in each sect, give two of the seven wave-3 people a private matter that has nothing to do with the head:
   a debt, a sweetheart in the next village, a mortal parent, a vice. Example for `dam_thiem_dang`: "He sells the
   burnt-out shell stubs of the false moon to Băng Tâm's north-shore fishers as luck charms; they call them the
   manor's moons, and Mục Tiềm Uyên would throw him off the piles if he knew."
8. **Wave 3 breaks secrets and dates.** *Must-fix* (small, exact):
   - `ninh_ha_vien` (Trấn Sa) "was the first to tell Bàng that Viên Cô Yên had sent Tất Dạ Lang". This undoes Viên's
     secret and makes hook 3 moot. Rewrite: "…he has seen Tất Dạ Lang's horse at the hall's back gate twice, and has
     told no one but his own file."
   - `cat_linh_hanh` (Tầm Kiếm) "guarded the Silver Thread mine… until 1165 and walked off the post in 1162". Change
     1165 to 1162.
   - Hộ Lâm hook 5 is titled "The wolves come back" but is about the brown bears. Retitle it "The bears come down".
   - `muc_han_nhan` (Nhạn Trận) "walked the walls breaking bows… which is how he got his name" in 1120, at 220 years
     old. Make it "which is how the plain came to call him the Cold Goose".
   - `ninh_hoa_van` (Nam Phố) "painted the same cloud talisman every dawn since, two hundred times over". Ten years is
     about 3,650 dawns, so write "every dawn since, three thousand sheets".
9. **Too virtuous outside Dạ Quang and Lạc Hà.** *Should-fix.* Of 485 people, 3 are Cruel, 2 Faithless, 3 Callous and
   none Craven. Brave appears 119 times and Diligent 115. The harsh ones cluster in Dạ Quang. The petty and funny ones
   cluster in Lạc Hà's vassals (Thiên Bảo, Mục Vân, Bình Thủy, Nam Phố's Kha). The Thúy Vi and Tùng Đào vassals have
   almost no one small-minded. **Fix:**
   - **Thải Chi:** "Ông Khổ Sâm hires the oldest orphans out to the River Hall's dike crews at a wage the valley keeps,
     and the mistress signs the contracts without reading them." This gives Nghiêm Hoàng Kỳ's bill quarrel teeth.
   - **Hộ Lâm:** make Lại Hoành Chi Greedy and Callous instead of aggrieved: "he sold the log, and he sold the poacher
     too".
   - **Hồng Lô:** give one cook or crewman Lazy or Craven.
10. **Thải Chi and Yên Quang share herb names.** *Should-fix.* Both sects name their people after herbs, and five names
    collide: Hoàng Kỳ (`nghiem_hoang_ky`/`au_hoang_ky`), Tử Tô, Ích Mẫu, Đương Quy and Cam Thảo. **Fix:** Yên Quang's
    names are tied to its poem and its dusk (Tử Tô is purple), so rename Thải Chi's five:
    - Nghiêm Hoàng Kỳ → Nghiêm Kim Ngân (嚴金銀)
    - Nghiêm Tử Tô → Nghiêm Bạc Hà (嚴薄荷)
    - Nghiêm Ích Mẫu → Nghiêm Hạ Khô (嚴夏枯)
    - Nghiêm Đương Quy → Nghiêm Xuyên Khung (嚴川芎)
    - Nghiêm Cam Thảo → Nghiêm Ngũ Vị (嚴五味)
11. **Doubled ties and stitched notes.** *Should-fix.* 36 people tie to the same person twice (39 doubles), and 50
    notes are machine-joined, for example `la_doan_cuong`: `child` "his daughter; Lã Đoán Cương's daughter; works the
    forge bellows" plus `disciple` with the same note. Others: `tieu_van_phong` → `thai_le_tuyet` (`feud` and
    `creditor`), `hua_van_cung` → `thu_phieu_binh` (`friend` and `former_master`), `giang_cuu_khuc` → `lu_thua_lang`
    (`debt` and `disciple`). **Fix:** one tie per pair, keeping the stronger kind, with a note in plain words. A
    checker rule should flag a doubled `to`.
12. **"The lord's rejects" founds too many sects.** *Should-fix.* At least six minor sects are defined as where the
    lord's gate sends its failures:
    - Vân Khởi: Thúy Vi's turned-away children
    - Vạn Hác: the pines' Wood-test rejects
    - Hàm Nguyệt: "half the manor is the pines' rejects"
    - Tầm Kiếm: the hermitage's turned-away men
    - Bình Thủy: `khuat_do_sinh`, who failed the dusk test
    - Nhàn Vân: "Wood and Water children who failed the dusk test"

    Ngạo Tuyết turns it round: its own gate does the rejecting. R2 makes this true, but it is a mechanic, not a
    people. **Fix:** let one sect be the opposite. Vạn Hác's ear test should poach: "the pines lose a child with an ear
    to the ravine every year, and the Coiled Root Hall has started testing for ears first."
13. **Four road-guard halls, three Kết Đan who refuse the headship.** *Should-fix.* Dạ Quang, Trấn Sa, Vụ Ẩn and Tuần
    Tùng all guard caravans or roads. Three Kết Đan elders refuse to lead: Trấn Sa's `tong_thoi_kim` ("holds hammers,
    not men"), Vụ Ẩn's `trieu_ngu_lang` ("a robber can guard a road… but not own one") and Yên Quang's `diem_tu_to`.
    **Fix:**
    - **Tuần Tùng:** make it a wolf-bounty hall that lives on pelts and the pines' bounty. Guarding comes second, and
      the head's no-running-beast rule then costs it money.
    - **Triều Ngũ Lang:** he wants the headship, and the sect refuses him for being a robber. That is the quarrel.
14. **Two sages carry too much weight.** *Should-fix.* In Ngạo Tuyết, Tư Không Hạc Niên gave Lăng a Spirit Root Fruit,
    and Lăng "owes the sage the fruit" with no terms. In Tầm Kiếm the same sage beat Sài. The debt is a tie that means
    nothing. **Fix:** "The sage's price for the fruit was the first disciple Lăng ever made, and in 1200 he writes to
    ask for Phó Ngạo Sương."
15. **Hàn Giang is both a person and a fallen sect.** *Should-fix.* Thẩm Hàn Giang (a person, Băng Tâm) and Hàn Giang
    Môn (a fallen sect) share the name. Trạc Anh's "sworn to fight for Hàn Giang's heir", next to "his sworn brother
    Thẩm Hàn Giang", reads as Thẩm's heir. **Fix:** in Trạc Anh (doan_thanh_lang secret, §9, hook 3) write "Hàn Giang
    Môn's heir" every time.
16. **Caps survive the ruling.** *Must-fix* (data). Caps still in this slice's items and techniques:
    - `le_hoa.items` "up to 30 %"
    - `trac_anh.items` "up to 20 %"
    - `da_quang.items` "up to 40 %"
    - `hong_lo.techniques` "(up to 3)"
    - `van_khoi.techniques` "(up to 5)", twice
    - `muc_van.items` "up to 20 % less"
    - `nam_pho.items` "grows, to 3 times"
    - `nhan_van.items` "to 30 %"

    **Fix:** give each a decay or closing cost, as ruling group 8 says. For example, Lê Hoa's "5 % less damage for the
    rest of the fight" stacks with no cap, and each stack fades after 10 s. Minh Loan's "at most once per second" rules
    are rate limits. I left them, but the user may want them checked.
17. **Every sect has one kind of cultivator.** *Nice-to-have.* In 20 of 25 sects, 14-18 of the ~18 people with a build
    share one archetype. Lãng Đào, a guild of 150 boat people, has 18 `phap_tu` and no healer, smith or fighter.
    Vạn Hác, Nhàn Vân and Nam Phố have 18 of one kind, and Yên Quang 17. **Fix:** two or three per sect off-archetype.
    Lãng Đào gets a `the_tu` rope master and a `linh_thuc_su` who keeps the serpent.
18. **Tang poems in the glosses.** *Nice-to-have.* The poems tie each region together: Lạc Hà's vassals all come from
    the Đằng Vương Các preface, the Băng Tâm vassals from frontier and snow poems. But nearly every head's gloss now ends
    "(poet: line)": Vương Bột 4, Vương Duy 14, and also Lý Bạch, Đỗ Phủ, Liễu Tông Nguyên, Vương Hàn, Khuất Nguyên and
    Zhuangzi. **Fix:** keep the conceit in Lạc Hà (it is the lord's), and outside it cite one line per sect at most.

**No-lean sects (7 here: Trạc Anh, Tầm Kiếm, Vân Khởi, Vạn Hác, Tuần Tùng, Mục Vân, Nhạn Trận).** They make sense. Each
has a concrete teaching that runs on no element and a test you could stage:
- Trạc Anh: the winter dive
- Tầm Kiếm: ask
- Vân Khởi: sit at the water's end until dusk
- Vạn Hác: the ear
- Tuần Tùng: nights held on the road
- Mục Vân: the herd decides
- Nhạn Trận: stand in a line

Their roots look like their regions, as R3 says. Three weak spots:
- Four of the seven are body schools, so a no-lean sect reads as "the body school".
- Trạc Anh's page says "the whole of Hàn Sương Châu has no Ice anywhere", yet its head `doan_thanh_lang` carries
  `bang 42` and an ice ring. Drop the variant, or explain it as a birth on the Pavilion's ice.
- Tuần Tùng's "staff and bow" has no gear behind it (item 5).

**What works on connection.** This answers the "non-connected" complaint better than any other theme in the slice:
- The 1173 Đào Sa burning joins Lãng Đào, Thải Chi, Nam Phố and Bình Thủy.
- Chiết Kích Nguyên joins Bình Thủy, Nhạn Trận, Yên Quang and Thiên Bảo.
- Băng Tâm's vassals come in mirrored pairs: Dạ Quang and Trấn Sa, Lê Hoa and Tầm Kiếm, Lãnh Giang and Trạc Anh.
- Lạc Hà's officials (Trầm Tịch Dương, Hách Ánh Hà, Tô Vãn Chiếu) recur as the same people with the same habits.

## The best items (a short reading list)

1. **Mục Vân Trang §9, "The three stones"** (`khau_muc_van`, `khau_thanh_thao`). A herder deducts three stones a year,
   with interest at the lord's own posted rate, and the Hall of Scales cannot find the error because there is none.
   It is funny, it turns the lord's law against the lord, and it is in public.
2. **Thiên Bảo Trai** (`phan_vat_hoa`, `khang_thien_chuy`). A greedy Nguyên Anh banker holds her lord's treasury key,
   and her honest refiner hides the working copy in slag. Wave 3's `thuong_toi_kim` "noticed Khang skipped the vow in
   1199": the only wave-3 line that adds a clue instead of a vote.
3. **Nhàn Vân Quán** (`tan_dao_anh`). Her reflection-double sits the morning lesson for her, "and last month Mễ Phù Vân
   corrected its brushwork for an hour and did not notice". It is the most original idea in the slice.
4. **Lãnh Giang Phái** (`bo_toa_tuyet`, `lu_doc_dieu`). The Pavilion licensed the school for Water only, and the ninth
   layer needs Ice. The land and admission rules become the plot itself.
5. **Dạ Quang Môn and Trấn Sa Đường together.** The raiders who knelt, the guards who watch them, and 312 night-glow
   cups on a shelf above the head's chair. Two sects written as one feud from both sides.
