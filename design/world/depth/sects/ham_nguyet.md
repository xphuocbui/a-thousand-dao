A manor of divers on the south shore of Hạo Nguyệt Hồ. Its people cultivate at the bottom of the lake and on the
stilt-halls over a still pool where the moon lies on the water. It farms the biggest pearls on the continent, it keeps a
shoal of spirit fish that cannot live without moonlight, and it owes the pines a tithe that it never quite pays in full.
Everyone on the lake knows Hàm Nguyệt's numbers are wrong. Only two people know why.

**Why.** There is no Ice in the whole of Lãnh Nguyệt Lâm. The nearest Ice qi is Băng Tâm's, far to the north. An Ice
lean cannot fit anywhere on the manor's ground. Water is the manor's real element, but at the shore house it is 24.6 %,
just under the first-lean line, because the shore stands in pine woods. The pool behind the reed bar holds 27.9 %.

**The story.** Nothing moves on the ground. The founder Đới Thiềm Quang built the manor's moon halls on piles over
Trầm Bích Đàm in 412 because the moon lies stillest there, and she put the shore house on the lake for the boats and the
market (**N-ham_nguyet-01**). The manor has always cultivated on the pool and traded on the shore. The game's seat
record simply moves to where the halls stand. The pool's name, the jade disc sunk in still water, is what the manor
calls the moon's reflection.

**What it costs.**
- Art: a small painting of stilt-halls on a still pool at 260; place 8 keeps its painting as the Shore House.
- Routes and province: a short causeway route 8 ↔ 260; the province seat record follows.
- Density: 1.37 against 1.41. **Founder's array** (amended 2026-11-25, ruling group 2: every moved sect gets one):
  the **Sunken Disc (Trầm Bích Trận)**, `seat_array_density` **+0.05** (proposed; World Generation rule 16a, Formula
  G: 1.41 − 1.37 = 0.04, rounded to 0.05), so the moon halls read 1.42. It is the jade disc the pool is named for, which
  Đới Thiềm Quang sank under the moon halls in 412 to hold the moonlit qi; the manor calls it the moon's own array.
  Raising the disc (a hook) drops the seat to 1.37.
- Lore lines: A006 ("on the long lake"), A013 (the beds of the manor at 8) and A015 stay true: the beds and the boats
  were always at the shore house.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The south shore is pine country with water in it (Wood 40-60 %, Water 13-30 %),
and its children are mostly Wood-rooted. They go to the pines. The north and east shore villages (Tuyết Lộc 46 %, Băng
Võng 43 %, Bắc Ngạn 45 %, Ngư Đăng 37 % Water) breed strong Water and thin Wood. These are the children who fail the
Lake Hall's Wood test (`tung_dao.md` 6), and the manor's gate asks only for Water. **Half the manor is the pines'
rejects**, and the manor is proud of it. A few Kiều children carry frost from the winter of 748 (C146). None of them has
been taught Ice.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The moon is easier to catch in the water.** The manor's one teaching is the founder's joke, taken seriously: what
  cannot be reached can be waited for where it comes down. Its people cultivate patiently, at the bottom of the lake and
  on the pool at night, and they believe that a thing worth having settles if you sit still long enough.
- **Nacre.** "What hurts you, wrap." An oyster makes its pearl from the grit that cuts it. The manor teaches its children
  to do the same with insult, debt and grief, and it fights the same way (section 5). A Hàm Nguyệt quarrel can last two
  hundred years without a raised voice.
- **The moon nights.** At the full moon the whole manor sits on the stilt-halls over the pool and no one speaks until
  the moon leaves the water. On moonless nights the keeper of the pool lights the false moon for the fish, a lantern of
  shell, and the divers do not go down.
- **The fish.** Four hundred spirit fish live in the pool, each named for a dead mistress or diver. Killing one is the
  manor's gravest crime. Eating any fish from the pool is forbidden. The manor eats lake fish, reindeer milk bought
  from Tuyết Lộc, pine nuts and oyster flesh after a harvest.
- **Dress.** Divers wear grey sealskin and go barefoot on the piles. Inner disciples wear undyed linen with a sash of
  the blue the lake turns in the evening. The mistress wears what she likes, and a pearl in each ear.
- **The dead go to the lake.** A dead diver is rowed out at the full moon and let down on a line where the beds are,
  so the oysters can wrap them. Their name goes to a new spirit fish.
- **The tithe and the pledge.** The manor pays the pines a tithe in pearls and keeps one Kiều child at the Lake Hall as
  a pledge (`kieu_han_nguyet`). It pays the tithe late every year as a matter of pride.
- **Taboos:** killing a spirit fish; diving on a moonless night; speaking during the moon sitting; selling the fist
  pearl. **Outsiders** are welcome at the Shore House market, and Băng Tâm's north-shore fishers drink there.

**Why it fits:** the outer method is learned on the beds, where Water qi comes in through the skin of a diver with empty
lungs. The main method is cultivated on the still pool at the full moon, laying Water qi round the dantian in rings like
nacre, one ring a layer. It heals, wards and waits. Its strongest art puts a reflection of the holder on the ground to
take the blow. The Nacre canon was made after the Long Lake War by a mistress who had sunk her boats and had to sit out
the raids. There is no Ice in the lineage. The frost in the Kiều line came from the winter of 748, and no one has known
how to teach it since.

## 6. Admission

- **Outer gate:** Water 30 or more. The manor's stewards walk the shore villages at the autumn harvest; many children
  come by themselves after failing the Lake Hall's Wood test (Ngụy Tiểu Ngư). The manor takes them gladly.
- **Inner gate:** Water 45 or more, and one night at the full moon on the pool without a word.
- **Exceptions:** none. The Kiều frost (Ice in a Water root) passes the gate on its Water.
- **The pines and the gate.** The Lake Hall would like the manor's Water children for its own outer gate. Lương Tạc
  Băng (`tung_dao`) calls the manor "the place the lake's children go to be forgotten". Kiều calls it "the place they go
  to be taught".

## 8. Relations

- **Tùng Đào Cung (lord), the Lake Hall:** the pines fought a war for the manor's pearls (C170) and the manor sank its
  boats for them (A013). It is grateful, and it pays late. Thiều Lăng Ba, the Lake Warden, drinks with Kiều and counts
  her pearls. Phương Ngọc Sa counts them again and has never matched Kha Toán Châu's ledger. Kiều Hàn Nguyệt, Kiều's
  grand-niece, is the pledge at Noãn Ốc. She carries frost the pines cannot teach. Lương Tạc Băng resents the manor for
  taking the lake's thin-Wood children.
- **Băng Tâm Các:** the raiders of 942. Since the arbitration (C171) its fishers work the north shore, and since 1152
  its skiff master Lục Hàn Chu (`luc_han_chu`), who raided the beds at twenty, has kept his skiffs off the manor's north
  beds for one pearl in ten. The skiffs sit on the water and watch. Mông Lộc Nhi has seen them.
- **Sương Tùng Các:** makes the ward lanterns that hang over the beds and the lake villages. Kiều buys them by the
  hundred and pays Văn Hàn Đăng in pearl dust, which he grinds into the resin paper for his strongest wards.
- **Thải Chi Cốc:** buys the manor's seed pearls ground for pills. Nghiêm Linh Chi and Kiều have traded pearl powder for
  cold-sickness pills every winter since 1150.
- **Lãng Đào Môn:** river people, not lake people. The two houses think little of each other's boats.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The north beds.** Kha Toán Châu keeps two ledgers. The second records what the north beds pay and what goes to Lục
  Hàn Chu. Lam Hàm Sương has begun to read the first and to notice the second's shape. Đinh Lưu Ly, who dives the north
  beds, is watching where the black pearls go and has not decided whom to tell.
- **The frost and the heir.** The old shore families want a frosted Kiều as mistress. Kiều chose Lam Hàm Sương, who has
  none, and sent the frosted girl to the pines as a pledge. Her brother Kiều Vọng Thiềm, without frost, is taught by the
  heir and resents her.
- **Fish against pearls.** Mục Tiềm Uyên says the manor sells too many pearls and breeds too few fish. He is the last
  disciple of the old mistress and the one person Kiều cannot dismiss.
- **The dead beds.** Lã Tư Châu is seeding Cựu Châu Trấn's shallows behind his master's back. If the beds live, the old
  town could be resettled, and the pines would want a tithe on it.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The tenth pearl.** Phương Ngọc Sa finally matches the ledgers, or Đinh Lưu Ly sells what she has seen. A vassal
   paying a Băng Tâm raider is a scandal on both shores. The player can carry the second ledger to Thiều Lăng Ba, to
   Băng Tâm, or back to Kiều for a price. (Kiều Nguyệt Châu, Kha Toán Châu, Phương Ngọc Sa, Đinh Lưu Ly, Lục Hàn Chu;
   places 8, 190.)
2. **Lục Hàn Chu wants his war.** He has taken the manor's tenth for fifty years and wanted another war for longer. If
   Băng Tâm's heir quarrel tips his way, the skiffs come in, and the manor learns what its pearls bought. (Lục Hàn Chu,
   Kiều Nguyệt Châu, Quách Bất Trầm, Mông Lộc Nhi; the north beds.)
3. **Frost for the pledge.** Kiều wants someone to teach her grand-niece Ice. Băng Tâm would; the pines would call it
   treason. The player can carry the girl north, carry a method south, or tell the Lake Hall. (Kiều Nguyệt Châu, Kiều
   Hàn Nguyệt, Thiều Lăng Ba, Lục Hàn Chu; places 97, 8.)
4. **The heir without frost.** The shore families back Kiều Vọng Thiềm against Lam Hàm Sương when the mistress goes into
   seclusion. The heir needs a frost of her own, a reason the frost does not matter, or a sister-in-law. (Lam Hàm Sương,
   Kiều Vọng Thiềm, Kiều Hàn Nguyệt.)
5. **A dark night.** The false moon fails on a moonless night, through a broken lantern or a thief, and the spirit fish
   begin to die. Mục Tiềm Uyên needs shell, oil and a Sương Tùng ward before dawn. (Mục Tiềm Uyên, Đào Nguyệt Lân, Văn
   Hàn Đăng; places 260, 948.)
6. **The Old Pearl Town.** Lã Tư Châu's oysters take at Cựu Châu Trấn. Resettling the old town needs the pines' leave,
   the manor's divers and the ice not to freeze again. (Lã Tư Châu, Kha Toán Châu, Kiều Nguyệt Châu; place 261.)
7. **The moon-fox.** The white fox seen on the lake in 1192 (A016) comes back to the pool at the full moon, and the
   frosted Kiều children dream of the founder walking on the water. Something is waking in the north; the manor wants
   it found before the pines or Băng Tâm find it. (Kiều Nguyệt Châu, Kiều Vọng Thiềm, Đinh Lưu Ly; places 190, 260.)
8. **The founder's footprints.** A diver on the north beds finds a woman's footprints on the lake bed, walking north,
   six hundred years old in nacre. (Quách Bất Trầm, Mông Lộc Nhi, Mục Tiềm Uyên; place 190.)
