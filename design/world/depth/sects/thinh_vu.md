The Tower is quiet, dark and patient. It believes you should listen before you strike, and strike once. It began as one
woman's tower on a fire mountain, where she sat listening to rain on a dead lotus. It inherited a dead sect's thunder,
lost a war against the Pivot with its founder in the field, and opened its gates to every master the righteous sects
had thrown out. It has lamps for the souls of its members and none for its halls. Thirty-four years ago it carried its
halls east onto the thunder ridge, within sight of the Pivot's border. The first elder stayed behind with the rain.

**Why Metal survives and Water goes.** All five people of the existing cast hold Metal at 60 or more. Only two hold Fire,
and those two are the two who hold Thunder. Water is the root of the head and the first elder, but the land the Tower
needs for its Thunder has none. So Metal stays the lineage's first element and every existing elder passes R2 on it.
Water stays in the Tower as an old family's root and as the head's sutra, but it is no longer what the sect recruits for.

**How the name still fits.** The founder listened to rain. Her heirs learned what comes after rain. The rain-chain she
hung from her eaves (N-thinh_vu-01) leads water down without a splash, and on the ridge the same bronze chains lead the
lightning down. The Tower still listens to the rain; on the ridge it listens for the thunder in it. The head is called
the Night Rain. The new seat is a drum.

**The story.** The Tower has held Thần Cổ Phong since 1046: when Phượng Minh burned Thủ Lôi Bảo in the Thunder Vein War
(V161), the Tower built its new guard higher on the ridge, on the drum-shaped summit above it, and named the guardhouse
for the man who burned with the old fort (N-thinh_vu-07). After the defeat at Đan Sơn Mạch (C190), Mạnh Dạ Vũ did not
leave the old tower for a year (A277). In 1165 the Pivot's envoys counted the Tower's disciples there and did not like the
number (C200). In 1166 the head moved the halls east (N-thinh_vu-09). He gave three reasons. The canon's second half
wants Thunder qi, and the cone has none: every Kinh Trập the Thunder disciples were already walking east to drill on
Liệt Khuyết Phong (A316). From the ridge the Tower can watch Kinh Lôi Mạch, Phượng Minh's mines at Lưu Kim and Ô Thiết,
and the Pivot's old border fort at Nam Vọng Bảo (V123). And a sect that has been counted should not be easy to count.
He gave no fourth reason, and the Tower guesses at it: Kỷ Vãn Tình's rooms were in the old tower. Quan Vũ Thanh refused to
take down the founder's chain and stayed with it. In 1170 Thạch Liên Hoàn and Lệ Tiêu Hồn laid the Iron Rain array from
the peak to the vein (N-thinh_vu-10), and Xuyên Lâm Môn, whose mines surround the peak, sent a hostage (N-thinh_vu-11).

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The Tower's ground holds three lands, and each breeds its own people.
- **The ridge** (Kinh Trập Sơn's high mountains: 41-60 % Metal, about 10 % Fire, Thunder 14-40 around 698, 863, 873,
  874, 875 and 208) breeds Metal roots about twice as often as an even land, with Earth under them. Fire is halved, so few
  ridge children have it, but those who do form Thunder up to five times as often (R1b): Hầu Vọng Xuân, Khúc Thanh
  Điện, Tống Lôi Nhi. Most ridge children are Metal and Earth with no Fire, and they fill the inner gate: Tôn Tuyệt Bích,
  Phương Đoạn Vũ, Cố Dạ Lan herself.
- **The cone and its foot** (the old seat, Lâm Linh Đỉnh, Thảo Lư Thôn, Thanh Đăng Trấn, Hạnh Hoa Thị: 17-53 % Fire,
  30-35 % Earth, 14-27 % Metal) breeds Fire, with Metal sometimes under it. There is no Thunder qi, so Thunder forms at
  the base rate. When it does, it comes strong: Lệ Tiêu Hồn and his niece, and the outer-gate girls Nhiêu Hạ Lôi and Âu
  Tiểu Linh. Fire-only cone children enter the outer gate on their Fire and stay there.
- **The south** (Thu Trì, Không Mông, Lãng Hoa: 32-40 % Water) breeds Water and Wood. Its children enter only if they
  have Metal at 30, and they rise only if they have it at 45. They were the old Tower's first families (the head, the
  first elder, Kỷ Vãn Tình), and they are the Rain faction now.
The ridge thinks the south is soft, and the south thinks the ridge has no ear. The cone's children are caught between
them, holding the Thunder that the inner gate's rule cannot see.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **Listen first, cut once (tiên thính hậu trảm).** The Tower's one teaching. Qi falls like rain, and a cultivator who
  listens long enough hears where the next drop will land. Then they cut once, late, where it lands. A second cut means
  the listening failed. Speed is not admired. Stillness before the cut is.
- **The rain-chain (vũ liên).** Every hall hangs bronze rain-chains from its eaves. On the cone they rang with rain; on the
  ridge they are lightning rods as well, and in storms they glow. Each disciple is given one bronze cup on the day they
  join and wears it on a cord at the neck. An elder wears a short chain at the belt. The founder's own chain of nine cups
  still hangs from the eaves of the Rain Tower. No one has taken it down, and taking down a chain is the Tower's oldest
  taboo.
- **The Night Rain (since 905).** No lamp is lit in the halls after dark (A274). The Tower lives by ear at night: its
  disciples learn the halls by the sound of their own steps, and a guest who brings a light is asked to put it out. A
  guest's name is never asked (A313). The Edict's seal reads "the rain does not choose the roof" (雨不擇屋): any master may
  come. The price is that every guest can be heard.
- **The life lamps.** In the soul vaults under Kinh Lôi Mạch a small bronze lamp burns for every sworn member, holding a
  thread of their soul. It is the roll-call and the leash: a lamp that gutters means its owner is hurt, and a lamp that
  goes out means they are dead or have been put out. The vault law of 1010 needs two hands to put one out from afar.
  The vaults are the only lit place in the Tower.
- **Dress.** Black oiled-silk rain cloaks and straw rain hats over undyed grey. The Thunder Hall wears its cloaks
  burn-scarred and does not mend them. The Rain Tower's people wear their hats indoors. Only the head carries an umbrella.
- **Kinh Trập (the waking of insects).** The first spring thunder is the Tower's new year. At dawn the outer gate sits the
  **Bell Test**: an outer disciple whose Metal has reached 45 hears Bộc Thủ Chung's bell answer inside their chest and
  walks into the inner gate. In the first storm after it, the inner gate climbs Liệt Khuyết Phong to raise their swords
  to the cloud (A316). Khải Lôi did the same, and a third of its disciples came down (A283). The Tower now loses about one
  in fifty.
- **Water and food.** The Tower drinks only rainwater, from cisterns under every chain. On the ridge it eats barley and
  salted meat carried up from the south, and nothing is cooked after dark. The south eats river fish and laughs at the
  ridge's bread.
- **Two burials.** The old **rain burial**: the dead are sunk in Tàn Hà Hồ under the founder's lotus, where Lư Tàn Hà
  keeps their names. The new **thunder burial**, since 1166: the dead are laid on Liệt Khuyết Phong's summit in the first
  storm, and the lightning takes them. Every member chooses in writing. The Hall of Rules counts the choices each year,
  and so does everyone else.
- **Taboos:** lighting a lamp after dark; asking a guest's name; a second cut where one would do; taking down a
  rain-chain; the Canon of Opening the Thunder (`opening-thunder-canon`, sealed in the vaults).
- **How others see them.** The righteous see a ma đạo den with a market. The southern farmers see the Tower under which
  their coast has been safe from pirates since the Purge (C157), and which takes its grain tax in kind (A288). The Pivot sees a sect that is counted and does not like
  the count. Merchants from the desert see the only market that sells everything (A315).

**Why it fits.** Every rank's method runs on Metal, which the ridge breeds. The outer primer also takes Fire, the other
half of Thunder, because the cone's children are Fire. The canon's first half is Metal, and any inner disciple can
cultivate it to the top. Its second half is Thunder, and only a root that has formed Thunder can open it, at the full
power the canon was written for. A Metal-only elder cultivates it at a little over half strength, and most of the Tower's
elders are Metal-only. The head's sutra needs Water as well as Metal. So the lineage has two summits, one for Water and
one for Thunder. In five generations one living person can climb both: Mạnh Lưu Ly. That is the Tower's succession
problem, written into its công pháp.

**Its weaknesses.** It cannot brew: no Wood, little Fire, so its pills come from a hired poisoner (Ô Thiềm Tử) and the
market. Its Thunder depends on the vein and on lightning drills that scar somebody every year. Its sutra is copied once
per head and burned at the head's death, so one bad succession could lose it.

**Members on the lineage:** 67 of 70 living. Three use outside methods with reasons: the guest alchemist Ô Thiềm Tử and
his servant-apprentice Mai Độc Ca (Wood poison methods), and the Xuyên Lâm hostage Úc Đả Diệp (his Gate's staff
method).

## 6. Admission (R2 as the Tower applies it)

- **Outer gate:** Metal or Fire at purity 30 or more, or Thunder at all. Recruiters walk the ridge mines and the cone
  villages at Kinh Trập, and the Rain Tower sends the south's children up with letters.
- **Inner gate and above:** Metal at 45, heard at the Bell Test. There is no Thunder test. A Thunder root with too little
  Metal stays in the outer gate, and that is the rule's sore point. Nhiêu Hạ Lôi holds Thunder 38 on Metal 22 and cannot
  pass, while Metal-only boys walk past her every spring. The Thunder Hall wants a second gate for Thunder roots.
  Tưởng Lậu Thanh says the canon's first half is Metal, so the gate is Metal.
- **Head:** by custom, Water and Metal, because the sutra needs both. No head has held Thunder.
- **Exceptions kept:** guest elders (khách khanh) since the Night Rain Edict, never taught the lineage (Ô Thiềm Tử); their
  servants (tạp dịch: Mai Độc Ca); Xuyên Lâm Môn's hostage (Úc Đả Diệp); and the Rain Tower's mortal staff.
- **Men and women** are both taken. Since the Edict the Tower takes ma đạo cultivators of any sect's past. Their past is
  not asked, but they are heard.

## 8. Relations

- **Thiên Xu Tông (the Pivot):** the enemy, grudge −15 (Broken Halberds, Cinnabar Vein). The Pivot broke the Tower's
  founder at Chiết Kích Nguyên (C017) and its head at Đan Sơn Mạch (C189, C190). Mạnh Dạ Vũ swore Doãn Ngọc Hành would
  answer for it, and since 1166 the Tower's seat has stood in sight of the Pivot's old border fort. Doãn's man Tào Mặc
  Toán keeps the Tower's stores. Nhiếp Vô Thanh keeps him alive and uses him to keep the Pivot calm.
- **Phượng Minh Cung:** the other enemy, grudge −15 (Thunder Vein, Cinnabar Vein), and the nearer one: its mines are a
  few days' walk from the new seat. Vu Ngô Đồng gave Quan Vũ Thanh terms in 1185 (A278) that the head has never seen.
  Hoa Triêu Dương holds Đan Sơn Mạch.
- **Lạc Hà Tông:** enemy on the Petal Shore (612-620), sworn ally against the Pivot (788-805), and since the truce
  nothing. Tô Vãn Chiếu's sect is the only great sect that has fought both with and against the Tower.
- **Vassals:**
  - **Xuyên Lâm Môn** (Úc Diệp Thanh) is chính and refused the Edict (A292). It holds the mines around the new seat
    (863, 873, 875), has a nephew at the seat as surety (N-thinh_vu-11), and now claims the risen isle of Hà Châu (A297).
    It is the vassal most likely to break away.
  - **Hồng Đậu Trang** (Nghê Hồng Đậu) is ma đạo and sells poisons and charms. Its mistress was born in Thu Trì, takes
    tea with Quan every winter, and was once Ô Thiềm Tử's lover.
  - **Thu Phần Các** (Lý Hận Bích) keeps the singing graves of Lôi Trạch (A314). Its soul arts are the vaults' nearest
    kin.
  - **Vũ Linh Đường** (Ân Thanh Linh) casts the rain bells, and was founded by a smith who cast the Tower's rain-chains
    (V149). Thạch Liên Hoàn has argued bronze with it by letter for a century. Since 1198 Nhiếp Vô Thanh knows
    that Vũ Linh's mistress hangs bells inside Thu Trì tuned to the Hall of Echoes' own ears (a wall runner sold one
    bell's place to Lục Nhĩ Thính). In 1199 she had the Rain Copper Mine's carts to the hall stopped until the line
    comes down and the mistress's book of ears is sent to her. The Thunder Hall offers the hall copper through Thạch
    Liên Hoàn's storm forge, for a bell line cast in lightning.

  them at half the Hall's price. The Tower's couriers pass the Pivot's gates on them. This spring Hoàng bought half of
  the Hall's rain-fast stroke from its chief painter (hoa_tinh §9).
- **Băng Tâm Các, Thúy Vi Tông:** distant.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The whole lineage.** Only a root with Water, Metal and Thunder can hold both the head's sutra and the canon's second
  half, and only Mạnh Lưu Ly has one. Mạnh Dạ Vũ has not named an heir. In 1186 he took a new disciple, the first in a
  century: Ung Thính Thiền, a blind boy with the head's own root, Water and Metal and no Thunder. The Tower read it as a
  counterweight. The Thunder Hall (Lệ Tiêu Hồn, Hầu Vọng Xuân) wants neither of them. It wants the headship rite changed,
  so that a Thunder head need not hold the sutra.
- **Rain and Thunder.** The Rain Tower and the ridge are two factions with two burials. The Rain faction (Quan Vũ Thanh,
  Lư Tàn Hà, Hứa Thanh Diêm) wants peace with Phượng Minh and the halls brought home. The ridge (Cố Dạ Lan, the Thunder
  Hall, the young Thunder disciples around Mạnh Lưu Ly) wants the vein, the drum and a war it can win. The head keeps a
  foot in each, and goes back to the rain every year.
- **The pass.** At Đan Sơn Mạch the head ordered Kỷ Vãn Tình's rear guard to hold so the column could escape. Phương Đoạn
  Vũ came out of it alive. Mạnh Lưu Ly is Kỷ's daughter and the head's. She has known both things since 1157, and her
  disciple Đàm Vãn Chung read the letter before she did. The head does not know she knows.
- **The lamps.** Lệ Tiêu Hồn has lit a lamp for the head, which the vault law forbids. The blind boy can hear it. Ninh
  Tịch Đăng holds the shears that make a lamp mortal from afar, and she refused her uncle once, in 1177.
- **The spymaster's channel.** Nhiếp Vô Thanh is sworn to report to the head alone. For thirty years she has also sent
  the Pivot the truth, through the Pivot's own clerk, to stop a war. It is treason by the letter of her oath, and she
  thinks it is the oath's purpose.
- **The drum.** Hầu Xuân Lôi sealed the Xuân Lôi Cổ and the rest of Khải Lôi's canon inside the ridge in 470 (C035). Hầu
  Vọng Xuân's drumstick hums at three places, and he has told his master of one. Two of the three lie in or beside
  Xuyên Lâm's mines.
- **The door.** Cố Dạ Lan kept the door in 951 while the head drained Văn Hải Khô. She is the head's surest sword, and the
  only living witness against him.

1. **The drum under the ridge.** Hầu Vọng Xuân's drumstick hums at three places, and two lie in Xuyên Lâm's mines. Lệ
   Tiêu Hồn knows of one. Whoever opens the sealed realm first gets the Xuân Lôi Cổ and the rest of the forbidden canon,
   and may raise Khải Lôi again. (Hầu Vọng Xuân, Hầu Tiểu Cổ, Lệ Tiêu Hồn, Úc Diệp Thanh; places 863, 873, 874, slot R-3.)
2. **The pass.** Mạnh Lưu Ly knows who ordered her mother's death and that he is her father. Phương Đoạn Vũ owes her the
   last order. A Kết Đan breakthrough, a confession or a duel brings it out. (Mạnh Lưu Ly, Mạnh Dạ Vũ, Phương Đoạn Vũ,
   Đàm Vãn Chung; places 874, 211.)
3. **The answer from Phượng Minh.** Quan Vũ Thanh has carried peace terms in her sleeve for fifteen years. If the head
   learns of them from anyone but her, she falls. If he accepts them, the Thunder Hall rises. (Quan Vũ Thanh, Hứa Thanh
   Diêm, Vu Ngô Đồng, Mạnh Dạ Vũ, Lệ Tiêu Hồn; places 7, 874.)
4. **The lamp that should not burn.** The blind disciple hears a lamp in the vaults with the head's breath in it. Telling
   the head ruins Lệ. Telling no one leaves a knife at the head's throat. (Ung Thính Thiền, Lệ Tiêu Hồn, Ninh Tịch Đăng,
   Mạnh Dạ Vũ; place 208.)
5. **The clerk who counts.** The Pivot's man in the stores is protected by the spymaster for reasons she cannot give. A
   runner who watches him, or the player, finds the letters. (Tào Mặc Toán, Nhiếp Vô Thanh, Lăng Ngân Lộ, Doãn Ngọc Hành;
   places 874, 81.)
6. **Thunder at the gate.** At Kinh Trập, Tống Lôi Nhi will pass the Bell Test on Metal 46, and Nhiêu Hạ Lôi, holding
   more Thunder, will not, again. Nhiêu's pills are poisoning her, and she owes money she does not have. The rule can bend,
   break, or kill her. (Nhiêu Hạ Lôi, Âu Tiểu Linh, Tống Lôi Nhi, Tưởng Lậu Thanh, Bộc Thủ Chung, Mai Độc Ca, Mạnh Lưu Ly;
   place 874.)
7. **The chain is cut.** Someone cuts the Iron Rain chain between the seat and the vein: Xuyên Lâm, the Pivot, Phượng
   Minh or the Rain faction. The seat thins and the vein's Thunder runs wild around the vaults. (Thạch Liên Hoàn, Tiêu Ứng
   Lôi, Lệ Tiêu Hồn, Quách Tử Tinh; places 874, 208.)
8. **The hostage and the isle.** Xuyên Lâm claims the risen isle at Hà Châu (A297) and wants its nephew home. The
   ridge girl who feeds the Gate the drill dates is his friend. (Úc Đả Diệp, Úc Diệp Thanh, Khúc Thanh Điện, Nghiêm Tố
   Tố; places 160, 28, 873.)
9. **The book in the cupboard.** An inner disciple has read a righteous sword manual from Trúc Ẩn Viện every night for
   fifty years. The spymaster knows, the Hall of Rules suspects, and the blind boy has heard it read aloud. (Diệp Ẩn Thư,
   Nhiếp Vô Thanh, Tưởng Lậu Thanh, Ung Thính Thiền; places 874, 436.)
10. **The last rain on the lake.** Lư Tàn Hà has twenty-two years left and has not chosen her burial. Mạnh Lưu Ly wants
    the founder's chain taken down and carried to the ridge to make the Regalia whole. The Rain Tower would rather fall.
    (Lư Tàn Hà, Yên Trích Thủy, Quan Vũ Thanh, Mạnh Lưu Ly; places 194, 7.)
11. **The forbidden duel.** Cố Dạ Lan has two swords left to measure. One is the Pivot's head. A duel would be a war,
    and refusing it has cost her two hundred years of pride. (Cố Dạ Lan, Doãn Ngọc Hành, Tư Không Hạc Niên, Tôn Tuyệt
    Bích; places 874, 187.)
