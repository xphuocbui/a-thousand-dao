The gate stands in black-pine forest in the middle of the cape, off every track, and most people who walk past it never see it. The sect is a guild of road guards with a formation art. Its lanterns hang at the
cape's crossroads. A traveller who pays a handful of rice can walk the road to Yên Ba or Phù Kiều under a moving
bank of mist that robbers cannot see into. The guards do not ask who the traveller is, and that has made the sect
useful to honest carters, to smugglers, and once to a robber. Its founder was given his art by a friend. Its lord calls
that a theft, and its head is the most careful man on the cape.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4): stay.** The seat passes: Wood 46 % (first lean, well over 25), Water 17 % (second, over 15). The forests round it are Wood-heavy and damp (Thúy Cái Lâm 1055, Ngưng Lục Lâm 1074, Hắc Tùng Lâm 1062), and mist comes off the cape's rivers every morning, which is all the sect needs. Nothing moves, and nothing costs.

**The ground.** The sect's roads run from the tea village of Trà Viên Thôn (1075) and the stonecutters of Thạch Công
Thôn (1057) through Phù Kiều Trấn's floating bridge (1064) and on to Yên Ba Thành (30) on the coast. A branch runs north
past the empty bell-casters' town of Đồng Sơn Trấn (1056, abandoned 1094). The robbers come down from the Ridge of the
Bobtails (Đoản Vĩ Cương, 1043), a lynx ridge beyond Đồng Sơn. The Forest of Lost Mist (Mê Vụ Lâm, 1050) is the
sect's training ground.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The forest villages of the middle cape (Trà Viên 45 %, Thạch Công 42 %, Thằng
Tác 52 % Wood) breed Wood roots, and they fill the inner gate. The river town of Phù Kiều Trấn (Wood 43 %, Water 27 %)
breeds Wood-Water: the founder and his sworn brother Tất Hành Vụ were born there, and so were half the sect's mist
layers. Carters from Yên Ba Thành (Water 38 %) and orphans of the coast come in on their Water and stay at the outer
gate's lanterns. The sect hardly ever turns anyone away.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The mist does not ask.** The sect's oath and its whole ethic. A guard who holds the mist over a traveller does not
  ask their name, their cargo or their business, and does not tell anyone afterward. That is what the road fee buys.
  Thúy Vi finds it disreputable. The carters find it the only honest thing on the road.
- **The mist hides; it does not strike.** The founder kept his sworn brother's one condition. The Haze Walk is never
  used to come at anyone unseen, and the sect's fighters step out of the mist before they hit. The road captain's axe
  has an edge that only bites coming out.
- **Lifting the mist off someone under it** is the one betrayal. A guard who does it is put out of the gate on foot,
  without a lantern, at night.
- **The road fee:** a handful of rice for the poor, silver for carters, and salt fish from the port house. Nobody pays
  for a child.
- **Dress:** grey-green oiled cloaks, wide bamboo hats, a lantern on a pole. On the road a guard gives no name. In the
  forest they use names freely, the founder's custom: "the mist knows who we are."
- **Food:** road food: rice balls, pickled fern, and the tea of Trà Viên, brewed strong in gourds.
- **The dead:** buried beside the road under a milestone with a mist seal cut in it, facing the way they were walking.
  The cape says every milestone has a guard under it. It is not quite true.
- **Outsiders:** treated as travellers, which means politely and without questions.
- **How others see them:** cheap, reliable, discreet and not quite respectable. The robbers of the Bobtail ridge call
  them the grey ghosts and leave lit lanterns alone.

**Why it fits.** The Haze Walk thins one body into the green haze of a forest slope. The founder saw that the same haze
could be laid on a road and locked there with stones, which made it an array. The Mist-Lock canon needs Wood to make the
haze and Water to hold it down in the damp, and the middle cape has both. The sect has no Earth and no Metal, so it cannot
build walls or forge blades. It makes ground that cannot be seen. Its weakness is Wind: a foe's Wind art lifts any
mist it lays. Members on the lineage: 10 of 10.

## 6. Admission (R2 as the sect applies it)

- **Outer gate:** Wood or Water at 30, and one season carrying loads on the roads without losing one. The sect takes
  carters' sons, tea-pickers, orphans of robbed caravans, and once a robber.
- **Inner gate and above:** Wood at 45 and the Primer's sixth layer: the first lantern, kept lit until a stranger passes
  under it unseen.
- **Exceptions:** none named. The sect does not ask about anyone's past, which is how the road captain got in.

## 8. Relations

- **Thúy Vi Tông (lord, loyalty 55):** the sect exists because a Thúy Vi elder gave his sworn brother the Haze Walk in
  1048, and half of Thúy Vi's elders call that a theft. Tất Hành Vụ still visits every spring and argues about it at
  dinner with his granddaughter Tất Quế Lạc. Thúy Vi's elders are moving to demand a tithe for the walk (lord hook 9),
  and Bàng means to avoid paying it without ever saying no. Diêm Hải Tảo's port-house carts use the mist roads to Yên Ba,
  and she pays in salt fish. Loyalty is the lowest of Thúy Vi's vassals because the sect is neutral and hides people
  Thúy Vi would like to find.
- **Tùng Đào Cung:** Hồ Tùng Nguyệt, Tùng Đào's first elder, already pays one spy in Thúy Vi's outer gate for the Haze
  Walk (Phù Liên Kiều). In 1192 her agent bought the debts of Luyện Tố Y's brother. The price is the walk's ninth
  layer, which only Tất can read.
- **Hồng Lô Cốc:** the ash carts come south on the mist roads; Bàng's guards are paid in red pills.
- **Hộ Lâm Đường, Vân Khởi Quán:** far away in the north; friendly by letter.
- **The robbers of Đoản Vĩ Cương (1043):** the sect's reason to exist and its road captain's old band. They killed Bối
  Trường Đình in 1190.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **What is under the gate.** The founder did not vanish in 1146. He died of old age in Bàng's arms on the Đồng Sơn
  road, at 198, and asked to be buried under the threshold of the seat's gate. He also asked Bàng to let the cape think
  the mist had taken him, so that robbers would believe it could take a man. Bàng left the lantern burning on the road
  and carried the body home in mist. Every spring when he relays the hidden-gate array, he lays it over the grave as well.
  The founder's grandson plays on the threshold stone.
- **The daughter's ledger.** Hàm Thanh Đăng believes Thúy Vi's Hall of Rules took the Haze Walk back in 1146 and her
  father with it. She keeps a list of everyone who has called the walk a theft, and Nhạc Chính Hành, who has kept that hall since 1150, is at the top of it. She wants the founder's lantern, and she suspects Bàng of something without knowing what.
- **The road captain.** Triều Ngũ Lang was a robber of the Bobtail ridge until the founder hid him in 1081. He is the
  sect's only Kết Đan and will not be head. The boy he carried out of the 1190 caravan, Ký Thất Lộ, has sworn to kill
  the men who did it, and those men are Triều's old band.
- **The ninth layer.** Luyện Tố Y owes Tùng Đào her brother's debts. The price is a layer she cannot get unless Tất
  Hành Vụ teaches it to her, and Tất trusts her because she is from his town.
- **The next head.** Bàng is 115 and a late Trúc Cơ with no core in sight. The candidates are the founder's daughter,
  the robber, and a proud girl of twenty-one who can lose him in his own mist.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The tithe.** Thúy Vi's elders send an envoy to Vụ Ẩn Môn for a tithe on the Haze Walk. Bàng will not refuse and
   will not pay, Tất Hành Vụ would rather die than see it paid, and Hàm Thanh Đăng sees her chance to accuse the Hall of
   Rules. (Bàng Yên Tỏa, Tất Hành Vụ, Hàm Thanh Đăng, Biện Vãn Tình, Nhạc Chính Hành; places 1063, 1110.)
2. **Under the threshold.** A frost heave cracks the gate's threshold stone. Hàm Tiểu Vụ finds bone under it, and his
   mother finds out what her father asked for and who kept it from her. (Hàm Tiểu Vụ, Hàm Thanh Đăng, Bàng Yên Tỏa;
   place 1063.)
3. **The ninth layer.** Luyện Tố Y asks Tất Hành Vụ to teach her the Haze Walk's ninth layer. Tùng Đào's agent waits
   at Hàn Tân with her brother's debts, and Phù Liên Kiều in Thúy Vi's outer gate is after the same thing. (Luyện Tố Y,
   Tất Hành Vụ, Hồ Tùng Nguyệt, Phù Liên Kiều; places 1064, 31, 1110.)
4. **The Bobtail ridge.** Ký Thất Lộ learns who carried him out of the 1190 caravan and where Triều Ngũ Lang came from.
   The robbers are raiding the Đồng Sơn road again. (Ký Thất Lộ, Triều Ngũ Lang; places 1043, 1056.)
5. **The cart that is not hers.** Thai Vãn Xa holds the mist over a cart on the port road that is not Diêm Hải Tảo's,
   and it is carrying something Thúy Vi's Hall of Rules is looking for. The mist does not ask, and the player may.
   (Thai Vãn Xa, Diêm Hải Tảo; places 30, 1063.)
6. **The dry gale.** A summer gale off the sea lifts every mist on the cape for three days, and every caravan is in the
   open. The robbers know. (Bàng Yên Tỏa, Triều Ngũ Lang, Khuyết Lạc Vụ; the cape's roads.)
7. **Lost in the Forest of Lost Mist.** Khuyết Lạc Vụ lays a mist in Mê Vụ Lâm that she cannot lift, and a traveller
   is inside it. (Khuyết Lạc Vụ, Bàng Yên Tỏa; place 1050.)
8. **The lantern.** Hàm Thanh Đăng asks the player to steal the Mist-Lock Lantern from the head. It is the founder's,
   and it is also the array's key. (Hàm Thanh Đăng, Bàng Yên Tỏa; place 1063.)
