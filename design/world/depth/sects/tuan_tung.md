The youngest sect under the pines. It is a hall of caravan guards on a hill above the market. They stood on the Tùng Hạ
road for three nights in the wolf winter of 1190 and were given the hill for it. They have no canon worth the name and
no Golden Core. They live on wolf pelts and the bounty the pines pay on wolf ears, and they have not lost a caravan
since. Its people are whoever was on the road that winter, from
anywhere.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4, R3): stay, and become no-lean (Wood, Metal → none).** The seat fails Metal (12.7 %, and not in the top
two). Better Metal ground lies 23-28 tiles off in the ravines and the mine (Cầm Tượng Trấn 1491, Bích Hác Sâm 1490,
Thạch Lục Khoáng 167), and none of it is on a road. A road warden's hall has to be where the roads meet. V043 puts it on
the hill above Tùng Hạ Thị, and the pines' own man (`vu_hanh_lam`) lives there. Its methods were never Metal methods
either: a staff and a bow, and the body that carries them. The old leans described the founders' gear, not their
cultivation.

**Why no-lean is true.** The hall recruits by căn cốt and by deed: a guard who can walk all night and shoot straight at
dawn. Its first wardens were a Pivot caravan master (Metal-Earth), a northern hunter (Wood), a Tùng Gian magistrate's son
(Wood-Water) and a Lạc Hà-born girl (Earth). No element-led method could have taught them all. The body school is cheap,
and it is honest about what the hall is.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** As a no-lean hall, its roots are its birthplaces': mostly Wood from the pine
villages (Phạt Mộc, Thanh Tùng, Bạch Lang: Wood 44-53 %), some Metal-Earth from the Pivot's caravan towns, and a little of
everything from the market. That mix is the hall.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **Stand on the road.** The hall's only teaching is what its founders did in 1190: stand between the wagons and what
  wants them, for as long as it takes. A warden is judged by nights held, not by layers.
- **Nobody is left.** No driver, mule or wagon is abandoned while a warden can walk. Trang Thiết Cước lost every wagon in
  1190 and kept every driver, and the hall counts that as its founding victory.
- **The leader first.** In a fight, shoot the strongest. The rest follow it home.
- **Dress and food.** Wolfskin and grey wool, a staff, a bow and a whistle. The wardens eat on the road: dried meat,
  millet cakes, pine-needle tea. In the hall they eat together, and the head serves.
- **The dead.** A dead warden is buried beside the road they kept, under a green pine like the dead of Thanh Tùng Thôn,
  with their whistle hung on a branch. Doanh Thủ Lộ's pine is at the third milestone above Tùng Hạ.
- **Taboos:** sleeping on watch; leaving a driver; taking a caravan's money without walking its road; shooting a beast
  that is running away. That last rule is the head's, and Diêm Lạc Nhạn hates it.
- **Outsiders** are the hall's work. Caravans pay what they can; the pelts and the pines' bounty pay the rest.

**Why it fits:** a road warden cultivates on the road. Qi is drawn in with each pull of the bow and driven into the bone
with each turn of the staff, so the body is the method. The outer gate hardens the flesh first. The canon pins a foe
with arrows and turns a crowd away from the wagons with the staff. The Three Nights art grows stronger the longer a
fight lasts. The Wolf-Winter Arrow marks the strongest foe and breaks the rest when it falls. The hall wants a real
Earth-tier canon and does not have one.

## 6. Admission (R3: by căn cốt and by the road)

- **What it recruits by:** căn cốt and nerve. A recruit walks the Tùng Hạ - Cổ Độ road through one night with a warden
  and is judged at dawn. Root does not matter.
- **Outer to inner:** a season of caravans without losing a driver, and the Unbroken Flesh Canon to its sixth layer.
- **Exceptions:** none needed. The pines' own riders, Vu Hành Lâm and Mã Lang Nha, ride with the wardens and are
  Tùng Đào's.

## 8. Relations

- **Tùng Đào Cung (lord):** the pines gave the hill (V043) and pay half the hall's keep. The Coiled Root Hall treats the
  wardens as its own auxiliaries. Vu Hành Lâm lives at the hall, and Trần Bách Xuyên, who keeps the pine-sea roads, is
  Hứa's friend. Hứa failed the palace's inner gate at fourteen and still cannot sit through the listening. The Hall of
  Voices thinks the wardens loud.
- **Ngạo Tuyết Phái:** the sword school's young men come down every autumn to pick fights. Hứa beat Ân Lãnh Phong with a
  staff in 1198 and the school has not forgiven it.
- **Thải Chi Cốc:** sets the wardens' bones for nothing. Nghiêm Tục Đoạn, the valley's bone-setter, is the son of the
  hall's first dead warden.
- **Thiên Xu's caravans:** the hall's best customers. Trang Thiết Cước is a Pivot man, and the Pivot's merchants trust
  him.
- **Lạc Hà Tông:** Liễu Khê Nhi was born on its side of the border, and the Root Hall does not like it.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **Wolf money.** Since the wolf winter the Coiled Root Hall has paid a bounty on every pair of wolf ears brought to
  Tùng Hạ, and the pelts sell at the market. The caravans pay what they can, and the ears and pelts pay the rest. The
  head's rule, never shoot a beast that is running away, costs the hall half of every winter's ears. **Diêm Lạc Nhạn**
  says so at every meal, and **Hứa Vãn Cung**, who serves it, keeps the rule.
- **The wagon.** Everyone on the road knows a wolf-king's cub rode in the column of 1190: the burned cage lay on the
  Tùng Hạ road for a week. Hứa burned it, and he will not name the wagon. Diêm calls that protecting a paying customer,
  and she is right about whose. The cage rode in one of **Trang Thiết Cước**'s own wagons, hired at Bích Khê to a buyer
  riding south, and Trang's silver built the hall. Naming the wagon would make the hall's founding victory a carter's
  crime.
- **A pelt for a running wolf.** Diêm pays the outer wardens a pelt from her own share for every wolf they bring in shot
  from behind. **Hoàng Thiết Huyền** has four. This autumn, three days out of Tùng Hạ on the Bạch Lang road, he left a
  mule-driver at the wagons to run one down, and the pack took the driver's leg. **Lạc Tùng Lang**, the road's warden,
  carried the man back to Tùng Hạ and cut Hoàng's arrow out of a wolf that had been running. He keeps the arrow. Trang
  paid the driver's caravan for the leg and wrote it down as a wolf bite.
- **The king come back.** The cub that got out of the fire of 1190 is a wolf-king now, and its pack is on the Bạch Lang
  road. Its ears and pelts are worth a year's bounty. Diêm means to have them, running or standing.
- **The magistrate's son.** Hứa's elder brother is a timber magistrate of Tùng Gian Thành who thinks the hall a
  disgrace, and he sits on the pines' resin accounts. His daughter Hứa Tịch Vân walked out to her uncle's hall at
  sixteen, and he has not written to her since.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The cub's wagon.** A wolf-king grown from a cub that escaped the fire of 1190 leads a new pack down to the Bạch
   Lang road, and asks, in its way, for the man who sold it. Diêm wants its pelt; Trang wants the wagon left unnamed.
   (Hứa Vãn Cung, Trang Thiết Cước, Diêm Lạc Nhạn, Lạc Tùng Lang; places 110, 921.)
2. **A Golden Core for hire.** The hall needs a Kết Đan elder. The player may find one: a loose cultivator, Lâm Dã Hạc
   once he forms his core, or a disgraced elder of a great sect. (Hứa Vãn Cung, Trang Thiết Cước; place 376.)
3. **The duel at Tùng Hạ.** Ân Lãnh Phong comes back with a real sword. (Hứa Vãn Cung, Ân Lãnh Phong, Phó Ngạo Sương;
   place 46.)
4. **The Lạc Hà warden.** The Coiled Root Hall wants Liễu Khê Nhi off the river road before the next toll quarrel.
   Lạc Tùng Lang refuses. (Liễu Khê Nhi, Lạc Tùng Lang, Trần Bách Xuyên, Nhiếp Cô Tân.)
5. **The third milestone.** Someone has cut Doanh Thủ Lộ's pine, the gravest crime on the pines' ground. Nghiêm Tục
   Đoạn comes down from the valley to see who. (Nghiêm Tục Đoạn, Hứa Vãn Cung, Sầm Thanh Tùng; place 46.)
6. **The magistrate's accounts.** Hứa's brother in Tùng Gian Thành stops the pines' subsidy over a resin quarrel, and
   the hall must earn its keep from Pivot caravans alone. (Hứa Vãn Cung, Trang Thiết Cước, Hầu Thiện Kế; places 32, 46.)
7. **A caravan of the Pivot.** A Thiên Xu surveyors' caravan bound for the grey pine vein hires the wardens, and the
   pines' vein-warden wants to know what they carry. (Trang Thiết Cước, Lý Hành Lộ, Tân Mặc Bách; place 206.)
8. **The arrow.** Lạc Tùng Lang asks the player where to take Hoàng Thiết Huyền's arrow: to the head, which breaks
   Diêm, or back to Diêm, which makes him party to her pelts. The driver's caravan wants a name. (Lạc Tùng Lang, Diêm
   Lạc Nhạn, Hứa Vãn Cung, Hoàng Thiết Huyền; places 46, 376.)
