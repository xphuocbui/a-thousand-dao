A cluster of fish-smoking sheds and boat-houses on the frozen river above Liên Giang Thành. In winter its people walk
out onto the ice, cut holes, drop lines, and sit beside them in the snow for days, cultivating the way Hàn Giang Môn's
fishers did before the Pavilion took the coast (A128). In summer they row. Its one licence and its one hope are the
same: to revive a sect that the school's lord destroyed.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4): stay.** The seat is river forest (Water 34 %, Wood 33 %) and passes for a Water sect. The cold river
itself is what the school cultivates on, and it runs past the door. Nothing moves.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The river towns and coast villages (Liên Giang 39 %, Lô Hoa 39 %, Tuyết Đăng 41 %
Water; Wood 27-32 %) breed Water roots about twice as often as the steppe does, with Wood beside them. The school's
people are almost all Water-Wood fishers' children. There is no Ice anywhere in Hàn Sương Châu, so Ice roots are born
here only by chance, and those few go to the Pavilion.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **Sit until the fish comes.** Everything worth having comes to the patient: fish, qi, the next layer. The school
  despises hurry, and Sử Phá Băng is its standing joke.
- **The hole is yours.** Each disciple cuts their own ice hole at the first freeze and sits by it all winter. To sit at
  another's hole is theft. At death a disciple's hole is cut one last time and left open until it freezes over.
- **The fish tax.** The school pays the Pavilion in ice-fish, the way Hàn Giang's fishers have since 499 (A131). Phạm
  Lãnh Vân of the Hall of Cold Law reads its books each spring.
- **Dress:** straw rain capes and wide hats in the old Hàn Giang shape, after Liễu Thoa Lạp's cape and hat (V077).
  The Pavilion tolerates it.
- **Food:** fish, smoked, salted, raw from the hole. The school eats no meat that walks.
- **Taboos:** sitting on spring ice; teaching Ice (the licence); speaking of Hàn Giang's fall in front of Pavilion men.
- **Outsiders** are welcome to sit, if they cut their own hole.

**Why it fits:** a river school on a Water river, with a rowing method for the outer gate and a sitting method for
the inner. The fishermen's chant and the boat-wake verse were sung on this coast before the school wrote them down. The
weakness is in the canon itself: it was rebuilt from memory, and its eighth layer is where everyone stops. The reason
(section 9) is that the true method needs Ice, and the licence forbids it.

## 6. Admission

- **Outer gate:** Water 30+. The river's children, and the Pavilion's outer-gate failures who would rather fish than
  walk the road (Uông Tuyết Nhạn is on her way).
- **Inner and above:** Water 45+ and one winter's sitting by a hole without leaving it.
- **Exceptions:** none. An Ice root is sent to the Pavilion, as the licence requires.

## 8. Relations

- **Băng Tâm Các (lord):** the licence-giver (loyalty 55). The licence was won with Thẩm Hàn Giang's word behind it, and
  his Hall of Cold Law oversees it. Lư owes Thẩm, and the coast's whole faction leans his way. The Pavilion's clerk
  reads the books, and its Frost Test failures come here.
- **Hàn Giang Môn (fallen, C099):** the sect the school exists to revive. Bồ Tọa Tuyết keeps its way, and Liễu Thoa Nhi
  carries its blood without saying so. Her brother Liễu Ngư Ca, the Pavilion's inner disciple, is Hàn Giang's hidden
  heir.
- **Trạc Anh Đài:** fellow coast people. The two sects split the boat escort, and Đoàn Thanh Lãng and Lư are friends.
  Sử Phá Băng drinks with the whalers.
- **The Liễu family of Liên Giang:** Lư suspects they kept the true manual, and he is right.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The ninth layer.** Lư Độc Điếu has sat at the eighth layer of his own canon for forty years. He thinks a layer was
  lost in the old men's memory. Bồ Tọa Tuyết knows better: the stone under the ruin's ice says the true sitting needs
  Ice, and a Water-only sitter stops at the eighth. The licence forbids Ice. Bồ has not told Lư, because the school
  would then be a fishing company with a nice story.
- **The Liễu girl.** Lư took Liễu Thoa Nhi as his disciple hoping she would show him the family's method. She sits it at
  night on the ice, where he can almost see.
- **The abacus.** Phương Lô Hoa keeps the school alive while her husband sits. Her disciple Ngải Hàn Tịch means to take
  the abacus from her.
- **The heir question at the Pavilion.** If Thẩm Hàn Giang becomes Keeper, the licence may be widened to Ice, and the
  school could become Hàn Giang in all but name. Everyone at Lãnh Giang knows this, and nobody says it.

## 10. Treasures and sets

- **Ice Hook (Băng Câu)**, Lư's bone fishhook, the first thing the school made. Held by Lư.
- **Snow Cape of the Old Sitter (Tuyết Thoa)**, Bồ's frost-white straw cape in Liễu Thoa Lạp's shape.
- **The stone under the ice** at Hàn Giang Môn's ruin (place 216). A hook, not an item.
- **Lone Line (Cô Tuyến)**, Lư Độc Điếu: Ice Hook, Ice-Hole Sitting Robe. Full set: in the sitting stance his
  linh lực leech heals him, and every 5 s the next hook is a sure hit.
- **Heap of Snow (Tuyết Đôi)**, Bồ Tọa Tuyết: Snow Cape of the Old Sitter, Old Sitter's Gourd, River-Bottom Stone. Full
  set: his ward grows while he sits, and when it breaks his next counter fires twice.
- The real relic of the coast is Liễu Thoa Lạp's lone fisher's rod, lost in the ruin; Liễu Ngư Ca is looking for it.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The stone under the ice.** The player finds Bồ Tọa Tuyết's stone at Hàn Giang Môn's ruin, or Bồ dies and leaves it to
   them. Telling Lư breaks his life's work; telling the Pavilion invites a ruling. (Bồ, Lư; place 216.)
2. **The Liễu way.** Lư catches Liễu Thoa Nhi sitting the true method at night. He can beg, threaten or let her be, and her
   brother at the Pavilion hears of it. (Lư, Liễu Thoa Nhi, Liễu Ngư Ca.)
3. **Widen the licence.** Thẩm Hàn Giang promises Ice to the school if the coast backs his claim to the Vessel. (Thẩm, Lư,
   Phương Lô Hoa, Đoàn Thanh Lãng.)
4. **Spring ice.** A young disciple sits on spring ice to catch up, as Đái Tuyết Lạp did. The player hears the ice crack.
   (Mạch Tiểu Điếu or Ngải Hàn Tịch; place 361.)
5. **The rod.** Liễu Ngư Ca comes home to search Hàn Giang's ruin for Liễu Thoa Lạp's rod, and the school must decide whether
   to help the Pavilion's man or the coast's heir. They are the same man. (Liễu Ngư Ca, Liễu Thoa Nhi, Bồ.)
6. **Smugglers on the ice-fish run.** Someone is moving Ice beast cores through the escort's boats. Sử Phá Băng and the
   whalers want to settle it with boathooks. (Sử, Ngạc Hải Tiêu; place 1440.)
7. **Bồ's last sit.** The old man walks out onto the river to die sitting. The school, the coast and Thẩm Hàn Giang come to
   watch. Whoever sits beside him at the end may learn what he never told. (Bồ, Thẩm, Mạch Tiểu Điếu.)
