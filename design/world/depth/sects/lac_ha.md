The sect is named for the line the west is proud of: the sunset clouds fly together with the lone wild duck, and the
autumn water is one colour with the long sky (lạc hà dữ cô vụ tề phi, thu thủy cộng trường thiên nhất sắc). Its
founder was the lone duck, and his daughter was called Tề Phi, "flying together". Lạc Hà is the richest sect in the west and the most
practical. It writes everything down, prices everything, and pays its debts. It has lost two wars to the Pivot, both
of them wars it started. It is patient in a fight because its method only gets stronger as the light goes.

**The story.** The founder planted maples round the first hall (A190), and his canon runs on Earth and Wood together,
cultivating fastest where the place's qi holds both (`sunset-cloud-canon`). For eight centuries the maples did that work, and no one read the warning he had written in
the canon's margin. The steppe dried. The maples died one by one, and the last of them died in 1131 (N-lac_ha-02).
After that the canon crawled on the dry seat: no one there turned it past its tenth layer, and the head Tô Vãn Chiếu stalled there for
forty-six years. The Ferry War showed the other weakness. Tùng Đào's raiders cut every rope bridge on the Ngư Ca border
(A202) and came within a day's ride of an open hall on flat ground. In 1176 the array master Quách Tiếp Vân told the
head that the high ground of his birthplace, the silver town abandoned in 1040 (V106), held Earth and Wood together.
In 1177 she put the move to the elders, and the vote went against Trầm Tịch Dương and Hình Trọng Sơn (N-lac_ha-06).
She paid for it with a loan from her own vassal, Thiên Bảo Trai (N-lac_ha-03). Quách laid the Cloud Bridge Array
across the gorge where the town's rope bridge had broken (N-lac_ha-04), and the sect moved up in 1185
(N-lac_ha-05). The eleventh layer turned in the head the next spring.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The sect's ground is four lands with two kinds of qi. The Earth belt is the
steppe and badlands of Thu Thủy Loan (region 12) and the old seat's side of Ngư Ca Xuyên (region 13), at 33-49 %
Earth: Hà Khúc, Xích Thổ, Song Tỉnh, Liên Chu, Mã Độ. The river land, the plain and the island are Wood and Water.
Ngư Ca Xuyên's forests run 33-43 % Wood, the grassland of Trường Thiên Nguyên (region 11) is near 30 % Water, and Cô Vụ
Đảo (region 10) is jungle and grassland with Earth at 25 % or less. Earth-rooted children are therefore mostly
steppe-born, and the inner gate (Earth 45) is a steppe people: herders' and well-diggers' sons, market children from
Vật Hoa Thị and Hạc Đinh Thị. The river and the island send Wood and Water children to the outer gate, and most of
them go on to a vassal on Placement Day (section 4). The new seat's mountain breeds Earth and Metal. The sect's quiet
prejudice is the reverse of the Ice sects': it trusts the steppe-born with its books and the river-born with its songs.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The glow deepens.** The sect's one teaching, taken from its method: what is patient gets stronger as the day goes
  down. Lạc Hà does not hurry a bargain, a breakthrough or a fight. It opens slowly and finishes late. Outsiders call
  it slow. The sect's elders answer that it has outlasted everyone it ever traded with.
- **The hour of the glow.** The day turns on sunset. The outer gate sits for its primer in the hour the sun takes to
  set, facing west on the steppe (a custom; the primer turns at any hour). Councils sit at dusk (the move was voted at dusk). Contracts are sealed at sunset with
  the red jasper Sunset Cloud Seal so that the sky witnesses them. Dawn is for work.
- **Everything is a contract.** A disciple's oath of entry is a written agreement with terms: years of service, and
  what the sect owes back (a method, a stipend, a burial facing west). The Hall of Scales tries a breach as a broken
  bargain. A disciple may leave by buying out the rest of the term. No other great sect allows that, and Lạc Hà is
  proud of it.
- **Dress.** Robes of Tang Ma hemp dyed with madder and steppe dust to the colours of evening. The outer gate wears pale
  ochre, the inner gate rust, true disciples and elders dusk violet. Only the head wears the founder's robe, which
  Hoàng Lan Chi re-dyes in his pot. The Geese Hall wears a grey goose-feather pin, the Boat Hall a stern-lamp clasp,
  the Maple Hall a pressed red leaf.
- **The Evening Song.** The river boats sang at dusk before the sect existed (A197 counts six hundred years of it).
  The founder heard them from the steppe, set Wood qi into their song, and gave each village back one verse of the
  art. Since 803 the villages sing one verse fewer (V091, V103), and since 805 so does the sect. The ninth verse is
  sung only over the dead.
- **The Evening Isle.** Elders go to Tịch Chiếu Đảo to die (A164). The keeper rows them out, they watch one last
  sunset from the beach, and they are buried there facing west. Younger dead are buried on the steppe below the old
  seat, also facing west. On the dusk of the Chiết Kích anniversary the whole sect is silent and lights no lamps.
- **Placement Day (Phân Đường Nhật).** Each spring, outer disciples who have failed the dusk test three times are
  placed with a vassal whose methods fit their root. Wood and Water children go to Bình Thủy Môn, Nam Phố Đường or
  Nhàn Vân Quán. Earth and Metal children go to Thiên Bảo Trai, Nhạn Trận Môn or Minh Loan Các. Wood and Earth children
  go to Yên Quang Các, and riders to Mục Vân Trang. The vassal pays a placement fee, and the sect takes a tenth of the
  placed disciple's earnings for thirty years. Outsiders call it selling children. The sect calls it finding them the
  right teacher, and its vassals are full of people who agree.
- **Food.** Steppe food: millet, mutton, Diêm Điền salt, river fish from the villages. The dusk meal is eaten in
  silence for the length of one verse, then loudly.
- **Guests.** A guest's first night is free ("the sunset is free"), and after that everything has a price, posted.
  The guest house on the Cloud Bridge has the best view in the west and reports to the envoy.
- **Taboos:** breaking a sealed contract; fighting in the hour of the glow (war excepted, and argued every time); going
  onto Chiết Kích Nguyên; speaking of Thanh Sương at the dusk meal; singing the ninth verse over the living.
- **How others see them:** rich, slow and not to be trusted with a promise that has no paper. The Pivot sees them
  as the sect that broke the Compact. The vassals see a lord that keeps its books honestly and charges for everything.

**Why it fits.** Every method is Earth or lives on Earth, and Wood comes in where the sect needs to last: the song's
sustain and the canon's upper layers. The steppe gives Earth and the river gives Wood, so the lineage needs both lands,
which is why the sect has never been able to choose between them. The Sunset Cloud canon is a long-fight method (memory:
sustain is king). Its cloud weighs the foe with Earth stacks that thicken each breath, and its damage grows as the fight
goes on. It is weak in the first ten seconds and dangerous after thirty. Lạc Hà's fighters plan for that: open
with a ward or a song, and win late. The elders' field art needs a rank of allies standing in it, so the sect fights as a line; it lost
at Thiết Tỏa Quan (508). It hires its pills from Yên Quang Các, its talismans from Nam Phố Đường and its ward
bells from Minh Loan Các rather than admitting crafters off its root. The one outside art a member holds is
`hundred-blades-canon`, which the head bought.

**The head's secret.** The Deep Vein Scripture (`deep-vein-scripture`, existing: "miners of a buried sect drank their
own blood to wake the stone, and the stone paid back every drop") is carved on the walls of a gallery under Vân Kiều.
Quách Tiếp Vân's father's guild sealed it in 1040, the year the silver ran out, and said the two were not connected.
It was opened in 1181, during the array work. The head reads it alone. It is a debt art, and she is a merchant.

## 6. Admission (R2 as the sect applies it)

- **Outer gate:** Earth or Wood at purity 30 or more. The Counting House's recruiters walk the steppe villages and the
  markets every spring. The Boat Hall brings in river children who sing, and the island hall brings island children.
- **The dusk test:** at the primer's sixth layer, the disciple's palms must hold the glow from sunset until full dark.
  In practice that needs Earth at 45, and the inner gate takes no one without it.
- **Inner gate and above:** Earth at 45 or more, no exceptions in the lineage. Wood-heavy disciples like Chử Hồng Diệp
  and Sài Thanh Phong entered by a point or two of Earth and are the sect's best healers.
- **Placement Day:** three failures and the disciple goes to a vassal (section 4). Tạ Quy Yến goes this spring.
- **Exceptions kept:** the guest armourer Vưu Chước Kim (khách khanh) and the servants (tạp dịch: the dyer, the cook,
  the isle's ferryman, the forge boy). No hostages: the sect takes surety in coin.

- **The loose west:** Kim Vạn Lý (Trầm's go-between in the debt market), Lạc Đan Thanh (sells Tăng Vân Tiêu his
  pills), Vệ Văn Cơ (Quách's creditor), Đoạn Thương Hải (the blind master of Đàm Ảnh Thành, Lận Viễn Phàm's friend).

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The ring in the vault.** To pay for the move the head pledged the Lone Duck Ring, the founder's ring and the key
  to the treasury arrays, to Thiên Bảo Trai. The sect thinks it is in the vault. Four people know: the head, Trầm, Tông
  Thủ Tín and Phan Vật Hoa. Hách Ánh Hà makes five, and the head does not know she knows. The clerk Phương Toán Ngọc has
  found that the vault has not been opened since 1179. Từ Bình Hành has asked twice to read the contract. Trầm has
  quietly bought a third of the debt himself, so the head pays part of her interest to her own master without
  knowing it.
- **The plain against the mountain.** The head made a trade peace in 1175 and moved the sect off the plain. Hình
  Trọng Sơn's war party wants the tolls back and the Compact broken: his daughter, Lô Niệm Ca, the Watch, and quietly
  Thịnh Kinh Hàn, who has never been to war. Cảnh Trường Thiên, the war elder, will not make war. Trầm will not pay for
  one. Hình's drafts with Cố Dạ Lan would put the sect back where it was in 788. The Watch already hangs Minh Loan war
  bells that the head has not ordered, paid for in the bronze of the plain's dead.
- **The sword on the plain.** Thanh Sương lies in the miasma (C159). The sect believes it fell with Nhan Tề Phi. Cảnh
  knows he dropped it and ran, and he knows the worse thing: the miasma was Lạc Hà's. In 802 the founder had an array
  of rot buried under the plain, to be sprung if the field broke, and Nhan Tề Phi sprang it. Ten thousand of the
  Pivot's dead are the sect's doing, and on the Pivot's side Đỗ Tinh Hà knew the array was there when he sent Quý
  down (Thiên Xu page). Hình Lạc Nhạn has seen something blue in the grey and has promised Đồ Tiểu Liên to go
  back in. The head wants it, because the twin swords together would wake for a head with no Thunder and no Ice.
  Bồ Tàn Chiếu, who saw Cảnh come home that night without it, has twenty years left.
- **The war elder's spring.** Nhạn Trận's founder saw Cảnh run past his file in 803 and named him on his deathbed
  to Mục Hàn Nhạn. Since 1175, the year after the gate lost a file on a rope bridge for Lạc Hà's hire fee, Mục has
  sold that silence for Cảnh's word that Lạc Hà will never again call or hire the gate's files for a war. So Cảnh
  will not make war, and each spring he flies a month down to Lạc Nhạn Hồ to drill the plain's levy for nothing.
  Mục bills the cities for it.
- **The locked gallery.** The head's secret art is carved under the seat. Quách knows because he found it. Miêu Thạch
  Lan has noticed the lock and the dust on the head's sleeves. Tô Mộ Vân read the first lines by the head's lamp and has
  them by heart.
- **The orphan of Đào Sa.** Lữ Tẫn Chu was raised by the man who burned his father's boat. He has known for twenty years
  and has learned this year that the order was the head's.
- **Thirty contracts for the ring.** The loan's term ends in 1209, and the head means to clear it with people. Over
  and above this spring's placements, thirty outer disciples go to Thiên Bảo Trai in one lot, their fees and thirty
  years of tenths struck off the debt. Only the Earth and Metal children will pass its gate. The rest serve until 1230
  as hired hands at its furnace and brooks, in a Metal house that will never teach them the method their oath
  promised. Phan Vật Hoa would rather take people than money. Her one condition, which Lạc Hà can barely meet: all
  thirty delivered to the autumn auction at Vật Hoa Thị, or nothing is struck off.
- **The list.** **Hách Ánh Hà** draws it at Vật Hoa Thị through the spring auction, and Thiên Bảo's steward Tiền Ngũ
  Phân sells her his mistress's top price as the bidding goes. She has left off the children she means to place
  herself: her grand-niece Hách Thần Hà, a herder boy from her village, and Tạ Quy Yến, promised to Nam Phố for a
  favour. Children who have failed the dusk test only twice fill the count, and so do children who have not yet sat
  it. **Tô Hiểu Chu**, the head's own blood, is on the list so that no one can say only the poor are sold. The head has
  said the list is the list.
- **The signer and the clerk.** Nhạc Trường Tín carries the one list down to the outer gate, a month and a half on the
  steppe road, and the thirty must walk the same road back by autumn. **Tô Bình Chu** must sign every placement on it,
  his son's among them, and has signed none. He is offering a poor family in Hà Khúc his stipend for ten years if
  their second child takes his son's place: a child enrolled at the gate only to be sold. **Tông Thủ Tín** is sending
  each family on the list the price of its child's buy-out at the old rate, and every child bought out is a hole Hách
  must fill.
- **The lightning.** Tử Điện has slept in every head's hand for 840 years. It woke for the guest smith Vưu Chước Kim
  the one time he cleaned it, and it hums for his forge boy Cổ Tiểu Thiết, who has a thread of Thunder no one has
  tested.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The ring in the vault.** Phương Toán Ngọc's discovery that the vault has not opened since 1179 reaches the Hall
   of Scales. Phan Vật Hoa raises the interest, and Trầm reveals he holds a third of the notes. The sect learns that its
   treasury key sits in a vassal's vault. (Tô Vãn Chiếu, Trầm Tịch Dương, Tông Thủ Tín, Phương Toán Ngọc, Từ Bình
   Hành, Phan Vật Hoa; places 558, 50.)
2. **Thanh Sương on the plain.** Hình Lạc Nhạn goes back into the miasma with Đồ Tiểu Liên. Cảnh Trường Thiên must
   follow her or let her go, and the player may go with either. Whoever brings the sword out decides who holds both
   swords. (Hình Lạc Nhạn, Đồ Tiểu Liên, Cảnh, Hình Trọng Sơn, the head; Chiết Kích Nguyên, place 223.)
3. **A second Broken Halberds.** The player finds Hình Trọng Sơn's draft pact with Cố Dạ Lan at the Watch. They can
   take it to the head, to Cảnh, or to the Pivot, or keep it. (Hình Trọng Sơn, Cố Dạ Lan, Cảnh, Doãn Ngọc Hành; place
   223.)
4. **The orphan's question.** On the Ferry War's anniversary Lữ Tẫn Chu asks the head to her face who gave the order.
   Lô Xướng Vãn steps between them, and Giang Cửu Khúc's yearly letter arrives the same week. (Lữ Tẫn Chu, Lô Xướng
   Vãn, Lô Niệm Ca, Tô Vãn Chiếu, Giang Cửu Khúc; places 139, 47.)
5. **The locked gallery.** Miêu Thạch Lan finds the gallery door open on a new moon, and the carving runs on past
   where the head has read. Tô Mộ Vân already knows its first lines. The art pays back every drop, and someone has been
   paying. (Miêu Thạch Lan, Tô Mộ Vân, Quách Tiếp Vân, the head; place 558.)
6. **The lightning wakes.** A forge accident at Tử Điện Thành shows the forge boy's Thunder. The head now has someone
   who could wake her sword, and Vưu Chước Kim, a guest, has a disciple the lineage would never have admitted. (Cổ
   Tiểu Thiết, Vưu Chước Kim, the head, Sử Thu Thủy; place 39.)
7. **Placement Day.** The player, a Wood-rooted outer disciple, fails the dusk test a third time and is placed. Tạ Quy
   Yến is in the same line, and Châu Phi Vân has come for him. The same week Hách Ánh Hà's list of thirty for Thiên Bảo
   Trai is read at the outer gate; the player's name is on it, and Tạ's is not. (Tô Bình Chu, Tạ Quy Yến, Châu Phi Vân,
   Ngạc Viễn Sơn, Hách Ánh Hà; place 4.)
8. **The last row.** Bồ Tàn Chiếu's lifespan is ending. Bồ Tiểu Lô means to row him herself. Before he goes he wants to
   tell someone what he saw on the night of 803: Cảnh coming home with the body and without the sword. (Bồ Tàn Chiếu,
   Bồ Tiểu Lô, Khúc Lão Điếu, Cảnh; place 196.)
9. **The outer ring.** Vệ Văn Cơ comes to collect from Quách, who cannot pay. The array's outer ring would lift the
   seat past the old seat's density, and the miners' grandchildren in Vân Gian would come up the mountain to build it.
   (Quách Tiếp Vân, Vệ Văn Cơ, Mạch Ngân Sương, Trầm; places 558, 560.)
10. **The lost fleet.** Lận Viễn Phàm fits out a third boat, and this time Giang Vọng Phàm means to be on it. Blind
    Đoạn Thương Hải says he has heard a sail in the pool of Đàm Ảnh Thành. (Lận Viễn Phàm, Giang Vọng Phàm, Luân Hải
    Yến, Đoạn Thương Hải; places 72, 226, 37.)
11. **The ninth verse.** Hồng Vãn Tình collects the missing tune from Lô Địch Thôn, the missing verse from Xướng Vãn
    Thôn and the lamps of Vãn Đăng Thôn. She wants the villages to sing the whole song at dusk again, and the sect to
    sing it too. (Hồng Vãn Tình, Lô Xướng Vãn, Diêu Tiểu Đăng, Thương Ngư Ca; places 538, 458, 555, 490.)
12. **Whose miasma.** Quý Thủ Lũy grows miasma-root at the Pivot's barrow watch; Hình Lạc Nhạn walks the same edge from
    the other side. If either finds the array's stones under the grey, the Pivot learns that Lạc Hà poisoned the plain,
    and Lạc Hà learns that the Pivot's marshal was sent onto it knowingly. Cảnh Trường Thiên is the only living
    member who can say which is worse. (Cảnh, Hình Lạc Nhạn, Hình Trọng Sơn, Đỗ Tinh Hà, Quý Thủ Lũy; Chiết Kích
    Nguyên, place 223.)
13. **The thirtieth signature.** Tô Bình Chu asks the player to find a family who will put a child in his son's place.
    Tô Hiểu Chu asks the player to stop his father. Hách Ánh Hà wants the list signed before the head counts it, and
    Phan Vật Hoa's condition voids the whole lot if one name is missing at the autumn auction. The player can carry the
    substitute's papers, tell the Hall of Scales that the oath of entry owes each disciple a method and a Metal house
    will never teach a Wood child one, or show the head the names Hách left off. (Tô Bình Chu, Tô Hiểu Chu, Hách Ánh
    Hà, Từ Bình Hành, Tô Vãn Chiếu, Phan Vật Hoa; places 4, 558, 50.)
