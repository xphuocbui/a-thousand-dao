The palace is warm, ceremonious and sure of itself. Its charter is a line of the Thi Kinh: the phoenix sings on that
high ridge, and the wutong grows there facing the morning sun. It believes the phoenix appears only in a well-governed
age, and that Phượng Minh is how the south knows it is well governed. It feeds its neighbours in bad years and lectures
them in good ones. It destroyed one sect under writ and another without one, and it keeps the second one's sword.

**The story.** Ngô Thê Đồng founded the palace in 480 on the ridge because it was the empty land of Cửu Thiều Cung,
where he had been born (C009, V138), and because the wutong grows there. For a hundred years the palace's
Fire stalled on Metal ground. In 584 it asked the Pivot for the Compact's first writ against Xích Thành Môn, which was
burning villages to feed its fire arts (C118). In 590 it destroyed the gate (C119). In the fighting the founder's first
disciple Nguyên Chước Ly burned Hỏa Ti Trấn, the fire-silk town at the foot of Chúc Dung Khẩu, when its reelers
barred the road (V165, N-phuong_minh-01). In 592 the founder raised the **Fire Court (Hỏa Đình)** on the ashes and sent
his inner gate down to it (N-phuong_minh-03). A reeler's girl, Tang Sơ Ti, dug the last unburned silk out of the sheds,
and he laid it as the threads of the **Nest Array (Phượng Sào Trận)**, which carries the vent's fire into the court.
He swore that no silk would be reeled at those vents again (N-phuong_minh-04). He never moved his own seat. The pipes
he had found were on the ridge, the wutong was on the ridge, and he died there in 1100 (C181, C182). His heir did not
share his reasons. After the Cinnabar Vein War, which was fought on the ridge's own northern edge at Đan Sơn Mạch, Hoa Triêu Dương
moved the head's court, the treasury and the Hall of Spoils down to the Fire Court in 1162 (N-phuong_minh-12). The
ridge palace became the **Wutong Court (Ngô Đồng Đình)**.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** No inhabited place on the palace's ground has much Fire: the best villages
of the fire coast (Cúc Viên, Tang Điền, Tang Âm, Phần Vũ, Thủy Điền) run 20-24 %, about even, and the only strong Fire
land is Hỏa Ti (47 %) and the vents (51-57 %), which nobody is born on. Fire-rooted members come from two places:
- **the ash-born (tro sinh)**: children born at the Fire Court itself since 592. At 47 % Fire the R1 factor is about
  2.3, plus 8 purity. They are 17 of the 46 named members of the inner gate and above, and most of its elders' halls.
- **the even coast and plain** (19-24 % Fire: Lai Nghi, Thủy Điền, Thạch Cơ, Tang Điền, Mạch Điền, Cúc Viên, Phần Vũ),
  where a Fire root at 45+ is ordinary luck: 23 of the 46. The palace recruits these from the Cúc Viên child each
  generation, the vassals' wards and the market towns.
- **six named exceptions** born on low-Fire land, each with a reason in their record: the head (the lake shore, A261),
  Vu Ngô Đồng (a bamboo camp), Chúc Hiểu Nghi (the market's Vân Môn blood), Miêu Trúc Sinh (seventy years to pass),
  and the ridge-born Mông Chu Tiêu and Nhậm Cương Thủ.

The rest of the ground breeds what the palace cannot use inward. The bamboo forests and jungle valleys of Thê Ngô Lĩnh
and the coast (Tử Trúc Lâm, Bích Lãng, Phi Hồ Cốc, Quế Viên: 37-43 % Wood) give **Wood** roots. These come in by the
outer gate's Wood door and mostly stay there, as bird keepers, gardeners and flute cutters. The ridge villages and mines
(Kiều Mạch, Lưu Kim, Đan Sa Quật: 32-45 % Metal) give **Metal**, and ridge-born disciples rise only when Fire comes with
it (Mông Chu Tiêu, Nhậm Cương Thủ). The jungle coast (Ngũ Thải, Hồ Tâm: 33-55 % Water) gives **Water**.
So the palace holds three peoples: the ash-born, who run it; the bamboo-born, who serve it; and the ridge-born, who
remember where it came from.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The charter poem.** Every disciple learns the Thi Kinh couplet on the first day: the phoenix sings on that high
  ridge, the wutong grows there facing the morning sun. The palace reads it as a law of rank: the tree (Wood) stands
  first and the bird (Fire) comes to it. Wood feeds fire, and the outer gate feeds the inner.
- **The Three Refusals (Tam Bất).** From Trang Tử's phoenix: it rests only on the wutong, eats only bamboo seed, drinks
  only from the sweet spring. The palace means by it that one takes nothing without right. Two of its vassals are the
  refusals made into halls: Trúc Thực Viên grows the bamboo seed (A243) and Lễ Tuyền Phái keeps the sweet spring (A256).
  The palace has broken the refusals twice, at Cô Thành and in the Thunder Vein War, and it knows it.
- **The Five Marks.** The Sơn Hải Kinh writes five words on the phoenix of Đan Huyệt: virtue on the head,
  righteousness on the wings, rites on the back, benevolence on the breast, trust on the belly. The palace is organised
  by them (§7), its main method opens them (§5), and an elder's rank is read in the colours sewn on their hem, one for
  each mark they have lit.
- **Dawn.** Both courts start the day with the dawn flute. The outer disciples breathe facing east. No council opens
  before the call has finished, and no one speaks over music.
- **Dress.** Outer disciples wear undyed hemp with a red cord. Inner disciples wear vermilion. Elders add the coloured
  hem. They carry fans, not swords. Only a guest elder may wear a blade in the Hall of Virtue, which is why the red sword
  in the Hall of Spoils is wrapped.
- **Food.** Rice from the plain, bamboo shoots and wild greens; no meat on rite days; Lễ Tuyền's spirit wine at
  funerals and at New Year. Guests are seated nearest the fire, and refusing the seat is an insult.
- **The dead.** The dead are burned at the Nest, on the lip of Chúc Dung Khẩu. Their ashes are carried up the long
  road to Quy Vũ Lĩnh, the Ridge of the Returning Feathers, and scattered from the wutong grove, and one red feather for
  each is kept in the Wutong Court. "Born in fire, return to the tree." The founder's ashes lie under the great wutong.
- **Taboos.** Reeling silk at the vents (the founder's vow); feeding the Ashen Phoenix pyre on anyone's blood but one's
  own; playing the Shao in nine movements; cutting the spotted bamboo of Tương Phi Đảo (A239); naming the sword in the
  Hall of Spoils.
- **Outsiders.** Courteous, generous and condescending. The palace feeds the south in famine (C134, A262), lends
  banners and birds to its vassals, and expects to be thanked in ceremony. It calls the Pivot's justice cold and its own
  warm. Its neighbours find the warmth more expensive.

## 6. Admission (R2 as the palace applies it)

- **Outer gate:** Fire or Wood at purity 30 or more. Recruiters walk the bamboo villages and the coast towns each spring.
  Cúc Viên Thôn sends one child every generation, the debt of 586 (A303). The vassals send wards: Phần Vũ Đường sends
  its head's child as surety (Hùng Diễm Nhi), and Bách Vũ Các sends bird keepers.
- **The Feather Kindling.** At the sixth layer of the Wutong Dawn Breath the disciple holds a vent bird's down feather
  in the palm and runs the breath. If it catches without burning the hand, their Fire is ready (45+), and they go up the
  Fire Court's stair into the inner gate. It may be sat once a year, without limit. Miêu Trúc Sinh sat it for seventy
  years.
- **Inner gate and above:** Fire 45 or more. No exceptions within the lineage.
- **Those who never pass** are offered the Wutong Court (birds, bamboo, flutes, funerals), a vassal (Trúc Thực, Bách
  Vũ, Lễ Tuyền take the palace's Wood roots gladly), or the road.
- **Exceptions kept:** guest elders (khách khanh), servants (tạp dịch), and vassal wards. A Light root, the palace's
  first in living memory (Chúc Hiểu Nghi), is not an exception: she passed on her Fire.

## 8. Relations

- **Thiên Xu Tông (the Pivot):** the palace's oldest patron. It granted the Compact's first writ (C118), imposed the
  Desert Road truce (C144), gave Kinh Lôi Mạch to Thính Vũ (C179), and in 1154 came into the Cinnabar Vein War on the
  palace's side (C189). The palace owes it, and the head has a private reason to fear it: the Tiêu Thiều Quản, and
  what she played for Doãn Ngọc Hành that night, which he took for a ridge flute.
- **Thính Vũ Lâu:** the enemy (grudge −15). The palace started the Thunder Vein War without a writ and lost it; Thính Vũ
  started the Cinnabar Vein War and lost it. Cố Dạ Lan keeps Dụ Đan Huỳnh's fan. Quan Vũ Thanh came for peace in 1185
  (A278). The palace answered only in private, through Vu Ngô Đồng at the Wutong Court: peace for a fifth of Kinh Lôi
  Mạch's yield. No reply has come, because Quan never delivered the terms (`thinh_vu.md`). The head reads the silence as
  a refusal. The war elder does not know there were terms at all.
- **Băng Tâm Các:** the old enemy of the Desert Road. The quarrel was "quietly forgotten" when the granaries opened in
  1060 (A262), but not by Phó Ám Hương, and the palace still holds the Red Plum.
- **Lạc Hà Tông:** an ally of the Pirate Purge (C152), and otherwise distant.
- **Vassals.** **Trúc Thực Viên** and **Lễ Tuyền Phái** are the doctrine's own halls (bamboo seed, sweet spring), loyal
  and well fed. **Bách Vũ Các** raises the messenger birds (Tạ Thanh Loan and Vu Ngô Đồng trade birds). **Vọng Triều
  Đường** watches the coast that Thôn Hải burned (Ôn Thủ Đăng, Phàn Vọng Hải's friend). **Chức Hà Các** weaves fire-silk
  without the vents (V172). The palace has not decided whether that pleases it; its array keeper secretly depends on
  it. **Phần Vũ Đường** sits four tiles from the Fire Court, revives Xích Thành's fire body arts in the same vents, holds
  the palace's banner and sends its head's daughter as surety.
- **Hồng Lô Cốc** (Thúy Vi's furnace vassal): the palace's alchemist's rival.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **War or peace.** Sử Hoành Dực will take the Hall of Righteousness up to the ridge if the head makes peace with
  Thính Vũ while Cố Dạ Lan lives. Trình Hi Quang drafted the 1185 terms, has guessed from Quan Vũ Thanh's letters to Chử Trì
  Âm that they never reached her head, and means to carry the next offer himself, with a clause of his own: the right
  wing fan returned. Sử's daughter Sử Niệm Huỳnh wants the peace. Nhậm Cương Thủ and the ridge
  would welcome Sử.
- **The ridge and the Fire Court.** The ridge says the head ran from the Tower in 1162. The Seal of the Wutong stayed
  with Vu Ngô Đồng, so the Five-Virtue Regalia is split between the courts. Vu is stalled at late Kết Đan because he has
  been feeding his Wood to the dying great wutong, and Kế Chu Sa cannot brew his breakthrough pill without that tree's
  seed. A Wood girl in the outer gate, Kha Thanh Miêu, may be what the tree needs.
- **The Shao.** The head plays eight movements in secret and once played them for Doãn Ngọc Hành. Liên Thiều Nghi wants
  the Shao restored openly, has rebuilt the eighth movement, and suspects the head. Lộ Văn Khánh hides the musician of
  1195 at Kích Khánh Đường and writes down his tunes. His sister Chúc Hiểu Nghi sleeps next to the head and hears the
  music in her breathing. She is the palace's only Light root, and the pipes are Light.
- **The Nest's silk.** The Nest Array is fraying. Tang Kế Ti has broken the founder's vow and reeled new silk at the vent.
  Her apprentice Trâu Kết Tuyến has found a strand that is too new, and the errand boy Dư Tân Hỏa saw her at night and was
  paid to forget. Her son buys Chức Hà's silk on the side.
- **The sword.** Nhiếp Tẫn Sa killed Phó Thanh Mai at Cô Thành and wants to return his sword and confess before Phó
  Ám Hương dies. The head will return it when it buys something. Du Diễm Ca has told Hạ Lăng Hàn where it hangs.
- **Succession.** The head is 250 with three quarters of her life ahead of her, and has named no heir. Trình Hi Quang
  means to earn it with the peace. Sử Hoành Dực would oppose him with the Hall.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The sword goes home.** Nhiếp Tẫn Sa asks the player, or Du Diễm Ca, to carry the Red Plum and his confession to
   Phó Ám Hương before Phó dies. The head forbids it until it can buy Băng Tâm's support, and Hạ Lăng Hàn is already on
   the road with Du's letter. (Nhiếp, Du, Hoa, Phó Ám Hương, Hạ Lăng Hàn; places 449, 227.)
2. **The terms in the sleeve.** Trình Hi Quang learns that Quan Vũ Thanh never delivered the 1185 terms and rides for the
   Tower with a new offer that adds the right wing fan. Sử Hoành Dực hears of it on the road, and must decide whether
   his wife's fan is worth the war he wanted. (Trình, Sử, Sử Niệm Huỳnh, Chử Trì Âm, Vu Ngô Đồng, Quan Vũ Thanh, Cố Dạ
   Lan; places 6, 211, 7.)
3. **The ninth movement.** The Pivot hears that Phượng Minh holds the Tiêu Thiều Quản. Doãn Ngọc Hành has to ask
   himself why he entered the Cinnabar Vein War. (Hoa, Doãn Ngọc Hành, Chúc Hiểu Nghi; places 6, 5.)
4. **The musician at Kích Khánh Đường.** Chúc Hiểu Nghi learns her brother never sailed. Lộ Văn Khánh has written down
   tunes no living score has, and Liên Thiều Nghi wants them. (Chúc, Lộ, Liên, Bốc Thự Dao; places 724, 53.)
5. **The Nest goes cold.** A strand fails in winter and the Fire Court's density drops. Tang Kế Ti must confess the new
   silk or let the array fail; Chức Hà's vent-less silk is the other answer. (Tang Kế Ti, Tang Tục Lạc, Thân Vân Cẩm,
   Trâu Kết Tuyến, Dư Tân Hỏa; places 449, 253, 1013.)
6. **The dying tree.** The great wutong drops twice its leaves. Vu Ngô Đồng's Kết Đan has stopped, Kế Chu Sa needs a
   living seed, and a Wood girl from the outer gate is the one the tree answers. (Vu, An Thúy Vũ, Kha Thanh Miêu, Kế
   Chu Sa, Bì Thuần; place 6.)
7. **The Regalia.** To wear the full Five-Virtue Regalia, the head must take the Seal of the Wutong from the ridge or
   go back up to it. Either way the ridge decides what the move of 1162 meant. (Hoa, Vu, Nhậm Cương Thủ; places 6, 449.)
8. **Kình Nha.** Phàn Vọng Hải's diver Ô Trầm Châu finds the pirate's sabre in Thôn Hải's hold and wants to keep it as his
   ancestor's. (Phàn, Ô Trầm Châu, Can Hải Trì; place 217.)
9. **The surety in the vents.** Hùng Diễm Nhi is caught tempering in Phần Vũ's vents at night. The palace must punish
