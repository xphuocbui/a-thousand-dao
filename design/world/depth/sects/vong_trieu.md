The hall was founded by the survivors of the pirate years, sworn by the burned piers of Châu Lệ that no harbour on this
coast will burn again (V141). It keeps a chain of lamp towers from the crumbling fort of Liệu Phàm Bảo in the north to
the reed village of Lô Châu in the south. A sail seen at night is passed from lamp to lamp to the palace fleet at Thạch
Cơ Thành. No pirate has come since the Purge of 781. The world thinks the hall is watching an empty sea. Its storm
elder agrees, and wants it watching the weather instead.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4, R3): stay at place 337 and become no-lean (leans Water, Metal → none).** The seat is coast steppe
(Water 28 %, Earth 25 %, Fire 19 %, Wood 16 %, Metal 12 %, density 0.79). Metal is last, so the old leans fail. The
audit offers Liệu Phàm Bảo (795, the fort, Water 32 %, Metal 26 %), and a re-lean to Water, Earth would also pass in
place. Neither fits what the hall is. It was never a school of an element. It is an oath. The founders were whoever
survived: pearl divers of Châu Lệ (Water), salt makers of Hải Diêm, reed cutters of Lô Châu (Wood), herders of the
sea pastures (Earth). Its craft is lamps, seals and watching, and none of it needs an element. The coast's land is
mixed (Âu Lộ 34 % Water, Hải Mục 29 % Earth, Thâm Tỉnh 38 % Earth, Lô Châu 29 % Wood). So the hall recruits by
kin and oath and teaches over any root.

| Option | Fit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| no-lean at 337 (R3) | always fits | **chosen**: the oath admits, not the root |
| re-lean Water, Earth at 337 | fits (28 % / 25 %) | rejected: would shut out half the sworn families |
| move to Liệu Phàm Bảo (795) | Water, Metal fits | rejected as seat; kept as the **northern lamp** (it is 52 tiles away and crumbling) |

**What it costs.** Nothing on the map. The sect record's `leans` becomes `[]` and is listed under no-lean sects in
`README.md`. Chronicle: V140 and V141 stay true. Add N-vong_trieu-01 to -04. Density 0.79 is thin, and the hall does not
care: it cultivates on towers.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** No-lean, so the hall looks like its coast. The members born at Âu Lộ and
the salt marsh come up Water (34-39 % land, about 1.7-1.9 times even). Those from Hải Mục, Tử Thái and Thâm Tỉnh come
up Earth, and those from Lô Châu come up Wood. The head is Water and Earth, and so is most of the hall.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The Oath of the Burned Piers.** "No harbour on this coast will burn again." Every keeper swears it at the founder's
  tower, naming the place their family lost: a harbour, a village, a boat, or (lately) a storm.
- **The lamp.** Every tower is lit at dusk and put out at dawn, and no keeper sleeps while their lamp is lit. A keeper
  who lets a lamp go out stands the next ten nights on the tower top without a lamp.
- **Dress.** Oiled grey coats and a brass lamp-hook at the belt. Elders carry a lit storm lamp at all hours.
- **Food.** Salt fish, laver from Tử Thái, millet. Lamp oil is rendered from fish, and the hall smells of it.
- **The dead** are buried under the tower they kept, in the footing, "to keep watch." The founder lies under the first
  tower above the piers.
- **Taboos.** Letting a lamp go out; lighting a false lamp (the wreckers' trick); burning anything on the shore,
  including the dead.
- **Outsiders.** Any ship in distress is sheltered and fed. Strangers on the shore at night are watched until dawn.

**Why it fits.** Watchers need a hard body for storms, seals for wards, and a soul that reaches far over water.
None of these is elemental, and the coast is mixed. The Lamp-Ward Seal Canon lasts as long as its maker remembers it,
which is the hall's whole discipline. **Catalogue gap:** the soul-song-seal family has no element-free main method, so
the hall's main and both secrets are proposals.

## 6. Admission (R3)

- **By oath and kin.** Any root. The hall takes a child of any family the pirates or the sea took, and the families of
  its keepers. A candidate stands one night on a tower top without a lamp; if they are awake at dawn, they swear.
- **Inner gate:** a winter on a tower learning the Tide-Reading Gaze, and a lamp kept for a year without going out.
- **Exceptions:** none needed. Servants are sworn too.

## 8. Relations

- **Phượng Minh Cung (lord):** loyal and a little forgotten. The hall's lamps feed the palace fleet's watch. The admiral
  Phàn Vọng Hải (`phan_vong_hai`) is Ôn Thủ Đăng's drinking friend, of the same pearl-diver stock, and the storm elder
  has written to him behind Ôn's back. The fleet's diver Ô Trầm Châu (`o_tram_chau`) dives with the hall's Trạm Châu Nhi.
  In 1190 the palace asked for a storm ward on its jetty and was refused.
- **Lễ Tuyền Phái:** the healers ride to the towers when a keeper falls ill.
- **Thôn Hải Môn** (fallen, 781): the enemy the oath was sworn against, four hundred years dead.
- **The coast villages (Âu Lộ, Hàm Trạch, Hải Mục, Lô Châu):** the hall's families.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **Lamps or walls.** Ôn Thủ Đăng keeps the chain lit and wants the three dark towers relit. Chân Định Lan wants the oil
  spent on breakwater arrays, and the steward Lê Bạch Diêm has the numbers to back her. The hall's young (Kỳ Lam Sa)
  side with Chân. The head will not move.
- **Why he will not.** In 913 Ôn asked his sworn brother to keep his lamp at Kim Sa while he went to a girl in Lam Điện.
  The storm tide came, and Dịch Thủy Thanh drowned at the lit lamp. Ôn has kept every lamp lit since, and he will
  not turn the hall to the storm that took Dịch. No one alive knows.
- **The unlit sails.** Kiển Mục Hải has twice seen sails pass south at night with no lamp, off the northern fort. His
  letter to the head has had no answer.
- **The diver.** Trạm Châu Nhi broke her family's vow and dives; the hall pretends not to know.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The unlit sails.** Kiển Mục Hải's sails are real: smugglers, or something that remembers Thôn Hải. The player
   carries his letter, or stands a night on his tower. (Kiển, Đinh Vọng Tinh, Ôn, Phàn Vọng Hải; places 795, 93, 217.)
2. **The next storm tide.** A storm tide is coming. Chân Định Lan and Ôn must choose between lamps and wards. If the
   player helps Chân build in time, a village lives. (Chân, Ôn, Lê Bạch Diêm, Kỳ Lam Sa; places 337, 791, 93.)
3. **The order from the palace.** Phàn Vọng Hải answers Chân's letter and the palace orders the hall to the sea-walls.
   Ôn must obey his lord or his oath. (Ôn, Chân, Phàn; places 337, 93.)
4. **The night of 913.** A grandchild of Lam Điện finds a girl's letter of 913 asking a young keeper to come to the
   festival. (Ôn, Dịch Thủy Thanh's grave; places 1020, 1019.)
5. **Kình Nha.** Trạm Châu Nhi and Ô Trầm Châu find the pirate's sabre in the hold. A keeper sworn against Thôn Hải
   holding its lord's blade is a scandal on the coast. (Trạm, Ô Trầm Châu, Phàn; place 217.)
6. **Relight the dark towers.** Three towers are dark for want of keepers. The player can find keepers among the coast
   families, at the cost of the hall's oil. (Ôn, Lê Bạch Diêm, Cao Tiểu Phàm; the coast.)
