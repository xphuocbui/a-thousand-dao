The Gate is a road with a sect on it. Its people are guides, porters and staff fighters who walk travellers from the
ridge down through the bamboo and the rain forest to the river and the sea. They carry lamps at night through a forest
whose lord forbids lamps. They refused the Tower's Night Rain arts in 907, and they hold the mines that now surround the
Tower's seat. The Tower holds the head's nephew as surety. Of the five vassals, this one is the most likely to leave.

**Why no-lean is true.** The Gate was founded by guides, not cultivators of a root (A289). Its staff canon cultivates
steadiness and its primer cultivates the legs and the lungs. Its old Thunder, Wood lean was its head's own root, read as
the sect's: Úc Diệp Thanh has Wood from the forest road and Thunder from the fort he was born in, and no one at the
Gate can teach him either. What the Gate recruits for is a body that can walk all day in the wet with a load. That is
bone, and the guide families of the road.

**What it costs.** Nothing on the map: the seat, its art, its routes and its province stay. The sect record changes
`leans` to `[]` and gains the lineage. Place 28 keeps its density (1.64). No chronicle line changes.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The Gate's ground runs down from the ridge to the sea, and it breeds three
kinds of guide.
- **The ridge** (the pass at 28 and the mines the Gate holds: Tử Tinh 863, Sa Kim 873, Thủ Lôi 875, Lôi Ngân 838, Lôi
  Tinh 861, Tử Ngọc 696, Nghênh Lôi 862, Nam Vọng 698: Metal 40-54 %, Earth 23-28 %) breeds Metal and Earth roots: the
  miners, haulers and wardens (Lỗ Kim Tuyền, Khúc Phụ Thạch, Nghiêm Quy Khứ). A ridge child with Fire forms Thunder there
  at up to twice the plain rate (Tử Tinh holds Thunder 37), and the Tower takes those children.
- **The bamboo** (Dẫn Lộ Thôn 885 and Phạt Trúc Thôn 886, the guides' and the staff-cutters' villages: Wood 48-49 %,
  Metal 25 %) breeds Wood with Metal under it. These are the guides and the Road Mother's line (Phí Thoa Y, Kỳ Khinh Mã,
  Cù Tiểu Thoa, Mẫn Thanh Trượng).
- **The river and the coast** (the ferry at Hà Châu 160, Vọng Hải Trấn 91: Wood 38-42 %, Water 20-29 %) breed Wood and
  Water: ferrymen and coast guides (Đinh Vãn Độ, Thôi Vọng Phàm).
Because the Gate takes any root, its hall looks like its road: Metal at the top, Wood in the middle, Water at the end.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **Do not listen (mạc thính).** The Gate's teaching comes from the song its guides sing on the road: *do not listen to
  the rain piercing the forest and beating the leaves; why not sing and whistle, and walk slowly* (莫聽穿林打葉聲，
  何妨吟嘯且徐行). The founder's line is that the rain is real and does not matter. The Tower, its lord, teaches the
  opposite: listen to the rain before you strike. Both sides have known this for five hundred years and have stopped
  finding it funny.
- **Walk slowly (từ hành).** A guide never runs. Running on wet ground kills travellers, and running in a fight wastes
  the staff's weight. A Gate disciple who is seen running on the road walks the next month carrying a porter's load.
- **The staff, the sandals and the cape.** *Bamboo staff and straw sandals, lighter than a horse; who is afraid? One
  straw cape in the misty rain, and let it be, all my life.* Every disciple cuts their own staff in the bamboo at Phạt
  Trúc Thôn, wears straw sandals and a straw cape, and is buried with the staff.
- **The Staff Road.** The dead are carried down the forest road one last time and buried at its side, with their staff
  planted upright on the grave. The first few miles below the pass are lined with old staffs, grey and leaning. Guides
  touch them as they pass.
- **The lamp.** Since the Night Rain Edict (A274) the Tower lights no lamp after dark. The Gate carries one ahead of
  every night crossing, through the Tower's own forest. Putting out a lamp on the road is the Gate's only taboo about
  light.
- **Every traveller is the road's guest.** The Gate guides anyone who pays the day's fee, righteous or ma đạo, and the
  poor for rice. It does not guide anyone to the gracious door of Hồng Đậu Trang after dark, nor to the graves of Lôi
  Trạch in autumn. A guide who leaves a traveller in the forest is put out of the Gate.
- **Food.** Rice balls wrapped in banana leaf, eaten walking; tea at the fords. The Gate eats nothing it has to stop
  for.
- **Taboos:** running on the road; leaving a traveller; taking a fee twice; putting out a lamp on the road; learning the
  Night Rain arts (since 907).
- **How others see them.** Travellers see the only people in the south who will walk them through the forest at night.
  The Tower sees a chính gate in its own house, holding its mines and keeping a lamp lit where it has forbidden lamps.
  The miners of the ridge see their employer, and inform for it.

**Why it fits.** A guide needs legs, lungs, a staff and a mind that does not panic in the dark. None of that needs an
element. The primer tempers the walk. The canon turns a long running fight into the Gate's advantage: every
step taken adds weight to the next swing. The palm scripture is the close-quarters art for when the staff is caught in
vines. The mind method shuts out soul and song, which is the Gate's answer to living under a sect whose Hall of Echoes
listens at every door.

**Its weaknesses.** The Gate cannot brew, cast or ward. It buys pills in the Tower's market and arrays from Vũ Linh
Đường. And it cannot teach its own head's Thunder.

**Members on the lineage:** 11 of 11 living. The dead brother Úc Từ Hành ran it too.

## 6. Admission (no-lean, R3)

- **What it recruits by:** bone (căn cốt) first, then kin. Every child of the guide villages may walk a day of the road
  with a load at twelve. Those who reach the pass without stopping are taken. The ridge's mining families and the
  coast's ferry families send their sons and daughters by custom.
- **Any root.** The Gate admits any root and teaches none, so its people's roots follow their birthplaces (R1).
- **Exceptions:** none needed. Under R3 a no-lean sect has no admission rule to break.
- **The Tower's deserters.** Since 1177 the Gate has taken back any Gate-born child who walks home from the Tower, and
  the Tower knows it.

## 8. Relations

- **Thính Vũ Lâu (lord):** a cold oath (loyalty 40). The Gate refused the Edict (A292) and never forgave it. In 1156 the
  head's brother died guiding the Tower's column out of Đan Sơn Mạch, with Kỷ Vãn Tình's rear guard
  (N-thinh_vu-08). In 1166 the Tower put its seat on Thần Cổ Phong among the Gate's mines, and the old head sat nine days
  at its gate in protest. Since 1170 the Tower has held the head's nephew Úc Đả Diệp as surety (N-thinh_vu-11). The
  Gate's miners count every cart the Tower cuts, and Lỗ Kim Tuyền and the Tower's Quách Tử Tinh have come to blows over
  the count. Through Khúc Phụ Thạch, the head reads the Tower's drill dates. In 1199 both sides claimed the risen isle
  at Hà Châu (A297). The Tower's people it deals with: Hứa Thanh Diêm (envoy; a letter to the Road Mother), Ninh Tịch
  Đăng (who did not cut the deserter's wick), Lệ Tiêu Hồn (who wanted it cut), Hầu Vọng Xuân (rival; his family's mine
  is the Gate's), Phương Đoạn Vũ (he came out of the pass).
- **The Pivot (Thiên Xu Tông):** the Gate's heart is righteous and leans toward the Pivot. It holds the Pivot's old
  border fort Nam Vọng Bảo (698, V123) and keeps it empty. If the Gate ever breaks away, it will break toward the Pivot.
- **Phượng Minh Cung:** burned Thủ Lôi Bảo, the head's birthplace, in 1042 (V161). The Gate holds the ruin.
- **Fellow vassals:**
  - **Cốc Vũ Quán** (Dương Hảo Vũ): friends. The Gate guides the abbey's pilgrims and its rain-callers on the road to
    Thu Trì, for no fee.
  - **Vũ Linh Đường** (Ân Thanh Linh): allies on the road. The bell lines hang over the Gate's crossings, and the Gate's
    lamps and Vũ Linh's bells keep the same forest.
  - **Hồng Đậu Trang** (Nghê Hồng Đậu): the Gate will not guide anyone to the gracious door after dark. Its guides know
    who scatters the red beans on the path (A279), and step around them.
  - **Thu Phần Các** (Lý Hận Bích): the Gate does not guide to the graves of Lôi Trạch in autumn, and says why only to
    travellers who ask twice.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The staff and the cape.** Nhậm Bình Sinh left the founder's staff to Úc and the founder's cape to Phí. Úc is head.
  Phí holds the road, the books and most of the guides' loyalty. Úc wants the Gate out from under the Tower, by the
  isle, the mines or the Pivot. Phí wants the road open and the Gate whole, and would pay the Tower to keep it so.
- **The unanswered letter.** The Rain Tower has offered Phí the headship if the Gate sets Úc aside, and the hostage's
  return with it. She has not answered. If Úc learns of the letter from anyone else, the Gate splits.
- **The door in the mine.** Úc uses the Tower's drill dates to search the mines while its Thunder disciples are away on
  Liệt Khuyết Phong. He has heard drumming under Tử Tinh Khoáng. He wants what lies behind it for his own untaught
  Thunder, and he has told no one, not even his disciple.
- **The deserter's lamp.** Nghiêm Quy Khứ's life lamp still burns in the Tower's vaults. Anyone who holds it can feel
  when he is hurt, and the vault law allows it to be put out from afar with the master lamp and the shears together.
- **The courier.** Kỳ Khinh Mã has carried letters to the hostage every season for twenty-five years and loves him. She
  teaches his daughter, who wants a war, and holds the girl back from it.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The drumming under Tử Tinh.** Úc Diệp Thanh times his searches of the mines to the Tower's storm drills, from dates
   Khúc Thanh Điện passes her father. The next time the Thunder disciples go up Liệt Khuyết Phong, he means to open the
   door. If Hầu Vọng Xuân's drumstick leads him to the same shaft, the Gate and the Tower meet underground. (Úc Diệp
   Thanh, Khúc Phụ Thạch, Khúc Thanh Điện, Lỗ Kim Tuyền, Hầu Vọng Xuân; places 863, 873, 187; the sealed realm is not
   pinned here.)
2. **The unanswered letter.** Hứa Thanh Diêm writes to the Road Mother a second time, and this time the courier is
   caught. Phí must answer the Tower, or answer Úc. (Phí Thoa Y, Hứa Thanh Diêm, Úc Diệp Thanh, Úc Đả Diệp; places 885,
   28, 7.)
3. **The wick.** Lệ Tiêu Hồn asks again for the deserter's lamp to be put out, and this time he brings the master lamp
   to the shears. Nghiêm Tố Tố hears of it in the vaults and runs down the road to warn her brother. A guide never runs.
   (Nghiêm Quy Khứ, Nghiêm Tố Tố, Ninh Tịch Đăng, Lệ Tiêu Hồn, Phí Thoa Y; places 208, 28.)
4. **The staff on the isle.** The Tower's envoy comes to Hà Châu a third time, with the Tower's people behind her. Đinh
   Vãn Độ will not pull the staff out. A duel on the mud, a bribe, or a judgement from the Pivot. (Đinh Vãn Độ, Hứa Thanh
   Diêm, Úc Diệp Thanh, Mẫn Thanh Trượng; place 160.)
5. **Letters every season.** Kỳ Khinh Mã's letters to the hostage are read by the Hall of Echoes. One of them mentions
   the mine. (Kỳ Khinh Mã, Úc Đả Diệp, Nhiếp Vô Thanh, Úc Yên Vũ; places 874, 28.)
6. **The lamp girl.** A Night Rain caravan meets Cù Tiểu Thoa on the forest road at night and orders her to put out her
   lamp. She refuses. The caravan's guard is a ma đạo master who came under the Edict. (Cù Tiểu Thoa, Kỳ Khinh Mã, Kiều
   Mãn Lâu; places 885, 42.)
7. **What happened at the pass.** Phương Đoạn Vũ was one of the four who came out of Đan Sơn Mạch. He knows how Úc Từ
   Hành died and who gave the order to hold. Úc Yên Vũ wants to ask him with a staff. (Úc Yên Vũ, Úc Diệp Thanh,
   Phương Đoạn Vũ, Mạnh Dạ Vũ; places 211, 874.)
8. **The staff and the cape.** One day the Gate will need a single head with both founder's pieces. A duel, a vote of
   the guides at the Staff Road, or a death decides it. (Úc Diệp Thanh, Phí Thoa Y, Kỳ Khinh Mã, Úc Yên Vũ; place 28.)
