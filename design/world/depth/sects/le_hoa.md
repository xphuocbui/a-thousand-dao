A hermitage of women on the highest mountain of the continent. It has two kinds of sister. The **white nuns** carry Ice
and live on the glacier. The **grey sisters** carry Metal, have no Ice, and hold the gate below. The world knows Lê Hoa
Am for two things: it turns men away, and its bell rings for the dead of Hồ Thiên. Only the abbess knows what the gate
is really for.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4): move the seat 6 tiles up onto Lăng Tuyệt Phong (184) and re-lean Ice, Water → Ice, Metal.** Place 15
is high mountain (Metal 42 %, Earth 27 %, Water 13 %, no Ice), so it fails an Ice sect. The fitting Ice land of Ngọc Hồ
Sơn (214, 239, the glaciers 1362/1392/1393) lies 110-140 tiles to the north. It is the Pavilion's ice, and moving there
would cut the nuns off from the cave they guard, from Hòe Âm and from the Tiêu clan. The one glacier in the southern
massif is Lăng Tuyệt Phong (5.2 km, the highest peak on the continent: Ice 10, Light 40, Metal 44 %, Water 13 %). It
fits Ice, Metal but not Water, so the hermitage re-leans its second element to the mountain's.

**The story.** The founder Kỷ Hoa Ảnh opened the hermitage at the foot of the mountain in 600 (A103). In 702, after she
sealed her master's cave and cast the bell from frozen lightning (A105), she raised the Upper Hermitage on the glacier
of Lăng Tuyệt Phong, where Ice is in the air. Băng Tâm's climbers had stood on that summit in 480 (A101) and left it
empty. The house at the foot became the gate. Every event the chronicle places at place 15 (A103, A109, A110) happened
at the gate, which is where outsiders meet the hermitage.

**What the move costs (to do when approved).**
- Chronicle: A103's "by the ice fields" stays true (the glacier is 6 tiles up). Add **N-le_hoa-01** (702, the Upper
  Hermitage). The seat record moves to 184; place 15 becomes the Gate Hermitage.
- Art: a small hermitage painting on the glacier at 184; place 15 keeps its painting as the gate.
- Routes and province: a stair route 15 → 184 of some 150 km, about ten days' climb; the province seat record follows.
- Density: 184 is 1.23 against 15's 1.63. **Founder's array** (amended 2026-11-25, ruling group 2: every moved sect
  gets one): the **Pear-Frost Ring (Lê Sương Hoàn)**, `seat_array_density` **+0.40** (proposed; World Generation rule
  16a, Formula G: 1.63 − 1.23), so the Upper Hermitage reads 1.63. In 702 Kỷ Hoa Ảnh hung the frozen-lightning bell
  (A105) at the ring's heart and planted the ice pears round it when she raised the Upper Hermitage (N-le_hoa-01); the
  nuns still call it a wall against the wind, not a gift of qi. Silencing the bell (a hook) drops the seat to 1.23.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The southern massif is Metal country (40-49 % Metal, 10-14 % Water). Its girls
are born Metal-rooted far more often than Water-rooted, and an Ice root needs Water, so the white robe is rare. The Cold
Jade Mine and the summit (Ice 10) make an Ice transmutation about twice as likely for a girl who has Water. The forest
villages of the valley (Hòe Âm, Hàn Khê Thôn, Hoàng Oanh Thôn: Water 25-42 %) send the Water girls. That is the
hermitage's two peoples: **grey sisters** of the mines and **white nuns** of the valley water. The summit's Light (40)
has woken a Light root in one novice, Diệp Quang Nhi, for the first time in six hundred years.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **Snow covers all and judges none.** The founder believed snow is heaven's mercy for the dead. The nuns pray for
  everyone buried under ice: Hồ Thiên's dead of 425 (C090), the garrison of Đoạn Tuyết Quan (V066), every miner lost in
  a shaft. Giản Tố Y keeps the book of names.
- **The bell.** The Bell of the Frozen Bolt is rung at every solstice for Hồ Thiên's dead. In the winter of 1199 it was
  rung every night, because the nuns believe their prayer keeps the Torch Dragon asleep (A098, A111).
- **The vow at the gate.** "Let no one who seeks the sword pass this gate." Since 790 it has been kept as *no men*,
  because every man who came up the mountain asked about the sword. Most nuns believe the rule is about men.
- **Two robes.** White nuns shave their heads and wear undyed white with a pear-wood bead. Grey sisters keep their hair,
  wear grey wool and carry swords. Grey sisters may marry and leave. White nuns may not.
- **Food and dead.** The nuns eat no meat and drink snow-melt tea. Their dead are laid in the glacier facing north,
  toward Chúc Long Sơn, so they can keep watch.
- **Taboos:** speaking the name of the founder's master; climbing above the hermitage without the abbess; ringing the
  bell in anger.
- **Outsiders** are fed at the gate and sent down. Women may stay three days. The miners of the valley are treated
  for nothing, and the hermitage is loved there.

**Why it fits:** the Winter Toll Canon is a monastery's bell rite that counts the cold into a foe. The hermitage rings
its bell the same way, and the nuns hold the canon to be theirs. The Pavilion's inner gate teaches a copy (proposal:
given in 702 when the Pavilion let the nuns take the summit; confirmed in the cross-sect pass, Băng Tâm's page names no other source). The Metal outer method fits
the mountain's girls, who will mostly never wear white. The Sealed Cold Sutra is taught to two people at a time.

## 6. Admission

- **Grey gate (outer):** Metal 30+, or Ice at all, and a woman. Girls come from the mines and villages of the massif.
- **White robe (inner and above):** Ice. A grey sister in whom Ice wakes on the glacier is given the white robe that
  night. Water girls without Ice and without Metal are sent on to Băng Tâm's Frost Test with a letter.
- **Exceptions:** none named. Diệp Quang Nhi, a Light root, sits in the grey gate on her Metal (R2 holds) and is
  taught nothing that fits her.
- **Men:** none, since 790. See section 9 for why.

## 8. Relations

- **Băng Tâm Các (lord):** a sister in spirit, and a loose rein (loyalty 65). The Pavilion leaves the hermitage alone
  and takes its Water girls. Thái Lê Tuyết and the Pavilion's first elder Tiết Hàn Mai were born in Hòe Âm the same
  spring of 760 and still write each other at the solstice. Each is near the end of a Kết Đan life.
- **Tầm Kiếm Đường:** the men she turned away. Its founder Tiêu Vấn Phong hates the gate and searches for what is
  behind the seal. The grey sisters and the Tầm Kiếm hunters meet on the mountain paths, and most of them get on;
  Hướng Thiết Lan and Tịch Lang Tung more than get on.
- **The Tiêu clan of Hòe Âm:** sends its daughters here since 1151 (Tiêu Ngọc Hòe) and its sons to Tầm Kiếm or the
  Pavilion (Tiêu Bạch Lộ).
- **The miners of the massif:** the hermitage's people. The Cold Jade and Silver Thread mines send most of its girls.
- **Hoắc Hàn Quang** (dead, C014): the founder's master, whose name is not spoken on the mountain.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The case of the mountain.** Last spring Tầm Kiếm Đường asked the lord for the right to cross above the Gate
  Hermitage. Băng Tâm's Hall of Cold Law took the suit, and its master Thẩm Hàn Giang is riding five months south to
  hear it at Hòe Âm Trấn in the autumn. A ruling for the lodge would put the massif's men behind him at the seat.
  Khương Lưu Ly's party backs the nuns. The Hall will put one question to the abbess under oath: what does the
  founder's vow bar?
- **Two answers.** Thái Lê Tuyết knows the vow bars the sword, not men. She has sat against the seal, and the Tiêu boy
  she sent down in 1150 had asked her first where the cold light was. If she swears to that, she wins, and she names
  in open court the master whose name the mountain does not speak, and the sword he left above her gate. If she swears
  the gate is for women, she lies to her lord, and Thẩm can rule that a women's rule does not close a road. If she
  loses, carters and hunters walk up past a sword that kills whoever draws it. She has written both answers and
  signed neither.
- **The prioress's pages.** Mạnh Tĩnh Chi wants the seal opened: the sword has spoken to her in a man's voice every
  winter since 1152. Tiêu Ngọc Khê carries the grey sisters' letters down to the Tầm Kiếm lodge, two months on the
  mine road, and Mạnh opens the bag at the gate first. She adds an unsigned page to each letter for Tiêu Hòe Sinh, the
  lodge's witness: the old stair above the gate, saddle by saddle, as she climbed it in 1152. She knows what waits at
  the top.
- **The walk of 1202.** Every fifty years the abbess and one nun sit against the seal for a season, and the next walk
  is two years off. Thái is 440, and she would take Ứng Lê Âm. Since the suit came she has been teaching Ứng the Sealed
  Cold Sutra after the evening bell, so that someone can sit against the seal if the court goes wrong. Mạnh walked
  in 1152 and means to walk again.
- **The grey and the white.** Grey sisters do the hermitage's work and seldom rise, and they follow Hướng Thiết Lan,
  who follows Mạnh. The Hall has summoned Hướng to swear that no man has passed the gate in her thirty years on it.
  The lodge's chief guide, Tịch Lang Tung, refused to swear against her gate, so she will face the head's nephew.
- **The Light root.** Diệp Quang Nhi carries a Light the hermitage cannot teach. Some nuns say the summit chose her.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The oath at Hòe Âm.** The Hall of Cold Law sits at Hòe Âm Trấn in the autumn, and the abbess must answer under
   oath what the vow bars. The player can ride down with her, carry one of her two answers to the bench, or tell Khương
   Lưu Ly's people what Thẩm means to rule. (Thái, Thẩm Hàn Giang, Khương Lưu Ly, Tiêu Vấn Phong; places 15, 66.)
2. **The voice in the seal.** Mạnh Tĩnh Chi's dreams grow louder after the mountain rumbles. A player who carries the
   grey sisters' letter bag down the mine road finds a page in it about a stair that no grey sister has climbed.
   (Mạnh, Tiêu Ngọc Khê, Tiêu Hòe Sinh.)
3. **The truth of 1150.** Tiêu Vấn Phong learns why he was really turned away, from Thái's oath, from Tiêu Ngọc Hòe, or
   from the player. Does he stop searching, or search harder? (Thái, Tiêu Vấn Phong, Tiêu Ngọc Hòe; place 406.)
4. **The cold light.** If the Hall rules for the lodge, Tầm Kiếm's first party goes up the stair to the cave Tiêu Kiến
   Quang ran from in 930 (A108). The grey sisters are sent to stop them, and Hướng Thiết Lan meets Tịch Lang Tung on
   the mountain with a sword in her hand. (Hướng, Tịch, Tiêu; place 15 and above.)
5. **The sword's price.** Whoever draws Hoắc Hàn Quang's sword wakes the tribulation that killed him (proposal: the
   frozen lightning of C039 is the same storm). The bell is the only thing that has ever answered it. (Ứng Lê Âm, the
   bell.)
6. **The walk of 1202.** Thái must choose who climbs with her to the seal: Ứng Lê Âm, who is learning the Sutra, or Mạnh
   Tĩnh Chi, who wants the seal opened. If she dies first, the choice falls to whoever holds the Sealed Cold Sutra.
   (Thái, Ứng, Mạnh; the cave above place 15.)
7. **The Light root.** Diệp Quang Nhi needs a Light method no nun has. The player can find her one, or a Light sect can
   take her away. (Diệp, Ứng.)
8. **The dragon wakes.** If Chúc Long Sơn erupts, the nuns go north to pray over the new dead, and Thái Lê Tuyết may be
   the first outsider to learn what Cố Lăng Sương has watched for twenty years. (Thái, Cố Lăng Sương; place 188.)
