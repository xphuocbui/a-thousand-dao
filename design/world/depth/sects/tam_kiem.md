A timber lodge at the forest's edge under the southern massif, twelve days' walk from Hòe Âm (about 180 km). It has a wall of maps
inside, a notch-filed sword at the head's hip, and a rule that any man may come in. Its people hunt, guide caravans and
Pavilion envoys over the passes, and search every cave on the mountain. It was founded by a boy the hermitage above
turned away, and it exists to find what the hermitage is hiding.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R3/R4): stay, and become no-lean.** The seat is mixed forest (Water 31 %, Wood 27 %, Metal 16 %, Earth 16 %),
and it fails a Metal-first lean. The audit offers mountain mines and forts with Metal 35-41 %. But Tầm Kiếm's men are
born in Hòe Âm and the valley villages (Water 25-42 %, Wood 24-34 %, Metal 9-16 %), and a Metal lineage would turn away
the very men the hall was founded to take. The hall's sword never ran on an element; it runs on understanding
(ngộ tính). The rules now say so. Nothing moves.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** Valley-born men are mostly Water-Wood, with Metal among the miners' sons. In a
no-lean hall their roots matter only to their own Đạo Đồ start; the hall's methods do not care. Recruits are chosen by
**understanding** (ngộ tính) and stubbornness, both of which the founder has in excess.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **Ask.** The hall's one teaching is in its founder's name: ask after the blade. A question is never wrong. A man who
  stops asking is asked to leave.
- **Any man may come in.** The door has no bar. The hall takes the sons the hermitage turns away, the Pavilion's
  Frost-Test failures, miners' boys, deserters. It takes no women, because the hermitage is already for them, and the
  joke is not lost on anyone.
- **The notch.** Each searched cave is a notch on the searcher's sword guard and a mark on the map wall. The head has
  412.
- **Guiding is honest work.** The hall pays its way guiding caravans and envoys over the passes, and it is the best at
  it on the massif. Taking money to show a stranger the caves is forbidden; Hách Tùng does it anyway.
- **Dress:** hunters' leathers, a sword, rope, a map case. The dead are buried in the cave they were searching when they
  died, if it is empty.
- **The hermitage.** Grey sisters met on the paths are greeted politely and given right of way. The hall's quarrel is
  with the gate, not the women.

## 6. Admission (R3)

- **Any root, men only.** The hall recruits by understanding (ngộ tính): the head asks a candidate one question about a
  track in the snow and takes him if the answer is a better question.
- **Inner gate:** the Primer's sixth layer and one season's guiding without losing a client.
- **Exceptions:** Sài Mộ Kiếm, guest elder (khách khanh), Metal-Water, hired in 1165. Thang Phục Tuyết carries the
  Pavilion's Clear Frost Breathing Primer from his Frost Test years, which is allowed: the hall teaches over any root.

## 8. Relations

- **Băng Tâm Các (lord):** the hall guides the Pavilion's envoys and pays its tax in furs (loyalty 55). The Pavilion
  finds the hall's purpose faintly comic and leaves it alone. It holds Phi Tuyết under its own seat, which the hall does
  not know.
- **Lê Hoa Am:** the gate that made the hall. Tiêu Vấn Phong's feud with the abbess Thái Lê Tuyết and the prioress Mạnh
  Tĩnh Chi is fifty years old. The paths are friendlier: Tịch Lang Tung brings mushrooms to the grey sister Hướng Thiết Lan
  every spring, and the abbess's own grand-nephew Thái Trường Thanh lives in the lodge.
- **The Tiêu clan of Hòe Âm:** the founder's clan. It sends its sons here or to the Pavilion and its daughters to the
  hermitage (Tiêu Ngọc Hòe, Tiêu Bạch Lộ).
- **Tư Không Hạc Niên** (loose Nguyên Anh at Tống Khách Thị): the man who beat the hall's sword teacher. Sài wants a
  second duel.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The suit.** Last spring Tiêu Vấn Phong asked the lord, Băng Tâm, for the hall's right to cross above Lê Hoa Am's
  gate, and Thẩm Hàn Giang's Hall of Cold Law will hear it at Hòe Âm Trấn in the autumn. Tiêu stopped believing in the
  cave twenty years ago. He filed because the abbess will have to answer him under oath, and part of him wants to
  lose: a refusal sworn before the lord would let him close the map wall.
- **The cave is real.** It is Hoắc Hàn Quang's sealed abode (slot A-1), above the hermitage's gate, and the sword inside
  carries a failed tribulation (le_hoa hook 5). If the hall finds it, the hall may be the first thing it kills.
- **The witness.** Tịch Lang Tung would not swear against the hermitage's gate, so the hall's witness is the head's
  nephew Tiêu Hòe Sinh, who wants the passes, not the cave. He will swear that the old stair above the gate crosses the
  high saddle and cuts a fortnight off the salt road from Hòe Âm. He has already sold next summer's first crossing to
  the salt carters and spent the deposit, and he means to charge the grey sisters the carters' toll on their own path.
  The stair reaches him as unsigned pages in his cousin's letters from the gate.
- **The map-seller.** Hách Tùng sells copies of the map wall in Hòe Âm. Since the suit was posted they sell for twice
  the price, with the hermitage's ground left blank, and he lets buyers think the blank is where the sword lies.
- **The teacher's duel.** Sài Mộ Kiếm will leave the hall the day he hears Tư Không Hạc Niên will fight him again.

## 10. Treasures and sets

- **The Asking Blade (Vấn Phong)**, the founder's unnamed hunter's sword with 412 notches.
- **Evening Sword (Mộ Kiếm)**, the sword Sài Mộ Kiếm lost with in 1130 and was handed back.

- **The Map Wall (Đồ Bích)**, Tiêu Vấn Phong: the Asking Blade, Hunter's Map Coat. Full set: each mark adds crit

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The 413th cave.** Lữ Hồi Lộ finds a cold silence near the top of Hồi Lộ Phong's stair. The head listens at it and
   cannot tell if it is the seal. (Tiêu Vấn Phong, Lữ Hồi Lộ; place 183.)
2. **The letter.** Before the hearing the abbess writes to Tiêu Vấn Phong at last. Thái Trường Thanh carries it, and
   Mạnh Tĩnh Chi tries to stop it. (Tiêu, Thái Lê Tuyết, Thái Trường Thanh, Mạnh.)
3. **The map in a stranger's hands.** A map Hách Tùng sold turns up with Triệu Huyết Y, who hunts old masters' abodes.
   (Hách Tùng, Tiêu, Triệu Huyết Y.)
4. **The mushroom basket.** If the Hall rules for the lodge, Hướng Thiết Lan is ordered to bar the paths above the gate,
   and Tịch Lang Tung must lead the first party up them. (Tịch, Hướng, Ngưu Phá Sơn.)
5. **The second duel.** Tư Không Hạc Niên passes through Hòe Âm. Sài Mộ Kiếm leaves the sword yard to challenge him, and
   the hall follows to watch. (Sài, Tư Không Hạc Niên; places 66, 48.)
6. **The boy at the gate.** A new Tiêu son is sent up the mountain to the hermitage, as Tiêu Vấn Phong was. The player is
   asked to take him, by the clan, by the hall, and by the abbess. (Tiêu Hòe Sinh, Tiêu Ngọc Hòe, Thái Lê Tuyết.)
7. **The Pavilion's sword.** The hall learns that Băng Tâm keeps a legendary ice sword, Phi Tuyết, under its own seat. Is
   that the cold light? It is not, and finding that out costs something. (Tiêu, Thang Phục Tuyết; place 214.)
8. **The hearing at Hòe Âm.** The Hall of Cold Law sits in the autumn. The player can sit the bench for the lodge, carry
   the salt carters' complaint when the deposit is gone, or show the founder whose hand wrote his nephew's stair.
   (Tiêu Vấn Phong, Tiêu Hòe Sinh, Thẩm Hàn Giang, Thái Lê Tuyết; place 66.)
