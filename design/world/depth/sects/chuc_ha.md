Fire-silk was reeled at the vents of Hỏa Ti until the palace burned the town in 590, and the palace's founder swore no
silk would be reeled there again (N-phuong_minh-04). The reelers' grandchildren in Tang Điền never stopped wanting it
back. In 1150 one of them found a way to put fire into silk with her own hands, and the pavilion was founded to do it.
Its silk keeps the letter of the vow, chars in a season, and is bought cheap by a palace that "has not yet decided if
that pleases it" (V172). The palace's own array keeper secretly depends on it.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4): stay at place 1013 and re-order Fire, Wood → Wood, Fire.** The seat is grassland (Water 27.5 %, Wood
25.05 %, Earth 19 %, Fire 16.7 %, Metal 11.7 %, density 0.90). As Fire, Wood it fails. As Wood, Fire it passes **by a
hair**: Wood is 0.05 over the 25 % bar and Fire 1.7 over 15 %. The only places in the pavilion's ground where Fire leads
are **Hỏa Ti Trấn (449)**, which is the palace's seat, and **Chúc Dung Khẩu (253)**, the Nest's vent. Those are the
vents the founder's vow closed to silk. The pavilion was founded on the premise that it would never go there. The
re-order says what its craft has always been: silk is Wood (mulberry, worm, thread), and the fire is put in by hand.

**What it costs.** Nothing on the map. The seat is off-road; a track to Hồ Tâm Thôn (163, 12 tiles, on the road) would
carry the silk. Chronicle: A300 and V172 stay true. Add N-chuc_ha-01 to -03.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The bamboo-house village beside the pavilion (Trúc Lâu 29 % Wood) and the
green-cane vein (Thanh Can Mạch, 36 % Wood) give Wood roots a little more often than even land. The silk towns it
recruits from (Tang Điền 21 % Fire, Tang Âm 20 %) are even, and a Fire root strong enough to set fire into thread is
ordinary luck there. The lake villages (Hồ Tâm 33 % Water) give Water girls who come for the looms and stay at the outer
gate. The pavilion's best fire-setter, Đậu Hồng Ti, is a Tang Âm girl.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The thread does not break.** The reelers' saying, and the pavilion's teaching: a life, like a cocoon's filament, is
  one thread, and a weaver's work is to draw it out whole. Breaking a thread on purpose is the worst thing a weaver can
  do. Breaking a promise is the same thing.
- **The vow kept.** The pavilion keeps the palace founder's vow more strictly than the palace does. No weaver goes to
  the vents, and the pavilion's fire comes from its own people.
- **Silk that chars.** Fire-silk woven by hand holds its heat one season and then chars. Every robe and banner must be
  rewoven, a thread at a time. The pavilion sees that as honest work, and its customers see it as an expense.
- **Dress.** Plain undyed silk at the looms, burn-pale fingertips, a shuttle through the hair for those who set fire.
- **Food.** Rice, river fish, and the silkworm pupae from the boiling pans, fried, which outsiders refuse.
- **The dead** are wrapped in the last cloth they wove and buried under the mulberry rows.
- **Taboos.** Going to the vents; cutting a thread on the loom in anger; selling a bolt as vent-silk.
- **Outsiders.** Merchants are welcome and watched. The palace's factor is served tea and no food.

**Why it fits.** The Wood method is the reelers' own, carried out of Hỏa Ti in 590. It never held fire, because the fire
was always the vent's. The Fire secret is Thân's, made from her mother's burned notes, and it puts the weaver's Fire
where the vent's used to be. Non-lineage members (R5): the guest dyer Nhiễm Thanh Lam (Water, stand-in) and the servant
Ngưu Thải Diệp (Earth, stand-in).

## 6. Admission

- **Outer gate:** Wood or Fire at 30 or more, and good hands. Children come from the silk towns, the bamboo village and
  the lake.
- **Inner gate and above:** Wood 45 or more. To set fire, Fire as well. Only the head and Đậu Hồng Ti set fire in
  the best bolts.
- **Exceptions:** the guest elder (khách khanh) Nhiễm Thanh Lam, hired dyer, Kết Đan, a realm above the head. She is
  never taught the lineage. Servants (tạp dịch) pick the leaves.

## 8. Relations

- **Phượng Minh Cung (lord):** loyal, poor and unsure of its welcome. The treasurer Vưu Cẩm Thư (`vuu_cam_thu`) buys
  its silk at a price that hurts, and her factor Chiêm Lợi Châu (`chiem_loi_chau`) beats it down at Tang Điền. The
  array keeper Tang Kế Ti (`tang_ke_ti`) splices its silk into the Nest Array in secret and owes for it. Her son Tang Tục
  Lạc (`tang_tuc_lac`) rides to the pavilion every month for the silk and for Thân. Tang Mộng Kiển is Tang Kế Ti's
  cousin.
- **Phần Vũ Đường:** sells it charcoal for the dye fires. The pavilion envies its vent and would never use one.
- **Lam Điện Thôn:** the dyers' village, the grandchildren of drowned Kim Sa (V171), and home of the guest elder.
- **Xích Thành Môn** (fallen) and its banner: the Xích Hà Kỳ was woven from vent fire-silk in 260 (C068). Thân wants to
  weave its equal by hand.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The strand.** Thân Vân Cẩm holds one strand of new fire-silk reeled at the vent. Tang Kế Ti reeled it, breaking the
  founder's vow, and it is better than anything the pavilion weaves. Thân could ruin the woman who owes her, or tell
  the palace that her own silk can keep the Nest alive if it is paid for. She loves the woman's son.
- **The cousin.** Tang Mộng Kiển would be the first to know vent silk by touch. Her reeler Lương Kiển Sinh would be the
  second. Neither has been shown the strand.
- **The price.** The pavilion is poor because the palace sets its price. Thành Kim Toa has begun selling bolts to
  caravans going north, against the pavilion's oath to its lord.
- **The banner.** Mâu Tú Linh is drafting the pattern of the banner Thân wants to weave, and Đậu Hồng Ti wants to try a
  vent to make it last.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The Nest goes cold.** A strand of the Nest Array fails (`phuong_minh.md` hook 5). Thân Vân Cẩm can offer the
   pavilion's silk openly, if the palace pays for it, or reveal the vent strand. (Thân, Tang Kế Ti, Tang Tục Lạc,
   Vưu Cẩm Thư; places 1013, 449, 253.)
2. **The cousin's touch.** Tang Mộng Kiển handles a bolt from the Fire Court and knows vent fire in it. (Tang Mộng Kiển,
   Lương Kiển Sinh, Tang Kế Ti; places 1013, 449.)
3. **Bolts going north.** The palace's factor catches Thành Kim Toa selling to a northern caravan. The pavilion must
   choose between its factor and its lord. (Thành, Chiêm Lợi Châu, Thân; place 94.)
4. **The banner.** Thân begins the banner to equal the Xích Hà Kỳ. It needs a season of fire no weaver has. Đậu Hồng Ti
   goes to the vent at night. (Thân, Đậu, Mâu Tú Linh; places 1013, 253.)
5. **A marriage.** Tang Tục Lạc asks Thân to marry him. The palace's array keeper's son and the head of the vassal she owes
   cannot marry quietly. (Thân, Tang Tục Lạc, Tang Kế Ti; places 1013, 449.)
6. **Indigo that holds.** Nhiễm Thanh Lam finds that her dye holds fire-silk's heat longer. She asks a price the
   pavilion cannot pay. (Nhiễm, Thân; places 1013, 1020.)
