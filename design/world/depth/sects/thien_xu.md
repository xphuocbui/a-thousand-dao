The Pivot does not take land. It weighs. It has beaten every great sect that tried to take the heart of the continent
and kept none of their ground, and since 510 it has enforced the North Star Compact: land changes hands only by a
fall, a gift, an oath, or a war the Pivot licenses by writ (C107). That makes it the political centre of the world,
and it is run like one. Its elders are judges, archivists, envoys and keepers of scales, and its war hall drafts writs
more often than it fights. Its people are miners and herders from the three lands it has held for nine centuries.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4): stay.** The seat passes the fit test. Its qi is Earth 33 % and Metal 29 %, the first lean, Metal, clears
the 25 % bar, Earth is the seat's top element, and its density of 2.11 is the highest in the Pivot's ground. Nothing moves, so there is no
art, route, province or chronicle cost. The founder chose the peak under the Northern Dipper (C002), and the ground
grew rich as the sect gathered: the Bắc Thần Phúc Địa.

**The seat and its peaks.** The halls stand on the Thiên Xu peak (5). Around it are the founder's tomb on Thái Nhất
Phong (632), Xu Trụ Phong (1481), where the Dipper Handle Canon is cut into the rock in seven columns, and Thạch
Quyền Phong (385), the outer gate and its great scale. The Pivot is spread across its land more than any other sect.
The judges sit at the Bridge Court in Thạch Kiều Thành (81), where the Compact was signed. The scales are kept at
Thiên Quyền Thị (52) and Song Kiều (82), the vein watch on Tinh Tủy Mạch (205), the barrow watch at Lang Yên Trại
(582) on the edge of the poisoned plain, and the harbour at Tinh Hán Thành (40).

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** The Pivot holds three lands, and each breeds its own root.

- **Thiên Tuyền Nguyên** (region 14) is a plateau of steppe and highland. Its herding villages are almost all Earth:
  Mao Ngưu Thôn (588) is Earth 62 and Metal 13, and Thanh Khoa (567) and Tô Du (592) are near 50 Earth. Under R1 a
  child born there is up to three times likelier to be Earth-rooted and about a third less likely to be Metal-rooted.
  These children fill the outer gate.
- **Ngọc Hành Sơn** (region 15) is the ore mountains. At the mines Metal runs from 43 to 58: Huyền Thiết (174) is 54,
  Bạch Ngân (176) 52, Thiên Ngoại (173) 57, and Thiết Châm Phong (1484) 58. Miners' children are Metal-rooted at more
  than twice the even rate. They walk through the Level Beam and fill the halls.
- **Dao Quang Lĩnh** (region 6) is a northern forest of Wood 40-56, Họa Tinh Đường's land, where few children pass
  the Pivot's gate. Its high peaks are the exception: Trích Tinh Phong (182), Metal 47 with Light 40, and the snow-jade
  diggings (1311) give the vein guard its rare Light roots (Hình Thủ Tinh, Thịnh Hàm Quang).
- **The cities** are mixed. Thạch Kiều is Earth 37 and Fire 24, Tinh Hán is Water 39, and Khai Dương is Wood 35 and
  Water 34. City children come in with odd roots and a Metal just good enough. The judges and envoys mostly come from
  the cities.

The sect's saying is that Earth carries you up the mountain and Metal lets you in. The plateau fills the outer gate
and the mines fill the halls. The Pivot weighs everyone the same, and it does not notice that the scale was set by the
ground.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **The Pivot does not move.** The founder's teaching, and the method's: the fixed star everything turns on. In law it
  means the Pivot takes no land, ever. After the War of the Pivot the founder said that a Pivot that takes land is only
  a larger sect (C106). It also means the Pivot cannot lose land, and the 1188 ruling turned on that.
- **The one who seals does not weigh.** The head keeps the Bắc Thần Ấn; the Chief Judge keeps the Weight. A case under
  the Compact is weighed at the Bridge Court and sealed at the seat, by two people. The rule is custom, not text. In
  1188 the head broke it, and the sect has not yet said aloud what it thinks.
- **Open scales.** Everything is weighed in public: ore at Song Kiều (A219), pills at Thiên Quyền Thị (V117), claims at
  the Bridge Court, where anyone may sit on the steps and listen. Each year on the day of the Compact the text is read
  aloud at Thạch Kiều by the youngest judge.
- **No music.** Since the War of the Nine Shao (C005) no song or sound art is taught and no instrument is kept at the
  seat. Time is kept by water clock and a single bell struck once at dusk. At a Pivot funeral, guests may sing, as the
  mourners of Cửu Thiều were let sing (A236), and the sect's own people stand silent.
- **Grey and the seven stars.** Disciples wear undyed grey, the colour of the bridge stone. Inner disciples wear a
  black cord with seven knots, and elders a small silver star at the collar for their hall's star. The head wears the
  stone seal on a cord at his chest.
- **Plateau food.** Barley, yak butter tea, mutton in winter. No wine at the seat except Lễ Tuyền Phái's hundred jars
  a year (A263), which are opened only at the Compact-day table.
- **Burial.** The dead are buried on the slope of Thái Nhất Phong below the founder, facing the pole. Soldiers lie
  where they fell. The Vạn Cốt Trủng on Chiết Kích Nguyên is the sect's largest grave, and Quý Trường Kích lies under it
  with his halberd (C160).
- **Envoys carry no iron** (N-thien_xu-06). Every new envoy climbs the ruined stair of Phá Quân Môn (218) once before
  carrying a first letter.
- **The count.** Since 1165 the Pivot counts Thính Vũ's disciples every year (C200). It counts the other sects too,
  more politely.
- **Outsiders.** Courteous, distant and even-handed: every sect's case is heard the same way. By custom the Pivot's
  elders take no Dao partner from a great sect's ruling house.
- **Taboos:** taking land; striking an envoy; altering a weight; a judge hearing a case of their own hall; music.

**Why it fits.** The land gives Earth everywhere and Metal in the mines, and the lineage is built the same way: a
balance method at the gate that either root can carry, a Metal method inside, and a founder's method that needs both
and makes the holder the fixed point. The methods reward standing still, which is how the Pivot fights and how it
rules. The weakness is the same: the Pivot has no fast art, no healer's method and no fire. It buys its pills from Hoa
Cái Đường, its blades from Thối Tinh Môn and its message birds from Cửu Cao Phái's cranes. The Phá Quân spear is the
one art it took by conquest, and the Thiên Quyền Hall has argued against teaching it for three centuries.

## 6. Admission (R2 as the Pivot applies it)

- **Outer gate:** Metal or Earth at purity 30. Each spring the outer gate's master walks the plateau villages with a
  balance and a bag of weights; the mines send their children without being asked.
- **The Level Beam:** at the sixth layer of the primer the outer disciple stands on the great scale of Thạch Quyền
  Phong holding the master's weights. If the beam stays level for an incense stick, and their Metal is 45 or more, the
  inner gate opens the same evening. Earth carries most of the plateau children to the sixth layer. Metal stops most
  of them there. One try a year, at the winter solstice, and no limit on years: Hạ Hầu Thạch has tried
  eleven times in eighty years.
- **Inner gate and above:** Metal 45 or more (the main method's element). A Light or Thunder root that rides on that
  Metal is taken and taught the lineage like any other (Hình Thủ Tinh, Thịnh Hàm Quang, Mặc Kinh Trập).
- **Exceptions kept:** the guest armourer from Thối Tinh Môn and the Pivot's hound (khách khanh), and the seat's
  servants (tạp dịch). Neither guest elder is taught the lineage.
- **No purchase.** A place in the outer gate cannot be bought. Mi Kim Bảo's father tried, and Chử Bình Quân handed the
  silver back.

## 8. Relations

- **Thính Vũ Lâu:** the grudge that matters (−15, from the Broken Halberds and the Cinnabar Vein). Mạnh Dạ Vũ swore at
  Đan Sơn Mạch that the Pivot would answer for it (C190). The Pivot has counted the Tower since 1165, and the count has
  doubled twice. Its envoy Phòng Bách Kế lives there, shown what the Tower wants him to see by Lệ Tiêu Hồn. In 1198 the
  Tower's war elder Cố Dạ Lan offered a thunder-stone forge for the Thunder-rooted girl Mặc Kinh Trập.
- **Lạc Hà Tông:** −10, from Chiết Kích. Its first elder Trầm Tịch Dương pays the junior judge Trọng Khắc Kỷ, and its
  head Tô Vãn Chiếu tells the Pivot's envoy Chiêm Như Ngọc nothing, courteously.
- **Băng Tâm Các:** obedient and cold since 1188. Sở Ngọc Hồ withdrew in silence (C195), and Ngụy Thanh Hàn, who wrote
  the petition, now holds the 803 guard roll Thích Minh Thư gave him. The Chief Judge and Sở Ngọc Hồ have written to
  each other since 1188 without mentioning it.
- **Tùng Đào Cung:** the oldest friendship, which the Cold Ford cracked (C070, C136). Đỗ Tinh Hà and Thôi Khinh Hồng
  hunted Mặc Huyết Sinh together (C024). Bách Vong Ngôn is the envoy to its lands and to its vassal Vạn Hác Viện.
- **Phượng Minh Cung:** the ally of 1154 (Hoa Triêu Dương), the holder of the first writ (C118). The south remembers
  that the Pivot stopped its raiders at Thượng Độ and gave Thính Vũ the Thunder vein (A295, C179).
- **Thúy Vi Tông:** grateful for the Cold Ford writ of 901 (C165), wary of the 672 crack. Its envoy Biện Vãn Tình
  drinks tea with Mâu Tri Viễn at Thạch Kiều.
- **Vassals.** **Thối Tinh Môn** (Cát Lưu Quang) forges the Pivot's blades and lends it a son as armourer. **Phất Y
  Cốc** (Thạch Tàng Danh) supplies swordsmen for hire and Can Tương's ore. **Củng Tinh Quán** (Thẩm Bắc Thần), the
  oldest vassal, reads the Dipper on the Tuyền Cơ Nghi; the Pivot did not listen to its omens in 501 and 789 (C102,
  C156). **Cửu Cao Phái** (Ung Hạc Minh) flies the Pivot's letters and circles the vein, and holds Phá Quân's old
  ground (C163). **Chân Kim Các** (Tưởng Bách Luyện) assays beside the Pivot at Song Kiều and shaves its weights.
  **Hoa Cái Đường** (La Tử Vi) sells it cinnabar and pills. **Họa Tinh Đường** (Kỷ Ngân Chúc) paints the Dipper into
  talismans that will not run in the rain (V059).

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The succession and the Confirmation.** Since 760 the Pivot's head has been confirmed before the seals of the
  seven great sects at Thạch Kiều (N-thien_xu-03). Doãn Ngọc Hành has named no heir, and the seat presumes his
  brother's descendant **Doãn Hi Hòa**, a Trúc Cơ of seventy. The elders want his chief disciple **Công Tôn Duẫn**, a Kết Đan of
  a century's service whom they believe the other sects would confirm. In 1197 Công Tôn asked the envoy **Mâu Tri Viễn** to sound
  them. Mâu holds their answers in a sealed letter and has shown it to no one.
- **The vein and the roll.** The vein guard went down to the plain in 803 and did not come back (N-thien_xu-04); the post stood empty for three years, though no order withdrew it. In 1188 the head sealed the archive's
  roll and refused Băng Tâm's claim himself. **Sử Ngưng Mặc** sealed the shelf and does not remember the duplicate she
  copied in 803. **Thích Minh Thư** found that duplicate in 1195 and gave a copy to Ngụy Thanh Hàn in 1196.
  **Hoàng Phủ Tịch** has noticed. **Phương Trực Ngôn's** unfinished judgement found for Băng Tâm. **Hình Thủ Tinh**
  believes the watch has never been withdrawn, and **Hác Thủ Nhai's** forebear died on the plain in 803.
- **The hawk and the scale.** **Ngũ Trấn Bắc** drafts a writ against Thính Vũ each spring; **Mâu Tri Viễn** buries it;
  the head is silent. **Nhiếp Minh Tiễn** lets Doãn Hi Hòa read the writs, and the heir agrees with the hawk.
- **The Three of the Same Year.** **Đỗ Tinh Hà** sent Quý Trường Kích onto a plain he knew was laid with miasma.
  **Lôi Vô Danh** heard the order and took the banner so no one could send the army again. He hid in abode A-3 on the
  Pivot's own plain until he died in 950. **Ngũ Trấn Bắc** is close to the abode. **Quý Thủ Lũy**, Quý's granddaughter,
  believes Lôi abandoned her family, and she would die for Đỗ.
- **The judge's silver.** **Trọng Khắc Kỷ** rules for Lạc Hà's toll-keepers and takes Trầm Tịch Dương's money. His
  clerk **Từ Tri Tiết** can prove it, and accusing her own master would finish her.
- **The forbidden zither.** **Bách Vong Ngôn** did not forget his message in 1080 (A062): he refused to deliver it. He
  has learned the zither by letter from Hà Thính Tùng, and his letter-carrier **Ngạc Tri Âm** is learning it from him.
  The head knows.
- **The plateau and the mines.** The outer gate is full of Earth-rooted herders' children who will never pass the
  Level Beam. **Chử Bình Quân**, the one who did, with a Metal of exactly forty-six, will not tip it for anyone, not
  even the head's kin **Doãn Thường Hi**.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The second petition.** Ngụy Thanh Hàn brings Băng Tâm's claim again, with the 803 guard roll. The archive knows
   the hand within an hour. The player can be the go-between, the witness, or the one who burns the copy. (Ngụy Thanh
   Hàn, Thích Minh Thư, Hoàng Phủ Tịch, Phương Trực Ngôn, Doãn Ngọc Hành, Sở Ngọc Hồ; places 205, 81.)
2. **The unfinished judgement.** Phương Trực Ngôn wants to read her 1188 judgement in open court. If she does, the
   head's ruling is overturned by his own judge, and the rule of the seal and the weight becomes the Compact's text, or
   the Pivot breaks. (Phương, Doãn Ngọc Hành, Từ Tri Tiết, Sài Thiết Diện; place 81.)
3. **The banner in A-3.** Ngũ Trấn Bắc is close to Lôi Vô Danh's abode on Thiên Tuyền Nguyên. Inside are the banner
   and Lôi's account of the order Đỗ Tinh Hà gave on Chiết Kích. Whoever finds it first decides what the Pivot learns.
   (Ngũ, Đỗ, Quý Thủ Lũy, Đồ Vô Cữu; abode A-3.)
4. **The tally's lost half.** Sử Ngưng Mặc has looked for the archive's half of the Thiết Mã tally for four centuries.
   The other half is in Thiết Mã Cảnh. With both and the banner, someone commands an army. (Sử, Đỗ, Ngũ; Thiết Mã Cảnh,
   seat 5.)
5. **The writ.** The count has doubled twice. Phòng Bách Kế suspects he is shown numbers on purpose. Ngũ drafts the
   writ; Mâu buries it; Doãn Hi Hòa has read it. A war with Thính Vũ comes, or the Pivot finds out it has been counting
   a decoy. (Ngũ, Mâu Tri Viễn, Phòng Bách Kế, Mạnh Dạ Vũ, Lệ Tiêu Hồn, Doãn Hi Hòa; places 7, 211.)
6. **The Confirmation.** Mâu Tri Viễn's sealed letter says which heir the great sects would confirm. Công Tôn Duẫn
   asked for it; Doãn Hi Hòa does not know it exists. (Mâu, Công Tôn, Doãn Hi Hòa, Nhiễm Thừa Nghiệp, Doãn Ngọc Hành;
   place 81.)
7. **The judge's silver.** Từ Tri Tiết can prove Trọng Khắc Kỷ takes Lạc Hà's money. She needs someone to carry it to
   the Chief Judge. (Từ, Trọng, Trầm Tịch Dương, Nguyên Hảo Khách, Phương; place 81.)
8. **The zither.** Ngạc Tri Âm is heard practising. The Pivot's oldest taboo meets its most harmless envoy. (Bách Vong
   Ngôn, Ngạc Tri Âm, Hà Thính Tùng, Doãn Ngọc Hành; seat 5, place 11.)
9. **Kình Nha.** Bộc Trầm Chu is dying and has told a different story every night of where Ô Kình Hải's sabre sank. One
   of them is true, and Thúy Vi's Diêm Hải Tảo has been buying the wine. (Bộc, Sa Định Ba, Diêm Hải Tảo; places 40,
   217.)
10. **The hound and the blood robe.** Đồ Vô Cữu has hunted Triệu Huyết Y since 1090. She is looking for her master's
    abode, and he knows the way there. (Đồ, Triệu Huyết Y, Đỗ; abode A-6.)
11. **The Thunder girl.** Thính Vũ wants Mặc Kinh Trập badly enough to offer a forge for her. The Pivot said no without
    asking her. (Mặc Kinh Trập, Cố Dạ Lan, Đái Hổ Thần, Đồ Vô Cữu, Mâu; places 186, 7.)
12. **The Level Beam and the kink.** The player, plateau-born with a strong Earth root, stands on the beam at Thạch
    Quyền Phong. Later, walking the steles with the Thiên Cơ Hall, they find the stele below Thủ Bi Trại forty paces off
    its line, with Tả Khắc Thạch's book of measurements as proof. (Chử Bình Quân, Hạ Hầu Thạch, Doãn Thường Hi, Tả
    Khắc Thạch, Thiện Thừa Củ; places 385, 669, 692.)
