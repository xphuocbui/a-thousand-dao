The gate is named for a line of the west: the wild geese in formation, startled by the cold (nhạn trận kinh hàn). Its
founders walked out of the grey on Chiết Kích Nguyên in 803 in file, and they swore their disciples would never again
fight in loose order (V096). Lạc Hà hires its files when it needs hard bodies, and Lạc Hà's cities pay it to drill
their farmers. Alone, a disciple of the gate is ordinary. Ten of them in file are not.

## 2. Land and seat

**Decision (R4): stay, and re-lean to none.** The seat sits on steppe at Water 38 %, Earth 24 %, Fire 16 %, Metal 13 %
(density 0.80). It fails Metal, Earth: Metal is under 15 % and not in the top two. No place in the gate's ground fits
either, because Metal runs 9-16 % from the coast to the high veins. A move would cut the gate off from the two cities it
drills for. Re-leaning to Water and Earth would fit the seat, but the gate never taught an element: it teaches a body
that stands and a file that holds. It becomes a **no-lean sect** and admits any root. The minor-sect rule allows a
re-lean, and this is the truest one for it.

**What it costs.** Nothing on the map: the seat, its art and its routes stay. The sect record's `leans` becomes `[]` and
its lineage is element-free (section 5). The province seat record is unchanged.

**How the land shapes its people (R1).** Trường Thiên Nguyên is a grassland plain of Water 27-38 % and Wood 22-30 %, with
a steppe belt of Earth 30-41 % along its high veins (Song Tỉnh, Khoáng Dã, Hắc Thứu Đài). The gate's disciples are farm
and ferry children of the plain, so their roots run Water and Wood, with Earth from the steppe villages. A file of the
gate looks like the plain's census. Its few Earth-and-Metal children come from Lạc Hà's Placement Day. It drills on the
shore of **Lạc Nhạn Hồ** (366), the lake where the geese alight, and its night ground is **Hắc Thứu Đài** (489), the
Black Vulture Terrace on the steppe.

## 4. Doctrine and customs

- **No one fights alone.** A disciple's name is entered in the Roll of Files by the file they stand in. A single fight,
  a duel or a show of personal skill is "loose order", the gate's one sin. The gate's people may not duel. Mục Tân Vũ
  does, at night.
- **Trade the point.** The one at the front takes the wind, and when she tires she falls back and the next takes it.
  This is the method (section 5), and it is also how the gate is run: a file leader steps back from the point after ten
  years so the next can take it. Halls and the headship are the exception, and the gate grumbles about it.
- **The spring levy.** Every spring the gate drills the mortal farmers of Thảo Đường Thành (74) and Nhạn Thanh Thành
  (38) on the lake shore for a month. The cities pay for it, and the gate takes its pick of the boys and girls who stand
  the line. Lạc Hà's toll guards and caravan escorts learned their spacing there.
- **The geese.** Wild geese are not eaten, shot or caged on the gate's ground. Since 1120 they may not be shot in Nhạn
  Thanh Thành either (A171).
- **Dress.** Grey-brown felt, short-skirted for marching, with a grey goose feather in the cap or braid. Lạc Hà's Geese
  Hall took its grey feather pin from the gate.
- **Food.** In file: the file eats from one pot, and no one starts until the last is seated. Millet, mutton, river
  fish, and salt from Diêm Điền (A159).
- **The dead.** Buried in rows below the gate, in the order of their file. Every row has gaps for the dead of 803 who
  were not brought off the plain. The anniversary of Chiết Kích is kept with Lạc Hà's silence, and the gate also stands
  in file at its rows from dusk to dawn.
- **Taboos:** breaking file; dueling; turning one's back on a falling filemate; going onto Chiết Kích Nguyên, which
  Lạc Hà forbids and the gate swears to.
- **Outsiders** are judged by one thing: whether they can stand still in a line. The gate is courteous, plain and
  rather dull to visit.

**Why it fits.** The gate drills mortals, farm children and placed children of every root, so its lineage cultivates
none. The outer gate learns to stand: the mountain-bone art roots the body and turns blows through the bone. The inner
gate learns the file. The canon passes strain down the line the way geese trade the lead, so a file fights as one body
and gets harder the longer it holds (memory: sustain is king). Its **Geese Stance** is the stance Lạc Hà's Cảnh Trường
Thiên carried into the Autumn Water and the Long Sky (`long-sky-one-colour`). The founders' secret is the walk out of
the grey: a file that shares its breath shares its poison. It is the reason the gate exists, and the reason Mục Tân Vũ
wants it.

## 6. Admission (no-lean, R3)

- **By the line, not the root.** Any root may enter. The test is the line: stand in the spring levy's line from dawn
  to dusk without moving. Most of the gate's disciples were levy children who passed it (La Nhất Hàng, 1196).
- **By kin.** The descendants of the 803 file have a place if they want it: Lương Thủ Hàng, and Mục Hàn Nhạn, whose
  grandfather walked in it.
- **Placement Day.** Lạc Hà sends the gate Earth and Metal children who failed its dusk test (Hạng Cô Nhạn, 1199), and
  takes a tenth of their earnings for thirty years. The gate takes them as it takes anyone, by the line.
- **Inner gate:** after three springs in file, when the file says so. **Exceptions:** none needed.

## 8. Relations

- **Lạc Hà Tông (lord, loyalty 55):** the gate was made by Lạc Hà's dead and drilled by Lạc Hà's war elder. Lạc Hà
  hires its files for hard bodies (`lac_ha.md` section 5), and its Geese Hall's drill master Thịnh Kinh Hàn spent ten
  years in its files. Mục Hàn Nhạn calls Cảnh Trường Thiên the gate's second founder. Loyalty is only 55 because the
  gate lost a whole file for Lạc Hà in the Ferry War, and Lạc Hà paid the hire fee and nothing more.
- **Lạc Hà's war party:** Hình Trọng Sơn counts the gate's files as his army, and Đổng Kiên Lũy writes to him. The head
  will not lend a file to a war against the Pivot, and he cannot say why (section 9).
- **Lạc Hà's cities:** Thảo Đường Thành and Nhạn Thanh Thành pay for the spring levy. The gate is liked on the plain.
- **Tùng Đào Cung:** the gate's one grudge, from the bridge of 1174. Ngô Bất Tán will not say its name.
- **Mục Vân Trang** (fellow vassal, horse herders of the same plain, 52 tiles off): the gate drills on foot and buys
  no horses. Khấu Mục Vân's riders and the gate's files have never fought together, and both think the other would lose.
- **Thiên Xu Tông:** the enemy of 803. The gate teaches its disciples that the Pivot's halberds broke the line, which is
  true.

## 9. Inside the sect

- **The man who ran.** In the grey of 803 the founder saw a young Kết Đan of Lạc Hà's field run past his file alone
  and empty-handed. In 806 the same man came to drill the survivors for nothing, and Thạch knew his face. That is why
  the gate swears never to break file. On his deathbed Thạch told Mục Hàn Nhạn it was Cảnh Trường Thiên. Mục has kept
  it for 160 years out of gratitude. If it came out, it would shame the gate's second founder and tell Lạc Hà that
  Thanh Sương was dropped, not lost.
- **Peace against war.** Mục keeps the gate out of Lạc Hà's quarrels. Đổng Kiên Lũy believes a gate that drills for
  war and never goes is lying to its farmers, and he writes to Hình Trọng Sơn. The war party would take the gate's files
  if Đổng were head.
- **The ten.** Mục Tân Vũ, the point of the first file, drills ten disciples at night on Hắc Thứu Đài from the founder's
  stolen notes. She means to walk them into the miasma with Lạc Hà's Hình Lạc Nhạn, breathing in turns as the founders
  did. Đổng knows and approves. Lương Thủ Hàng noticed the notes were gone and covered for her.
- **Succession.** Mục is 300 of a Kết Đan's 500. His daughter is a Trúc Cơ. If he died now, Đổng would be head.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **Ten into the grey.** Mục Tân Vũ's ten and Lạc Hà's Hình Lạc Nhạn walk into the miasma of Chiết Kích Nguyên in
   file, breathing in turns. The player can be the eleventh, warn the head, or warn Cảnh. (Mục Tân Vũ, Hình Lạc Nhạn,
   Lam Tề Vũ, Ninh Bắc Hàng, Mục Hàn Nhạn, Cảnh Trường Thiên; places 489, 223.)
2. **The man who ran.** Inside the grey the ten find the line where the founders' file stood in 803, and a track
   that left it running. The founder's notes say a man ran, but not who, and Mục has always known. (Mục Hàn Nhạn, Mục Tân Vũ, Cảnh
   Trường Thiên, Bồ Tàn Chiếu; places 367, 223.)
3. **Files for a war.** Hình Trọng Sơn asks for the gate's files under his pact with Thính Vũ. Mục refuses without a
   reason, and Đổng goes over his head. (Đổng Kiên Lũy, Hình Trọng Sơn, Mục Hàn Nhạn, Thịnh Kinh Hàn; places 366,
   223.)
4. **The spring levy.** The player drills with the farmers of Thảo Đường on the lake shore for a month. One recruit
   will not stand still, and he turns out to be a Tùng Đào spy counting the files. (Đổng, Cát Trì Cổ, Ngô Bất Tán;
   places 366, 74.)
5. **The bridge of 1174.** Ngô Bất Tán learns that the Tùng Đào raider who cut the ropes is alive and trading in Hạc
   Đinh Thị. The gate does not duel. (Ngô Bất Tán, Tôn Lạc Hồ, Mục Hàn Nhạn; places 51, 80.)
6. **The lone goose.** Hạng Cô Nhạn, placed from Lạc Hà, wants a single fight at the Lạc Hà festival. A disciple of the
   gate in a duel is a scandal, and if he wins, a bigger one. (Hạng Cô Nhạn, Thịnh Kinh Hàn, Lương Thủ Hàng, Tô Bình
   Chu; places 19, 367.)
7. **Geese in the cold.** A cold year comes again, and the archers of Nhạn Thanh Thành are hungry. The city's ban
   (A171) holds only if the gate stands on the walls. (Mục Hàn Nhạn, Mục Tân Vũ, Kim Vạn Lý; place 38.)
8. **The point passes.** Mục Hàn Nhạn falls ill at the spring levy, and the banner must be carried. Đổng or Mục Tân Vũ
   carries it, and whoever does decides whether the gate goes to war. (Mục Hàn Nhạn, Đổng Kiên Lũy, Mục Tân Vũ, Lương
   Thủ Hàng; place 366.)
