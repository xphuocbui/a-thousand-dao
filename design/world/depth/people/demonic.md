A ma đạo cultivator in this world does not need to hide. Since the Night Rain Edict (C167, 905) Thính Vũ Lâu takes any
master who comes by night, asks no name, and sells their pills in the Night Rain Market (C198). These people are the
ones who would not go. Each has a reason, and the reasons are the group's shared story. The Tower lights a life lamp
for every sworn member and keeps a thread of their soul in it (N-thinh_vu-06). In 951 its head drained his own guest
master, Văn Hải Khô, though the Tower recorded a deviation (N-thinh_vu-05). Every ma đạo cultivator knows the first
fact, and the second is a rumour that no one at the Tower has ever denied aloud. Those who believe it choose the road.

None of them is ma đạo for its own sake. A lime-burner's child was bought for her Darkness. A bell-caster sat with the
starving. A karst girl's ink went black. A boy ran from a stockade with a sabre. A well-keeper's son was taken by the
man who killed his sister. What they share is that their roots or their trades lead to arts that take from other
people, and that no righteous sect will teach a root like theirs anything else.

## 2. Where they live, and what the land made them (R1)

- **The karst of Ngư Ca Xuyên (region 13).** The limestone north of Nam Phố Đường carries Darkness at 16-26 variant
  qi: U Lan Cốc (547), Oanh Hồi Hạp (543), the Jade Milk spring (249) and the sunken market of Hãm Thạch Trấn (548,
  fallen into a cave in 912, V105). Its villages, Hầu Đầu (546), Hôi Diêu (549) and Phóng Mộc (551), are Wood-heavy
  but hold Earth and Water. By R1b a child there with both is three to four times likelier than an even-land child to
  wake Darkness. That makes the karst the continent's one breeding ground for Darkness roots, and no lineage of any
  sect runs on Darkness alone (Thu Phần Các's soul arts take no element). The children go up to the gorge. Triệu Huyết Y
  is one of them, a hundred and fifty years earlier.
- **Đào Sa Thị (47), the gold-washers' market** on the Lãng Đào river (Water 37 %). Triệu hid here in 1092-1094 (A079)
  and comes back. Its people are Water-Wood, and so are those she has taken from it.
- **Hắc Ưng Phong (695)**, a bare Metal peak (58 %) with Darkness in the rock (26) in the Pivot's own Ngọc Hành Sơn,
  above the black-iron mines. The Darkness makes the Pivot's patrols sick, so they keep them short. Khoái Thiết Ưng
  has lived there since 940. His people are Metal-rooted miners' children.
- **Hạnh Hoa Thị (54) and Thu Trì Thành (42)**, the Tower's two markets. The soul pedlar keeps a room in the first, and
  the bounty man lives in the second.
- **The far west (region 10)**: Tình Sa Thôn (125), Đàm Ảnh Thành (37) and Triều Âm Thành (72), the three river towns
  whose qi wells Văn Hải Khô drained before 906. Water and Wood land. Tình Sa's well is still bitter.
- **Độc Thiềm Lâm (821)**, the poison-toad wood west of the Tower, where the expelled alchemist keeps a grove of dead
  trees.

## 4. Ways and customs

- **No lamp.** The one rule they all keep. No one in this group will let another cultivator hold a thread of their
  soul. That is why they are not at the Tower, why Hám sells souls but will not sit in a lamp, and why Triệu's offer
  to Cung is a method and not a sect.
- **Pay or be paid.** Every household has a ledger, real or kept in the head: Hám writes the name and price of every
  breath, Triệu sells the Red Debt a layer per payment, Khoái fights only those who climb up to duel. They are not
  raiders. That is the line the righteous sects do not see and they do.
- **The karst's custom.** In U Lan Cốc nobody goes down to the villages on market days, and the children tie each
  other's ankles at night after the fourth layer. They bury in the gorge, under the orchids.
- **Names.** A ma đạo name is a robe's name, a bell's name or a place's name. Triệu took her robe's. Sào took the
  gorge's flower. They do not use the names they were born with in front of strangers.
- **How the righteous see them.** As one thing, "the ma đạo", hunted under the North Star Compact (C024). How they see
  each other: as six households with nothing in common but the road, several of whom would sell the others.

**Why roots and methods line up.** Darkness needs Earth and Water, and the karst gives both, so the karst children
cultivate Darkness. Triệu's own Darkness is fed through a Water method because no Darkness method is whole. That is
what Mặc's walls would fix, and it is the same hunger she has recognised in Cung Mặc Sương. The Hắc Phong blood sabre
runs on the Metal of the ore mountains. Hám's soul arts run on Metal, the bell-casters' element. None of the
group's methods is Heaven tier. The Blood-Ink Canon would be Earth tier if it were whole.

## 7. The abode (slot A-6)

The chronicle says Triệu is "hunting for her master's abode", and the Pivot's page says Đồ Vô Cữu "knows the way
there". Both are true for one reason. **Mặc's disciples never walked to the abode.** They came and went through a door
written in blood on a talisman stamped with his seal, so neither of them ever knew where it stood. In 1090 Đồ Vô Cữu
tracked Mặc on foot and walked the hunters to the real door (C024). Triệu ran out through the burning back passage
onto a mountain she did not know. Since then she has narrowed it to three mountains, two in the Pavilion's ground
(Ngọc Hồ Sơn) and one in the Pivot's (Ngọc Hành Sơn). These are the slot's three candidates, and this page does not
choose between them. Inside are the Blood-Ink Canon's last six layers on the walls and the brush Huyết Mặc Bút
(legendary, lost, sought by Triệu). The seal that opened the talisman door, `dm-blood-seal`, was taken from the dead
man's belt by Đồ Vô Cữu, and he keeps it at the Pivot's seat by Đỗ Tinh Hà's leave. To finish the canon she needs the place, the seal and the
brush, and that means Băng Tâm's mountains, the hound's keeping and the hound's memory. It is why she writes to a Băng
Tâm disciple and to a Dạ Quang elder whose riders guard the mines below the first candidate.

- **Thiên Xu Tông**: hunts under the Compact. Đồ Vô Cữu (`do_vo_cuu`) has hunted Triệu since 1090. He found his
  stockade brother Khoái twice and let him go twice. He buys heads from Biệt Tri Phản. The Pivot's own elders are said
  to buy from Hám, which is why the hound does not walk to the apricot market.
- **Tùng Đào Cung**: Thôi Khinh Hồng (`thoi_khinh_hong`) killed Mặc. Her River Hall's steward at Đào Sa, Khang Tẩy Kim
  (`khang_tay_kim`), owes Triệu her life and gave her a nephew.
- **Băng Tâm Các**: Sở Ngọc Hồ (`so_ngoc_ho`) killed Mặc; her second disciple Cung Mặc Sương (`cung_mac_suong`) has
  Triệu's offer and has not refused it.
- **Dạ Quang Môn**: Đồ Xích Linh (`do_xich_linh`) buys the Rite of the Red Debt from Triệu; Cáo Sa Lang was hers
  before Hạng put him out.
- **Thính Vũ Lâu**: the house they would not enter. The Night Rain Market (Kiều Mãn Lâu, `kieu_man_lau`) buys Hám's
  threads; Tra believes the head Mạnh Dạ Vũ (`manh_da_vu`) killed his master, and he is right (N-thinh_vu-05).
- **Thu Phần Các**: Lý Hận Bích (`ly_han_bich`) swore to break Hám's bells after he took a singer from Lôi Trạch.
- **Nam Phố Đường**: Châu Phi Vân (`chau_phi_van`) let Sào go in 1097. Đới Huyền Nghiên (`doi_huyen_nghien`) is going
  the same way, and Sào has written to him.
- **Thúy Vi Tông**: Úy Thanh Diễm (`uy_thanh_diem`) expelled Dưỡng Khô Chi. Tề Tử Tô (`te_tu_to`) is reading his
  margins.
- **The markets**: Lạc Đan Thanh (`lac_dan_thanh`) buys a soul thread a year from Hám for the breakthrough pills he sells
  to cultivators who have already failed once. Giả Hồi Xuân
  (`gia_hoi_xuan`) works in the same market as Hám and saves the people he would buy.

## 9. Secrets the cast plays out

- Triệu does not know where her master's abode is, and only the man she most wants dead does.
- The Blood-Ink Study set (robe, needle, seal) is whole only if someone robs the Pivot's hound in his own quarters.
- Hám's ledger names righteous buyers, and the breakthrough pills Lạc Đan Thanh sells to those who have failed once hold a
  bought breath.
- Sào drinks from her own village. Ngư killed a raftsman in 1193, and the gorge buried him.
- Tòng Bích Linh frees a soul a year and writes it in the ledger as spoiled.
- Mật Thu Ninh's lamp speaks, and the voice is changing.
- Tra holds the third copy of the Hollow Tide. The Tower's head does not know it exists.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The hound's memory.** Đồ Vô Cữu is the only living person who has walked to Mặc's door. Triệu needs him alive
   long enough to remember, and the Pivot needs him to say nothing. (Triệu, Đồ Vô Cữu, Đỗ Tinh Hà; slot A-6.)
2. **The seal in the hound's room.** Đồ Vô Cữu keeps the Blood-Ink Seal at the Pivot's seat. Triệu cannot walk into the
   Pivot herself. A player, a desperate Biệt Tri Phản or a bought servant could. (Triệu, Biệt, Đồ Vô Cữu; place 5.)
3. **The dark ink, answered.** Cung Mặc Sương says yes, or no, or tells her master. Khang Lãng Sa carries the letter,
   and he is half in love with the reader. (Cung, Triệu, Khang Lãng Sa, Sở Ngọc Hồ; places 191, 214.)
4. **The raftsman in the gorge.** A woodcutter finds the body Sào buried in 1193. Nam Phố blames Đới Huyền Nghiên,
   whose ink is darkening. Lạc Hà sends someone up the karst. Sào must give up Ngư or the gorge. (Sào, Ngư Mộng Tê,
   Sung Ngọc Khê, Đới Huyền Nghiên, Châu Phi Vân; places 547, 551, 249.)
5. **Two heirs, one wall.** Tra Hàm Tuyền learns that his martial aunt has narrowed the abode to three mountains. He
   offers her the Hollow Tide layer she lacks, which Văn taught him and not Mặc, for a share of the walls, or he sells
   her to Biệt. (Tra, Triệu, Biệt; places 37, 47.)
6. **The ledger.** Tòng Bích Linh's ledger, stolen or confessed, names Lạc Đan Thanh and righteous elders as buyers of
   souls. Whoever holds it can ruin a market and three sect elders. (Hám, Tòng, Lạc Đan Thanh, Kiều Mãn Lâu, Lý Hận
   Bích; place 54.)
7. **The lamp that speaks.** Mật Thu Ninh's lamp speaks in a voice that is not her son's. Tòng wants to free it, Hám
   wants it paid for, and something in it wants out. (Mật, Tòng, Hám; place 66.)
8. **Black Eagle Peak.** A Thiên Xu disciple climbs Hắc Ưng Phong to duel Khoái Thiết Ưng and loses. The Pivot has to
   act on its own mountain, and the hound has to choose a third time. (Khoái, Đồ Vô Cữu, Khoái Hồng Ngạc, Bộ Thiết
   Sinh; places 695, 174.)
9. **The margins.** Tề Tử Tô writes to the man who wrote the margins she reads. Dưỡng Khô Chi has sixty years left and
   an heir he never expected, inside the Furnace Hall that expelled him. (Dưỡng, Tề Tử Tô, Úy Thanh Diễm; places 821,
   1110.)
10. **The red-robe girl.** Xá Tiểu Sa follows Khang Lãng Sa to a meeting she should not see, and Triệu takes an
    interest. Khang Tẩy Kim must choose between her life-debt and a second child of Đào Sa. (Xá, Khang Lãng Sa,
    Khang Tẩy Kim, Triệu; place 47.)
11. **The bitter well.** The player draws water at Tình Sa Thôn and hears the story. The old man with the gourd has
    been back to the well every year since 885. (Tra Hàm Tuyền, Tra Cam Tỉnh, Dung Tỉnh Nhi; places 125, 37.)
