The north's sects are old and their gates are narrow. The Pavilion takes Ice and nothing else into its inner gate, the
pines take Wood and Water, Thúy Vi takes Wood and Earth, the Pivot weighs Metal on a beam. Everyone else is here. Some
failed a test, some were never asked, some were born with a root no gate in the north can teach, and a few simply would
not kneel. They live on the edges of the sects' ground and off the sects' trade: they dig the dead halls the sects left,
guard the caravans the sects tax, ferry the rivers the sects quarrel over, and sell to anyone. Most of them know a sect
elder by name, and most owe one something.

## 2. The land and its loose people (R1)

Roots follow the birthplace and nothing else, so the loose of the north look like their villages, except for the odd
ones, who are why they are loose.

- **The pine sea and the cold-moon forest** (regions 1, 4, 5) are Wood land (40-60 % Wood). Their loose are Wood-rooted
  with a thread of Water, like the sects that turned them away: Thư Phiêu Bình, Quản Tuyết Tung, Mạch Phục Linh.
  Lâm Dã Hạc's Metal 56 on 10 % Metal land is the iron streak the pines' examiner heard, the same one the chain-smith
  Vưu Liên Hoàn carried (C104).
- **The frost coast and the lake shore** (regions 1, 9) are Water land, and so are their diggers and fishers (Phàn Cổ
  Đăng, Nhâm Độ Hàn, Liên Bất Ngữ). The Pavilion took the ones with Ice. The ones without it walked back from the
  Frost Test on the mere at Hàn Giao Chử (Khổng Vọng Băng, Hà Khổ Hàn).
- **The desert and steppe** (region 8) breed Earth and Fire (Hô Diên Liệt, Xa Linh Nhi). Even cities stay mixed:
  Trường Vân Thành bred Ngũ Bất Tề's five weak elements, a root no gate takes.
- **The odd roots**: a Fire root from an ice-cutters' village (Dư Chúc Nhi, Fire factor 0.55 at Tạc Băng Thôn); a Wind
  root born on a boat in a gale (Chiêm Thính Phong; no land in the north holds wind qi, so the roll was luck); a
  Thunder root from the moonstone shafts where the vein's thunder runs (Lỗ Kinh Thạch; Nguyệt Thạch Khoáng holds
  Thunder 26); a Light root from the snow-jade mine (Yến Lưu Huy; Tuyết Ngọc Khoáng holds Light 14); a Darkness root
  from the ash village by the dark gorge (Mặc Thán Nương; Thiêu Hôi Thôn holds Darkness 10). None of them has a teacher
  north of the desert.

## 4. How the loose live

- **Licences and tenths.** The frost coast's ruins belong to the Pavilion, and nobody digs them without a licence from
  the Hall of Cold Law, which takes a tenth of every find. Phàn Cổ Đăng has held one for a century. Unlicensed diggers
  are fined the first time and lose a hand the second. Thẩm Hàn Giang's books show what the tenth really comes to.
- **Markets are their halls.** Tùng Hạ Thị under the pines, Tống Khách Thị on the frost coast, Lam Yên Thị below Thúy
  Vi, Quy Điểu's winter ice market, the caravan stops of the desert road. A loose cultivator is known by the table they
  drink at, and the market is where they buy their methods, one torn manual at a time.
- **The Loose Table.** At Bì Tứ Nương's wineshop in Tùng Hạ Thị a long table stands under one pine. Anyone who sits at
  it is loose and is hired from it. A sect disciple who sits there is ignored, and one who sits there in sect robes is
  asked to leave. Hứa Vãn Cung drank there for thirty years and has not sat down since he founded his hall.
- **Methods by the page.** No one here was taught a lineage. They hold what they bought, found or kept: a Yellow primer
  from a smith's widow, a Profound method from a dead guard's saddlebag, a sect's outer primer kept after the sect sent
  them away. Most will stop at the sixth layer of a Yellow method and never know what a seventh feels like.
- **Owing.** A loose cultivator survives on debts: owed by a caravan master, owing a sect elder for a licence, a pass or
  a silence. Most of the circles below are held together by who owes whom.
- **The dead.** Diggers stand their dead in the ice as the Pavilion does, but facing the sea, not south. Robbers bury
  theirs under the road they died on. Guards are buried where the caravan was, and the caravan pays.

## 5. The circles

### The Loose Table (Tùng Hạ Thị, place 46)

Lâm Dã Hạc, the woodcutter's son with iron bones; Thư Phiêu Bình, the old guard who taught the boy Hứa Vãn Cung the
road; Giả Chỉ Lộ, who walks buyers to ruins and leaves them at the last turn; Mạch Phục Linh, who digs ginseng on the white apes' ridge; and Bì Tứ Nương,
who keeps the table and sells foundation pills she does not guarantee. Their sect neighbours are Tuần Tùng Đường on
the hill above the market, which needs a Golden Core and is waiting for Lâm, and Ngạo Tuyết's young swords, who come
down every autumn to pick fights.

### The frost-coast ruin crew (Tống Khách Thị, place 48)

Phàn Cổ Đăng, 190, the coast's digger, with her old bronze lamp; Dư Chúc Nhi, the Fire-rooted girl she bought from an
ice-cutters' village; Chiêm Thính Phong, the Wind root who feels air move through stone; Khổng Vọng Băng, who failed
the Frost Test three times and cuts the ice; and their fence Liên Bất Ngữ at Tuyết Đăng Trấn, who ships Ice beast cores
south inside the ice-fish run. The crew has dug the outer halls of Hàn Giang Môn (never the ice under its own hall), Sóc Phương Thành, Tầm Tung Viện and Bắc Minh Quán. The
one hall it has never opened is the fire hall of Băng Hỏa Môn (1430), where Phàn's Dao partner died in 1171.

### The Cold Ford crossing (Hàn Tân Thành, place 31)

Nhâm Độ Hàn poles the river mouth at night and remembers no faces; his daughter Nhâm Liễu Ngạn carries Thúy Vi's
letters across his river. Cù Lô Nhi, the reed foundling, poles his second boat. On the quay, Cát Châu Nương keeps the
dice house where half the coast's debts are written, and passes what she hears to Tùng Đào's first elder Hồ Tùng
Nguyệt. Her best debtor is Luyện Đắc Bảo, whose sister at Vụ Ẩn Môn is paying for him with the Mist-Lock Lantern's key.

### The desert road (Đà Linh Trấn, Ngư Đàm Thôn, Trường Vân Thành)

Hô Diên Liệt, son of a Dạ Quang rider and a Trường Vân guard's daughter, rides for the Hồi Lan merchants who trust
neither Trấn Sa nor Dạ Quang. Xa Linh Nhi left Dạ Quang's outer gate the year the young blades took up the cups again.
Ngũ Bất Tề tells the Desert Road War at the inns for his supper. At the oasis of Ngư Đàm, Mông Thủy Hương, widow of the
guard Hà Khổ Hàn, keeps the fish ponds and keeps her daughter Hà Tiểu Băng, the most Water-gifted girl the desert has
bred, away from the Frost Test that broke her father.

### The Bobtail ridge (Đoản Vĩ Cương, place 1043)

Khấu Thiên Tứ, the robber chief, a Golden Core who answers to no sect, raised in the camp beside Triều Ngũ Lang before
the mist took Triều in 1081; his lieutenant Dương Tam Đao, who led the 1190 raid; and Hầu Tiểu Lục, who held the
horses that night at twelve and wants out. The band feeds the charcoal and tea villages of the cape and is hidden by
them. It is Vụ Ẩn Môn's reason to exist.

### The hunters of the cold-moon forest (Lãnh Nguyệt Lâm)

Quản Tuyết Tung of Liệp Hộ Thôn, who sold the wolf-king's cub; Nhung Tử Điêu, the last sable trapper, who helped him
dig it out; and Âu Vãn Chiếu, who followed the moon-fox into Phản Cảnh Lâm in 1192 and now walks lost travellers out
by the evening light.

### Those who stand alone

Lỗ Kinh Thạch, the Thunder-rooted moonstone miner; Tuân Tuyết Liên, who gathers snow lotus above Lê Hoa Am's gate
and loves the crew's ice-cutter; Yến Lưu Huy, the Light-rooted jade miner who failed the Pivot's beam by five; Mặc
Thán Nương, the charcoal-burner's girl with a Darkness root, to whom a woman in red has promised a book; Đinh Vạn Tác,
the Đào Sa hemp smuggler, and his wife Liêu Tẩy Sa, who keeps his ledger in her gold pan.

## 6. Relations with the sects

- **Băng Tâm Các** owns the frost coast, its ruins and its licences; it made most of the north's failed candidates.
  Its Frost Test is the door through which most loose of regions 7-9 did not pass (Khổng Vọng Băng, Hà Khổ Hàn), and
  Ôn Sa Ngọc still comes to the oasis every spring. Thẩm Hàn Giang's Hall of Cold Law takes the diggers' tenth.
- **Tùng Đào Cung** turned Dư Chúc Nhi away at its outer gate, warned Lâm Dã Hạc off his own, and pays Cát Châu Nương. Its wolves came because of the cub.
- **Tuần Tùng Đường** needs a Golden Core and is waiting for Lâm Dã Hạc; Thư Phiêu Bình will not swear to it.
- **Vụ Ẩn Môn** and the Bobtail band are each other's reason: the mist hides carts, the band robs the ones that do not
  pay. Triều Ngũ Lang and Khấu Thiên Tứ are sworn brothers on opposite sides of it.
- **Tầm Kiếm Đường** was built on a manual Phàn Cổ Đăng sold; Giả Chỉ Lộ buys its map-seller's honest copies to learn the passes he leads buyers up.
- **Trấn Sa Đường and Dạ Quang Môn** both want Hô Diên Liệt, and neither has him.
- **Lãnh Giang Phái**'s escort is hunting Liên Bất Ngữ (lanh_giang hook 6).
- **Thiên Xu Tông** weighed Yến Lưu Huy on its beam three springs running and found her five short; she left its outer gate.

## 9. Story hooks

1. **The fire door.** Phàn Cổ Đăng takes the crew to Băng Hỏa Môn (1430) and asks Dư Chúc Nhi to open the hall that
   killed Thiệu Tàn Đăng. The player can go in with her, warn her, or tell her that Hồng Lô Cốc on the cape would teach
   her Fire properly. What is inside: the split sect's fire half and a burned man's bones. (Phàn, Dư Chúc Nhi, Chiêm
   Thính Phong; places 1430, 48, 9.)
2. **A Golden Core for hire.** Tuần Tùng Đường wants Lâm Dã Hạc to form his core and take its elder's seat. Lâm wants
   a Profound Metal method first and is hiding a cracked foundation. The player can find him the method, the pill, or
   the truth about the jar behind Bì Tứ Nương's counter. (Lâm, Hứa Vãn Cung, Bì Tứ Nương; places 46, 376; tuan_tung
   hook 2.)
3. **The man who sold the cub.** A new wolf king leads a pack down to the Bạch Lang road (tuan_tung hook 1). Quản Tuyết
   Tung wants it dead before anyone asks where the first cub came from; Diêm Lạc Nhạn wants every wolf dead; Nhung Tử
   Điêu is drunk enough to tell. (Quản, Nhung, Diêm Lạc Nhạn, Hứa Vãn Cung; places 921, 110, 938.)
4. **Two axes.** The Bobtail band raids the Đồng Sơn road again (vu_an hook 4). Khấu Thiên Tứ sends word to Triều Ngũ
   Lang that he wants one night across a fire, and Ký Thất Lộ learns where the band sleeps. Hầu Tiểu Lục can lead either
   of them there. (Khấu, Triều Ngũ Lang, Ký Thất Lộ, Hầu Tiểu Lục, Dương Tam Đao; places 1043, 1056, 1063.)
5. **The lantern's key, from the quay.** Cát Châu Nương sends Luyện Đắc Bảo to his sister with a deadline. The player can
   pay the debt, steal the ledger, or carry the key. (Cát, Luyện Đắc Bảo, Luyện Tố Y, Hồ Tùng Nguyệt; places 31,
   1063; vu_an hook 3.)
6. **The girl on the ice.** Hà Tiểu Băng walks to the frozen mere at Hàn Giao Chử to sit the solstice night uninvited
   beside Hồ Tiểu Đàm. Her mother sends the player after her; Đỗ Sương Nhi, who keeps the test, must decide whether a girl who is no
   outer disciple may sit, and Ôn Sa Ngọc's letter follows her up the road. If Ice answers her, the oasis has its
   first Ice root; if not, she has the road her father took. (Hà Tiểu Băng, Mông Thủy Hương, Hồ Tiểu Đàm, Ôn Sa Ngọc, Đỗ Sương Nhi; places
   116, 191.)
7. **Cores in the fish.** Sử Phá Băng's escort cuts open an ice-fish and finds an Ice beast core (lanh_giang hook 6).
   The trail leads to Liên Bất Ngữ, and through him to Phàn's crew and a Thiên Bảo Trai buyer at Mộc Lan. (Liên, Sử
   Phá Băng, Phàn; places 1440, 68.)
8. **The walk that was right.** A buyer Giả Chỉ Lộ left at the last turn went on alone in 1197, came back and paid
   double. The player hires the same walk, and must get past the last turn. (Giả, Hách Tùng, Lạc Đan Thanh; places 46, 45.)
9. **The free sabre.** A cup with blood in it is found at a stop (tran_sa hook 1). Viên Cô Yên and Ô Liệt Phong each
