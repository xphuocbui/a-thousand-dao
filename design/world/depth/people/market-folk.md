The market folk are not a guild. What binds them is paper: every one of them owes or is owed by another, and most
by a sect. At the centre of the paper sits Kim Vạn Lý, the richest merchant of the west, who owes everyone a little
and is owed by everyone a little more. Around him are the trades the sects cannot or will not do for themselves: a
pill-seller who makes what no sect furnace can, a healer who cures what sect alchemists refuse, a formation master who
left his abbey to sell its craft, and a banker who lends to elders who cannot be seen borrowing.

**How the land shapes them (R1).** Markets and port cities sit on even or Water-leaning land, so most market folk
have mixed two-element roots near 40-55, the kind no great sect's inner gate would take. That is one reason they are
in the market. The strong roots come from outside the cities: Kim Vạn Lý's Metal from the red-gold diggings of Xích
Kim Khoáng (169), Liêu Thính Kim's from the Metal of the Shao market's hills, Trịnh Chu Sa's Wood from Nam Phố's
forest shore (79), Bào Tế Hải's Water from the white sands of Bạch Sa (57). Two roots do not fit their birthplace,
and the market tells a story about each. Giả Hồi Xuân is Wood 78 in a market of 10 % Wood ("the apricot tree gave
it to her"). Lạc Đan Thanh is Fire 80 from a Wood village where Fire is 22 % ("he got it from the mortar").

## 4. Customs

- **The market peace.** A market keeps its holder's peace: no blood is drawn inside its bounds, and a quarrel goes
  to the market's scale. Thiên Quyền Thị's weights are the standard every market's scale is checked against (A207).
  A cultivator who breaks the peace loses the stall, and the sect's collector takes the rent anyway.
- **The stall rent.** Every stall pays its sect. Lạc Đan Thanh rents his stall from Phan Vật Hoa, and that is why
  Thiên Bảo has never asked where his pills come from.
- **Three prices.** A market alchemist names three prices: one for a sect, one for a loose cultivator, one for a
  mortal. Lạc Đan Thanh names one, whatever he likes (A186), and the market calls it shameless and pays it.
  Giả Hồi Xuân names none for mortals.
- **The closed ledger.** When a market cultivator dies, their ledger is closed and buried with them: debts written
  in it die, and so do credits. An heir may open it instead, and then takes on both. Most do not. Quản Phục Thu did.
- **Paper is an oath.** A signed note binds the signer's name, not their purse. Quách Tiếp Vân signed Vệ Văn Cơ's fee
  in his own name, so it is his, not Lạc Hà's.
- **Bought methods.** Market folk cultivate what they can buy: a catalogue scripture from a season auction, a fallen
  sect's manual, or the outer method of a sect they left. A sect would call half of them stolen. The market does not
  ask.
- **Selling to both sides.** A market does not take sides in a war. Lê Tuyền Phái will not sell to a sect at war;
  every stall in Vật Hoa Thị will. This is the oldest reason the sects distrust the market folk.
- **Dress.** Merchants wear plain cloth and one good thing (Kim's gold coin, Từ's brass weight). Healers of the
  apricot clinic wear apricot-brown. Talisman sellers keep their stamps on a cord at the throat.

## 6. Houses and circles

- **The Kim house (Nhạn Thanh Thành, 38).** Kim Vạn Lý, his son Kim Tích Phúc (the caravans), his grandson Kim Đắc
  Lộc (in Mục Vân's stables), the clerk Quản Thận Ngôn, the bet-runner Đái Song Lục, the road sword Diêu Thiết Tiên,
  and factors on every road: Hô Diên Linh in the desert, Kỷ Minh Tiền at Hồi Lan, Sử Quế Hương in the far south. It
  trades wool, wine, tea, spice and debt. It owns a third of the Bào ship-house.
- **The Bào ship-house (Yên Ba Thành, 30).** Founded on the white sands of Bạch Sa by the junk-builder Bào Thừa Phong,
  who came when the Hỗ jetty made Bạch Sa a town (A029) and built the Hỗ fleet its junks. Bào Tế Hải, his daughter
  Bào Thính Triều, the shipwright Quan Liêm Chu. The Hỗ (clans page) sail what the Bào build; Kim owns a third of
  the yards and wants the Hỗ fleet as well.
- **The Từ counting house (Thiên Quyền Thị, 52).** Từ Bỉnh Quyền and her heir Từ Thiên Toán. It never says who owes it.
- **The Quản (Ngũ Cốc Thành, 78).** What is left of the banker ruined with Kim Cốc: Quản Phục Thu and her brother.
- **The Tăng counter (Hạc Đinh Thị, 51).** Tăng Bách Thông and his daughter Tăng Phân Ly; his son Tăng Vân Tiêu is
  Lạc Hà's caravan disciple.
- **The apricot clinic (Hạnh Hoa Thị, 54).** Founded by Mẫn Tế Thế around 920; Giả Hồi Xuân, Nhiêu Tử Uyển, Vu
  Ngân Kiều, the courier Hà Khổ Sâm.
- **Lạc's stall (Vật Hoa Thị, 50)**, **Vệ's workshop (Tinh Hán Thành, 40)**, **the Trịnh stall (Đào Sa Thị, 47)**,
  **Hoàng Phủ Duyên's teahouse (Tương Phùng Thị, 49)**, **Bàng Thủ Khế's pawn shop (Tống Khách Thị, 48)**.

## 7. Relations with the sects

- **Thiên Bảo Trai** (Phan Vật Hoa): the market's landlord and its rival lender. She sold Lạc the painting and rents
  him the stall; she sells Kim the Lạc Hà notes he buys for Trầm and knows whose they are; she wants Từ's paper on Kim.
  Tiền Ngũ Phân loses at Kim's table and buys Bì Tiểu Thố's names.
- **Lạc Hà Tông:** the lord of half the western markets. Trầm Tịch Dương buys the sect's own debt through Kim; Tô Vãn
  Chiếu wins Kim's bets; Quách Tiếp Vân owes Vệ the outer ring; Tăng Vân Tiêu owes Lạc for three pills.
- **Thiên Xu Tông:** the market's scales. Đặng Quân Bình checks Từ's weights and is owed Kim's tolls. Thiện Thừa Củ
  despises Vệ for turning the Thiên Cơ Hall down. Quý Thủ Lũy hired Vệ for the barrow wards.
- **Củng Tinh Quán:** Vệ's abbey. Thẩm Bắc Thần was his master; Phó Cương Đẩu has lost the stele work to him twice.
- **Thúy Vi Tông:** Diêm Hải Tảo borrows Kim's hulls and drinks with Bào; Úy Thanh Diễm and Đậu Tam Thất know Lạc
  copies their seals; Dư Tục Đoạn trades cases with Giả by letter.
- **Thính Vũ Lâu:** the apricot market's lord. Ô Thiềm Tử sends Giả his poisons; Kiều Mãn Lâu's Night Rain Market is
  undercut by Kim in the desert; Giả told Ung Thính Thiền the truth about his eyes and taught Bồ Ngọc Châm her salves.
- **Cốc Vũ Quán:** Dương Hảo Vũ is Giả's oldest friend; Lam Nhuận Chi walks the hard cases down the road.
- **Nam Phố Đường:** sent Trịnh Chu Sa away in 1120 and burned his stall with its own talismans in 1173.
- **Lê Tuyền Phái** (Nhạc Thuần Nhưỡng: forty jars, which since 1198 he sets against the school's note for the silver it paid the Pivot's steward), **Phất Y Cốc** (Khúc Liệt Dương: a year's pay), **Mục Vân
  Trang** (Khấu's horses, Dư Bôn Vân, Kim Đắc Lộc), **Minh Loan Các** (Khang Viễn Đạc's caravan bells), **Phượng

## 8. Inside: the web of paper

- **Kim Vạn Lý's wheel.** Since the cold year the Kim house has run on Từ Bỉnh Quyền's credit. Kim pays nothing until
  it is called, and loses his festival bets to people who matter, deliberately: a lost bet is a gift no treasurer can
  call a bribe. Trầm's money passes through his hands between purchases. If anyone called everything at once, the
  richest merchant of the west would be Từ's by autumn.
- **The Quản revenge.** Quản Phục Thu wants exactly that. Her brother copies Kim's books for her. She is offering to
  buy the small debts Kim leaves unpaid (Khúc Liệt Dương's year's pay, Nhạc's forty jars) so she can call them on one
  day. Her brother has begun to like Kim.
- **The four copies.** Quản Doanh Hư sold four copies of Kim Cốc's canon in 1004. One is the Từ house's. Three are
  unknown. The Golden Valley Suite is split three ways: the coin with Từ, the seal with the Quản, the pearl in the ash.
- **The Peach Spring.** Lạc Đan Thanh holds the only map of Đào Nguyên Cảnh, and 1200 is an opening year. He has been
  in once and remembers nothing; his best pill is ground from what he brought out, and one stone is left. He had the
  guide Giả Chỉ Lộ (loose-north) beaten for walking buyers toward the Spring.
- **Where the pills come from.** Not all of Lạc's pills are his. He sells the hermit Khuyết Vãn Hà's (hermits-sages)
  and takes a third; his copies of Thúy Vi's ride north in the Hỗ fleet's second holds (clans); and his pills for
  those who have failed once hold a soul thread bought from Hám Tàn Chung (demonic), who works the apricot market
  that Giả Hồi Xuân heals in.
- **The poisons in the healer.** Giả Hồi Xuân tastes every poison Ô Thiềm Tử sends her. She has perhaps forty years
  left. Her heir refuses to taste.
- **The wards on the barrow.** Vệ Văn Cơ quit the Vạn Cốt Trủng because its wards are his master's work, and they
  are failing on one side.
- **The hulls.** Kim ordered the hulls he lends Diêm Hải Tảo built for southern water. He knows about her chart.

## 11. Story hooks

1. **Call it all in one day.** Quản Phục Thu buys Khúc Liệt Dương's and Nhạc Thuần Nhưỡng's claims on Kim Vạn Lý, or
   asks the player to. If she calls them together, Từ Bỉnh Quyền must carry Kim or take his house. (Quản Phục Thu,
   Kim Vạn Lý, Từ Bỉnh Quyền, Khúc Liệt Dương, Nhạc Thuần Nhưỡng; places 78, 38, 52.)
2. **The clerk who liked him.** Quản Thận Ngôn must choose between his sister and the old man whose books he copies.
   Either way, someone learns what Trầm's money has been covering: his sister, or Trầm Tịch Dương himself.
   (Quản Thận Ngôn, Quản Phục Thu, Kim Vạn Lý, Trầm Tịch Dương; place 38.)
3. **The last stone.** Đào Nguyên Cảnh opens in 1200. Lạc Đan Thanh needs a companion who will remember the way, and
   Thúy Vi's port house wants the painting. (Lạc Đan Thanh, Đằng Thạch Lục, Diêm Hải Tảo, Kê Lăng Hoa; places 50, 57.)
4. **The ledger of 1150.** Diêm Tử Tô finally asks. Lạc's ledger clears him and shames Vi Ngưng Yên. Lạc must
   decide whether to show it. (Lạc Đan Thanh, Diêm Tử Tô, Tào Mộ Tử; places 50, 423.)
5. **A healer with forty years.** Giả Hồi Xuân's poisons begin to win. Nhiêu Tử Uyển must taste or let the clinic
   fail, and Ô Thiềm Tử learns what his gifts have cost. (Giả Hồi Xuân, Nhiêu Tử Uyển, Ô Thiềm Tử, Dương Hảo Vũ;
   places 54, 42.)
6. **The barrow wards.** The side of the Vạn Cốt Trủng that faces the old field gives way. Only Vệ Văn Cơ can read
   the wards, and only Thẩm Bắc Thần can say why they were laid that way. (Vệ Văn Cơ, Thẩm Bắc Thần, Quý Thủ Lũy;
   places 40, 22, the Vạn Cốt Trủng on Chiết Kích Nguyên.)
7. **The outer ring.** Vệ Văn Cơ goes up to Vân Kiều to collect from Quách Tiếp Vân, who cannot pay. Vệ will take the
   fee in something other than stones. (Vệ Văn Cơ, Quách Tiếp Vân, Phong Kính Sa; place 558.)
8. **The southern hulls.** Bào Tế Hải tells Diêm Hải Tảo what Kim's hulls were built for, or Bào Thính Triều does.
   The three of them sail, with or without Kim's leave. (Bào Tế Hải, Bào Thính Triều, Diêm Hải Tảo, Kim Vạn Lý,
   Quan Liêm Chu; places 30, 57.)
9. **The Golden Valley Suite.** If Ân Toán Châu's find in the ash of Kim Cốc Thành is Lục Châu, Thiên Bảo will auction
   it, and Quản Phục Thu and Từ Bỉnh Quyền will both bid. (Quản Phục Thu, Từ Bỉnh Quyền, Phan Vật Hoa; places 219,
   50.)
10. **The unstamped contest.** Trịnh Hồng Tiên sits Nam Phố's prize contest at Hạc Đinh Thị under her own name, and
    wins with her father's unstamped hand. (Trịnh Hồng Tiên, Trịnh Chu Sa, Châu Phi Vân, Trác Thư Lan; places 51,
    47.)
11. **The bought-back notes.** Tăng Phân Ly tells her brother that their father has been buying his pill notes back
    from Lạc. Tăng Vân Tiêu, forty years at the peak of Trúc Cơ, has to decide what he owes and to whom. (Tăng Phân
    Ly, Tăng Bách Thông, Tăng Vân Tiêu, Lạc Đan Thanh; places 51, 50.)
12. **The match.** Hoàng Phủ Duyên brokers a marriage between Kim's grandson and Bào's daughter to settle the third
    of the yards. Neither wants it. (Hoàng Phủ Duyên, Kim Đắc Lộc, Bào Thính Triều, Bào Tế Hải, Kim Vạn Lý; places 49,
    30, 19.)
