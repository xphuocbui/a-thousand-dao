The hermits are not a society. They are a handful of very old people who chose to have no gate, and a trail of
disciples, servants and seekers who stayed near them. They know each other the way mountains do: by what they can see
from where they stand. Nearly every great loose cultivator of the last thousand years has crossed to Dục Nhật Đảo once,
to ask the oldest man alive one thing, and has come back without the answer.

The question is real. A sect cultivator meets heaven inside an array his sect built, with elders holding the lines, and the one
sect Hóa Thần alive, Thôi Khinh Hồng (`thoi_khinh_hong`), has had a sect's ground and arrays behind her for a thousand
years. A hermit meets it alone. Since the calendar began,
one hermit has lived through it, Mạc Quy Khư (C029). Hoắc Hàn Quang died in his (C014). Cao Đan Hà died making a pill
for it (C022). Yến Tang Điền died in his, and his disciple lived only by holding a mirror (C180). Each of them asked
Mạc first.

**Roots (R1).** No sect chose these people, so their roots are their birthplaces' and their accidents', and they are
the odd ones the genre expects of loose cultivators:

- **Mạc Quy Khư** was born on even land (Tang Âm Thôn, 162: no element above 24 %) with a muddy root of all five.
  Phù Tang Cảnh burned it into Primordial Chaos in the year 0. That is a root change, not a birth root.
- **Tư Không Hạc Niên** is pure Metal (94) from a Metal mine (Không Thanh Khoáng, 1457: Metal 46 %). Under R1's factor
  his land gives Metal ×2.3. No sect of the north-east runs on Metal alone, which is part of why he never joined one.
- **Đoạn Thương Hải** and **Kê Thính Đào** are Water children of the island coast whose Thunder came from bolts (1060,
  1148), not from land; Cô Vụ Đảo has no Thunder qi.
- **Giản Thu Địch** has Wind (64) because she was born at Tập Trận Khoáng (603, Wind 31), one of five windy places on
  the continent. Phất Y Cốc and Cửu Cao Phái both hunt children from that mine; Lương Lạc Mai got there first.
- **Khuyết Vãn Hà** is Wood and Fire from a cassia village of the hot coast (Quế Viên Thôn 1025: Wood 43, Fire 22).
- The servants and seekers follow their villages: frost-coast Water (Nhiễm), vent Fire (Hác), plum-valley Wood with
  no Metal (Bốc, from Hàn Mai Thôn at 7 % Metal, which is why Ngạo Tuyết turned her away).

## 4. Ways and customs

- **One master, one disciple (nhất sư nhất đồ).** A hermit teaches one person at a time and rarely more than three in
  a life. Văn Nhân took one; Hoắc took one at a time; Lương took a last one at 450. To teach a second while the first
  lives is thought greedy. Tư Không has broken the rule the other way: he has taught no one since 1088 (oath).
- **The sealed abode (phong động).** A hermit is not buried. When one dies, the disciple, or the first person to find
  the body, closes the cave with what the dead made: the sword, the furnace, the fan, the flute, the method cut into the
  wall. Whoever opens it later inherits, and pays what the abode asks. That is why A-1, A-2, A-4 and A-5 still hold
  their legendary items, and why the people who know where they are (Lê Hoa Am, Khuyết Vãn Hà, Giản Thu Địch) do not
  open them.
- **The question (vấn đạo).** Pilgrims cross to Dục Nhật Đảo from Thiên Nhai Trấn each spring. Since 1100 Mạc answers
  one question a year (A311); the ferryman writes down every one in the Book of Questions, which now runs to a hundred
  entries and a long list of the unchosen. No one is chosen twice. The question everyone wants to ask, how he survived,
  has been asked forty-one times, and the answer is always the same: "With a still heart." It is not true.
- **The farewell cup (tiễn bôi).** At the first dawn of spring the keeper of the Farewell Cup tavern pours a cup at
  the farewell pavilion of Tống Khách Thị for a traveller who left in 900 (A138). Hermits passing through drink there, and leave their own cup upturned if they
  do not mean to come back.
- **No gate, no oath.** A hermit holds no land, so the North Star Compact's land clauses (C107) neither protect nor
  bind them: there is nothing to take by writ and no levy to call. The Pivot can still license a hunt against a loose
  cultivator who preys on others, as it did against Mặc Huyết Sinh (C024), so a hermit's safety is to harm no one. In practice sects buy their help (Úy Thanh Diễm went to Thúy Vi; Sài Mộ Kiếm to Tầm Kiếm Đường) or wait
  for them to die and dig.
- **Courtesy.** Hermits name their master's cave when they meet, not their own names. Nobody asks a hermit's age.

## 6. How one becomes a hermit

There is no gate. People come to this life in three ways: a hermit picks them (Văn Nhân picked Mạc, Yến picked Đoạn,
Đoạn picked Kê Thính Đào, Lương picked Giản, Cao picked Khuyết), they
outlive or leave a sect (Yến after 705, Hác after Hồng Lô), or they come to ask something and stay (Trâu, Cam, Bốc).
A hermit takes a disciple for a reason no sect would accept: a boy who sat on a shore for a winter, a girl who whistled
down a mine shaft, a fisher's daughter the lightning did not kill.

## 8. Relations

- **Phất Y Cốc (`phat_y`).** Tư Không's master was its founder Quách Lĩnh Thù, which the valley does not know. Its head
  Thang Vô Tạ owes Tư Không the draw of 1130 and wears the sleeve he cut. Its Wind boy Lộ Khinh Trần is the kind
  of hand Cô Hạc was made for.
- **Ngạo Tuyết Phái (`ngao_tuyet`).** Lăng Đạp Tuyết is Tư Không's former disciple; the Metal in his root cost him years of
  his life, and only the two of them know. Bốc Tuyết Nha, turned away at his gate, sits in Tư Không's tavern.
- **Tầm Kiếm Đường and Lê Hoa Am (`tam_kiem`, `le_hoa`).** The hermitage guards Hoắc's cave; the hall hunts it. Sài Mộ
  Kiếm wants his second duel with Tư Không. Nhiễm Hàn Châu bought the hall's map from Hách Tùng.
- **Thúy Vi Tông and Hồng Lô Cốc (`thuy_vi`, `hong_lo`).** Úy Thanh Diễm (Thúy Vi's Green Furnace) and Đồng Hồng Lô both
  want the Purple Smoke Furnace in A-2. Khuyết Vãn Hà knows where it is. Ngụy Đan Chu of Hồng Lô and the vent-sitter
  Hác Tẫn Đăng both read the cape for Cung Hỏa Vân's abode.
- **Nhàn Vân Quán and Lạc Hà Tông (`nhan_van`, `lac_ha`).** Diệp Tố Quyên, Tần Đảo Ảnh and the servant Ô Lão Nghiễn are
  Đoạn's eyes at the pool. Lận Viễn Phàm shares his search for the fleet, and Kê Thính Đào sails on his boats. Cam Thủ
  Nguyện blames him for her son.
- **Thính Vũ Lâu (`thinh_vu`).** Its war elder Cố Dạ Lan has wanted Tư Không's sword for two hundred years.
- **Tùng Đào Cung (`tung_dao`).** Thôi Khinh Hồng is the only other Hóa Thần alive. Her father was one of the fishers
  who saw Mạc's master walk into the moon (A001).
- **Phượng Minh Cung (`phuong_minh`).** Holds the coast and the realm Phù Tang Cảnh on its map, but has never set foot
  on Dục Nhật Đảo against the old man's wish. It counts the pilgrims.
- **The market (`market-folk`, `loose-north`).** Lạc Đan Thanh's buyer takes Khuyết's pills at Đan Kiều Trấn (759), and
  he takes a third. In Tống Khách Thị the ruin-digger Phàn Cổ Đăng is the one person Tư Không plays go with.

## 9. Secrets

- **The vase.** Mạc did not survive his tribulation; the Quy Khư Bình did. He told Hoắc to go with empty hands so
  that no one would come for the vase, and Hoắc died. He means to carry it back into Phù Tang Cảnh before he dies,
  which would leave no hermit a way through.
- **The ferryman's voice.** Mạc has slept through the last springs. Thư Vấn Triều has answered two years' questions in
  his place. If one of those answers was wrong, nobody but Thư knows it.
- **The mirror.** Đoạn knows the mirror drank the strike meant for him, and has guessed the same of Mạc. He also knows
  Diệp burned the painting of 705.
- **The traveller and the sleeve.** Tư Không's master was Phất Y's founder, alive until 900. Tư Không won on Cùng Mục
  Phong, and won by cutting his master's sleeve.
- **The herb.** Khuyết Vãn Hà changed one herb in Cao Đan Hà's pill without telling her. The recipe as written may
  be right, and Úy Thanh Diễm does not know it.
- **The second flute.** Something was playing with Lương Lạc Mai when she died, and it stopped when the door opened.

## 12. Story hooks

1. **The ferryman's answer.** The player crosses with Thư Vấn Triều and is chosen to ask. The old man on the rocks does
   not move, and the answer comes from the boat. If the player notices, Thư begs them to keep it, or Mạc wakes. (Thư Vấn
   Triều, Thư Tiểu Chu, Mạc Quy Khư; places 1006, 198.)
2. **The vase goes home.** Phù Tang Cảnh's gate opens. Mạc means to carry the Quy Khư Bình back inside. Whoever learns
   what it does (Đoạn has guessed) must choose: let the one tool that saves a hermit's tribulation leave the world, or
   stop the oldest man alive. (Mạc, Đoạn Thương Hải, Úy Thanh Diễm; place 198, Phù Tang Cảnh.)
3. **The cup of 900.** The player learns from Đậu Tiễn Xuân's ledgers, or from Tư Không on the night of the cup, that the traveller was
   Phất Y's founder. Tell the valley, or not. Thang Vô Tạ wears the sleeve; Lộ Khinh Trần has the Wind Cô Hạc wants.
   (Tư Không, Đậu Tiễn Xuân, Thang Vô Tạ, Lộ Khinh Trần; places 48, 24, 186.)
4. **The girl with no Metal.** Bốc Tuyết Nha asks Tư Không every morning for what he gave Lăng Đạp Tuyết. If he says
   yes, it will cost her years, and Lăng's secret comes out at the plum gate. (Bốc Tuyết Nha, Tư Không, Lăng Đạp Tuyết,
   Phó Ngạo Sương; places 48, 284, 109.)
5. **Two duels owed.** Sài Mộ Kiếm and Cố Dạ Lan have both been refused for decades. This spring both come to the
   market, and Bốc Tuyết Nha is standing at his table when they do. (Tư Không, Sài Mộ Kiếm, Cố Dạ Lan, Bốc Tuyết Nha; places 66, 874, 48.)
6. **The recipe as written.** Khuyết Vãn Hà, the herb girl of 920, wants to confess the herb. Úy Thanh Diễm needs
   the furnace in A-2 and does not know the pill may have been right. Đồng Hồng Lô wants the same furnace with sixty years
   left. (Khuyết, Úy Thanh Diễm, Đồng Hồng Lô, Phàn Đỗ Nhược; place 328, abode A-2.)
7. **The second flute.** Giản Thu Địch stands at the wall her master died at. She asks the player to come with her
   through Lương's door and play the last three layers while she listens. Something answers. (Giản, Lương Lạc Mai;
   place 386, abode A-5.)
8. **Empty hands.** Nhiễm Hàn Châu finds Hoắc's cave with Hách Tùng's map. The sword holds the unspent bolt. If it falls,
   Lê Hoa Am's bell is the only thing that has ever answered it, and Mạc's lie of 699 comes out with it. (Nhiễm Hàn Châu,
   Hách Tùng, Tiêu Vấn Phong, Thái Lê Tuyết; places 66, 15, abode A-1.)
9. **The sail in the pool.** Đoạn hears it again. Kê Thính Đào and Lận Viễn Phàm want to sail west of Tịch Chiếu Đảo.
   Diệp Tố Quyên's burned painting showed a fog bank there. Cam Thủ Nguyện wants to go too. (Đoạn, Kê Thính Đào, Lận
   Viễn Phàm, Diệp Tố Quyên, Cam Thủ Nguyện; places 37, 196.)
10. **The mirror's price.** Word gets out that a mirror took a Hóa Thần bolt. A Nguyên Anh with years running out comes
    to the pool to buy, borrow or take it. (Đoạn, Kê Thính Đào; place 37.)
11. **Fire on one side.** The cape's vents smoke as the seasons turn, and the realm of fire and snow opens. Hác Tẫn Đăng
    and Ngụy Đan Chu race for Cung Hỏa Vân's abode, and whoever finds it finds out how he died. (Hác, Ngụy Đan Chu;
    places 245, 1080, abode A-4, realm R-2.)
