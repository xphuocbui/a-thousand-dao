# auth-eff-2: Effects d27-d49 (proposals, unpriced)

Wishlist group (d), second half. d26 was dropped by the 2026-11-25 ruling (no stone-burning arts). Records and wiring
are in `auth-eff-2.json`; fight readings follow `duel-readings.md`. Nothing here edits an existing file.

- **d27 Lamp-Ward (Thủ Đăng):** each ward seal turns 1 more blow, +1 per 3 seals up. Carried by the Lamp-Ward Seal
  Canon (on_thu_dang, kien_muc_hai, le_bach_diem).
- **d28 The Earned Rest (Đắc Hưu):** a Marching Dirge finale makes The Held Rest last 0.5 s per Measure (each later finale's rest half as long), with
  Heavy Breath on the foe. Carried by The Monk's Rest (ha_thinh_tung, quan_tich_am, cu_dong_moc).
- **d29 Return to the Scabbard (Quy Sao):** 3 s with no damaging art fired, and the foe counts as unstruck: first-hit
  pieces re-arm, and the next sword art cannot be evaded. This uses the same count as auth-eff-1's d10. Carried by the Rain-Chain Sword Scripture (ton_tuyet_bich), The Undefeated set (co_da_lan) and Grey Sleeve
  and Seal (thang_vo_ta, The Last Step, where it counts as a vanish).
- **d30 Brittle from Its Own Blow (Tự Kích Tự Thúy):** each blocked hit marks the foe with Quench, and Quench on a
  burning foe spends the Burn for more marks. Carried by the Star-Quench Canon (bang_lanh_toi, cat_luu_quang,
  dinh_hoa_lan).
- **d31 True Gold Does Not Fear the Furnace (Chân Kim Bất Phạ Hỏa):** each buff or ward share stripped returns 1.5 %
  linh lực and a Part the Dross stack. Carried by the True Gold Canon (tuong_bach_luyen, phung_xich_kim,
  tuong_ngoc_can).
- **d32 A Crane for the Rider (Hạc Linh):** crane hits give Wind buff stacks, and Crane Descends with no beast calls an
  8 s crane spirit. Carried by the Crane Bond Rite (ung_hac_minh, cu_van_ly, tong_phi_thu). **Refused:** the crane
  king as a standing companion, which is a Spirit Beasts record.
- **d33 Bitter Medicine (Khổ Dược):** under 10 Poison stacks, Poison ticks heal you for half instead of hurting. Carried
  by Hundred Herbs Tasting (nghiem_linh_chi, lu_niem_ha).
- **d34 Charmed (Mê Hoặc):** a new curse, 2 pulses: the foe's arts wait and its basic attacks miss. The charmer's own
  damaging arts are held too, so both sides pause while the poison ticks; a hit of hers that lands ends it; 10-pulse
  lockout. Laid by The Second Cup art, the Tea at the Manor set and Fan of the Gracious Door
  (nghe_hong_dau, lieu_da_thai).
- **d35 Rainfast Ink (Vũ Bất Thoát):** talisman marks and talisman-laid stacks cannot be cleansed. Carried by the
  Rain-Fast Ink Art (han_thuy_mac, ky_ngan_chuc).
- **d36 Birds Pay Court to the Phoenix (Bách Điểu Triều Phượng):** each bird hit lays the plumage colour you lack, and
  a spent full plumage heals you and powers up the birds. Carried by the Five-Virtue Phoenix Canon (vu_ngo_dong). New
  item: the Wutong Twig Ring (Ngô Đồng Chi Hoàn), which gives an_thuy_vu the bird half.
- **d37 When the Thread Breaks (Đoạn Ti):** a slow art or a Displace snaps the thread (linh lực recoil); an unbroken
  thread ties a Knot that adds Wood and Burn stacks. Carried by the Unbroken Thread Canon (than_van_cam,
  tang_mong_kien).
- **d38 Touchstone (Thí Kim Thạch):** once per art firing, a hit strips one buff stack. Carried by the Hallmark Seal
  (tuong_bach_luyen). The name avoids a clash with the Assay art.
- **d39 Drunk (Túy):** a new curse whose stacks each run their own 3 pulses: slower timers, 10 less accuracy a stack, and +10 % Water damage
  taken at 3. Laid by the Hundred Jars set, the Hundred Jars seal, Open the Cask and Last Cup (nhac_thuan_nhuong).
- **d40 Sleep (Hôn Thụy):** a new curse, 3 pulses: the foe's clock stops. The caster's damaging arts and basic attack
  are held while it sleeps; a hit that does land wakes it at 20 % less; 6-pulse lockout. Laid by the Lamp of the Karst Children (sao_an_lan).
- **d41 Banked Healing (Tàng Liệu):** healing is stored while the furnace is unlit (the store decays by a twentieth each second), and pours back at once
  when the furnace lights itself at 40 % health. Carried by The Dusk Field set and the Cold Furnace (diem_tu_to).
- **d42 Horse and Rider Bear It Together (Nhân Mã Đồng Đảm):** the companion takes 15 % of each hit, tires, and rests
  on a joint strike. Carried by the Đạp Vân bridle ring (khau_muc_van). **Refused:** the `companion_health_pct` half,
  because a companion has no health in a fight.
- **d43 Heat the Edge (Tôi Phong):** Fire arts heat the sword to 5 steps of pierce, then a certain crit quenches it as
  Thunder. A formal record of the Fallen Star Forge set (cat_luu_quang).
- **d44 The Sky of a Moment Ago (Tiền Khắc Thiên):** the first time you drop below half, the last 3 s of health and
  linh lực lost come back. A formal record of The Idle Cloud set (diep_to_quyen).
- **d45 A Formation That Walks (Trận Tùy Thân):** your diagram is laid even in an ambush or a blind fight, with fuel
  paid. Carried by The Carried Array set (ve_van_co).
- **d46 Ring Within the Ring (Hoàn Trung Hoàn):** a second ring on a ringed foe Pins it, and both rings deal damage.
  Carried by the Rings of the Armillary set (to_tinh_nghi).
- **d47 The World Before (Tiền Thế):** when the Vase spills, the foe's clock stops for 2 s. A formal record of What the
  Old Man Kept (mac_quy_khu); it needs a new engine action.
- **d48 Thaw (Giải Băng):** each Fire hit sheds one Ice stack from you, heals a little and adds a Fire buff stack.
  Carried by the Thawing Brazier (du_chuc_nhi).
- **d49 The Wind Lifts the Mist (Phong Khởi Vụ Tán):** a foe's Wind art ends your newest mist; a Lantern-Stone takes
  the gust instead. Carried by the Mist-Lock Array Canon and the Road Mist Primer (all of vu_an). The three open
  questions get defaults and stay open.

**New engine pieces asked for:**
- turning a tick taken into healing (d33);
- the rolling "lost in the last N s" readable (d44);
- laying a diagram in ambush and blind fights (d45);
- stopping a side's clock (d47);
- the `mist` tag (d49);
- three new status rows: Charmed, Drunk and Sleep (Charmed and Sleep also hold their caster's damaging arts);
- tags to confirm: `crane` and `bird` on companions, and `talisman` on stacks.

**Wiring:** the conventions follow auth-eff-1. A technique passive is a new passive art (`arts`, add). Replacements
carry their suggested text. The status rows go into the Effects GDD with its amendment, not through the wiring pass.

**Caps:** lifted by the user's ruling (2026-11-25, group 8).
- d28's 6 s: each later finale's rest is now half as long.
- d39's 3 stacks: each Drunk stack now runs its own 3 pulses.
- d41's 30 %: the store now decays by a twentieth each second.

d43's 5 steps stay. They are the quench threshold, which the payoff empties, not a ceiling.
