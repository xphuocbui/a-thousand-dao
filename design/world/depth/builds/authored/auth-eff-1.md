## Flags for the review

- **Needs an engine addition.** d4 needs damage taken to be held back. No action does this today. d14 needs an
  immunity to delays.
- **Conflict with duel-readings 3d.** d20 uses the wish's hit clock. 3d's pressing-stance reading would fill momentum
  on every pulse.
- **Caps lifted** (user ruling 2026-11-25, group 8):
  - d3's 40 %;
  - d5's 30 % store: the Green now decays by a tenth each second;
  - d12's 5 Debts;
  - d15's 3 wards: each standing ward now costs linh lực upkeep;
  - d18's 10-stack consume;
  - d20's 10 momentum: the halving is its close. d2's Know the Way Out is clipped by
  `escape_bonus` unless that cap is lifted.
- **Dropped clauses.** d7 drops "a companion falls" and d17 drops "take each ally's damage", because companions never
  fall and take no damage. d21 changes "breaks a summon" to "breaks a ward".
- **d4 gap.** With exactly two allies (for example muc_han_nhan with his banner planted) neither the Alone clause nor
  the three-ally clause applies. The alternative is to read A File of One as "fewer than three allies".
- **Damage types.** Free "Wood" and "Earth" hits are Physical hits tagged `moc` or `tho` (Combat Damage Types).
- **Tags to confirm.** d1 needs a `fan` tag, which no art carries yet.
- **Catalogue techniques.** d11 and d12 go on `mountain-heart-canon` and `ruin-ledger-scripture`, so every holder of
  those techniques gains them.
