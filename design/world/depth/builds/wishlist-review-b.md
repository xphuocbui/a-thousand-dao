# Build wishlist: review-b (Thiên Xu, Thính Vũ, Phượng Minh, their vassals, and five outside groups)

A build review for "working and fun" (brief `_brief-build-review.md`; format `README.md` here). It covers the tier A
and B builds and each lineage taken as a build family. Nothing here changes a build. These are proposals for the
user's review, and nothing is added to `effects.md`, the Modifier System, the tree or the teaching list.

I tested every build against the fight as `design/gdd/combat.md` runs it: one fighter plus companions, no positions,
no allies (Open Question 27), auto-resolved, with fatigue ending every fight by about 65-90 s. Many lineages in this
batch are written around verbs that fight cannot express: standing still, stepping, crossing a line, allies in
hearing, someone dying nearby. review-a's ra-G1 Duel readings already translates most of them; rb-01 adds the two it
lacks.

I did not repeat W1-W10, the targets wishes, the sets wishes (bt, tl, tp, tx) or review-a's ra-G1 to ra-G4. Where an
entry
adds to one, it says so and gives the new reason.

## Summary: the strongest ideas

1. **rb-21 Voices from the Ground (Địa Thanh).** Thu Phần's chorus gains a voice only when someone dies, so in a duel
   it barely grows. Voices should come from the foe's falling health and from fallen companions.
2. **rb-07 Bronze Answers (Đồng Ứng) and rb-08 Return to the Scabbard (Quy Sao).** Ten of fifteen Thính Vũ holders
   have Metal and no Thunder, so they are locked out of two of their canon's three arts. Their one-cut swords also
   peak in the
   first second of a 60-second fight. Give them a Metal peal, and a sheath that re-arms the first cut.
3. **rb-23 The Culm Stores for Years (Tàng Tiết Kinh Niên).** The Sixty-Year Flower would store its bloom across
   seasons, not seconds. Ninh Trúc Thực has stored for 160 years and never flowered. When he does, it is a regional
   event.
4. **rb-03 The Army Fights, the Marshal Waits (Quân Chiến Soái Đãi), with rb-04.** Make the Army-Breaker Spear's wait
   ignore the strikes of the holder's own wardens and edges. The marshal then stands in his orbit while the army
   grinds, and kills with one thrust. With it, teach the Pivot the Seven Stars and Army-Breaker Wedge diagrams it is
   named for (rb-04).
5. **rb-10 Five Marks from Five Arts (Ngũ Văn Ngũ Pháp) and rb-01 Moving (Hành).** Five-Colour Plumage should need
   five different kinds of art, one per hall of the palace, not five Fire arts. And ra-G1 needs a "Moving" reading, or
   Xuyên Lâm's and Phất Y's whole canons do nothing.

Count: 32 entries (2 batch-wide, 4 Thiên Xu, 3 Thính Vũ, 3 Phượng Minh, 15 for the vassals, 5 for the groups).
Hồng Đậu, Vũ Linh, Lễ Tuyền and the hermits are reviewed and get no entry.

## Batch-wide

### rb-01 Moving and the step out of the centre (Hành / Xuất Xu)
- **Kind**: Other (two more readings), adding to review-a's ra-G1 Duel readings and to sets-bt-01 Stillness and
  sets-bt-04 Displacement.
- **Rule or line**: ra-G1 reads "stand still" (no evade, no movement art for 2 s), "a foe who moves" and "knock back".
  It
  has no reading for the opposite verb, and my batch has two whole lineages built on it. Add:
  - **Moving** (the holder): while a step, dash or footwork art or stance is up, and for 1 s after each evade. "Each
    second you keep moving" counts seconds Moving. The Contract Sword Canon's "each second Moving" counts seconds Moving in which
    you strike nothing.
  - **The step out**: a knock-back, drag or sweep taken (ra-G1's delay) also ends the holder's stand-still for 2 s. A
    lineage that bills a step out of its centre then pays it: The Pivot Does Not Turn clears half its Weights, and
    Fixed Pole resets.
- **Where it would live**: the same place as ra-G1 (depth `schema.md` and the Effects GDD's read list).
- **Fun**: the stance duel. The Pivot elder plants and lays Weights while the Xuyên Lâm staff circles and builds Walk
  Slowly. A crane's dive (Crane Descends) or a reeled thread (Reel In) knocks the Pivot off its centre. Being moved
  becomes a counter you can build for or against (Heavy Keel, Fixed Pole).
- **Who needs it**:
  - Moving: `uc_diep_thanh`, `phi_thoa_y`, `ky_khinh_ma`, `nghiem_quy_khu` (xuyen_lam, Walk Slowly and Rain on the
    Leaves); `thang_vo_ta`, `lam_khe_ngon` (phat_y, Terms).
  - The step out: `doan_ngoc_hanh`, `do_tinh_ha`, `phuong_truc_ngon` (thien_xu); `tham_bac_than`, `to_tinh_nghi`
    (cung_tinh).
  - The pushers who make it matter: `ung_hac_minh` (cuu_cao), `than_van_cam` (chuc_ha).
- **Why**: without "Moving", Xuyên Lâm's canon and Phất Y's Contract Sword Canon do nothing on the timeline. Without "the step
  out", the Pivot's one stated price ("a step out of the orbit costs the Weights already laid") can never be paid, so
  its stance has no counter. ra-G1's ally reading also covers this batch's healers and lamp-keepers (Cốc Vũ's "most
  wounded ally", Lễ Tuyền's mist, Vọng Triều's Set the Lamp, The Flowering), so I add no separate ally wish.

### rb-02 Forewarned (Tiên Giác), one state and different answers
- **Kind**: Other (a shared combat state), with one Effect per lineage.
- **Rule or line**: **Forewarned** means you know the foe's next art (the timeline already knows its timer).
  Lineages then differ in what they do with it:
  - Thính Vũ **cuts it**: a counter-hit when it fires (Where the Drop Falls, Rain on the Withered Lotus).
  - Vũ Linh **goes first**: your next art fires at once (It Rings for You).
  - Củng Tinh and Vọng Triều **ward it** (Read the Water, Read the Swell).
  - The Hall of Echoes **steps aside**: an evade (`nhiep_vo_thanh`).
- **Where it would live**: the combat rules (the state), and each technique's own answer as written.
- **Fun**: the same moment, "I heard it coming", plays four ways. A Tower sword and a Vũ Linh bell-caster no longer
  play alike.
- **Who needs it**: `manh_da_vu`, `ung_thinh_thien`, `nhiep_vo_thanh`, `lu_tan_ha` (thinh_vu); `an_thanh_linh`,
  `thieu_van_linh` (vu_linh); `tham_bac_than` (cung_tinh); `on_thu_dang`, `kien_muc_hai` (vong_trieu);
  `doan_thuong_hai` (hermits-sages).
- **Why**: six methods and four items here say "sees the foe's next art a moment before it comes" in six wordings.
  Examples are Seen in the Water, The Bell Hears First and Heard Through the Roots. They belong to neighbouring sects
  (the Tower and its vassal, the Pivot and its abbey), so with no split they would play the same.

### rb-03 The Army Fights, the Marshal Waits (Quân Chiến Soái Đãi)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**: Hits by your summons, flying edges and formation strikes do not break Momentum or the wait of The
  Called Blow; only your own arts and basic attacks do. While you wait, each of those hits adds Momentum too.
- **Where it would live**: the Army-Breaker Spear (Phá Quân Thương Quyết) as a passive at its last layers, or a Dao
  Quang Hall notable.
- **Fun**: the marshal's fight. He stands in the orbit while wardens and flying edges grind and the War Drum beats.
  Then the one thrust lands, through block and ward, on a foe already heavy with Weights.
- **Who needs it**: `do_tinh_ha`, `ngu_tran_bac`, `nhiep_minh_tien`, `cuu_bat_ngu` (thien_xu).
- **Why**: Đỗ Tinh Hà's main canon orbits flying edges and calls Stone Wardens, and his spear wants "3 s in which you
  strike nothing". As written, the two fight each other, or the rule is ambiguous. The Called Blow's other clause,
  "if it kills, the wait is halved", fires at most once a fight even under ra-G1's kill reading (the foe's first drop to
  25 %). Better: "if it breaks a ward or a summon". The Army-Breaker's Harness set (`tx-breaker-harness`) is the
  closest piece: it pays each second of waiting in Earth stacks, but does not say whether a summon's hit breaks the
  wait.

### rb-04 Teach the Pivot the diagrams it is named for (Truyền Bổn Trận)
- **Kind**: Other (the formation teaching list, `assets/data/sects_teaching.json`).
- **Rule or line**:
  - Add **Seven Stars of the Northern Dipper** (`that_tinh`) to Thiên Xu at elder (truong_lao).
  - Add **Army-Breaker Wedge** (`pha_quan`) to Thiên Xu for the Dao Quang Hall at elder.
  - Add **Seven-String Zither** (`cam_am`) to Phượng Minh's Hall of Rites.
- **Where it would live**: the teaching list; the Formations session owns it.
- **Fun**: the head's Seven Lamps Turn item and the Seven-Star diagram together. The man stands at the pole and the
  seven lamps turn around him.
- **Who needs it**:
  - Seven Stars: `doan_ngoc_hanh`, `thien_thua_cu`.
  - Army-Breaker Wedge: `ngu_tran_bac`, `nhiep_minh_tien`.
  - Seven-String Zither: `lien_thieu_nghi`, `lo_van_khanh` (phuong_minh).
- **Why**: the Pivot is named for the Dipper's first star, its seat array is the Seven-Star Array, and its head
  carries the Seven-Star Array Flag. Yet the list gives `that_tinh` to Băng Tâm, Thối Tinh, Tầm Kiếm and Họa Tinh,
  and not to Thiên Xu. The Pivot holds Phá Quân Môn's spear art, but `pha_quan` goes to Xuyên Lâm, Phần Vũ, Chân
  Kim, Nhạn Trận and Trấn Sa. Phượng Minh's Shao-playing hall has no music diagram, while the weavers of Chức Hà do.

### rb-05 The Pole Draws from the Vein (Xu Cực Dẫn Mạch)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**: While you stand still (ra-G1's reading), each pulse you regenerate linh lực for every 3 Earth stacks
  (Weights) on the foe, with no cap. Stepping out of the orbit cuts the supply along with the Weights.
- **Where it would live**: the Heavenly Pivot True Canon (Thiên Xu Chân Kinh), as a passive from its middle layers.
- **Fun**: an engine. The Weights pay for the seals and the edges, and the seals lay more Weights. A foe who cannot
  move the Pivot watches it get cheaper to run.
- **Who needs it**: `doan_ngoc_hanh`, `phuong_truc_ngon`, `su_ngung_mac`, `thien_thua_cu`, `dang_quan_binh` ("few
  arts, none wasted"), `trong_khac_ky` (thien_xu).
- **Why**: the canon's whole fight is standing still behind arrays, seals and summons, all of them costly. Its only
  linh lực help comes from items (the Compact Inkstone) and the Metal land's Dew Drop and Autumn Dew. Sustain is king,
  and the method has none of its own.

### rb-06 When the Handle Loses Count (Thất Số)
- **Kind**: Effect (a definition the technique already promises).
- **Rule or line**: The Dipper Handle "loses count" when you fire a non-Metal art, or two arts within 1 s, before the
  seventh star. The count then stops until you have stood still for 3 s, when it restarts from the first star.
- **Where it would live**: the Dipper Handle Canon (Đẩu Bính Kinh).
- **Fun**: the master/disciple contrast the cast already writes. `nhiem_thua_nghiep` "rushes the handle and loses the
  count" and builds crit and speed, while `cong_ton_duan`, his master, "does not lose". With a defined rush, the
  impatient disciple really plays worse, and learns.
- **Who needs it**: `nhiem_thua_nghiep`, `hoang_phu_tich`, `cuu_bat_ngu`, `mac_kinh_trap`, `doan_hi_hoa` (thien_xu);
  `loc_bat_quy` (loose-south, the deserter who stopped at layer 7).
- **Why**: the canon says "a disciple who rushes it loses count, and the turning stops for the rest of the fight", but
  "rushing" is undefined. Stopped for the rest of a fight is also harsh enough that every NPC generator would avoid
  the canon.

## Thính Vũ Lâu (thinh_vu)

### rb-07 Bronze Answers (Đồng Ứng), the canon's first-half peal
- **Kind**: Art (or a rule on Answering Peal).
- **Rule or line**: The Metal twin of Answering Peal, for roots without Thunder. Each crit and each counter-cut (Where
  the Drop Falls) adds a Peal. At 3 Peals your next Metal crit fires your last Metal art again at 60 %. The bronze
  rings, but no bolt comes.
- **Where it would live**: the Canon of the Thunder Heard in the Rain (Văn Lôi Kinh), opened only when the holder's
  root has no Thunder.
- **Fun**: the Thunderless elders get their own echo. The sword cuts, the chain rings, and the cut repeats in the
  ringing: the storm without the lightning, which is the head's whole tragedy.
- **Who needs it**: `co_da_lan`, `manh_da_vu`, `quan_vu_thanh`, `phuong_doan_vu`, `nhiep_vo_thanh`, `kieu_man_lau`,
  `tuong_lau_thanh`, `lu_tan_ha`, `hua_thanh_diem`, `ung_thinh_thien` (thinh_vu).
- **Why**: the canon carries Answering Peal and Chain Arc (both Lightning) and Sundering Wedge. Ten of its fifteen
  living tier A and B holders have no Thunder, and the lineage opens Thunder arts "only to roots that formed it". So
  those ten are locked out of two of the canon's three catalogue arts and fight it with Sundering Wedge and Rain Before
  the Peal alone. "A little over half its power" should mean a weaker canon, not a canon of one art.

### rb-08 Return to the Scabbard (Quy Sao)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**: After 3 s in which you strike a foe with nothing, that foe counts as unstruck again. "First hit",
  "first sword hit on each foe" and "No Second Cut" pieces then re-arm, and Where the Drop Falls may mark again.
- **Where it would live**: the Rain-Chain Sword Scripture (Vũ Liên Kiếm Kinh) or the Drawn-Out Edge Canon, as a
  passive.
- **Fun**: the duel becomes a rhythm of draws: cut, step back, sheathe, wait, cut. The swordswoman who "has never lost
  a duel" looks like it.
- **Who needs it**: `co_da_lan` (Dạ Lan's No Second Cut), `ton_tuyet_bich` (thinh_vu); `thang_vo_ta` (phat_y, the
  Final Count and Step Back).
- **Why**: these builds are one cut drawn out until it lands. In a 30-60 s fight they peak in the first second, then
  take "a little less" for the rest. That is the opposite of "waits, then one cut". The Undefeated set
  (`tvl-dalan-suite`, One Cut Drawn Out) is the closest piece: waiting adds crit damage to the next sword art, up to
  5 s. It pays the wait but does not re-arm No Second Cut's first-hit crit and pierce.

### rb-09 Not a Drop Spilled (Bất Lậu Nhất Trích)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**: Each second in which you fire no art fills a bronze cup: your linh lực regenerates faster for each
  cup held, with no cap. Your next art spends the cups and deals more for each.
- **Where it would live**: the Rain-Chain Sword Scripture (the inner gate's method: "qi is led down link by link and
  never spills").
- **Fun**: the patient sword lasts longest. It does nothing for three seconds, then lands one heavy cut that paid for
  itself.
- **Who needs it**: `ton_tuyet_bich`, `dam_van_chung`, `diep_an_thu`, `boc_thu_chung`, `tao_mac_toan`, `co_da_lan`
  (thinh_vu).
- **Why**: below the head (The Night Is Long, the umbrella's Under the Umbrella), nothing in the lineage gives linh
  lực, and the waiting sword is paid for waiting only in damage (Rain Before the Peal). The Founder's Rain-Chain item
  has the cups idea on hits taken, but that is one relic.

## Phượng Minh Cung (phuong_minh)

### rb-10 Five Marks from Five Arts (Ngũ Văn Ngũ Pháp)
- **Kind**: Effect (a rule change to Five-Colour Plumage).
- **Rule or line**: Each colour is laid by a different kind of art, in the virtue's place:
  - virtue (head): a Fire main art;
  - righteousness (wings): a fan art;
  - rites (back): a song art;
  - benevolence (breast): a heal or Wood art;
  - trust (belly): a ward.

  A colour already held does not count again. At five colours the phoenix sings as written. A Fire-only build may
  still fill the plumage, but only by repeating colours (one colour per 2 Fire arts) at half the song's strength.
- **Where it would live**: the Five-Virtue Phoenix Canon (Ngũ Đức Phượng Hoàng Kinh).
- **Fun**: the slot order is a five-part chord, and the five halls of the palace meet in one fighter. The head's build
  wants one art from each hall. The fan duelists can still sing, more slowly.
- **Who needs it**: `hoa_trieu_duong`, `trinh_hi_quang`, `nhiep_tan_sa`, `vu_ngo_dong`, `tang_ke_ti`,
  `lien_thieu_nghi`, `dien_phong_nien`, `lo_van_khanh`, `su_hoanh_duc`, `thich_hong_linh` (phuong_minh).
- **Why**: as written, "each Fire art lays the next colour", so the five colours are only a count to five. The
  Wood-start Halls of Rites and Benevolence can lay plumage only through Furnace Heart, the canon's one Fire art. The
  palace's identity (five virtues, five halls)
  is not in the fight.

### rb-11 The Next Movement (Tấu Tiếp), and Playing Past the Break (Tục Phổ)
- **Kind**: Art (one art with a counter) and an Effect.
- **Rule or line**:
  - **The Next Movement**: each firing plays the next movement in order, with its own small rule. The head's Nine Shao
    plays 1-8; the Remnant Score plays 1-7. A movement skipped (another song art fired between) restarts the piece.
  - **Playing Past the Break**: once a fight, after the seventh movement of the Remnant Score, the player may attempt
    the eighth. A thần hồn and khí vận roll decides it. On a success she gains the Eighth Movement's effect; on a
    failure, a soul backlash on herself.
  - **Dawn Clouds**: Dawn Clouds Take the Colour ends only when a hit gets past your ward or body shield, not on any
    hit.
- **Where it would live**: Nine Shao Phoenix Advent and the Shao Remnant Score.
- **Fun**: the fight is a piece of music the foe can hear coming. The eighth note is a gamble Liên Khánh Dao died
  making.
- **Who needs it**: `hoa_trieu_duong` (Panpipes of the Shao); `lien_thieu_nghi`, `lo_van_khanh` (the Remnant Score);
  `chuc_hieu_nghi` (the Dawn-Call Pipe).
- **Why**:
  - Nine Shao names movements 1, 5, 8 and 9 but has no second, third, fourth, sixth or seventh, and its eighth needs
    eight Shao stacks.
  - The Remnant Score says seven movements are written but carries two arts.
  - "Ends if the player is struck" ends almost at once in a fight where both sides hit every second.
  - One counted art covers the missing movements without authoring seven more. The Seven Movements set
    (`pm-seven-movements`, Never the Eighth) already counts "each movement of the Remnant Score you play", so the
    count is assumed and only the technique lacks it.

### rb-12 Birds Pay Court to the Phoenix (Bách Điểu Triều Phượng)
- **Kind**: Effect (a lineage passive for the Wutong Court).
- **Rule or line**: Each hit your spirit-bird companions land lays the next plumage colour you lack, at most once a
  pulse. Each full plumage you sing heals your companions for a share of their health.
- **Where it would live**: a Wutong Court passive on the Five-Virtue Phoenix Canon. The palace already teaches the
  Myriad Beasts Pay Homage diagram at elder, which fits beside it.
- **Fun**: "his birds fight first, and the fan finishes" becomes an engine. The birds paint the plumage, and the
  phoenix sings it off.
- **Who needs it**: `vu_ngo_dong`, `an_thuy_vu` (phuong_minh).
- **Why**: the palace's bird keepers have only the tree's Beast Pen and Beast-Soul Totem companion stats. Their own
  lineage has no piece that joins birds to plumage. The Wutong Court set (`pm-wutong-court`, The Birds Fight First)
  is the closest piece: bird hits lay Wood buff stacks that fan arts spend. It feeds the fan, not the canon, and only
  for whoever wears it.

### rb-13 Brittle from Its Own Blow (Tự Kích Tự Thúy)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**:
  - Each foe hit you block (the River Tongs' block rating) gives that foe a Quench mark.
  - The Quench art, fired at a foe who carries your burns, spends every 3 burns for 1 extra Quench mark.
- **Where it would live**: the Star-Quench Canon (Thối Tinh Kinh). The first clause could also be the River Tongs'
  line, since Băng Lãnh Tối holds no Iron Hide.
- **Fun**: three smiths, three fights. The head heats and quenches his own blade (sets-tx S2). The son without Fire
  ("the hammer without the fire") counts to the Ninth Blow. The quench-keeper blocks with the founder's tongs and lets
  the
  foe's own blows make it brittle.
- **Who needs it**: `bang_lanh_toi` (first), `cat_luu_quang`, `dinh_hoa_lan` (thoi_tinh).
- **Why**: Bang Lãnh Tối's intent ("he lets a foe's own blows make them brittle") has no piece. sets-tx S2 charges the
  smith's own blade from Fire arts; this is the other side, reading the foe's burns and the foe's blows. W2-metal-marks
  covers only the tree's scaling of the marks.

## Củng Tinh Quán (cung_tinh)

### rb-15 The Twelve Hours (Thập Nhị Thời)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**: The fight's clock is read as hours, one every 5 s. Each ring the holder lays is stamped with the
  hour it was laid in and is strongest during that hour. Rings from different hours lock together: at 3 different
  hours the armillary turns (all rings refresh, as The Handle Turns does), and every further hour adds to the whole
  ring's damage and slow, with no cap.
- **Where it would live**: the Armillary Array Canon (Tuyền Cơ Trận Kinh).
- **Fun**: the abbey's fight is a clock you watch fill. The star-reader lays a ring each hour, and the foe sees the
  armillary close around it.
- **Who needs it**: `tham_bac_than`, `to_tinh_nghi`, `pho_cuong_dau`, `sam_lac_thiet` (cung_tinh); `ve_van_co`
  (market-folk, the abbey's runaway).
- **Why**: the abbey's main method (Fixed Pole: "while the holder has not moved for three seconds") is the Pivot's
  The Pivot Does Not Turn in other words, and Earth stacks gathering is the Pivot's Weights again. The two neighbours
  would play the same. Their arrays are the one piece that can differ, and the canon already says "strongest at the
  hour it was cast" without defining the hour.

- **Rule or line**: S6 makes the crane king answer each attack the rider evades. Add the other half: each crane hit

- **Why**: a new reason beside S6. S6 serves the head and her named king. The other riders' bonded cranes come from

## Hoa Cái Đường (hoa_cai)

### rb-18 Pill Smoke (Đan Yên)
- **Kind**: Art (for any censer or alchemist method).
- **Rule or line**: Burns one prepared pill from your pill slot in the censer. You get the pill's effect at half
  strength, and the foe takes a cloud that deals damage over time by the pill's grade and element. A pill that would
  fail (Ground Like Paint) still makes the smoke.
- **Where it would live**: the Canopy Furnace Canon (Hoa Cái Lô Kinh), and the hermit Purple Smoke Furnace Canon.
- **Fun**: the alchemist fights with her stock. A failed batch is ammunition, and a blind founder fills the room with
  fume.
- **Who needs it**: `la_tu_vi`, `uy_dan_duong` (hoa_cai); `ke_chu_sa` (phuong_minh, "throws burning pill-smoke and
  waits"); `o_thiem_tu` (thinh_vu, "poison clouds through a censer"); `khuyet_van_ha` (hermits-sages);
  `lac_dan_thanh` (market-folk); `phung_xich_kim` (chan_kim).
- **Why**: pills enter a fight only as prepared items (Combat rule 12), and the censer base (`huong_lo`) has no common
  art; it "holds an incense soul art". Seven
  alchemist builds in this batch say they fight through pill-smoke. The closest pieces are sets: Vermilion and Azure
  (`mf-vermilion-azure`: each pill taken lays Fire stacks on foes) and The Sealed Room (`hc-blind-founder`: fume each
  second). The hermit's Smoke over the Burner is an art, but it burns no pill.

## Họa Tinh Đường (hoa_tinh)

### rb-19 Stars on the Foe (Tinh Tại Địch)
- **Kind**: Effect (a rule change to the Seven-Star Talisman).
- **Rule or line**: Each Seven-Star Talisman paints its star as a mark on the target, not as a count on the thrower. A
  foe's cleanse wipes painted stars, unless they were painted under The Rain Does Not Take It. The seventh star on one
  foe detonates the figure.
- **Where it would live**: the Painted Dipper Canon (Họa Đẩu Kinh).
- **Fun**: the Hall's two techniques finally need each other. The figure is fragile, and the rain-fast ink is what
  lets it close.
- **Who needs it**: `ky_ngan_chuc`, `lac_to_binh`, `han_thuy_mac`, `lang_mac_khanh` (hoa_tinh).
- **Why**:
  - The canon counts "each one thrown in the same fight" on the thrower. The Candle and Ink set (`ht-lamp-ink-brush`,
    Seven Stars in One Stroke) already counts the seventh per foe and makes marks uncleansable for 8 s afterwards.
    This moves that reading into the canon for every painter, and makes the cleanse the threat before the seventh
    star, not after.
  - The Pivot's bloc has four sevens: the Dipper Handle's seven Metal arts, the Seven Steps, the Seven Lamps item and
    this one. Counted on the thrower they all play alike; as marks on the foe, the Hall's is the one that can be
    smudged.
  - W6-rainfast-marks is already met by the Rain-Fast Ink Art's own art, so the tree half of that wish is optional.

## Xuyên Lâm Môn (xuyen_lam)

- **Kind**: Effect.

- **Why**: the element-free staff school wins "in a long running fight", but has no linh lực source of
  its own. Its tree route (Willow Dance, Bamboo Joints) is speed.

## Thu Phần Các (thu_phan)

### rb-21 Voices from the Ground (Địa Thanh)
- **Kind**: Effect (a rule change to Add a Voice).
- **Rule or line**: The chorus gains a voice:
  - each time the foe loses 20 % of its health;
  - each time a companion or summon on either side falls.

  (Ground voices from graveyard and battlefield tags were removed by the 2026-11-25 ruling; their autumn doubling waits
  on a season ruling.)

  The canon's cap of 8 voices is lifted (the house default is no caps); Autumn Comes spending the chorus is the bound.
- **Where it would live**: the Canon of the Autumn Graves (Thu Phần Kinh), and the Bell of the Autumn Graves' Toll for
  the Dead.
- **Fun**: the chorus swells as the foe bleeds, so even a duel ends in a choir.
- **Who needs it**: `ly_han_bich`, `bao_thu_xuong`, `co_ky_am`, `tham_tho_bich`, `on_tich_nhai` (thu_phan);
  `ham_tan_chung` (demonic, the soul bell).
- **Why**: Add a Voice fires "when a foe or ally dies within hearing", and the bell tolls "when anyone dies within
  hearing". In a duel the only death ends the fight. ra-G1's kill reading (the foe's first drop to 25 %) gives one voice
  at most, so the canon "weak at the start of a fight and grows as the fight grows deadly" barely grows. grave-chorus covers only
  the tree's soul damage.

## Cốc Vũ Quán (coc_vu)

### rb-22 Water Wears the Stone (Thủy Tích Thạch Xuyên)
- **Kind**: Effect.
- **Rule or line**: Each heal you receive drips a Wood hit on the foe that grows with the Wood buff stacks you hold, at
  most 3 times a pulse.
- **Where it would live**: the Canon of the Good Rain (Hảo Vũ Kinh), as Moistens Without a Sound's second half.
- **Fun**: the soft rain is also the stone's end. The abbess never strikes, and the foe still wears away.
- **Who needs it**: `duong_hao_vu`, `thoi_tri_tiet`, `luc_dao_hoa`, `la_tiem_da` (coc_vu).
- **Why**: the abbey has no damage art (Follows the Wind "slows slightly"). Fatigue takes the same from both sides
  after 60 s (Combat Formula A2), so a build that deals nothing loses to anyone who deals a little: outlasting is not
  a win. Thorn Lullaby has this shape for soul damage. The Grain Rain
  banner's slow start (heals halved for 10 s) is a good moment and needs no change.

## Trúc Thực Viên (truc_thuc)

### rb-23 The Culm Stores for Years (Tàng Tiết Kinh Niên)
- **Kind**: Other (a world state) and an Effect.
- **Rule or line**:
  - Bloom layers are stored outside fights: each season the holder does not flower adds a layer, with no cap.
  - The Flowering spends all of them at once in one fight.
  - Afterwards the holder's Wood arts are sealed for a season, not only for the fight, and an old holder pays a life
    cost.
  - Store the Culm (the stance) stays as the in-fight trickle.
- **Where it would live**: the Sixty-Year Flower (Lục Thập Niên Hoa), with the stored layers kept on the character
  record.
- **Fun**: a once-in-a-life moment. Ninh Trúc Thực has stored for 160 years, about 640 seasons. When he flowers, the
  region remembers it, and his heir finally understands why he was made to wait.
- **Who needs it**: `ninh_truc_thuc`, `man_tiet_thanh` (truc_thuc).
- **Why**: the art says "spends years storing Wood qi" and the cast says "stored for 160 years and never flowered",
  but the in-fight stance gains a layer "every few seconds". Years should mean years. This follows the house rule on
  scaling (no cap, the cost is the bound).

## Phần Vũ Đường (phan_vu)

### rb-24 Into the Vent (Nhập Huyệt)
- **Kind**: Art.
- **Rule or line**: Sets yourself alight: lays your own burns on you. Each tick of a burn you carry, from any source,
  becomes a heat stack, and your healing is increased while you burn.
- **Where it would live**: the Burning Feather Body Canon (Phần Vũ Thể Kinh).
- **Fun**: the brawler who sets himself on fire to get started. His health bar drops on purpose, and the fist that
  follows hits like the vent.
- **Who needs it**: `hung_phan_vu`, `tao_tan_kien`, `trang_hoi_y`, `doi_liet_cuong` (phan_vu).
- **Why**:
  - Feathers Burn turns "Fire damage taken" into heat, and the canon's flesh "mends fastest when it is burning". Against
    a foe with no Fire, nothing burns the holder and the engine never starts. The sets come close for two holders:
    Master of the Sittings (`pvd-sitting-master`) turns blocked hits into heat for `tao_tan_kien`, and Standing in
    the Fire (`pvd-standing-fire`) heals on a broken shield for the head. The canon's own "mends while burning" is
    still unreachable.
  - It would also separate the hall from its liege. Phượng Minh and Phần Vũ between them have four once-a-fight rises
    (Ash Rebirth, Nirvana, Red Wall Rebirth, Stand in the Ash); this gives the vassal a verb that is not one more rise.

## Bách Vũ Các (bach_vu)

### rb-25 Moulted Feathers as Fan Inlays (Thoái Vũ Khảm)
- **Kind**: Item (a material kind and an inlay rule).
- **Rule or line**: Each bird beast drops its own moulted feather. A feather is inlaid in a fan (`phien`) and adds one
  small rule of its kind, for example:
  - crane: evade;
  - luan: a heal on a burn;
  - vent bird: a longer burn.

  The Hundred-Feather Fan passive counts distinct kinds, with no cap.
- **Where it would live**: materials and crafting inlays (which also keeps an inlay sink for linh thạch).
- **Fun**: the collector's hunt, PoE style. The keeper's fan shows every kind she has, and the player chases the one
  bird whose moult is missing.
- **Who needs it**: `ta_thanh_loan`, `ta_thanh_thuoc` (bach_vu). The Phượng Minh fans would also take them.
- **Why**: the passive counts "different feather kinds (by item grade)", but there are no feather kinds, so it reads
  the item's grade.

## Vọng Triều Đường (vong_trieu)

### rb-26 The Lamp Flares (Đăng Bùng)

- **Why**: the lineage's only damage is its outer gate's fists. With fatigue, a ward that never strikes cannot win.

## Ma đạo (demonic)

### rb-28 Move the Sickness (Di Bệnh)
- **Kind**: Art.
- **Rule or line**: Takes your own oldest debuff, status or curse stacks and lays them on the foe as yours, a few
  stacks per firing. At the ninth layer it also moves a wound share.
- **Where it would live**: the Withered Branch Method (Khô Chi Pháp).
- **Fun**: the curser's nightmare. Everything laid on the heretic healer comes back. It also gives a hard counter to
  the batch's many stack-layers (the Pivot's Weights, Mercury, Shao).
- **Who needs it**: `duong_kho_chi` (demonic). It would also fit Giả Hồi Xuân's Draw the Poison, in reverse.
- **Why**: the method's whole idea is moving a sickness into another living thing. It carries only borrowed catalogue
  arts (Blight Canker, Bloodroot Pact), so the build plays as a plain Rotting Canopy poisoner.

### rb-29 Drink Within a Breath (Nhất Tức Ẩm)
- **Kind**: Art.
- **Rule or line**: For 1 s after each crit, the health you leech is also paid to you as linh lực.
- **Where it would live**: the Black Wind Blood Sabre (`black-wind-blood-sabre` in `people/demonic.techniques.json`).
- **Fun**: the robber fights longest when wounded and wounding. Every crit is a drink.
- **Who needs it**: `khoai_thiet_ung` (demonic); `do_liet_co` (loose-south, the other Hắc Phong rogue).
- **Why**: the method's concept is "blood taken from a living foe within a breath of the cut becomes linh lực at
  once", but it carries only borrowed arts (Debt Blade, Shearing Palm). The Black Wind Sabre item (Within a Breath)
  doubles the health leech after a crit and gives no linh lực. The soul pedlar's equivalent need (burning a held
  breath) is already met by the Pedlar's Pack set (`dm-soul-pedlar`, A Breath Bought), so it gets no entry.

## Market folk (market-folk)

### rb-30 Thrown Coin (Phi Tiền)
- **Kind**: Effect (for coin-dart arts and the `tien_tieu` base).
- **Rule or line**: A merchant's coin darts can be laid like a formation diagram. In a foreseen fight the thrower
  burns a fixed fuel of linh thạch, by realm and grade (Formations F2's shape), and the darts run at 100 %. Unpaid,
  they run at the carried share (70 / 50 %). The stones are burned, not priced: they buy no stat.
- **Where it would live**: the `tien_tieu` base's line, or Golden Valley Ledger Canon's arts.
- **Fun**: the richest merchant in the west literally throws money, and the player sees it in the Breakdown's fuel
  line.
- **Who needs it**: `kim_van_ly`, `tu_binh_quyen` (market-folk); `kieu_man_lau` (thinh_vu, "throws coins");
  `vuu_cam_thu` (phuong_minh, coin darts).
- **Why**: four merchant builds fight with thrown coins. The coin base (`tien_tieu`) has a common art, Coin Shot,
  and the Losing Hand set has a "coin art", but no coin spends anything.
- **Risk**: this edges on the 2026-11-14 rule that stones are money only. It follows the formation-fuel precedent (a
  sink, not a price). Drop it if that reads as pricing damage.

## Loose cultivators of the south (loose-south)

### rb-32 Know the Way Out (Thức Lộ), adding to ra-VA1
- **Kind**: Effect, adding to review-a's ra-VA1 Lost on the Road.
- **Rule or line**: Each failed escape adds to your next escape chance, with no cap, until one succeeds.
- **Where it would live**: a Đạo Đồ notable beside the escape notables (Misty Rain, Water Reflection, Cardinal
  Points), or the escape-art records.
- **Fun**: the slippery digger. The player has to kill him fast or watch him go, and a flight is his win.
- **Who needs it**: `khau_bach_thu`, `bien_am_pham` (loose-south); `mau_tri_vien`, `phong_bach_ke` (thien_xu);
  `hua_thanh_diem` (thinh_vu); `hoang_phu_duyen` (market-folk); `nham_do_han` (loose-north).
- **Why**:
  - Seven builds here "fight only to leave". Escape is one roll per art (Combat Formula I, 0.05-0.95) with a realm
    penalty.
  - The pieces that help are all flat or once a fight: `escape_pct` notables (+3-4 each), `evade_pct [escape]` on the
    Envoy's Jade Tally, the Guide's Lamp, and the Digger's Kit's The Way Out.
  - ra-VA1 makes escape certain after five misses, but only inside a mist field. These escapists have none.
  - ra-VA1's open question applies here too: whether a flight counts as success for the caller.
