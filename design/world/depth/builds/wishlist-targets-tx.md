# Build wishlist: targets-tx (Đạo Đồ targets for Thiên Xu, its vassals and four outside groups)

Pieces the batch's builds wanted and the tree does not have. Each person got the closest existing route for now; the
wish key is in their `tree.targets_note`. Proposals for the user's review; nothing here is added to the tree.

## W1-earth-weight: Weighed Foe (Quyền Trọng)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `stack_effect_pct` [tho/debuff] (existing stat, new scope), about the size of the other lands'
  debuff-stack notables (+16-20 %).
- **Rule or line:** +16 % increased effect of your Earth debuff stacks (Weights).
- **Where it would live:** an Earth-land notable near Heavy as a Mountain (3357) or the Millstone idea (E5), so the
  Pivot and the abbey reach it early.
- **Who needs it:** phuong_truc_ngon (thien_xu), to_tinh_nghi (cung_tinh), quan_phuc_thu (market-folk); the whole
  Pivot and Củng Tinh lineage lays Earth stacks (Heavenly Pivot True Canon, Bowing Stars Canon).
- **Why:** the tree scales Wood, Fire, Ice and Thunder debuff stacks (Strangling Vines, Fire Feeds Fire, Frost Seal,
  Coursing Thunder) but no node touches Earth stacks. The routes use Heavy as a Mountain and Millstone (slow damage)
  instead, which helps the slow, not the Weights.

## W2-metal-marks: Quench Mark (Thối Ấn)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `stack_effect_pct` and `stack_duration_pct` [kim/debuff] (existing stats, new scope).
- **Rule or line:** +16 % increased effect and +20 % increased duration of your Metal debuff stacks.
- **Where it would live:** a small Metal-land idea near the Frost Moon / Snow Blade clusters (3887, 3891), or as the
  Star-Quench Canon's lineage notable.
- **Who needs it:** bang_lanh_toi (thoi_tinh: Quench marks); la_tu_vi, uy_dan_duong, thieu_ngan_hong (hoa_cai:
  Mercury stacks from the Nine-Cycle Mercury Art, a Metal art); phung_xich_kim (chan_kim: debuffs that stack).
- **Why:** no node scales Metal debuff stacks. The routes take crit-damage and cold nodes instead, which make the hit
  bigger but not the mark longer.

## W3-assay-strip: Assay (Thí Kim)

- **Kind:** Effect.
- **Rule or line:** once per art firing, a hit on a foe who carries buff stacks removes one of them (the oldest).
- **Where it would live:** the True Gold Canon / Hundred Refinings Art (chan_kim lineage), or a Metal-land notable
  beside Demon-Revealing Mirror (4785).
- **Who needs it:** tuong_bach_luyen (chan_kim), whose build is "strips what is false from a foe".
- **Why:** nothing on the tree removes a foe's buffs; Ward Breaker (3732) only bills wards. His route takes the
  mirrors' thần thức and Hundred Refinements instead.

## W4-seal-scope: Seal Script (Ấn Văn)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `attack_pct` [seal]: needs a `seal` form tag on seal arts (the Nine Seal Ledger's arts count a
  `seal` counter today but carry no tag).
- **Rule or line:** +6 % increased damage of seal arts (and an art-speed twin, +10 % [seal]).
- **Where it would live:** a Metal-land notable pair near the Golden Bell idea (M7), where the judges already go.
- **Who needs it:** phuong_truc_ngon, su_ngung_mac, trong_khac_ky (thien_xu: Nine Seal Ledger holders).
- **Why:** curse nodes (Endless Knot, Water land) and Golden Bell's wards are the closest; neither scales a seal.

## W5-earth-poison-resist: Miasma Lungs (Chướng Phế)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `status_resist_pct` [poison] (existing stat and scope).
- **Rule or line:** +20 % poison resistance, paired with health.
- **Where it would live:** an Earth-land notable near Steady Steps (3379) or Warding Stone (3569).
- **Who needs it:** quy_thu_luy (thien_xu), uy_dan_duong (hoa_cai), phung_xich_kim (chan_kim), khau_bach_thu
  (loose-south): all poison-proof bodies on Earth or Metal starts.
- **Why:** the only poison resistance on the tree is Pine Strength (6169, 6170) in the Wood land, 44 points from the
  Earth start. The routes take general status resistance instead.

## W6-rainfast-marks: Rainfast Ink (Vũ Bất Thoát)

- **Kind:** Effect.
- **Rule or line:** your debuff stacks and talisman marks cannot be removed by a foe's cleanse.
- **Where it would live:** the Rain-Fast Ink Art (hoa_tinh), or a notable in the Water land's Brush and Inkstone idea.
- **Who needs it:** han_thuy_mac, ky_ngan_chuc (hoa_tinh).
- **Why:** stack-duration nodes (Snow Lotus) make marks last longer but do not stop a cleanse.

## W7-metal-curse: Hunter's Snare (Liệp Sách)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `curse_effect_pct` (existing stat), on a Metal-land notable.
- **Rule or line:** +10 % curse effect, paired with accuracy.
- **Where it would live:** the Drawn Bow idea (4207-4259) in the Metal land.
- **Who needs it:** sam_van_nuong (loose-south): Metal curses and slows from range.
- **Why:** curses live in the Water, Wood and Darkness lands; the Metal land's range nodes give accuracy and damage only.

## W8-song-scope: Between the Notes (Âm Gian)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `attack_pct` [song] (existing `song` tag on arts; no tree node scales it).
- **Rule or line:** +6 % increased damage of song arts, paired with soul resistance.
- **Where it would live:** the Clear Gong idea (Metal land) or the Bamboo Flute node (6151, Wood land).
- **Who needs it:** gian_thu_dich (hermits-sages, flute), bach_vong_ngon (thien_xu, zither hidden in the blades),
  chuc_du_am (loose-south, bell songs).
- **Why:** the gong, bell and flute nodes give soul resistance and linh lực, not song damage.

## W9-stone-wardens: Stone Wardens (Thạch Vệ)

- **Kind:** Modifier. `companion_power_pct` and `companion_health_pct` (existing stats) on an Earth- or Metal-land
  notable, optionally scoped to summoned constructs.
- **Rule or line:** +10 % companion power, +15 % companion health, for wardens and constructs a holder calls up.
- **Where it would live:** an Earth-land notable near the Array Eye idea (E8), where the Pivot's war array already goes.
- **Who needs it:** ngu_tran_bac (thien_xu): "summon and formation damage", with the Heavenly Pivot True Canon's
  Stone Warden Call.
- **Why:** companion pieces sit in the Water land (Paper Army, a paper-puppet theme) and the Wood land (Beast Den, Wolf
  Pack, beasts); none fits stone wardens, and both are far from his Metal start.

## W10-health-leech: Blood Drinker (Ẩm Huyết)

- **Kind:** Modifier. A new stat, `leech_khi_huyet_pct` (health leech: a share of hit damage returned as health), the
  health twin of the existing `leech_linh_luc_pct`.
- **Rule or line:** 2-3 % of hit damage dealt returns as health.
- **Where it would live:** a Wood-land notable near Seedling / Taproot, or the Metal land's Vajra Body idea (M4).
- **Who needs it:** khau_thien_tu (loose-north): "Wood's health and leech first", a robber who never had a healer.
- **Why:** the tree has only linh lực leech (34 nodes) and no health leech; his route takes plain health and regeneration.
