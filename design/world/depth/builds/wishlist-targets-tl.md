# Wishlist: targets-tl (Tùng Đào, Lạc Hà and their vassals)

Pieces the Đạo Đồ routes in this batch wanted and could not find. Each person below has the closest existing route
for now, and their `tree.targets_note` names the wish key. Proposals for the user's review; nothing is added to the tree.

## targets-tl/debt-stone

- **Name**: Debt Stone (Trái Thạch)
- **Kind**: Effect
- **Rule or line**: Damage you take in a fight is written down as debt. Once the debt reaches 20 % of your maximum
  health, your next hit deals extra damage equal to a share of the debt (say 25 %), and the debt is cleared.
- **Where it would live**: an Earth-land notable, in a small idea beside Stone Skin or the Bulwark (about d 20 from the
  Earth start), so the Hall of Scales and the Counting House can reach it after the sect route.
- **Who needs it**: `lu_tan_chu`, `tang_van_tieu`, `phuong_toan_ngoc`, `tong_thu_tin`, `tu_binh_hanh` (lac_ha).
- **Why**: Lạc Hà's clerks and judges fight by "letting the foe write the debt, then calling it" (Ruin Ledger
  Scripture). The tree's reprisal pieces are Thorn Hedge (thorns, Wood land, 35+ points from Earth) and Mirror Lake
  (counter, Water land). The Earth land has none, so these routes take plain defence for now.

## targets-tl/wood-talisman

- **Name**: Painted Leaf (Họa Diệp)
- **Kind**: Modifier (existing stats, new placement): `attack_pct [talisman]` and `art_speed_pct [talisman]`, plus one
  line pairing `attack_pct [talisman]` with `status_duration_pct`
- **Rule or line**: 2-3 notables, the same values as the Water land's Brush and Inkstone (6 % talisman damage, 10 %
  talisman art speed).
- **Where it would live**: a small Wood-land idea near the start (d 10-18), beside Binding Vine.
- **Who needs it**: `chau_phi_van`, `ninh_hoa_van`, `ly_khe_thu` (nam_pho); `ta_quy_yen` (lac_ha); `van_han_dang`
  (suong_tung).
- **Why**: Nam Phố Đường and Sương Tùng Các are Wood talisman lineages, but every talisman notable is in the Water
  land (Ink Trace d 19, Brush and Inkstone d 23 from Water, 40+ from Wood). The Wood scribes take Binding Vine and
  Hollow Bamboo now, and the hall-mistress walks across to the Water brush.

## targets-tl/geese-line

- **Name**: Geese Line (Nhạn Hàng)
- **Kind**: Effect
- **Rule or line**: While at least one ally fights beside you, you take 15 % of the damage each ally takes in their
  place, and you gain block rating for each ally standing (capped by the number of allies).
- **Where it would live**: an Earth-land notable near Rampart or Five Peaks (the field and file lineages start on Earth),
  or a keystone for a file commander.
- **Who needs it**: `canh_truong_thien`, `thinh_kinh_han` (lac_ha, the Geese Hall); `muc_han_nhan` (nhan_tran);
  `trang_thiet_cuoc` (tuan_tung).
- **Why**: these builds are "a line of allies stronger than its parts" and "the first share of every blow his file
  takes". The tree has no piece that looks at allies. Rampart, Battlements, Bulwark and Golden Bell are the closest,
  and they guard only the holder.

## targets-tl/metal-stacks

- **Name**: Ringing Bronze (Minh Đồng)
- **Kind**: Modifier (existing stats, new scope): `stack_effect_pct [kim/debuff]` and `stack_duration_pct [kim/debuff]`
- **Rule or line**: 2-3 notables at the Wood land's Binding Vine values (16-20 % Metal debuff stack effect, 20-25 %
  duration).
- **Where it would live**: a Metal-land idea beside Golden Bell (d 14-20 from the Metal start).
- **Who needs it**: `yen_boi_ngoc`, `nguy_tram_am` (minh_loan).
- **Why**: the Singing Luan Bell and Unstruck Bell canons lay Metal stacks on foes who move or strike. The Wood, Fire,
  Water, Ice and Thunder lands have debuff-stack lines, but the Metal land has none, so the bell-casters take Golden
  Bell and the tolls (ward and block) for now.
