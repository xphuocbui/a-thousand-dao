# Wishlist: targets-tp (Thính Vũ, Phượng Minh and their vassals, the demonic group)

Đạo Đồ pieces the builds in this batch wanted and could not reach. Each person took the closest existing route and
names the wish key in `tree.targets_note`. Proposals for the user's review; nothing here is added to the tree.

## rain-chain-conductor

- **Name**: Rain-Chain Conductor (Vũ Liên Dẫn Lôi)
- **Kind**: Modifier. Existing stats `attack_pct` scoped [kim] and [loi], on a new Metal–Thunder border notable pair
  (like Metal and Water, 1475 / Cold Steel, 1477).
- **Rule or line**: `attack_pct 6[kim], attack_pct 6[loi]` on one notable and `resist_pct 150[lightning], can_cot 1.33`
  on its pair. The border would sit about 20 points from the Metal start. Optionally a second pair on a Fire–Thunder
  border near the Fire start.
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ border notables, Metal–Thunder (and Fire–Thunder).
- **Who needs it**: `thach_lien_hoan`, `dam_van_chung`, `ninh_tich_dang` (Thính Vũ); `chu_tri_am` (Phượng Minh, the
  Tower's guest sword).
- **Why**: The Tower's canon is written as Metal first, then Thunder. Its outer primer is Metal or Fire, the two halves
  of Thunder. On the tree, though, the nearest Thunder notable is 46-48 points from the Metal start (Thunder Trident
  8073, Thunder Root 846) and more than 30 from the Fire start. A Metal-start Thunder root wanting "a little Thunder"
  spends about 48 points on empty roads to get one notable. So the three Metal-start members take Metal only for now,
  and `ninh_tich_dang` (Fire start) takes Fire only.

## grave-chorus

- **Name**: Grave Chorus (Quỷ Xướng Hợp Thanh)
- **Kind**: Modifier. Existing stat `x_soul` (soul attack damage) scoped [song], placed in Wood and in Water.
- **Rule or line**: `x_soul 5[song], soul_res_pct 6.6` on a notable about 15-20 points from the Wood start, and the same
  near the Water start, e.g. inside Hollow Bamboo (Wood) and Mirror Lake (Water).
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ notables in el-moc and el-thuy. The alternative is a passive on the Thu Phần and Shao
  lineage techniques.
- **Who needs it**: `bao_thu_xuong`, `tham_tho_bich`, `co_ky_am` (Thu Phần); `lien_thieu_nghi` (Phượng Minh, the
  Shao score); `ham_tan_chung` (demonic, the soul bell).
- **Why**: `x_soul` exists only in Fire (Soul-Burning Lamp 2811 / 2812, d 23), Thunder (Heaven's Rumble 7821) and
  Darkness (Wandering Soul 7247, Soul Ward 7366). The Thu Phần members open from Wood or Water, the Shao score from Wood,
  and the soul bell from Metal. Those starts reach soul *resistance* (Bamboo Flute, Still Pond, Clear Gong) but no soul
  *damage*. So their soul-song arts get no tree support.

## lamp-ward

- **Name**: Lamp-Ward (Thủ Đăng)
- **Kind**: Effect, with a Modifier fallback.
- **Rule or line**: Effect: your ward seals turn 1 more blow before they burn out, and +1 more for every 3 seals you
  hold. Modifier fallback: `ward_efficiency_pct 4, than_thuc_pct 3` on a Water notable about 20 points from the Water
  start.
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ notable in el-thuy (the Effect), or a passive on the Lamp-Ward Seal Canon.
- **Who needs it**: `on_thu_dang`, `kien_muc_hai`, `le_bach_diem` (Vọng Triều).
- **Why**: Vọng Triều's method is seals and wards, but its members are Water-rooted. On Water, `ward_efficiency_pct`
  appears only in the Ice crown (Frozen Ward 7677 and Icicle Crown 7687, d 38). Earth's ward pieces (Battlements 3487,
  Array Keeper 3105) are 45-50 points from the Water start. So the Water keepers take Mirror Lake, Water Film and the
  Star Chart (sight and soul resistance), which are not wards.
