# Build wishlist: targets-bt (Băng Tâm, Thúy Vi and their vassals, the clans)

Proposals for the user's review (format: `README.md` here). Each person below has the closest existing route for now,
and their `tree.targets_note` names the wish key. Keys bt-02, bt-04, bt-05, bt-07 and bt-09 were withdrawn: ward duration (`cast_ward_duration_pct`) and temper stacks (`stack_effect_pct[kim/buff]`) exist on items, and flood momentum, blood-paid arts and steam are technique rules (Spring-Flood Raft Art, Night-Glow Blood Sabre Canon, Water-over-Fire Canon). The remaining keys keep their numbers.

## bt-01 Lingering Green (Lưu Thúy)
- **Kind**: Modifier. New stat `buff_duration_pct` (increased), unscoped or scoped `[moc]`.
- **Rule or line**: Buffs you grant to yourself and your allies last X % longer (haze field heals, Bark Closes, pill
  wards).
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ notable in the Wood land's healing ideas (near Weeping Willow / World Tree).
- **Who needs it**: `thieu_thanh_dai`, `au_duong_tan_di` (thuy_vi).
- **Why**: The tree has `stack_duration_pct` for debuffs, `status_duration_pct` for statuses you inflict, and
  `stack_effect_pct[thuy/buff]` for Water buffs. Nothing makes an ally's buff last longer, and Thieu's intent is
  "healing and buff duration". She has healing and health notables for now.

## bt-03 Blinding Mist (Mê Vụ)
- **Kind**: Modifier. New stat `accuracy_loss_effect_pct` (increased): the accuracy loss you inflict is stronger.
- **Rule or line**: Accuracy loss you lay on foes is X % stronger.
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ notable in the Wood land's Bamboo Grove or the Water Trigram idea.
- **Who needs it**: `ham_thanh_dang` (vu_an). It would also serve Thúy Vi's Haze on the Path users.
- **Why**: The tree's only accuracy stat raises the holder's own `accuracy_pct`. Her intent is "accuracy loss on
  foes". Grove Shade and Bamboo Grove stand in for now.

## bt-06 Opened Veins (Khai Mạch)
- **Kind**: Modifier. New scope `[bleed]` on `stack_effect_pct` / `stack_duration_pct` (a Physical damage-over-time
  stack).
- **Rule or line**: Your bleed stacks deal X % more and last X % longer.
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ notables in the Metal land's sabre ideas (near Ring Sabre / Sweeping Sabre).
- **Who needs it**: `hang_luu_hoa` (da_quang). The Crescent of Red Sand art also bleeds.
- **Why**: Stack lines exist for Ice, Fire, Wood and Lightning debuffs only. Her intent is "the sabre's bleed second".
  She has Fire notables for now.

## bt-08 Hundred Echoes (Bách Hưởng)
- **Kind**: Modifier. Scope `[palm]` on `attack_pct` and `art_speed_pct`, placed outside the Darkness land.
- **Rule or line**: Palm arts deal X % more damage and are X % faster.
- **Where it would live**: Đạo Đồ notables in a Water or Earth small cluster near the body ideas.
- **Who needs it**: `phi_nhan_tam` (trac_anh) and `nong_bach_thach` (van_khoi), both on the Hundred-Echo Palm
  Scripture.
- **Why**: The only palm-scoped node on the tree is Draining Palm (7583) in the Darkness land, far from their Water and
  Earth starts. They have Water sustain and Earth block for now.
