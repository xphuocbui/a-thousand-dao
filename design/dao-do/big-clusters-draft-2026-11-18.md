# Đạo Đồ: big-cluster content (APPROVED 2026-11-18)

**Approved by the user 2026-11-18** ("approve go with recommend"): the list as drafted, with the recommended answers
to the open questions: #14 Portable Cave Abode in the hub (H2); #22 and #23 stay far out; Darkness keeps 5; one
sustain line per land is enough (small and medium clusters may carry more).

Step 4 of the 11-18 plan (`rules-2026-11-18.md` decision 6.5): content first, for big clusters only. Claude drafted
this list; the user approves or edits it before anything is drawn. Built from `design/gdd/dao-do.md` (rule 17's 25
capabilities, the land themes in `tools/design/dao_do_rework/regions/common.py`) and `design/gdd/effects.md`.

**How to read it**
- **80 big clusters** (9+ nodes each, PoE 2's share, 14% of about 570 clusters). Each is drawn **once**, as a picture
  (rules 3.3); small and medium clusters are drawn without content and filled later.
- **Picture** is the silhouette it becomes. All 80 are different, and none is copied from PoE 2.
- **Idea** is the working idea for builds (`builds-are-the-game`): the concept its notables stack. Notables carry
  plain modifiers; anything with a rule is an Effect, hand-designed later and unpriced (`effects.md`).
- **Cap** is the capability (rule 17) on one of its notables (a capability notable, Core Formation minimum, rule 16).
  All 25 are placed, each in the land of the system it touches.
- **Band** is where it sits: inner (v 27-50), middle (50-69), rim (69-84), or the island/hub. Big clusters lean to
  the rim (PoE 2: big 8% -> 12% -> 19% from the inner half outward).
- Names are English with the Hán-Việt; all provisional.

**Count:** 5 lands x 10 = 50; blends 11 (two each, three in Water-Wood with Wind's land); hub 4; Darkness 5; Light 4;
Thunder 4; Shadow Shrine 2. Total **80**.
