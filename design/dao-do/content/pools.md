# Đạo Đồ content: land pools (method §4.7)

Hand-written stat lines (stat [scope]) each land's medium and small clusters draw their theme from. Weight: ●●● common,
●● regular, ● occasional. Each land's talent (its road's old attribute, `common.py`) is its staple.
A medium cluster takes a line that supports the nearest big picture without repeating that picture's main line; a
small cluster takes one line, and neighbouring copies of one drawing take different lines.
