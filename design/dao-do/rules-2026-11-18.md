## 2. Why every session converged on the same tree (do not repeat)

1. **The shape library came first,** then the space was filled with it. 53 fixed drawings were stamped across 1,026 figures, and the 10 commonest covered 80%.
2. **Scanners ranked the candidates,** and the compact round shapes that fit most places won. "Hand-picked from a ranked list" turned into "the script picked".
3. **The gaps between roads are all about the same size,** so the figures filling them came out the same size.
4. **Shapes were drawn empty.** Nothing tied a shape to what it grants, so nothing pushed shapes apart.
5. **The judging was density and interlock** against PoE, never variety.
6. **The old rulebook contradicted itself.** It said "each shape at most 3 times in a land", and also "a few simple motifs repeated into a texture beat a collection of different drawings". Fire ended with 26 cups.

**Not the cause:** cluster rules R2, R3 and R4. PoE 2 obeys them almost perfectly (section 5).

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 How we work (process, not layout)

1. **Hand-place everything.** Scripts may only compile, check, render and report. They never choose or rank placements.
2. **No ranked candidate lists to pick from.** The old `hubscan2`, `groupscan`, `hostscan` and `joinscan` are retired. A fit check of one placement the author already chose is fine.
3. **Judge by eye, from full-resolution screenshots** beside the same PoE 2 crop. Use 18, 36 and 72 unit windows, at 20 or more pixels per unit for 72 units.
4. **Look for variety first, then density.** The user gives the verdict; Claude's reads are not verdicts.
5. **No commits unless the user asks.** Then commit only your own paths: `git commit -- <paths>`.
6. **The PoE 2 data is never committed.** It is third-party. Read it only with `python -I`.
7. **Ask before any road change** beyond what the user approved.

### 3.2 Hard rules (PoE 2 follows them; checked by the compile)

- **Cluster rules** (`tools/design/dao_do_rework/cluster_rules.py`):
  - **R0:** joined.
  - **R2:** no dead-end minor node; a leaf must be a notable.
  - **R3:** the first notable is 2+ steps past where the figure opens.
  - **R4:** at least one notable.
  - R2 and R3 are strict, with no exceptions (user, 2026-11-18), although PoE 2 breaks them 4-8% of the time.
  - R1 (mirror) stays removed.
- **Spacing, measured per node (user, 2026-11-18: "nodes/edges not from the same cluster").** Every gap is
  from one node to the nodes and links around it. A road node counts as its own cluster. A node it links to
  directly, and the links that touch it, never count.
  - **Other clusters and roads:**
    - a node at least **0.9** from any node of another cluster or a road;
    - a node at least **0.6** from any link of another cluster or a road (a link with neither end in its own cluster).
  - **Its own cluster:**
    - nodes at least **0.7** apart;
    - a node at least **0.45** from its own cluster's links, including the cluster's link to its road.
  - **Exception, the Sun island (cell 931), user 2026-11-19:** traced from a picture drawn finer than the rules,
    so its spacing is looser: 0.6 to other nodes and roads, 0.35 to other links and roads, 0.5 inside a cluster,
    0.3 to its own links. It applies whenever a Sun node is one end of the measurement. Everywhere else is unchanged.
  - No crossings.
  - PoE 2, measured per node 2026-11-18 (5th percentile / median; share closer than the rule):
    - other node 0.91 / 1.71 (4.6% under 0.9);
    - other link 0.71 / 1.32 (1.7% under 0.6);
    - own node 0.76 / 1.06 (about 3% under 0.7);
    - own link 0.62 / 1.00 (0.3% under 0.45).
  - This replaces "nodes of different clusters 0.95 apart" and "a node 0.3 from any link". The old wording
    was read at cluster level, and it let a neighbour's link pass 0.3 from a node, which PoE 2 never does.
- **Keystones (as PoE 2):**
  - alone: one link, to a road node, never into a cluster;
  - no halo: other nodes keep only the normal 0.95 spacing (user, 2026-11-18; PoE 2 median 1.51, min 0.99);
  - in the outer half of the tree;
  - far from the starts.

### 3.3 Cluster tiers (new)

| Tier | Size | Role | Reuse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 1-5 nodes | the texture | Families repeat freely; each copy is drawn fresh (3.4). |
| Medium | 6-8 nodes | variety | Most are drawn once; a drawing appears at most three times in the whole tree (PoE 2: 160 once, 22 twice, 9 three times). |
| Big | 9+ nodes | the pictures | Each is drawn once in the whole tree, as a picture (PoE 2: a sword, skull, four-point star, snowflake, long river). Most hold nodes inside their outline, joined into the one cluster. |

**Size mix, form mix and copy counts are reported targets, not quotas** (user, 2026-11-18): the compile reports them and the eye decides.

**Size mix:**
- about half of all clusters at 3-5 nodes;
- about a third at 6-8 nodes;
- about a seventh at 9+ nodes (PoE 2: 14%).

**Gradient:** more small clusters toward the centre, more big ones toward the rim.

**Form mix (reference, not a quota):**
- about 40% chains, mostly a curved hook ending in a notable;
- about 35% single loops;
- at most 15% multi-loops (wheels, spoked rings);
- about 10% branches.

### 3.4 How a small cluster is drawn

One small drawing = family node positions + link style + hand + turn + entry + link length. Vary these between copies.

- **Link style.** The same node positions, linked differently, read as different figures:
  - arcs bowing out;
  - arcs bowing in (cusps);
  - straight;
  - open or closed.
  PoE 2 has 3-5 link styles per common drawing.
- **Hand.** Mirror the copies roughly half and half.
- **Turn.** The cluster faces any direction. It is not always pointing outward.
- **Entry.** The angle at which the road link enters the cluster: about half straight, the rest turned 15-90°, each turn chosen for a reason (into open space, away from a neighbour), never at random. This applies to every tier (user, 2026-11-18). PoE 2, straight / 15-45° / 45-90° / 90°+: small 47/30/19/4%, medium 52/32/15/2%, big 33/38/21/8%.
- **Link length.** From about 1.0 to 3.7 units, varied.

### 3.5 Spacing of copies and neighbourhoods

- **Twins far apart.** Two copies of one drawing sit about 6+ units apart; PoE 2's median is 13. A closer pair is allowed only as a deliberate pair, usually on one road node (PoE 2: 26% of copies are closer than 6; two thirds of those share a road node).
- **Varied neighbourhoods.** Within about 6 units of a cluster, most clusters are different drawings.
- **Hubs.** A road node carrying several clusters carries different drawings.

### 3.6 Connection, nesting and combos

- **No blobs.** Joins and hung figures count toward the cluster they join, so a joined group must stay within its tier. Joins go mainly to roads; figure-to-figure joins only on purpose. The 11-18 tree had a 173-node blob; PoE 2's largest cluster is 43.
- **Nesting** is only:
  - a figure inside a big cluster, as part of it;
  - small clusters inside the cells of a road web.
  Never drop a separate figure inside another.
- **Combos** are interlocked figures, as in `reference/img*.png`. They are still wanted. Build them from different drawings, never from two copies of one medium or big shape.

### 3.10 Cells (user, 2026-11-18)

**The tree is drawn cell by cell, not land by land.** The roads are laid out first so that they make good cells;
then each cell is filled. Measured on PoE 2 with PoE 1 as a check, on cells the user corrected by eye
(viewer: https://claude.ai/artifact/HE5yxPUfSz6yg7gudjguQQ; script: `tools/design/dao_do_rework/cells/cells2.py`). Ranges are 10th-90th percentile unless marked.

**A. How a cell is counted**
1. **Walls** are road nodes, starts and jewel sockets, and clusters that link two road nodes (2 exits).
2. **Junction figures are walls.** A cluster with 3+ exits where a road ends at it (as our emblems) divides the
   cells round it.
3. **Hub figures are content.** A cluster with 3+ exits sitting inside a cell (every exit on a closed wall, a start,
   or a short road looping back to it) never splits the cell with its arms.
4. **A cluster's own loops belong to its cell,** also when they touch a start.
5. **A small pocket** between a cluster and its road (under 25 units², at most 2 road nodes, no start) belongs to the
   cell across the cluster.
6. **A start's link is always a wall,** so start areas stay their own cells.
7. **Gaps under about 20 units² are leftovers,** not cells to fill (a third of PoE 2's faces, median 10).
8. **Curves are read about 1 unit out from a node.** GGG lets some arcs graze a straight road inside the node icon.

**B. Rules for creating cells**

*Layout*
1. **Rings and spokes.** About 3 rings (PoE 2: at about 35%, 60% and 80% of the radius) with spokes between them;
   about half the road length runs round the tree, about 30% outward. Most cells are ring segments.
2. **Road links 2-6 units long,** median 3.8.
3. **The rim stays open.** About 15-18% of clusters hang outside the outermost wall (PoE 2 84 of 560).
4. **Starts get their own cells, big and sparse** (PoE 2's start star 479 units² with 2 clusters; PoE 1's class
   rooms 350-400 with 1-4).

*Size and shape*
5. **Cells grow outward.** Filled cells' median area: 52 in the inner third, 98 in the middle, 136 in the outer;
   overall 21-188, median 100. Outside the start areas no filled cell is over about 290.
6. **Neighbours differ in size:** median area ratio 2.0 (both trees), a quarter differ 4-5x or more; about 4
   neighbours per cell.
7. **Compact, not round:** roundness (4πA/P²) about 0.6 (0.44-0.74); no long slivers as real cells.

*Walls*

8. **About 60% of a wall's length is road** (PoE 2 63%, PoE 1 72%); the rest is clusters linking two road nodes,
   median 1 per cell (0-2).
9. **A wall carries a median of 8 road nodes** (4-12).

*Contents*

10. **Capacity about 19 units² per cluster** (0.28 nodes per unit²; PoE 1 sparser at 34). A cell holds a median of
    5 clusters and 26 nodes: 1-3 in the inner third, 7-9 in the outer.
11. **Contents hang only from the wall's road nodes** (100% in both trees, never from a wall cluster); about 0.6
    clusters per road node.
12. **Mix inside a cell:** about 60% small, 33% medium, 7% big; 30% of cells hold one 9+ cluster, 5% hold two.
13. **Some cells are one hub figure** (12 of PoE 2's 75 filled cells): its arms reach the wall, its loops inside.

*Symmetry (user, 2026-11-18: "cells in poe are slightly symmetric"; option 1 chosen)*

14. **Each land is visibly symmetric across its own axis: about 70-80% of its road nodes mirrored** (user,
    2026-11-18; a Wood proposal at 22% "twin cells" was rejected: "that doesn't look symmetric at all").
    - Measure it by **road nodes with a mirror partner of the same kind within 0.6**, plus the screenshot. The earlier
      twin-cell test (centre within 2.5, area within 1.35x) passes cells that look nothing alike; do not use it.
    - PoE 2: 55% of road nodes and 50% of cluster nodes mirrored across their class area's axis. PoE 1: 34% / 32%
      (it mirrors left-right across the whole tree instead, 73% of cells). Ours stripped: 49% overall, Wood 41%.
    - About a quarter of each land stays deliberately different, so it does not read as a stamp.
    - **Open:** whether figures mirror too (PoE 2 mirrors half its cluster nodes; the 11-18 decision unmirrored ours).
    - `tools/design/dao_do_rework/proposal/apply.py` takes `"mirror": <land axis>` on an add and places its reflection; both ends must land on nodes.

*Layers (user, 2026-11-18: "poe 2 clusters have layers for the inner ring, middle ring, and outer ring")*

15. **Three ring layers.** PoE 2's roads alone close three rings (rays from the centre cross a road at 100% of
    angles in each band; radius p10/50/90: inner 18/21/25, middle 37/39/41, outer 51/52/53 on a radius of 63).
    - Ours (stripped, counting figures as walls): the start road and middle road already close as pentagons through
      the emblems (inner 100%, radius 27/31/34; middle 98%, radius 43/49/58), but only 59-68% of angles cross a road,
      and the middle ring swings 15 units where PoE 2's swings 7. The outer band (62-80) is closed only 74% of the way.
    - Approved 2026-11-18: add an outer ring at about PoE 2's 80% (judged by band width against the middle ring); keep
      the rim ring at 84 and leave the band outside the outer ring uncut; spokes between rings mirrored per land.
    - Open: rounding the start and middle pentagons (changes A2 roads; the user's call).

**C. How a cell is filled (user, 2026-11-18; worked out on Fire cell 14 over 8 passes, approved: "yup works")**

1. **Centre figure first, drawn on orbits.** Each cell gets one main figure, built on concentric circles round one
   centre, before anything else (combos first, 3.6). Cell 14's: a ring of 8 (two notables) bridging a top and a bottom
   road node, held between two arcs on an outer orbit (radius 3.5 against the ring's 1.9), notables at the arc tips;
   three clusters on four road nodes that read as one figure ("the combo in the middle is tasteful").
2. **Never the same composition twice (user: "don't be repetitive, be creative").** Each cell gets its own kind of
   centre figure and its own layout. Cell 14's ring-between-arcs is used there only. Ways to vary it:
   - the figure: a wheel, a spiral, a bridge across a road, a fan off one hub, two figures facing each other, a big
     picture (3.3), a hub figure filling the cell (B13), or no centre figure at all in a quiet cell;
   - its place: centred, off to one side, hung from a corner, or straddling the wall into the next cell;
   - the fill around it: different families and densities from the neighbouring cells.
3. **The fill comes from the family catalogue** (`design/dao-do/families/`), never pieces made up on the spot. The
   made-up hooks, forks and wall-glued arches of passes 1-6 were "so bad". Each copy picks its own link style, hand,
   turn and stem (3.4).
4. **One stem, one road node.** A small piece hangs from one road node on a stem of about 1.0-2.0 and faces open space
   (PoE 2: 85% have one link out). It is not glued to the wall at both ends.
5. **Road nodes are hubs.** Most wall road nodes carry a piece and some carry two (cell 14: 11 of 14 road nodes used,
   3 with two pieces).
6. **About PoE 2's density.** Cell 14 holds 63 nodes in 206 units² (0.31 per unit²; the PoE 2 cell beside it 66 in 176,
   0.37). A calm 41-node version was too sparse ("how is our cell bigger, but has so much fewer nodes").
7. **Notables of different pieces do not clump.** The 0.9 spacing rule passes two notables side by side; keep them
   about 1.5 apart by eye.
8. **No accidental pairs.** Neighbouring road nodes carry different families, and no two neighbours read as twins
   unless meant (cell 14's Diamond and Hook did; the Hook became a Crescent curving away).
9. **Method.** Place by hand in (r, θ); compile onto a copy of the map; check 3.2 spacing, R0-R4 and crossings; render
   at 32.3 px per unit beside a PoE 2 cell of the same size; judge by eye. When a piece fails, try a few directions,
   keep those that pass, and choose one by eye.
10. **Not by image models or illustrations.** Image-model fills (2026-11-18) scattered pieces, joined roads between
    road nodes and left dead ends; traced illustrations were "too much eye candy". Both rejected.

## 4. The PoE 2 graph

- **Data:** GGG's own export, `grindinggear/poe2-skilltree-export`, version 0.5.5, at `design/dao-do/reference/poe2/data.json`. It is untracked; never commit it.
- **Loader:** `tools/design/dao_do_rework/tree_view/trees.py`, function `load_poe2`. It drops ascendancy nodes, masteries, blighted nodes and proxies, and scales PoE 2 so its node stride matches our 1.2 (about 0.0048).
  - Kinds: `travel` = attribute node (PoE 2's roads), `small`, `notable`, `keystone`, `jewel`, `start`.
  - Every curved link carries GGG's own arc centre.
- **Rendering:**
  - `tree_view/build.py` builds the zoomable page: `--poe2 <data.json> --ours <map.json> [--rot DEG]`.
  - `tree_view/shot.py` takes screenshots.
- **Live artifact:** https://claude.ai/artifact/JiqzrBTubbEdBCqgLShsbM, with PoE 2 and our stages as tabs.
- **Counting clusters:** a cluster is a connected component of small, notable and jewel nodes once the roads, starts and keystones are removed. Never use the data's `group` field; it splits clusters.
- **Same drawing:** node kinds plus sorted pairwise distances in 0.3-0.8 unit buckets, so any turn or mirror counts as the same drawing. Quote results as a range over the buckets.

### 5.3 PoE 2's small clusters (1-5 nodes)

**How common:**

- **Twins.** The nearest copy of the same drawing is 2.4 / 13.2 units away (10th percentile / median).

## 7. Tools kept

  `python -I tools/design/dao_do_rework/skeleton/layout.py design/dao-do/layout-hand/*.json out.json`

  - The base roads, starts and emblems come from the skeleton; everything else comes from the layout files.

  - Pieces: `{"piece","at","facing","spoke","host"?,"bend"?,"tilt"?,"mirror"?}`

  - Roads: `{"road"}`, `{"arc"}`

- **Frames:** lands and blends use u across and v = distance from the map centre. Discs are centred on the disc.
