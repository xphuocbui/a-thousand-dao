**Generated** by `tools/design/dao_do_rework/content/ideas_report.py --write` from `content/src/ideas.py`; edit the
data, not this file.

**What this is:** step A of `content-method.md`: every big picture (9+ nodes) of the frozen v5 graph
(`cells/versions/v5-frozen-2026-11-19/`) with the build idea its notables stack and the stat lines they carry.
Option A of `cluster-audit-v5-2026-11-19.md` (user, 2026-11-19): one idea per big picture, the 80 approved ideas of
`big-clusters-draft-2026-11-18.md` bound onto v5's pictures and new ideas (★) for the rest. An idea only has to fit
its land. Fire's ideas were approved by the user on 2026-11-19; the rest follows the approved method, for the user's
one review when the whole tree is filled. Calls made on the user's behalf: `content-decisions.md`.

**How to read it**
- **Where:** cell, the piece's distance from the centre (inner < 50, middle 50-69, rim 69 and out) and its angle off
  the land's axis (the side it leans to).
- **Picture:** the drawn piece; **pair** = the same picture on both sides of the axis, one idea on both copies.
- **Lines:** the plain stats its nodes stack (Modifier System Stat List), scopes in brackets: element art tags
  kim / moc / thuy / hoa / tho (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth), variants bang / phong / loi (Ice, Wind, Thunder),
  damage types physical, force, fire, cold, lightning, light, chaos; `debuff` / `buff` for element stacks. Small
  nodes carry the first two lines; notables pair them with the others. Numbers come later (step C), from Formulas C.
- **Capability · Effect · pending:** the capability on one of its notables (tier from Formulas E), the rule part
  that becomes the picture's one Effect, and stats waiting for their key (choice 2a).
