## How a modifier line is priced

`W = |value| × price ÷ breadth(scope)`. Breadth: no scope 1.0; one broad tag 1.25; one family tag 1.5; one narrow
tag 2.0; two tags: one step narrower than the narrower of the two when the second is family or narrower (two
family tags = 2.0). A reduction on a lower-is-better key (art cost, thần thức cost) is the bonus.

## Scope tags (Modifier vocabulary)

- **broad** (÷1.25): art, cultivator, force, hit, physical, soul
- **family** (÷1.5): artifact, basic, beast, body_gear, buff, chaos, cheap, cold, companion, costly, creature, curse, debuff, fire, formation, hoa, kim, light, lightning, moc, over_time, palm, quick, seal, secluded, slow, sword, talisman, tho, thuy, travelling, utility, ward, ward_gear, weapon
- **narrow** (÷2.0): am, bang, body_shield, cleanse, escape, hon_don, lock, loi, phong, pierce, poison, quang

Element tags are `kim moc thuy hoa tho` and the variant ids `phong loi am hon_don quang bang`; damage types
`fire cold lightning light chaos physical force soul`; element stacks are scoped `[<element>, buff]` or
`[<element>, debuff]`.
