# What the builds asked the body family for

> From the Missing pieces of design/builds/engine-v3/*.md (2026-11-12/13).

- (art-thief) A palm-scoped treasure that adds Drink or counts toward Answering Palm's third palm: body.
- (bare-fist) A technique that drives the basic punch (Stored Breath, basic speed and coefficient): the Uncarved Stone Fist has no library twin; the body family should own an item or Effect for it.
- (burial-shroud) A body-guard or pierce modifier piece (the old build's body guard 40 and pierce 30): Body family.
- (deity-warden) A ward item that pays Physical and drains (the Iron Gate's per-hit ward): the body family.
- (mirror-hide) A Return-channel Effect (a free Physical hit scaled by the damage stopped, with a cap): body family, ring or armour.
- (mirror-hide) A health-payment art for the build, so Debt of Stone has something to read: body family.
- (ninth-bell) A ward item that beats the build's own ward: a family-neutral one from general or body.
- (starter-wall) An armour ward above the starter drain ward for a body-and-Earth wall: owned by the body family.
- (unique-mountain-splitting-axe) A body shield that the fight can hold at 30 % at the start of a fight, so the axe's gate is open without the stance: the body family should own a piece for it.
- (unique-mountain-splitting-axe) A Debt source that does not need hits: the body family (Marrow Tithe Strike is the only one, and it costs a slot).
- (unique-vajra-hide-armour) A fifth art slot's worth of canon arts: Marrow Tithe Strike, Last Breath Fist and Bulwark Knuckle already fill three; a Debt-reading art from the body family would give the Last Marrow a second user.
