# What the builds asked the wood family for

> From the Missing pieces of design/builds/engine-v3/*.md (2026-11-12/13).

- (beast-carry) A companion's weapon or collar giving companion damage, companion speed and a companion hit filter (Sure Bite, Marked Prey on companion hits): wood family, or `general`.
- (beast-carry) A beast-ward item, a shield or redirect that protects the companion's health bar (the Shared-Heart Contract's Down and Rouse): wood family.
- (beast-carry) An item Effect that feeds the beast's 0.5 s timer from art speed (Bonded Spirit): wood family.
- (starter-venom) A Wood or Chaos ring that adds Poison stacks laid per art, not duration only (Wood family).
- (starter-venom) A necklace that turns Poison ticks into healing or linh lực for the wearer, the keystone leech the guide lists (Wood family).
- (starter-venom) A light armour for the Wood poisoner with a cleanse-immunity window (Wood family; the guide's Moss-Ledger).
- (starter-venom) A grade 3 Wood treasure of at most 15 thần thức, so that Cycle Seal and Bulwark are not forced to fill the limit (Wood family).
- (status-collector) A Wood-family item that lays Poison and Stagnation (so Blight Canker need not give up a slot): Wood.
- (two-clocks) A Wood necklace with modifiers (Poison duration and effect): wood family.
- (unique-chaos-last-bargain-locket) A way to be low on health without dying, so a Down-triggered clause has something to cash: Wood (health-pay arts) or the Chaos family.
- (unkillable) An item that turns health overflow into Poison (Surplus Life), owned by the wood family.
- (unkillable) A cleansing item for Shedding Bark, owned by the wood family.
