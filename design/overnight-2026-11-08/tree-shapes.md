# Meridian Map (Kinh Mạch Đồ) Redesign: Three Shapes

> Written overnight on 2026-11-08, for the redesign that follows the Combat revision. **Nothing was changed.** All counts are proposals.
> Read: kinh-mach-do.md (Summary, Overview, rules 1–4, 16–17, 20–21, Formulas A–D), the systems-index row for #10, modifiers.md rules 11–13, effects.md rules 4, 13–14 and the Starter Effects.
> **Not found:** the user's words about the wheel are not in systems-index.md. The index says only "not adopted"; the rejection is in project memory.

> **User answers 2026-11-08 (supersede this brief's recommendation):**
> 1. Liked all of: small clusters, long roads, the organic layout, hidden rim clusters. *"I actually prefer the
>    old one overall layout more. It looks more PoE like, the only thing i do not like, is that it is not as good
>    as PoE."* So the redesign **improves today's layout** toward PoE quality; none of Shapes A–C is adopted.
> 2. The **spiritual root picks the start** (PoE-style starts; touches Spiritual Roots and NPC Builds).
> 3. Capabilities: **mix**. Slot raises become keystones with a real closing cost; closeless ones become major Effects.
> 4. Effect budget: **no cap** on tree keystones or Effects per build ("We will balance this out later").

## The result

**I recommend Shape A, the Body Map (Nhân Thân Đồ).** It is the only shape that builds the layout from the tree's own Player Fantasy ("you read the map the way a cultivator reads their own body"), and it keeps the most of today's tree. Shape B maps the breadth tiers most exactly, but it is a grid, and a grid risks reading like a diagram, which may be what sank the wheel. **First ask what the user liked about the old shape** (project memory says to), and show pictures of A and C before writing rules.

**Every shape must hold:**
- **Breadth:** mostly universal and broad, and **no `more`** (Modifiers rules 11–12).
- **Slot raises:** rare modifiers go **on keystones only**, never on an acupoint or a notable (rule 13).
- **Effect tiers:** minor, major and keystone. A keystone states what it closes (Effects rules 4, 14).
- **Capabilities:** of the 25, ~13 become keystones; ~12 close nothing and still have no place.
- **Points:** 51 by the end of Qi Refining (Luyện Khí), the most common life end. So ~50 points must already make a build.

## Shape A: the Body Map (Nhân Thân Đồ)

```
                    * Crown (Bách Hội)
              [ UPPER DANTIAN ]  soul, divine sense
   *--hand==arm===+      |      +===arm==hand--*
   sword, crit    [ MIDDLE DANTIAN ]   palm, ward, talisman
               Lung/Kim    Heart/Hỏa
           Liver/Mộc  Spleen/Thổ  Kidney/Thủy
      ======= Belt Vessel (Đới Mạch): broad ring =======
                  [ LOWER DANTIAN ]  <- start
                   /              \
          leg: escape, tempo   leg: body, endurance
                 *                   *
   front spine (Nhâm Mạch): sustain | back spine (Đốc Mạch): body, health
```

**How it reads.** It is your body. You start in the lower dantian (Đan Điền), walk to your element's organ, then out along a limb, which is your style. Climbing the spine is the realm journey: the lower dantian in Qi Refining, the middle one in Foundation Establishment and Core Formation, the upper one in Nascent Soul.

| Breadth | Where |
|---|---|
| Universal | The two spines; the rings around the three dantians |
| Broad | The Belt Vessel ring (physical, force, hit, art) |
| Family | The five organs (element) and the limbs (form) |
| Narrow | Hands, feet and crown, next to the keystones |

**Slots:**
- **13 keystones:** the ends of the 12 regular meridians (fingers and toes) plus the crown.
- **28 majors:** 2 per organ, 2 per dantian, and 12 on sealed joint clusters (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees) for the closeless capabilities.
- **~60 minors** on the notables.

**Root and elements.** The organs are equally far from the start, and purity scales them as today. A two-element root finds its organs side by side. Variants keep rule 20: innate ones off the lower dantian, a transmutation off its organ, a convergence joining two organs.

**Build families:**
- **Sword and crit:** the right arm.
- **Palm, ward and talisman:** the left arm.
- **Body cultivators (thể tu):** the back spine and a leg.
- **Escape and fortune:** the other leg.
- **Soul:** the upper dantian.
- **Sustain and companion:** the front spine.
- **Dark path (ma đạo):** a hidden "reversed meridian" (nghịch mạch).

**Risks:**
- Soul sits high, so it comes late in a 51-point life. It needs an early path on the front spine.
- The two arms invite mirrored content.
- An upright silhouette wastes space, against rule 21's density.
- A body has no rim, so growth (rule 19) needs an outer aura band.

**Keeps:** the dantian start, purity, depth (as the climb), hidden clusters, variant placement by origin, and the point rules.

## Shape B: the Weave (Kinh Vĩ Đồ)

*Kinh* is the warp and *vĩ* the weft; *kinh vĩ* means longitude and latitude.

```
        Water   Wood   Fire   Earth   Metal      <- warps (element)
  F3 *----+------+------o------+------+----*     Blade III, Soul III ...
          |      |      |      |      |
  F2 -----+------o------+------+------o-----     Status II, Ward II ...
          |      |      |      |      |
  F1 -----o------+------+------o------+-----     Blade I, Ward I, Body I
  ==========================================     selvage: start, universal
  +  knot (narrow)    o  major knot    *  keystone
```

**How it reads.** It is a table you can walk. For Fire sword, go up the Fire warp to the Blade weft, and the knot is yours. Each form weft repeats on three floors, stronger each time, so no form waits for a late realm.

| Breadth | Where |
|---|---|
| Universal | The bottom selvage; the side selvages (elementless builds) |
| Broad | The landings between floors |
| Family | The warps (element) and wefts (form) |
| Narrow | The knots: element × form is two family tags, rule 11's exact definition of narrow |

**Slots:**
- **13 keystones:** the 5 warp tops, the 6 top-floor weft ends and 2 corners.
- **~24 majors**, on knots where the pair makes a real build: Fire × Blade is Flame-Tempered Edge, Water × Status is Rime Lock.
- **~60 minors** on the other knots.
- **Closeless capabilities:** on "loose threads", hidden clusters off the edges.

**Root and elements.** The warps follow the generating cycle (tương sinh), and a knot scales by its warp. Neighbouring elements make two-element roots cheap, but Metal and Water sit at opposite ends. Variants: an innate one off the selvage, a transmutation off its warp, Thunder (Lôi) and Darkness (Ám) as diagonals.

**Build families.** Every element × form pair has a place, so hybrids plan easily.

**Risks:**
- It looks like a spreadsheet, possibly the wheel's problem again.
- Every knot has two ways in, so routes matter less.
- 90 knots invite filler: what does Earth × Companion say?
- The Metal–Water gap needs a fix.

**Keeps:** one start, purity, hidden clusters, variants, the point rules. Growth is clean: new floors or wefts.

## Shape C: the Star Field (Tinh Đồ)

```
       + Soul            o Wood home          + Companion
           \                 |                  /
  + Ward --- o Water ------ (*) Pole Star ------ o Fire --- + Status
           /           (five-element start)      \
     + Talisman                                    + Sword
              o Metal home ---------------- o Earth home
                    \          + Body           /
                     ...... dark constellation (hidden) ......
  o  element home     +  form constellation (30–50 acupoints)
```

**How it reads.** Named constellations, each a whole build arc that ends at its brightest star, the keystone. You start at your root's home star. A five-element root starts at the Pole Star, beside today's Five Elements Cycle (Ngũ Hành Luân Chuyển). Short lanes join the constellations.

| Breadth | Where |
|---|---|
| Universal | The lanes |
| Broad | 3–4 hub stars where lanes meet |
| Family | The constellation bodies (element or form) |
| Narrow | The constellation tails and side arms |

**Slots:**
- **~18 keystones**, one per constellation, with rival pairs where needed.
- **~30 majors** and **~70 minors**.
- **Closeless capabilities:** in **dark constellations** (ám tinh), which appear only when unsealed. That fits the "silhouette you have been eyeing" fantasy.

**Root and elements.** The root picks where you start, not only what pays. A multi-element root picks one of its homes at birth. A convergence variant is a comet lane between two homes.

**Build families.** Each family has its own constellation, the clearest of the three. A hybrid takes two neighbours. Rule: every form constellation sits within 2 lanes of at least 2 homes.

**Risks:**
- Picking a constellation is close to picking a class.
- The root-based start changes Spiritual Roots and the NPC allocator.
- The layout is drawn by hand, so most of today's generator is lost.
- The gaps between constellations clash with rule 21 ("no empty gaps").

**Keeps:** purity, keystones that close something, hidden clusters, variants as extra constellations, the point rules. Growth is easy: new constellations at the edge.

## Comparison

| | A Body Map | B Weave | C Star Field |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fits the cultivation fantasy | **Strong** | Medium | Medium |
| Legibility | Good | **Best** | Good |
| Long-route planning | **Kept** | Weaker | Weaker (class-like) |
| Tiers map onto the shape | Good | **Exact** | Good |
| Element fairness | Fair (equidistant organs) | Metal–Water gap | Fair; the start differs |
| A 51-point Qi Refining life | Lower torso, one organ, part of a limb | Floor 1, 1–2 knots | A home and one constellation |
| Today's generator reused | Partly | Mostly | Little |
| Risk of looking bad, like the wheel | Low–medium | **High** | Low |
| Keystones / majors / minors | 13 / 28 / ~60 | 13 / ~24 / ~60 | ~18 / ~30 / ~70 |

**Recommendation: A.** Borrow B's knot rule where a limb meets an organ, so element × form crossings hold the narrow modifiers. Fix A's two weak points early: give soul a front-spine path and the body an outer aura band. Mock up A and C as pictures first.

## Questions for the user

1. **What did you like about the old shape, and what looked bad in the wheel?** For example the small clusters, the long roads, the round layout, or the hidden clusters at the rim.
2. **One start for everyone, or a start chosen by the root?** Only C needs one, and it touches Spiritual Roots and NPC Builds.
3. **Where do the slot-raising and closeless capabilities go?** Rule 13 allows slot raises (#2 +1 artifact slot, #16 +2 beasts) only on keystones. Each needs a closing cost, or it moves to items or the Way (Đạo). The other ~10 need major slots.
4. **What is the tree's Effect budget?** How many tree keystones may one build hold? Should the tree host majors, or leave them to techniques (công pháp) and the Way? And does it stay at ~1,300 acupoints on today's point curve?
