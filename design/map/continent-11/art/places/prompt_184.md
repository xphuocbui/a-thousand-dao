Art brief: A small painted miniature of a tall white glacier pyramid with no roofline rising out of a sea of soft cloud, clearly the tallest form on the map, a tiny cairn of pale stones on its point, the signature element. Rules:

- Never: text, labels, banners with writing, flags with symbols, people larger than specks, a frame, a vignette, clouds over the place.
