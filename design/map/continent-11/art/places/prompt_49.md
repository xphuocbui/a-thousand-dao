The place: Tương Phùng Thị, market. Paint it as a market town: a low cluster of roofs around a big open market square full of awnings, warehouses at the edge.

Art brief: A market miniature on calm coastal water: a loose, irregular raft of many small boats lashed together, joined by planks, each with a tiny faded green or ochre awning, against a short stone quay with a few grey-tile sheds. Rules:

- Match the map exactly: the same golden-hour light from the TOP LEFT with soft shadows to the bottom right, the same painterly style, colours and level of detail as the surrounding terrain; the building scale fits the trees and hills around it.

- Never: text, labels, banners with writing, flags with symbols, people larger than specks, a frame, a vignette, clouds over the place.
