Paint ONE place into this tile of a painted grand-strategy map. The input is the finished map around the place, seen straight down; the place stands at the exact CENTRE of the image.

The place: Thính Vũ Lâu, great seat. Paint it as a great sect's seat: vertical, stacked pagoda roofs and terraces climbing the slope, the tallest outline in the land, walls and gates, a main hall.
How it looks: Out of the rain forest rises a dormant volcanic cone, dark with banana groves on its terraced flanks, and on its crown a slender many-eaved tower, the tallest thing in the south. From every eave of every storey hang long bronze rain-chains, so that in a downpour the whole tower runs with water and rings like a struck bell. No lamp burns in any window.
Art brief: The tallest outline in its land: a dark dormant volcanic cone with terraced banana groves on its flanks, crowned by a slender many-storeyed tower of dark timber and black-grey tile, every eave hung with long bronze rain-chains that catch the light; stepped halls on narrow terraces climbing one side of the cone; all windows dark. A thin veil of rain cloud trailing from the summit. Warm golden-hour light from the top left gleams on wet leaves and bronze. No lit lamps or lanterns, no text, no figures larger than specks, nothing outside the footprint.

Rules:
- Paint the place as a small, finely detailed miniature, as if a model-maker had built it into this painted relief: seen from above at a gentle angle so roofs, walls and towers read clearly, its base sitting naturally on the terrain that is there (follow the slopes, the shore, the river).
- Keep it centred and compact: the whole place fits inside a circle about 25 % of the image width across the centre. Short roads or paths may lead out of it.
- Match the map exactly: the same golden-hour light from the TOP LEFT with soft shadows to the bottom right, the same painterly style, colours and level of detail as the surrounding terrain; the building scale fits the trees and hills around it.
- Leave everything outside that central circle exactly as it is in the input.
- Never: text, labels, banners with writing, flags with symbols, people larger than specks, a frame, a vignette, clouds over the place.
