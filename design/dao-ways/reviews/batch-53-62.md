# Review: Đạo Way drafts 53–62 (Đao, Thương, Xạ, Chưởng, Ấn, Vũ, Diệt, Phệ, Trấn, Độc)

**Summary.** All ten drafts keep the facet slot table (1 major and 2 minors per facet, 2–6 capstones, no arts), and the
Hán-Việt names and new-piece lists are tidy. The real trouble is in the timeline arithmetic and in rules the drafts
assume. Three self-stack Effects are dead on the fight clock (Fury, Spin, and Hunger, which can never build because every
hit leeches). Vũ's On the Beat doubles every basic attack, since `basic_cooldown` equals `pulse_interval` (both 1 s).
Two "carry the excess past a shield" pieces (Cleave Through, Collapse) do nothing, because Combat Defence rule 9 already
passes the remainder on at full strength. Several Effects have no matching action or event (an over-max linh lực start, a
Silence on an escape slot that the Status Catalogue forbids, a self health drain, "when the foe cleanses"). Several foe
stat changes skip the status route (Effects rule 9 and the curse limits; Modifier System rule 6a). Several capstones have
no real closing cost (Furnace Palm, Poison Saint's Gut, Plague Bearer, Heaven Prison, Hawk Never Misses). Several stack and
trigger texts have two readings. Cross-Way overlaps worth merging are Name the Art / Name the Silence and the Silence and
Sealed Meridian lockout riders (57 vs 61), the "no cap" capstone family, and the ward-shield bypass cluster. Stat keys
mostly exist in `modifiers.md`. The recurring slip is stat notables that use keys outside the Way's own `theme_stats`
(rows 38–39).
