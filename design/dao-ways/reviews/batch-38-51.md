# Review: Đạo Way drafts 38–51 (Tình, Vô Tình, Tâm, Thiền, Bồ Đề, Ma, Sát, Hồng Trần, Mộng, Huyễn, Mị, Nhẫn, Tiêu Dao, Hoa)

**Summary.** I read the 14 drafts against Combat rules 4–13 and 22–24, Effects rules 3–9a with the Status Catalogue, Dao rules 3, 4, 10 and 12, and the node-modifier catalogue. The biggest problems are rule breaks that no draft labels `Needs rule`: Nhẫn's fatigue reduction, counted chance in minors and in a foe-side effect, new curses and hit-floors outside the closed action and curse lists, and companion share read live. Several Effects do nothing as written: Sát's capstone cap and post-cull riders, Tình's Cold Fury, and Vô Tình's Heartless Strength with no companion. A few pieces look dominant at their stated cost: Vô Tình's Steady Power, Thiền's Vows, Sát's Cull, Huyễn's Decoys, Mộng's Dream and Tình's Anger. Across the batch, the notables use stat keys outside their declared `theme_stats`, and the curse-duration extenders and reducers need one shared rule. Keys such as `tam_ma_resist_pct`, `ngo_tinh`, `body_shield_pct` and `block_rating` do exist in the Modifier System or Character Stats, so I did not flag them. Some rows below say "I could not confirm" where I did not trace a rule to its source.
