# Review: Đạo Way drafts 73–94 (21 files; 83 is not in this batch)

> Reviewer pass, unattended, 2026-11-11. Drafts were not edited. Rules read: Combat Core Rules 4–13, 22–24; Effects rules 3–9a and the Status Catalogue; Dao rules 3, 4, 10; the tree modifier catalogue; plus spot checks in Combat Defence, Modifier System and Character Stats for stat keys.

## Summary

The batch is mostly life-around and world-state content that waits on keys (`Needs key`), so few mechanics break a fight rule outright. The real defects cluster in six places. (1) **Layer and cap rules**: Cô's Lone Blade feeds L3 from an Effect (L3 is gear only); Thủ's capstone raises `ward_cap`, which is retired; Hóa's Small Form exceeds `evade_cap` with no label; several capstones say "no cap" on an L5 bonus when L5 is already capped at 100. (2) **Damage-reduction wording**: "hits on you are reduced by N %" (Vô Vi ×2, Hoàng, Phân Thân) is not a step in the defence path and is not the dampen formula. (3) **Effects that cannot fire**: Dragon Scales' Soul and Chaos Scales (soul hits and Chaos ignore or pass body shields), Long's "stack" stat, Thú's "companion dies" and "companion alive" (a companion cannot be removed), Phân Thân's Clone Swap (a companion cannot be targeted), Nho's "forced now" Command, Nghịch's Turnaround, Đại Đạo's "conversions applied to its defences". (4) **Ambiguity** a programmer would split on: whether the basic attack counts as "an art firing" (Vô Vi's whole engine), whether Si's chosen tag may be `hit`/`art` (it then costs nothing), what "that element's damage type" means for Wood/Earth/Metal/Water in Hóa, how Giant Form's heal interacts with form switching (a heal loop). (5) **Dominant pieces**: Thệ's oaths (free for most builds), Kiếp's Marks (farmable on weak foes), Hóa's 5-Form capstone, Cô's Walk Alone. (6) **Overlaps**: three Ways turn "being behind or hurt" into L5 (Nghịch, Hận, Kiếp); four Ways give home ground or foreseen fights (Thủ, Hoàng, Thần, Vực); three Ways let you pick rules for the foe (Hoàng, Thiên, Vực). Items already marked `Needs key` / `Needs rule` are not repeated unless the label does not cover the problem.

> 40 rows (the cap). Dropped as lowest value: percent-vs-points wording on resistance and comprehension, "dampened N %" vs "reduced N %", `creature damage` foe tags, Long's `stack effect` stat, Roar tag choice, Hóa Slip Through (Bound/Hooked do not exist), Hiếu's capstone close, Hiệp's Duel pills clause, and three minor overlaps (Bow Before Me/Soft Catch, Roar of Kings/Kneel, Borrow the Beast/Beast Form).
