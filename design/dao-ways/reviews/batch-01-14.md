# Review: Đạo Ways 01–14 (overnight 2026-11-11)

Read-only review of `01-am-duong.md` to `14-hu-thuc.md` against README conventions, Combat rules 4–13 and 22–24,
Effects rules 3–9a and the Status Catalogue, Dao rules 3, 4, 10 and 12, and the node-modifier catalogue.
Pieces already labelled `Needs rule` or `Needs key` are not restated unless the draft hides a second problem behind the label.

The batch has three recurring faults. (1) Many capstones "close" something the build never uses, or something the
paired major already imposes, so they are free power. The worst are 02, 05, 11 and 13; 08's Before Taiji and 14's
Empty Becomes Full are dominant outright. (2) Several Effects use actions that are not on Effects rule 6's closed
list. They remove or shorten the foe's buffs, apply fight-long resistance, block or evade shreds that are not
statuses, grant counted chance from a major or minor, and make Silence name `escape`. (3) Several bonuses sit where
the timeline cannot reach them: Bloom at Night's cap, Endless Night, Great Void, Balance's cap and Overcome's
scaling. "First hit" bonuses land on the basic attack, and fight-long stat debuffs are not statuses. Stat-key
hygiene is also loose: most Ways use notable keys outside their own `theme_stats` (Dao rule 12). Numbers are not
judged here, only mechanics.
