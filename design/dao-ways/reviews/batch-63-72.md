# Review: Đạo Way drafts 63–72 (Kỹ, Đan, Trận, Họa, Cầm, Kỳ Go, Tửu, Ti, Điếu, Thâu)

> Overnight review 2026-11-11. Drafts were not edited. Checked against Combat rules 4–13 and 22–24, Effects rules 3–9a and the Status Catalogue, dao.md rules 3, 4, 10, 12, and the tree-modifier catalogue.
> Facts used from the rules: `basic_cooldown` and `pulse_interval` are both 1 s, so the basic attack and the companion hit every second. The effect-action list is closed (Effects rule 6). Counted chance is for a keystone only (Effects rule 12). Wards have one of four payments (Combat rule 22). Caps stay with their owners (Effects rule 7).

## Summary

Across the ten drafts the commonest real defects are: (1) Effects that read "the next hit", "the previous hit's form tag", "the foe's art fires" or "arts in slot order" without saying what to do with the basic attack, the companion, wards, talismans and the escape art. The basic attack fires every second and has no form tag, so Cầm's Resonance and Ti's Webs break at once, and Họa's Write the Character can never complete in a build that slots a ward or an escape. (2) Actions outside the closed list: shortening the foe's timers (Điếu), a ward with no payment (Trận), a cap breach (Tửu), removing the foe's self-stacks and lowering its art speed (Cầm), and a certain flee with no escape art (Kỳ). (3) Counted chance on minors and majors (Kỹ, Đan, Tửu, Điếu) when only a keystone may grant it. (4) Numbers that cannot be reached or that cancel another piece in the same Way: Tửu's Tipsiness caps, Đan's yield capstone against Double Batch, and Điếu's L5 arithmetic. (5) Pieces with no real cost: Tửu's wines, Đan's Thrown Pill, Họa's Living Scroll, Thâu's Steal the Art, Điếu's Lone Angler. Every draft also has stat lines outside its `theme_stats`, which dao.md rule 12 forbids. Three clusters in this batch overlap: prepared ground in an unprepared fight (Kỹ, Trận, Họa), talisman-first builds (Kỹ, Trận), and "when the foe's art fires, mine fires" (Kỳ, Ti).
