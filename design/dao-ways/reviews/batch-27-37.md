# Review: Đạo drafts 27–37 (Sinh Tử, Luân Hồi, Nhân Quả, Bỉ Ngạn, Hoàng Tuyền, Quỷ, Cốt, Hồn, Huyết, Thể, Liên)

Reviewed overnight 2026-11-11 against Combat rules 4–13 and 22–24, Effects rules 3–9a and the Status Catalogue, dao.md rules 3, 4, 10 and 12, Combat Formulas E2, F and G, and the modifier catalogue and `modifiers.md` key list. The drafts are mostly consistent and label their big new rules. The real problems fall into five groups. (1) A handful of Effects aim at things that no longer exist or never fire: `regen_cap`, a status-resistance cap, a soul-resistance cap, Chaos on a body shield, Fear and Stagger effects, and a full regeneration block that the 2026-11-10 decision replaced with dampening. (2) Several mechanics break rules without a `Needs rule` label: curses that deal damage and outlive the fight, a heal-received "event", a foe stat debuff outside the closed action list, curses over the 5-pulse cap, and a refusal that heals to 50 %. (3) The unflagged key `status_duration_pct`, which nine or so nodes use and which does not exist. (4) Dominance and no-cost problems: Eternal Return, the Huyết health-to-shield loop, the Thể capstone that counts as gear and leaves every slot empty, and the White Bone Demon cost that is not a cost. (5) Majors that overlap: Down-refusal and "win at the edge, keep a permanent gain" appear in three Ways each, and "regen/shield +1 % per căn cốt point" appears twice. Nothing below restates a `Needs key` / `Needs rule` label the draft already carries, except where the label hides a second problem.
