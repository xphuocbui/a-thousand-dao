# Review: Đạo Way drafts 15–26 (Kim, Mộc, Thủy, Hỏa, Thổ, Lôi, Phong, Băng, Vụ, Ngọc, Quang, Ám)

> Overnight review 2026-11-11. Reviewer read README, Combat rules 4–13 and 22–24, Effects rules 3–9a and the Status
> Catalogue, Dao rules 3, 4, 10, 12 and the tree modifier catalogue. Drafts were not edited. Things a draft already
> labels `Needs rule` / `Needs key` are not repeated.

## Summary

The twelve drafts are close to the template, but five problems repeat across them. (1) Many Effects use triggers that
are not engine events: a heal, a wave phase starting, a batch expiring, an art "waiting", a tick, a ward "fully
stopping" a hit. (2) Several Effects use actions the closed action list does not have and do not say so: consuming or
stripping stacks on the foe, a self-hit, changing a foe's stat directly, raising a cap, a refusal that returns at 30 %
health or fires twice, a standing status with no fee. (3) Stack grants often give no duration, which Effects 9a
requires. (4) Many stat notables use keys missing from the Way's own Theme stats (Băng, Ngọc, Quang, Lôi, Phong, Mộc,
Hỏa, Vụ, Ám). (5) Overlaps: three Ways have "your stacks never expire" capstones, three Ways have an art-speed or
timer-slow control piece, and Vụ and Ám both have "opening window" majors and capstones. Several closes cost the build
nothing (Immovable, Gravity Well, Lord of Shadows, Heaven's Verdict). Ám's "until a foe's hit lands" window is almost
empty because nothing fires at t = 0. Nothing here changes the macro targets.

## Findings

| File | Piece | Problem | Severity | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20-loi.md | Carry the Tribulation (major), Carry Your Own Tribulation (capstone) | A bolt that hits *you* needs a self-damage action. The closed action list (Effects rule 6) has none, and the draft does not flag it. Also the cap is dead text: 4 % of max health × 3 L5 per 1 % = +12 at best, so "cap +45" never binds, and "no cap" in the capstone changes nothing. | high | Flag `Needs rule` (self-hit action, once per 10 s, typed Lightning, meets resistance). Fix the numbers so the cap can be reached (L5 +10 per 1 %), or drop the cap and make the capstone raise the bolt (8 % per 5 s) instead. |
| 20-loi.md, 21-phong.md, 15-kim.md | Nine Heavens (crit cap +5 pp), Never in One Place capstone (evade cap +10 pp), The Wind Has No Floor (`cooldown_min` −20 %), Unbreaking (resistance cap +10) | Four capstones raise caps. Effects rule 7 says caps stay with their owners, and the action list only lets an Effect *lower* a cap or raise a host's cap (formation level, stack cap). Dao rule 4 forbids stat lines raising these caps. The drafts do not label them `Needs rule`; only `_shared-rules.md` R14 mentions them. | high | Label each `Needs rule` and keep the R14 limit (at most one cap-shift capstone per Way). Or rewrite them as a close-and-pay: Nine Heavens as "crit chance above the cap converts to crit damage". |
| 18-hoa.md, 23-vu.md, 24-ngoc.md | Rise from the Ashes, Nirvana, Better Broken Jade | Effects rule 11: a refusal sets health to **1**, works once per fight, and the step order is refusal first (step 1), then last acts (step 2). Hỏa returns at 30 % (and 15 % the second time) and Nirvana fires twice. Ngọc's "deal 20 % as Light *and* refuse Down if it kills" needs the last act's result before the refusal, which is the wrong order. None of this is labelled. | high | Hỏa: refusal sets 1 health, then a restore of 30 % is a separate heal (and blocked by `no_heal`). Say that, or flag `Needs rule` (R5). Ngọc: make the shatter a step 1 refusal plus a free hit, with no "if it kills". Nirvana must name the second refusal as a rule change. |
| 23-vu.md | The Sea Swallows (capstone) | Drowned "has no batch duration while the target stays below 50 % linh lực." Effects rule 9: a duration-less status needs a standing fee from its caster and a cleanse. This has neither, and with no stack cap the drain grows without bound. | high | Give it the standing-status treatment (a Formulas B fee) or a long fixed duration. Cap the total tick per pulse as a share of max linh lực. |
| 23-vu.md | Drown (major) | The tick has no damage type ("element-free qi tick"), yet Combat rule 4 says every tick meets the resistance of its type. A programmer cannot tell which resistance applies. Uncapped batches also mean one fast build drains a pool in a few pulses (3 hits a second × 4 pulses = 12 batches = 7 % of max a pulse). | high | Name a type (Force like Qi Disruption, or Chaos) and say which resistance it meets. Add a per-pulse cap or one batch per pulse. |
| 23-vu.md, 26-am.md | Unseen until Too Late; Strike from Nowhere | **Two Ways, the same piece.** Both are "re-arm the opening state after N s without being hit" capstones (Vụ 4 s, Ám 6 s), on openers that are nearly the same (Veiled: first hit crits; Unseen Hand: crits until hit). Each draft's "Kept apart from" only argues about flavour. Vụ's "every hit out of the veil crits" can also be read two ways: the hit that leaves the veil, or every hit made while unveiled (always-crit, past the crit cap). | high | Differentiate: Vụ's veil = foe accuracy loss only; Ám's = L5 and crit. Rewrite Vụ's capstone as "the hit that leaves the veil", and tie "re-arm" to a different trigger in each. |
| 26-am.md | Unseen Hand, Night Tithe, Slip Away, Strike from Nowhere | The window "until a foe's hit lands on you" is nearly empty. Nothing fires at t = 0 (Combat rule 10 Start): arts start on full cooldown and the basic attack first acts at `basic_cooldown`, so both sides' first hits land at about the same instant. Unless Ám is the requesting side, the foe's hit lands first and Unseen Hand is spent before it did anything. Night Tithe ("hit a foe that has not yet hit you") is true for at most one hit. | high | Make the window a time (the first 8 s) or a count of your hits, not "until hit". Fix Night Tithe the same way. |
| 15-kim.md, 16-moc.md, 19-tho.md | Hundred Temperings, The Forest Outlives Everything, Immovable | **Three Ways, the same capstone:** "your element buff stacks never expire." The conditional forms in Hỏa (Furnace Never Cools), Băng (Heart of Ice) and Phong (Clear Breeze) make six Ways with a "stacks do not expire" capstone. Their costs also differ in value. Mộc's 60-stack cap and Kim's 1 stack/s are mild, and Thổ's is free (see below). | high | Keep it in one Way (Thổ or Mộc) or change the others: Kim: stacks never expire but cap at 30; Mộc: stacks fall one at a time, not all at once. At least make each cost bite. |
| 19-tho.md, 25-quang.md, 26-am.md | Immovable; Gravity Well; Heaven's Mirror; Lord of Shadows | Closing costs that cost the build nothing. Immovable and Gravity Well close evade and escape, but the Way's own direction 4 (Rooted) and its major Deep Roots want no evade. Lord of Shadows closes "no ward in an art slot", which an Ám build never slots, for two 3 s untargetable windows. Quang's Mirror forbids placing curses, which cancels Exposed (a curse): Laid Bare, Sunlit, First Light, No Hiding and Judgement all stop working. | high | Make the cost something the build uses: Immovable closes ward slots or healing, Lord of Shadows closes the escape art and evade. Quang's Mirror: say "you cannot place curses other than Exposed", or move the capstone to a Way with no Exposed engine. |
| 15-kim.md, 18-hoa.md, 22-bang.md, 25-quang.md, 16-moc.md, 19-tho.md | Kim Minh, Thu Tro, Băng Toái, Tịnh Trừ, Blossom, Mountain Falls | "Consume N Burn / Metal / Ice stacks on the target", "remove 3 buff stacks from the target", "consume all your Wood / Earth buff stacks." The action list only has "cleanse element stacks" (debuffs on its own side). There is no action to remove a foe's debuffs or buffs, or to spend your own buff stacks. None is labelled `Needs rule`. | high | Add one shared `Needs rule`: "consume stacks of an element from a side (oldest first)", with a Breakdown line. List all six pieces under it in `_new-pieces.md`. |
| 17-thuy.md, 16-moc.md | After Rain; Seed Fall; Find the Gap | Triggered on "a heal of yours above 5 % / 10 % of max health", which is not an event. The events are hit landing, being hit, crit, evade, knockout, art fired, status applied, pulse, pool threshold, shield break, cap clip, ward payments, Down step. Find the Gap ("art fully stopped by a ward") and Băng's Cold Bill ("hit fully stopped by your ward") read a defence result that is not an event either. | high | Make "a heal restored" and "damage to health = 0 after wards" events (Effects rule 5 addition), or rewrite as a pulse check on healing done in the window. |
| 17-thuy.md | Riptide | "When your crest begins" is not an event, and the wave has no wake-up. Combat rule 10 only wakes arts for a `soul_lockout` end, time-guard thresholds, escape channels and wards. A wave start at 12 s, 24 s... does nothing. | high | Make it a conditional that reads fight time (gain stacks at the first instant of each wave), or name "wave phase" an event. The same fix for the 6 s / 12 s edge: say which phase t = 6.000 belongs to. |
| 18-hoa.md | Wildfire | "When a Burn batch expires" is not an event. "Once per batch" is also ambiguous: the reapplied batch is a new batch, so it may reapply again (40, 20, 10... stacks to 1). No duration for the reapplied batch. | high | Define a pulse check reading batches that expired this pulse, say the child cannot reapply, and give its duration. |
| 16-moc.md | Root Drinks the Rot | Ticks are not events. "Each tick heals 15 % of its damage" can mean per tick, or per pulse from the sum. Also "damage" can be pre-resistance or what reached health. | medium | Pulse trigger: heal 15 % of the Poison damage that reached the foe's health this pulse. Name the variant. |
| 16-moc.md | Green Blood (capstone) | "Health regeneration counts as linh lực regeneration for Ice-stack cost raises" is not implementable. Ice raises art cost; it does not read regeneration. | high | Delete the clause, or say what it means (for example: health regeneration also restores linh lực at 50 %). |
| 16-moc.md | Strangle | "Regenerates 30 % less (a Stagnation-like dampen, not a curse slot)". A foe effect that is not a status does not exist in the action list, and "not a curse slot" sidesteps the 3-curse limit in Combat Formulas F. | medium | Use the Stagnation curse or add a Poison-linked curse record (one of the 3 slots). |
| 16-moc.md | Shared Root | Raises the companion's power share per Wood buff stack, which is live. The action only allows "multiply a sheet share, read at compile". Stack count is not known at compile. | medium | Make it a live modifier on companion damage (a `companion_damage_pct` line scaled by stacks), not a share change. |
| 16-moc.md | Blossom, with Forest Outlives Everything | Heals 1 % of max health per stack, no cap but the stack cap. With the capstone (60 stacks, never expire) that is 60 % of max health every 12 s plus 60 Poison stacks. Against fatigue and sustain-is-king this is the strongest heal in the batch; the capstone's cost (60-stack cap) hardly binds. | medium | Cap the heal at 25 % per Blossom, or make the capstone close Blossom. |
| 18-hoa.md | Nine Furnaces (capstone), Swallow the Furnace | "Pills in a fight take no art slot: up to 3 fire at their guard." Combat rule 13 does not slot pills (they are preparation, rule 12); a pill is not an art with a guard. Swallow the Furnace says pills are taken *before* the fight, so the two contradict. Not labelled. | high | Flag `Needs rule`. Decide whether pills can fire in a fight (R4's "Pill Belt" asks the same), and drop "up to 3 fire at their guard" if not. |
| 25-quang.md | Radiance (capstone) | "Ignore evade and ward shields' first 10 %." That is a partial ward bypass (R2 `bypass_share`), not an action in Effects rule 6, and "first 10 %" can mean 10 % of the shield value or of each shield. Not labelled. | medium | Flag `Needs rule` with R2, and say "10 % of each ward shield's value is skipped". |
| 25-quang.md | Day Breaks | Applies Thunder debuff stacks from a Light Way. Stacks belong to the source's element tag (Effects 9a) and Light has no stacks. It also fails the name-hidden test (Dao rule 12): a Thunder stack in Light's graph. | medium | Replace with a Light status (a second Exposed turn) or move it to Lôi. |
| 25-quang.md | Judgement, Sin Revealed, Phán Xét facet | The condition "a foe that has placed a curse on you" is false against most enemies (few cast curses, and Sin Revealed's `demonic` tag is pending). The facet is mostly dead against ordinary foes. | medium | Add a fallback that always has some effect (for example, +L5 per curse on *you* cleansed), or give the facet a minor that works without a cursing foe. |
| 22-bang.md | Ice Heart (major) | "Immune to Fear-type and heart-demon Effects": no such status exists in the Status Catalogue (Afraid is only proposed in `_new-modifiers.md`). Half the major does nothing today. | medium | Tie it to something real (Silence and Sealed Meridian immunity) or hold until Afraid exists. |
| 22-bang.md | Frozen Breath | "When the foe's art waits because it cannot pay" is not an event, and "waiting" is not a readable. | medium | Trigger on a pulse where the foe has an art ready but unaffordable (readable: enemy linh lực below the next art's cost). |
| 19-tho.md, 22-bang.md | Earthquake, Gravity Well, Heavy Air; Frozen Clock, The World Stops | **Two Ways, the same piece:** foe art-speed / timer control (Thổ: push timers 0.6 s, −15 % art speed curse, −8 % art speed; Băng: timers 20–40 % slower, a 2 s freeze). Each is flagged `Needs rule`, but their majors and capstones aim at the same thing, so one rule decision gives both Ways the same role. | medium | Split the roles: Băng = *cost* (Ice raises cost, no timer effects); Thổ = timer pushback. Move Frozen Clock and World Stops to another Way, or merge the two Ways' control into one. |
| 15-kim.md | Purge the Dross, Sole Edge | Purge the Dross needs "nothing converted". Sole Edge converts every non-Soul part to Physical, so with that capstone Purge never fires. Also a hit with three non-Physical parts needs three conversions and the limit is 2 per hit (Combat rule 6), so "every non-Soul part" cannot always be true. | medium | Say Purge counts hits whose parts are all Physical *after* conversion. Limit Sole Edge to "the two largest parts". |
| 18-hoa.md | Slag; 22-bang.md White Silence; 16-moc.md Strangle; 19-tho.md Shaken, Grit, Heavy Air; 23-vu.md Dampened | Effects whose payload changes the *foe's* stat (block, accuracy, crit damage, art cost, Fire damage, regeneration) directly. Effects grant modifiers to their owner; the foe is changed only by statuses (curses: max 3, max 5 pulses). Seven of these put a hidden foe debuff outside the curse limit. | medium | Add one shared `Needs rule` ("an Effect may place a conditional modifier on the foe, not counted as a curse") or rewrite them as the owner's modifier (Grit: "your hits take 25 % less crit damage"). |
| 15-kim.md, 16-moc.md, 18-hoa.md, 19-tho.md, 21-phong.md, 22-bang.md | Anvil Body, Bramble Coat, Splinter, Forest Fights, Blossom, Catch, Molten Strike, Wall Remembers, Frostbite, Lava Body, Edge Bites and others | Stack grants with no duration ("apply 2 Poison stacks", "gain Earth buff stacks"). Effects 9a: each grant states how many stacks and how long ("for the fight" counts). A programmer cannot tell if it lasts the fight or one pulse. | medium | Add a duration to each (4 or 6 pulses, or "for the fight"). A one-line lint: every `apply stacks` has a duration. |
| 24-ngoc.md | Flawless (major), Jade Purity (capstone) | Both give the same bonus: the major makes the stack purity factor "50 % more", the capstone "×1.5". If both are held, +50 % and ×1.5 stack (additively 2.0, multiplicatively 2.25), and the draft does not say. The capstone then has no extra rule except the close. | medium | Give the capstone a different rule (for example, stacks also get purity from the second element), or state the stacking. |
| 17-thuy.md | Overflow Shield | "Regeneration lost at a full pool becomes a ward shield." The only shield an Effect can give is a body shield ("Restore a body shield") or a ward payload change. A free ward shield is not an action. | medium | Make it a body shield, or flag `Needs rule`. |
| 17-thuy.md | Water Wears the Mountain | "That ward bills 3 % more per hit, no cap": only drain and per-hit wards bill, and Leaking Ward's own cap is +50 % (`curse_drain_raise_max`). A reserved or cast ward does nothing here, and an uncapped version overrides the curse cap. | medium | Limit to drain and per-hit wards and cap at +100 %. |
| 26-am.md | Mark for Death | "If one kills, restore 20 % of max linh lực and health": a kill ends the fight (the foe is Down at the defeat check), so the rider heals nothing that matters in a duel. | medium | Move the heal to "a crit on a target below 30 % health restores 3 %", or drop the kill rider. |
| 21-phong.md | Ten Thousand Blades | "Quick arts hit twice at 55 % damage": 110 % total, and each hit rolls crit, ward payments, evade and on-hit Effects separately (Thousand Cuts adds Bleed and Wind stacks per hit). That is a free +10 % plus a doubling of every per-hit effect. It is also ambiguous whether the two hits count as one firing for triggers and the trigger budget (same gap as Ngọc's Refract, which is flagged). | medium | Flag `Needs rule`. Use 50 % + 50 % (100 %) so the gain is only the per-hit Effects. State that the two hits are one firing for `soul_lockout` and trigger limits. |
| 20-loi.md | Storm Battery | A plain number on an existing key. `linh_luc_on_crit_pct` is in modifiers.md and the catalogue ("linh lực regained on a crit, +0.6 % notable"). Modifier vs Effect (README, Effects rule 3). | medium | Make it a stat notable on `linh_luc_on_crit_pct`. |
| 24-ngoc.md, 15-kim.md, 18-hoa.md, 19-tho.md, 22-bang.md, 25-quang.md | Smooth Grain, Read Twice, Talisman of Jade, Sharpened by Use, Settled, Sink In, Lingering Chill, Lingering Light, Lit Path | More plain numbers dressed as Effects: artifact-active cost −20 % (a tag-scoped `art_cost_pct`), technique mastery +15 % (`technique_mastery_pct`, proposed), talisman stock +1 (a catalogue one-off), "+2 pulses" on stack duration (`stack_duration_pct`), "first art 1 s sooner" ("first firing comes sooner"). They spend `minor` slots on numbers. | medium | Move them to stat notables or road lines, or give each a condition. |
| 22-bang.md, 24-ngoc.md, 25-quang.md, 20-loi.md, 21-phong.md, 16-moc.md, 18-hoa.md, 23-vu.md, 26-am.md, 19-tho.md | Theme stats vs notables | Stat lines use keys not in the Way's Theme stats and not flagged new: **Băng** (`crit_damage_pct`, `khi_huyet_pct`, `status_resist_pct`, `escape_pct`, `evade_pct`); **Ngọc** (`accuracy_pct`, `crit_damage_pct`, fire `resist_pct`, `artifact_defense_pct`, `than_thuc_pct`, seal `attack_pct`); **Quang** (`status_resist_pct`, `crit_damage_pct`, chaos `resist_pct`); **Lôi** (`khi_huyet_pct`, `soul_res_pct`, physical `attack_pct`); **Phong** (`crit_damage_pct`); **Mộc** (`formation_power_pct`); **Hỏa** (`block_rating`, `health_regen_pct`); **Vụ** (`crit_chance_pct`); **Ám** (`khi_huyet_pct`, `health_regen_pct`); **Thổ** (`resist_pen_pct` for Split Ground); **Thủy** (`escape_pct` for Ferryman). Dao rule 12 says stat lines use only the Way's `theme_stats`. | medium | Add the keys to each Theme stats line (or change the notable). One lint pass over the twelve files. |
| 19-tho.md | Deep Roots | "For each 5 s in which you have not escaped or evaded": it can mean a running streak that resets on an evade, or a count of clean 5 s windows kept over the fight. | medium | Say "streak: resets to 0 on an evade or an escape". |
| 19-tho.md | Stone Skin with Immovable / Wall Remembers | Earth buff stacks have no cap and dampen Physical taken, and Stone Skin adds block per stack. With Immovable (never expire) and Wall Remembers (up to 8 per hit) a tank reaches several hundred stacks, which silences every Physical foe. The only cost is evade and escape, which that build never uses. | medium | Cap the Earth dampen on the Way (for example at 60 % total) or have Immovable also stop gaining new stacks after 100. |

## Not reported

Overlaps already named by the drafts and by `_shared-rules.md` (R1, R2, R4, R5, R10) are not repeated except where the
draft does not carry the `Needs rule` label. Names, titles and flavour were not reviewed. The Burn/Poison scale and
Water/Ice buff numbers were not checked against Formulas D.
