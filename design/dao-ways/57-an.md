# 57 · Ấn Đạo · *Pháp Ấn* (Dharma Seals)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct` (seal), `art_speed_pct` (seal), `art_cost_pct` (seal), `status_duration_pct` (curse),
> `defense_pct`, `ward_efficiency_pct`, `cast_ward_duration_pct`, `than_thuc_pct`, `crit_damage_pct` (seal).
> **Road:** `attack_pct` seal +2.

## Build directions

1. **Mark and Detonate.** Quick seals stamp Seal Marks on the foe; a heaven seal detonates every mark (Ấn Liên +
   Thiên Ấn). A setup-and-payoff build with a visible counter on the foe.
2. **The Binder.** Seals that are curses: Sealed Meridian and Silence on tempo, the foe's best layer locked or its
   best tag silenced. A control build that wins by subtraction.
3. **The Ward Seal.** Wards cast as seals: cheap cast wards that also mark the foe.
4. **A Seal for Every Answer.** Seal arts that read the foe and change their rider (Ứng Ấn): one loadout that
   answers several enemy types.

## Facets

### 1. Ấn Liên (Seal Chains)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` seal +1.5 · `art_speed_pct` seal +0.75
- **Stat notables:** Linked Hands (Liên Thủ): seal ×2 · Chain of Signs (Chuỗi Ấn): seal art speed ×2 · Practised
  Fingers (Ngón Thuần): seal cost −1.5, seal +0.5 · Knot Seal (Ấn Nút): seal crit damage +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Seal Marks (Ấn Ký).** Each seal hit stamps 1 Seal Mark on the target (a debuff stack, cap 9, for the fight; cleansable like a debuff). Your next slow seal art consumes them all (R19): L5 +6 per mark.
  - Minor: Unbroken Chain (Chuỗi Không Đứt). Cleanses remove at most 2 Seal Marks a pulse. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Ninth Mark (Ấn Thứ Chín). At 9 marks, your next seal art also applies Sealed Meridian on L2.

### 2. Phược Ấn (Binding Seals)
- **Small:** `status_duration_pct` curse +2 · `attack_pct` seal +1
- **Stat notables:** Tight Binding (Trói Chặt): curse duration ×2 · Lock Sign (Ấn Khóa): seal +1, curse duration +1 ·
  Heavy Seal (Ấn Nặng): seal crit damage +2 · Silent Sign (Ấn Câm): curse duration +1, status resist +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Seal the Meridian (Phong Mạch Ấn).** Your seal crits apply Sealed Meridian on the layer (L2, L4 or L5) with the most points in the foe's last hit (L2 before its first hit).
  - Minor: Name the Art (Gọi Tên Chiêu). Each Seal Mark consumed adds +2 % to that seal art's crit chance (cap +18 %). *(starter)*
  - Minor: Bind Tighter (Trói Thêm). Curses you apply to a target already bearing a curse of yours last 1 pulse
    longer.

### 3. Thiên Ấn (Heaven Seals)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` seal +1.5 · `crit_damage_pct` seal +1.5
- **Stat notables:** Sky Seal (Ấn Trời): seal ×2 · Falling Heaven (Trời Sập): seal crit damage ×2 · Great Sign
  (Đại Ấn): seal +1, thần thức +1 · Weight of Law (Trọng Pháp): seal penetration +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Heaven Seal (Thiên Ấn Lạc).** Your slow seal arts deal more by your thần thức (L5 +1 per 3 % of thần thức
    above the foe's, cap +30) and cannot be evaded.
  - Minor: Long Sign (Ấn Dài). Your slow seal arts cost 20 % less while the foe bears 5+ Seal Marks. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Seal from Above (Ấn Từ Trên). When your slow seal art fires, your quick seal arts' timers end 1 s sooner.

### 4. Khoái Ấn (Quick Seals)
- **Small:** `art_speed_pct` seal +1 · `art_cost_pct` seal −1
- **Stat notables:** Flicker Sign (Ấn Chớp): seal art speed ×2 · Cheap Sign (Ấn Rẻ): seal cost ×2 · Finger Dance
  (Ngón Múa): seal art speed +1, seal +1 · Snap (Bật): seal crit chance +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Thousand Signs (Thiên Ấn Quyết).** Your quick seal arts' cost reduction also applies as art speed for
    them (1 % cost reduction = 1 % art speed).
  - Minor: Flash Seal (Ấn Lóe). Your first quick seal each fight fires at half cooldown. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Seal Echo (Ấn Vọng). Every 3rd quick seal you fire stamps 2 Seal Marks instead of 1.

### 5. Hộ Ấn (Seal Wards)
- **Small:** `defense_pct` +1.2 · `cast_ward_duration_pct` +2
- **Stat notables:** Ward Sign (Ấn Hộ): defense ×2 · Long Seal (Ấn Bền): cast ward duration ×2 · Seal Wall (Tường
  Ấn): ward efficiency ×2 · Mirror Sign (Ấn Gương): soul resistance +1, defense +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Seal Ward (Ấn Hộ Thân).** Your cast wards carry the `seal` form tag (for modifier scopes only) and stamp 2 Seal Marks on the foe when cast.
  - Minor: Sealed Body (Thân Phong). While a cast ward of yours is up, curses on you last 1 pulse less. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Answer Seal (Ứng Ấn). When the foe's art hits your cast ward, your next seal hit stamps 2 more Seal Marks.
