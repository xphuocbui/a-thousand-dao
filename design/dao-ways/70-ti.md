# 70 · Ti Đạo · *Hồng Tuyến* (the Red Thread)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `status_duration_pct`, `attack_pct` (physical), `body_shield_pct`, `artifact_defense_pct`, `co_duyen_pct`,
> `formation_power_pct`, `than_thuc_pct`.
> Also used below, listed here (dao.md rule 12): penetration, soul resist, physical resist, renown, `art_speed_pct`.
> `status_duration_pct` and `co_duyen_pct` are proposed or world keys (`Needs key`).
> **Road:** `status_duration_pct` +2.

## Build directions

1. **The Web.** Lay webs in preparation: traps that fire when the foe's arts fire (Mạng Nhện). The trap build: L4
   that acts, not only multiplies.
2. **Binding Threads.** Slow the foe: Bound stacks (a named curse, cap 5) lower its art speed and evade (Trói). A control build with a
   counter-sized cap.
3. **Puppet Strings.** Tie a thread to the foe's art: when it fires, yours fires too (Dây Rối). Mirror tempo.
4. **Silk Armour.** Thread shields: body shields woven from silk that refill when your threads are cut (Thiên Y).
5. **Fate Threads.** The red thread of fate: bonds and fortune in the world (Sợi Duyên).

## Facets

### 1. Sợi Duyên (Fate Threads)
- **Small:** `co_duyen_pct` +1 · `than_thuc_pct` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** Red Thread (Chỉ Đỏ): fortune ×2 · Fate's Weave (Dệt Mệnh): thần thức ×2 · Tied Fortune (Buộc Phúc):
  fortune +1, thần thức +1 · Matchmaker (Nguyệt Lão): renown +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: The Red Thread (Hồng Tuyến).** Tie a thread to an NPC (once a year): fortunate encounters with them are
    certain once per year, and their fate is visible to you. `Needs key` (Event Engine).
  - Minor: Lucky Knot (Nút May). A fortunate encounter through a Red Thread NPC gives one more reward roll. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Thread of Return (Chỉ Về). Once a season, a thread leads you home instantly.

### 2. Trói (Binding)
- **Small:** `status_duration_pct` +2 · `attack_pct` physical +1
- **Stat notables:** Tight Silk (Tơ Chặt): status duration ×2 · Cutting Thread (Chỉ Cắt): physical ×2 · Many Strands
  (Nhiều Sợi): status duration +1, physical +1 · Steel Silk (Tơ Thép): penetration +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Bind (Trói).** Your hits apply Bound, a named curse (stacks to 5, 3 pulses; durations clamp to 1–5, R22; R17: a fight-long foe debuff is a named curse): −3 % art speed and −1 pp evade per stack.
    `Needs key` (the Bound entry in the Status Catalogue).
  - Minor: Snag (Vướng). Your first hit each fight applies 3 Bound. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Tighten (Siết Thêm). A foe at full Bound stacks takes +10 % Physical from you.

### 3. Mạng Nhện (the Web)
- **Small:** `formation_power_pct` +1.2 · `attack_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Wide Web (Mạng Rộng): formation ×2 · Sticky Strand (Sợi Dính): attack ×2 · Hidden Web (Mạng Ẩn):
  formation +1, attack +1 · Spider Patience (Nhện Kiên Nhẫn): status duration +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Webs (Giăng Mạng).** In preparation (foreseen or ambush; a preparation choice, Effects rule 8, R8), lay up to 3 Webs: each fires on the foe's next non-basic art
    (once per Web), dealing a trigger hit (`c_trig` 0.8 of your strongest art, inside `trig_budget`) and
    applying 3 Bound. `Needs key` (traps).
  - Minor: Quick Web (Mạng Nhanh). One Web is laid even in a blind fight (the preparation choice is allowed in blind, R8; Combat rule 12's table). *(starter)*
  - Minor: Web Remembers (Mạng Nhớ). A Web that fired re-lays itself after 10 s (once).

### 4. Dây Rối (Puppet Strings)
- **Small:** `than_thuc_pct` +0.5 · `art_speed_pct` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** Puppeteer's Hand (Tay Rối): thần thức ×2 · Quick Strings (Dây Nhanh): art speed ×2 · Tangled (Rối
  Tung): thần thức +1, art speed +1 · Wooden Smile (Nụ Cười Gỗ): soul resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Strings Attached (Buộc Dây).** Tie one of your arts to the foe's most-slotted form tag: whenever the foe
    fires an art with that tag, your tied art makes a free hit in the same instant (`c_trig` 0.6 of that art, inside `trig_budget`; it does not use or reset the art's timer). Once per 3 s. Where the foe's tagged arts fire in the same instant, the earlier slot counts.
  - Minor: Jerk the String (Giật Dây). When your tied art hits this way, it deals +10 %. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Cut Strings (Cắt Dây). If the foe Silences your tied art, the Silence ends 1 pulse sooner.

### 5. Thiên Y (Silk Armour)
- **Small:** `body_shield_pct` +1 · `artifact_defense_pct` +1.5
- **Stat notables:** Heavenly Silk (Lụa Trời): body shield ×2 · Woven Ward (Áo Dệt): armour defence ×2 · Fine Mesh (Lưới
  Mịn): body shield +1, armour +1 · Spider Silk (Tơ Nhện): physical resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Seamless Robe (Thiên Y Vô Phùng).** Your body shield refills 3 % of max health each time one of your Webs
    fires or your tied art's free hit lands.
  - Minor: Silk Lining (Lót Lụa). While a Web of yours is laid and unfired, your body shield is +15 %. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Thread Snare (Bẫy Tơ). When your body shield breaks, apply 5 Bound.
