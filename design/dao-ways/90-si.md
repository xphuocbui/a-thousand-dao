# 90 · Si Đạo · *Si* (Obsession)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct` (one chosen tag), `crit_damage_pct`, `art_speed_pct` (chosen tag), `tam_ma_resist_pct`
> (negative on Điên), `ngo_tinh` (comprehension).
> **Road:** `attack_pct` (chosen tag) +2.

## Build directions

1. **One Thought.** Choose one tag at the glimpse (sword, fire, poison, seal, basic...). Every stat line in the Way
   scopes to it and is 50 % larger. Everything else you do is worse. The "master of one thing" min-max.
2. **Infatuation.** A focus stack that grows each time you use your chosen tag and resets when you use anything
   else (Si Mê): the build punishes variety and rewards purity of loadout.
3. **Madness.** Heart demon as fuel: the higher your tâm ma, the stronger the obsession (Điên), with qi deviation
   as the real price.
4. **The Peerless Art.** One technique's art, mastered past its limits: an art that gains a level each time it
   kills (Tuyệt Kỹ).

## Facets

### 1. Nhất Niệm (One Thought)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chosen tag +3 (the 1.5× rule applies to every Si stat line)
- **Stat notables:** One Thing (Một Thứ): chosen tag attack ×2 · Single Point (Một Điểm): chosen tag crit damage ×2 ·
  Narrow Gate (Cửa Hẹp): chosen tag attack +1.5, crit +1.5 · Tunnel Vision (Tầm Nhìn Hẹp): chosen tag art speed +3
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: One Thought (Nhất Niệm).** At the glimpse, choose one element, form or effect tag (never `hit`, `art`, `basic`, a mode, source or derived tag; the build must own at least one art without it). Your hits with it deal more (L5 +25); hits
    without it deal less (L2 −30). `Needs key` (a per-character chosen tag).
  - Minor: Narrow Mind (Tâm Hẹp). Your arts without the chosen tag cost 25 % more; with it, 15 % less. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Single-Minded (Chuyên Tâm). Silence on your chosen tag lasts half as long.

### 2. Si Mê (Infatuation)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chosen tag +2 · `art_speed_pct` chosen tag +1
- **Stat notables:** Smitten (Say Mê): chosen tag attack ×2 · Devotion (Tận Tụy): art speed ×2 · Rapt (Đắm Đuối): attack
  +1.5, art speed +1 · Lovesick (Tương Tư): crit damage +3
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Infatuation (Si Mê).** Each hit with your chosen tag gives 1 Obsession (cap 25): L5 +2 each. A hit without
    the tag resets Obsession to 0.
  - Minor: Lingering Obsession (Mê Lâu). A hit without the tag halves Obsession instead of resetting it. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Lost in It (Chìm Đắm). At 25 Obsession, +10 % art speed.

### 3. Quên Mình (Self-Forgetting)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chosen tag +2 · `khi_huyet_pct` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** Forget to Eat (Quên Ăn): attack ×2 · Forget to Sleep (Quên Ngủ): max health ×2 · No Self (Vô Ngã):
  attack +1.5, max health +1 · Absorbed (Nhập Tâm): crit damage +3
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Forget the Self (Vong Ngã).** Every bonus you hold that does not scope to your chosen tag is converted:
    each 1 % of an unscoped damage bonus becomes 1.2 % for the chosen tag. `Needs rule` (a scope conversion of
    modifiers; a big one).
  - Minor: Single Gear (Đồ Duy Nhất). Artifacts whose role matches your tag give +15 % artifact power. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Careless (Bất Cẩn). Your ward strength −15 %, your chosen tag attack +10 %.

### 4. Điên (Madness)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chosen tag +2 · `tam_ma_resist_pct` −1 (a price)
- **Stat notables:** Wild Eyes (Mắt Dại): attack ×2 · Fever Mind (Tâm Sốt): crit damage ×2 · Laughing Madman (Kẻ Điên
  Cười): attack +1.5, crit +1.5 · Mad Strength (Sức Điên): max health +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Madness (Si Cuồng).** Your hits deal more by your heart demon (an in-fight readable, Character Stats rule 9a, R13; L5 +1 per 2 heart demon, cap +40); your qi
    deviation chance is +50 % at breakthroughs.
  - Minor: Embrace It (Chấp Nhận). Heart demon above half its limit no longer lowers your fight stats. *(starter)*
    `Needs key` (heart demon fight penalty).
  - Minor: Fevered (Sốt Ruột). +10 % art speed while heart demon is above half.

### 5. Tuyệt Kỹ (the Peerless Art)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chosen tag +2 · `ngo_tinh` (comprehension) +1
- **Stat notables:** Polished Art (Chiêu Luyện): attack ×2 · Master's Insight (Ngộ Thầy): comprehension ×2 · Ten
  Thousand Repetitions (Vạn Lần): attack +1.5, comprehension +1 · Signature Move (Chiêu Riêng): crit damage +3
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Peerless Art (Tuyệt Kỹ).** Choose one art. Each cultivator it defeats gives it +1 Mastery (permanent, cap
    50): +1 % damage and −0.5 % cost per Mastery. `Needs key`.
  - Minor: Practice (Luyện Tập). Mastery also grows by 1 per season of seclusion. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Known Art (Chiêu Quen). Your Peerless Art's first firing comes 1 s sooner.
