# 55 · Xạ Đạo · *Xạ Nhật* (Shooting the Suns: Bow)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none; needs a `bow` form tag.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct` (bow, physical), `accuracy_pct`, `crit_chance_pct` (bow), `crit_damage_pct` (bow),
> `pierce_pct`, `art_speed_pct` (bow), `than_thuc_pct`, `travel_speed_pct`, creature damage (Needs key).
> **Road:** `accuracy_pct` +0.5.

## Build directions

1. **Never a Miss.** Accuracy past what the foe's evade needs becomes crit chance (Ưng Nhãn), so the build that
   over-stacks accuracy is a crit build that the evade foe cannot dodge.
2. **The Long Shot.** One enormous slow bow art with a draw that grows the longer it waits (Viễn Xạ): a
   damage-per-linh-lực build.
3. **Rain of Arrows.** Volleys: bow arts hit several times at a fraction each; feeds per-hit Effects and breaks
   per-hit wards.
4. **The Quiver.** Spirit arrows paid in thần thức, not linh lực, so the whole pool is ward (Linh Tiễn).
5. **The Hunter.** Bonus against creatures and for hunts; the material-farming Way.

## Facets

### 1. Viễn Xạ (Long Shot)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` bow +2 · `crit_damage_pct` bow +1.5
- **Stat notables:** Heavy Draw (Kéo Nặng): bow ×2 · Far Mark (Đích Xa): bow crit damage ×2 · Steady Arm (Tay
  Vững): bow +1, accuracy +1 · Sun-Shooter's Pull (Lực Xạ Nhật): physical penetration +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Full Draw (Mãn Cung).** While a slow-tagged bow art is ready but held by its guard (not by cost), it gains L5 +5 per second the guard has held it (cap +40), spent on its next firing.
  - Minor: Patient Aim (Ngắm Lâu). Your slow bow arts cannot be evaded. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Loosed (Buông Dây). After a slow bow art fires, your basic attack deals more for 3 s (L5 +20).

### 2. Tiễn Vũ (Rain of Arrows)
- **Small:** `art_speed_pct` bow +1 · `attack_pct` bow +1
- **Stat notables:** Volley (Loạt Tên): bow art speed ×2 · Arrow Storm (Bão Tên): bow +2 · Full Quiver (Ống Đầy):
  art cost −1.5, bow +0.5 · Rapid Nock (Lắp Nhanh): bow art speed +1, bow +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Rain of Arrows (Tiễn Vũ).** Your quick bow arts fire 3 arrows at 40 % each (120 % total); each arrow is
    a hit for Effects and wards. (Effects rule 16, R23; shared with Ngọc's Refract)
  - Minor: Scatter (Tản). Each arrow that lands applies 1 Wind debuff stack for 3 pulses.
  - Minor: Quick Hands (Tay Nhanh). Your basic attack with a bow equipped hits twice at 55 %. *(starter)*

### 3. Ưng Nhãn (Hawk's Eye)
- **Small:** `accuracy_pct` +0.5 · `crit_chance_pct` bow +0.8
- **Stat notables:** Hawk Sight (Mắt Ưng): accuracy ×2 · Dead Centre (Hồng Tâm): bow crit chance ×2 · Clear Air
  (Trời Trong): accuracy +1, crit chance +0.5 · Wind Reading (Đọc Gió): accuracy +1, bow +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Never a Miss (Bách Phát Bách Trúng).** Accuracy above what the target's evade requires converts to crit
    chance at 1 for 2 (so 10 pp spare accuracy gives +5 pp crit), inside the crit cap. `Needs key` (spare accuracy
    readable).
  - Minor: Called Shot (Chỉ Điểm). Your first bow crit each fight applies Exposed for 1 pulse. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Eye on the Heart (Nhắm Tim). Your bow crits on a target above 80 % health deal more (L5 +15).

### 4. Linh Tiễn (Spirit Arrows)
- **Small:** `than_thuc_pct` +0.9 · `attack_pct` bow +1
- **Stat notables:** Spirit Quiver (Ống Linh): thần thức ×2 · Mind Fletching (Lông Tâm): bow +1, thần thức +1 ·
  Ghost Arrow (Tên Ma): soul resistance +2 · Phantom Draw (Kéo Ảo): bow art speed +1, thần thức +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Spirit Arrows (Linh Tiễn).** Your bow arts cost thần thức instead of linh lực (at the same share of
    your thần thức), which regenerates at your linh lực regeneration share.
    (thần thức as a second pool: Combat rule 11, R12; spent thần thức is lost soul defence, which is the build's price).
  - Minor: Thought Arrow (Niệm Tiễn). Your bow hits deal more by your thần thức over the foe's (L5 +1 per 4 % of the
    gap, cap +15).
  - Minor: Unseen Shaft (Tên Vô Hình). Your bow hits cannot be blocked. *(starter)*

### 5. Truy Tung (Tracking)
- **Small:** `travel_speed_pct` +2 · creature damage +2
- **Stat notables:** Spoor (Dấu Vết): creature damage ×2 · Hunter's Pace (Bước Thợ Săn): travel ×2 · Pelt and Horn
  (Da Sừng): material yield +2 · Trap Line (Dây Bẫy): creature damage +1, accuracy +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: The Hunt (Đi Săn).** Creatures you defeat drop one more material roll; hunts you start are Foreseen
    fights (full preparation). `Needs key` (Event Engine).
  - Minor: Wounded Prey (Thú Bị Thương). Your hits deal more to a creature below 50 % health (L5 +15).
  - Minor: Track the Fleeing (Đuổi Theo). Foes cannot escape from you while they bear 5+ Wind debuff stacks.
    *(starter)*
