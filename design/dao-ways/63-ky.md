# 63 · Kỹ Đạo · *Thiên Công* (Heaven's Craftsmanship)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none. Holds the folded Wealth seed (Giám Bảo: spirit stones as
> payment).
> **Theme stats:** `artifact_power_pct`, `artifact_attack_pct`, `artifact_defense_pct`, `talisman_ink_pct`, `formation_power_pct`,
> `than_thuc_cost_pct`, `sell_price_pct`, crafting success keys.
> **Road:** `artifact_power_pct` +0.9.

## Build directions

Theme comes before combat value (dao.md rule 12): most of Kỹ Đạo's strength is in **crafting**, and its fight pieces
reward a character who made their own gear.

1. **Self-Made.** Gear you crafted yourself is stronger (Thiên Công capstone): the crafter's fight bonus. Every
   artifact, talisman and formation from your own hands gets a premium.
2. **The Talisman Brush.** More talismans, stronger talismans, talismans that do not use slots (Họa Phù): the
   PoE trap-and-mine player's build.
3. **The Puppeteer-Smith.** Build puppets (artifacts that act), then fight with them (Khôi Lỗi Thuật; Hồn Đạo uses
   puppets made here).
4. **The Appraiser.** Spirit stones as a combat resource: pay stones to power artifacts in a fight (Giám Bảo).

## Facets

### 1. Luyện Khí (Refining)
- **Small:** refining success +1 · `artifact_power_pct` +0.9
- **Stat notables:** Steady Hammer (Búa Vững): refining ×2 · True Temper (Tôi Đúng): artifact power ×2 · Fine Work (Việc
  Tinh): refining +1, artifact power +1 · Master's Mark (Dấu Thầy): artifact attack +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Made by My Hand (Tự Tay Rèn).** Artifacts you refined yourself have +20 % artifact power when you wear
    them, and +1 quality step chance when refined. `Needs key` (maker field on items).
  - Minor: Salvage (Thu Hồi). Breaking down an artifact returns 30 % more materials. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Quick Forge (Rèn Nhanh). Refining takes 20 % less time.

### 2. Họa Phù (the Talisman Brush)
- **Small:** `talisman_ink_pct` +1 · talisman power +2
- **Stat notables:** Steady Brush (Bút Vững): talisman power ×2 · Rich Ink (Mực Đậm): ink ×2 · Fluent Strokes (Nét Thuần):
  talisman power +1, ink +1 · Paper Stock (Kho Giấy): talisman power +1, crafting speed +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Talisman Sleeve (Tay Áo Phù).** Two talismans you slot take no art slot. `Needs rule` (the one slot pool;
    the catalogue's "Talisman sleeve" capability, given a home).
  - Minor: Extra Copy (Bản Thêm). Talismans you draw come in batches of +1. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Quick Release (Phát Nhanh). Your talismans fire 0.5 s sooner after their guard is met.

### 3. Khôi Lỗi Thuật (Puppets)
- **Small:** `artifact_attack_pct` +0.75 · `than_thuc_cost_pct` −0.8
- **Stat notables:** Jointed Wood (Gỗ Khớp): artifact attack ×2 · Light Frame (Khung Nhẹ): thần thức cost ×2 · Iron
  Puppet (Rối Sắt): artifact defence +2 · Living Strings (Dây Sống): artifact attack +1, thần thức cost −1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Craft Puppets (Chế Khôi Lỗi).** You can refine puppet artifacts: an artifact kind that acts on its own in a
    fight (see Hồn Đạo's Puppet Theatre; without it, one puppet may act at 60 %). `Needs key` + `Needs rule`.
  - Minor: Spare Puppet (Rối Dự Phòng). A broken puppet repairs itself after the fight. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Puppet Armour (Giáp Rối). Each puppet equipped gives +5 block.

### 4. Chế Trận (Formation Making)
- **Small:** `formation_power_pct` +1.2 · formation upkeep −1
- **Stat notables:** Array Craft (Nghề Trận): formation power ×2 · Cheap Stones (Đá Rẻ): upkeep ×2 · Portable Array
  (Trận Mang Theo): formation +1, upkeep −1 · Array Flags (Cờ Trận): defense +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Folding Array (Trận Gấp).** Formations you made can be set up in an ambush fight (not only foreseen) at
    70 % power. `Needs rule` (Combat rule 12).
  - Minor: Array Discs (Đĩa Trận). Formation materials cost 20 % less. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Self-Running Array (Trận Tự Vận). Your formations need no upkeep for the first season.

### 5. Giám Bảo (Appraisal)
- **Small:** `sell_price_pct` +1 · appraisal +1
- **Stat notables:** Keen Eye (Mắt Tinh): appraisal ×2 · Fair Price (Giá Đúng): sell price ×2 · Treasure Sense (Cảm Bảo):
  appraisal +1, fortune +1 · Deep Purse (Túi Sâu): buy price −2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Stones as Fuel (Linh Thạch Làm Khí).** Your artifact actives may be paid in spirit stones instead of linh
    lực, at a fixed rate per realm. `Needs key` (stone payments in fights).
  - Minor: Know the Value (Biết Giá). You see items' true quality before buying. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Lucky Find (Nhặt Được Bảo). Loot rolls +1 quality step chance one time in 10 (counted).
