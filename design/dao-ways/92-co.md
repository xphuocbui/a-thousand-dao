# 92 · Cô Đạo · *Thiên Sát Cô Tinh* (the Lone Star of Calamity)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none. The ◇ Khắc Thân facet (allies weaken) is archived (no allies
> in combat); replaced here by **Cô Kiếm (the Lone Blade: one weapon, no companion)**.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct`, `khi_huyet_pct`, `crit_chance_pct`, `crit_damage_pct`, `evade_pct`, `tam_ma_resist_pct`,
> `travel_speed_pct`.
> **Road:** `attack_pct` +1.2.

## Build directions

1. **Walking Alone.** Power for every missing piece of the social game: no companion, no sect, no Dao partner, no
   disciples (Độc Hành). The solo player's Way, in numbers.
2. **The Lone Star.** A self-buff that grows with time spent travelling alone (Cô Tinh): life-around-the-fight.
3. **The Lone Blade.** One weapon artifact, no others: its power doubles (Cô Kiếm).
4. **Cold Heart.** Heart demon immunity from solitude (Lạnh): a stable build for the long life.

## Facets

### 1. Độc Hành (Walking Alone)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` +1.2 · `khi_huyet_pct` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** One Shadow (Một Bóng): attack ×2 · Own Feet (Chân Mình): max health ×2 · Alone Strong (Một Mình
  Mạnh): attack +1, max health +1 · Self-Reliant (Tự Lực): crit chance +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Walk Alone (Độc Hành).** With no companion attached, your hits deal more (L5 +15).
  - Minor: Lone Wolf (Sói Cô Độc). With no companion, +10 % art speed. *(starter)*
  - Minor: No One to Save (Không Ai Cứu). With no companion, your Down-step last act deals double.

### 2. Vô Môn Phái (No Sect)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` +1 · `travel_speed_pct` +2
- **Stat notables:** Rogue Cultivator (Tán Tu): attack ×2 · Free Road (Đường Tự Do): travel ×2 · Self-Taught (Tự Học):
  comprehension +2 · Wild Grass (Cỏ Dại): max health +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Rogue Cultivator (Tán Tu Chi Đạo).** With no sect, your technique learning is 20 % faster and your fights
    deal +10 % (no sect's protection, no sect's limits). `Needs key` (sect membership readable).
  - Minor: Nobody's Disciple (Đệ Tử Của Không Ai). With no sect, prices for you are not raised by sect enmities.
    *(starter)*
  - Minor: Wanderer's Luck (May Kẻ Lang Thang). With no sect, +10 % fortunate encounters.

### 3. Cô Tinh (the Lone Star)
- **Small:** `crit_chance_pct` +0.4 · `crit_damage_pct` +1.5
- **Stat notables:** Lone Star (Sao Lẻ): crit chance ×2 · Bright Alone (Sáng Một Mình): crit damage ×2 · Distant Light
  (Ánh Xa): crit chance +1, crit damage +1 · Star of Calamity (Sao Sát): attack +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: The Lone Star (Thiên Sát Cô Tinh).** For each season you have travelled without a companion or a sect, +1
    Loneliness (cap 40): +0.5 % crit damage and +0.1 pp crit chance each. A companion or sect resets it. `Needs key`.
  - Minor: Solitary Season (Mùa Cô Độc). Loneliness counts double in seasons of seclusion. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Ill-Starred (Sao Xấu Chiếu). Your crits on a foe with a companion deal +15 %.

### 4. Cô Kiếm (the Lone Blade) — replaces ◇ Khắc Thân
- **Small:** `artifact_attack_pct` +0.75 · `attack_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** One Blade (Một Lưỡi): artifact attack ×2 · Bonded Steel (Thép Kết): attack ×2 · Blade and Man (Người
  Và Kiếm): artifact attack +1, attack +1 · Edge Alone (Lưỡi Đơn): crit damage +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: The Lone Blade (Cô Kiếm).** With exactly one artifact equipped (a weapon), its artifact attack gains +100 %
    (`artifact_attack_pct`, L1; L3 stays gear-only, Combat rule 5).
  - Minor: Light Pack (Hành Trang Nhẹ). With one artifact, your thần thức unused by artifacts adds to soul resistance.
    *(starter)*
  - Minor: Blade Remembers (Kiếm Nhớ). Your one weapon gains +1 % attack per cultivator it has defeated (cap +20 %).
    `Needs key`.

### 5. Lạnh (Coldness)
- **Small:** `tam_ma_resist_pct` +1 · `evade_pct` +0.4
- **Stat notables:** Cold Eyes (Mắt Lạnh): heart-demon resist ×2 · Aloof (Xa Cách): evade ×2 · Ice in the Veins (Băng
  Trong Huyết): heart-demon resist +1, evade +1 · Untouched Heart (Tâm Không Động): status resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Cold Heart (Lãnh Tâm).** You gain no heart demon from loss, grief or betrayal events; fear-type Effects do
    nothing to you. `Needs key` (heart demon sources).
  - Minor: Indifference (Thờ Ơ). Charm and taunt-type Effects on you are refused. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Frost Gaze (Ánh Băng). The foe's first crit each fight is not a crit.
