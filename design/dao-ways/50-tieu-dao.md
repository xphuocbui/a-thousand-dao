# 50 · Tiêu Dao Đạo · *Tiêu Dao Du* (Free Wandering; Free and Easy Wandering)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `travel_speed_pct`, `escape_pct`, `co_duyen_pct`, `evade_pct`, `tam_ma_resist_pct`, `art_speed_pct`,
> `comprehension`.
> **Road:** `travel_speed_pct` +2.

## Build directions

Tiêu Dao is freedom of **movement and choice in the world**. Cô Đạo prices solitude as fight power; Tiêu Dao prices
roaming as fortune and options.

1. **The Wanderer.** Travel fast, meet more chance encounters, never be pinned down: the fortune-farming build (Vân
   Hạc, Kỳ Ngộ).
2. **Never Trapped.** Escape that cannot be stopped (Thoát): the survival archetype NPCs and cautious players want.
3. **The Carefree Heart.** Heart demon resistance and comprehension from an untroubled life (Tự Tại).
4. **The Free Agent.** No sect obligations, all sect markets: trade and learn from everyone (Vô Môn).

## Facets

### 1. Vô Môn (No Sect)
- **Small:** `comprehension` +1 · `buy_price_pct` −1
- **Stat notables:** Open Door (Cửa Mở): comprehension ×2 · Any Market (Chợ Nào Cũng Được): buy price ×2 · Learn
  Anywhere (Học Mọi Nơi): comprehension +1, buy price −1 · Nobody's Man (Người Của Không Ai): fortune +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Guest of Every Sect (Khách Vạn Môn).** With no sect, you may buy from every sect's market at their outer
    disciples' price, and learn one technique a realm from any sect for a quest. `Needs key` (Sects).
  - Minor: No Dues (Không Nộp). With no sect, your mission income is +10 %. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Free Pass (Qua Tự Do). Sect territories do not count you as an intruder.

### 2. Vân Hạc (Clouds and Cranes)
- **Small:** `travel_speed_pct` +2 · `evade_pct` +0.4
- **Stat notables:** Crane's Flight (Hạc Bay): travel ×2 · Cloud Rider (Cưỡi Mây): evade ×2 · Long Road Light Pack
  (Đường Dài Gánh Nhẹ): travel +1, evade +1 · High Wind (Gió Cao): art speed +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Clouds and Cranes (Nhàn Vân Dã Hạc).** Travel is 30 % faster, and travel encounters you choose to avoid are
    always avoided. `Needs key` (Travel).
  - Minor: Wandering Feet (Chân Lang Thang). +5 % travel speed per region visited this year (cap +25 %). *(starter)*
  - Minor: Crane Stance (Thế Hạc). In fights, +3 pp evade while above 80 % health.

### 3. Kỳ Ngộ (Chance Meetings)
- **Small:** `co_duyen_pct` +1 · `comprehension` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** Lucky Road (Đường May): fortune ×2 · Odd Encounter (Gặp Lạ): fortune +1, comprehension +1 · Hidden
  Cave (Động Ẩn): fortune +2 · Old Hermit's Gift (Quà Ẩn Sĩ): comprehension +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Fortune on the Road (Kỳ Ngộ Trên Đường).** Each new region you enter gives a counted chance of a fortunate
    encounter (every 3rd new region guarantees one). `Needs key`.
  - Minor: Eye for Treasure (Mắt Bảo). Fortunate encounters give one more reward roll. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Stranger's Kindness (Lòng Tốt Người Lạ). NPCs met on the road are more often friendly.

### 4. Tự Tại (a Carefree Heart)
- **Small:** `tam_ma_resist_pct` +1 · `health_regen_pct` +0.05
- **Stat notables:** Easy Mind (Tâm Nhàn): heart-demon resist ×2 · Laugh It Off (Cười Xòa): regen ×2 · Unworried (Không
  Lo): heart-demon resist +1, regen +0.05 · Wine and Song (Rượu Và Ca): status resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Carefree (Tiêu Dao Tự Tại).** Heart demon decays 1 per week while travelling, and your breakthrough
    heart-demon checks are +1 pp (q-extras) if you have travelled this season.
  - Minor: Light Heart (Lòng Nhẹ). Fear-type Effects on you are halved. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Free Spirit (Hồn Tự Do). In fights, curses on you last 1 pulse less while above 50 % health.

### 5. Thoát (Escape)
- **Small:** `escape_pct` +1 · `evade_pct` +0.4
- **Stat notables:** Slippery (Trơn Tuột): escape ×2 · Quick Exit (Lối Thoát Nhanh): evade ×2 · Always a Way (Luôn Có
  Lối): escape +1, evade +1 · Vanish (Biến Mất): travel +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Unbound (Vô Câu Vô Thúc).** Your escape art ignores the pursuer's realm and tags, and effects that stop
    escape (No Escape, Sealed Place, Heaven Prison) only halve its chance.
  - Minor: Ready to Run (Sẵn Chạy). Your escape art's cooldown is 30 % shorter. *(starter)*
  - Minor: No Shame (Không Ngại). Fleeing a fight gives no heart demon.

## Kept apart from

Cô Đạo (solitude as fight power), Phong's Lãng Du and Càn Khôn's Súc Địa (travel speed as a side facet), Hồng Trần
(life among mortals). Tiêu Dao is **roaming as fortune, escape that cannot be stopped, a carefree heart**.
