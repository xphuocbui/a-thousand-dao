# 88 · Đại Đạo · *Đại Đạo Chí Giản* (The Great Way Is Simple)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct`, `attack_pct` (basic), `art_cost_pct`, `khi_huyet_pct`, `defense_pct`, `crit_damage_pct`.
> **Road:** `attack_pct` +1.2.

## Build directions

1. **Empty Slots.** Each art slot left empty gives power (Giản): fewer arts, each stronger. The minimalist.
2. **The One.** One art and one ward: the build that slots exactly two things and is good at both (Nhất).
3. **No Forms.** The basic attack grows into the main channel, with no arts at all (Vô Chiêu). The bare-fist
   build's Way twin (Thể Đạo is the body; Đại Đạo is simplicity).
4. **Letting Go.** Fewer pieces of every kind: fewer Daos held, fewer artifacts, fewer techniques, each stronger
   (Bỏ). A life-around build that rewards a focused character.

## Facets

### 1. Giản (Simplicity)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` +1.2 · `art_cost_pct` −0.5
- **Stat notables:** Plain Way (Đường Thẳng): attack ×2 · Less (Ít Hơn): art cost ×2 · Uncluttered (Gọn): attack +1, art
  cost −1 · Clear Purpose (Rõ Ràng): crit damage +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: The Great Way Is Simple (Đại Đạo Chí Giản).** For each empty art slot, your arts deal more (L5 +10 per slot,
    cap +40) and cost 5 % less.
  - Minor: Quiet Room (Phòng Trống). With 2+ empty slots, +10 % linh lực regeneration. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Nothing Extra (Không Thừa). Your arts with no rider deal more (L5 +8).

### 2. Nhất (the One)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` +1 · `defense_pct` +0.6
- **Stat notables:** One Art (Nhất Chiêu): attack ×2 · One Ward (Nhất Hộ): defense ×2 · One Mind (Nhất Tâm): attack +1,
  defense +1 · One Breath (Nhất Khí): max linh lực +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: One Art, One Ward (Nhất Chiêu Nhất Hộ).** While you slot exactly one damaging art and one ward, the art
    deals more (L5 +35) and the ward is 35 % stronger.
  - Minor: Single Focus (Chuyên Nhất). Your one art's cooldown is 10 % shorter. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Single Guard (Hộ Nhất). Your one ward costs 20 % less.

### 3. Bỏ (Letting Go)
- **Small:** `khi_huyet_pct` +0.5 · `attack_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Let It Fall (Buông): attack ×2 · Light Pack (Hành Lý Nhẹ): max health ×2 · Unattached (Không Vướng):
  attack +1, max health +1 · Clear Hands (Tay Trắng): status resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Letting Go (Xả Bỏ).** For each Dao slot you leave unglimpsed (of the 4 a life can hold), +4 % to every stat
    line in this Dao; for each artifact slot left empty, +3 % max health. `Needs key` (empty Dao slots readable).
  - Minor: Few Things (Ít Đồ). With 2 or fewer artifacts, your arts cost 10 % less. *(starter)*
  - Minor: One Technique (Nhất Pháp). With no auxiliary technique, +8 % to your main technique's bonuses.

### 4. Quy Chân (Return to the True)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` +1 · `crit_damage_pct` +1.5
- **Stat notables:** True Form (Chân Hình): attack ×2 · Original Face (Bản Lai): crit damage ×2 · Raw Strength (Lực
  Thô): attack +1, crit damage +1 · Unadorned (Mộc Mạc): defense +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Return to the True (Phản Phác Quy Chân).** Your hits ignore the foe's buff stacks and conversions applied
    to its defences (its element buffs do not reduce your damage). `Needs rule`.
  - Minor: Plain Hit (Đòn Mộc). Your basic attack ignores 15 resistance points. *(starter)*
  - Minor: No Tricks (Không Mánh). Feints and false images do not fool you (your hits ignore Afterimages).

### 5. Vô Chiêu (No Forms)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` basic +2 · `khi_huyet_pct` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** Formless Fist (Quyền Vô Chiêu): basic ×2 · Natural Strike (Đòn Tự Nhiên): basic +1, crit damage +1 ·
  Effortless (Không Gắng): basic +1, max health +1 · Every Move (Mọi Động Tác): crit chance +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: No Forms Beat All Forms (Vô Chiêu Thắng Hữu Chiêu).** With no damaging art slotted, your basic attack deals
    more (L5 +50) and its cooldown is 20 % shorter.
  - Minor: Ordinary Hand (Tay Thường). Your basic attack crits with +5 pp. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Flowing Form (Chiêu Theo Thế). Each basic hit gives +1 % basic attack damage for the fight (cap +30 %).

## Kept apart from

Kiếm's One Sword (one sword art), Chưởng's Empty Hand (palm-only empty slots), Thể's Không Thủ (bare hands as the
body), Vô Cực (emptiness of the pool). Đại Đạo is **fewer pieces, each stronger, everywhere in the build**.
