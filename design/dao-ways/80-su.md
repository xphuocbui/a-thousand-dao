# 80 · Sư Đạo · *Truyền Đăng* (Passing the Lamp)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none. The ◇ Đệ Tử (Disciples who fight) facet is archived; replaced
> here by **Môn Hạ (Pupils in the World)**: disciples who live, learn and act as NPCs, never in your fights.
> **Theme stats:** `ngo_tinh` (comprehension), renown, `than_thuc_pct`, `tam_ma_resist_pct`, technique mastery, `attack_pct`.
> **Road:** `ngo_tinh` (comprehension) +1.

## Build directions

1. **The Teacher.** Teaching makes you learn: every season spent teaching gives you comprehension (Giảng Đạo). A
   comprehension build that plays the world.
2. **The Lineage.** Arts you pass down grow in your hands too (Đạo Thống): techniques you have taught gain mastery.
3. **The Mentor.** Help NPCs break through; their success gives you renown and a Dao Heart-like reward (Chỉ Điểm).
4. **The Legacy.** What outlives you: a legacy item and a lineage record, and a bonus to your next life if the game
   allows lives in a row (Di Sản).

## Facets

### 1. Môn Hạ (Pupils in the World) — replaces ◇ Đệ Tử
- **Small:** renown +1 · `ngo_tinh` (comprehension) +1
- **Stat notables:** Many Pupils (Nhiều Trò): renown ×2 · Patient Master (Thầy Kiên Nhẫn): comprehension ×2 · Open Door
  (Cửa Mở Rộng): renown +1, comprehension +1 · Master's Robe (Áo Thầy): max health +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Take Pupils (Thu Đồ).** You may take up to 3 NPC pupils (events). Each gives +2 % comprehension and does
    small tasks each season (gathers herbs, sells goods). `Needs key` (NPC pupils).
  - Minor: Good Teacher (Thầy Giỏi). Your pupils learn 25 % faster. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Pupil's Report (Trò Báo Tin). Each pupil brings one rumour a season.

### 2. Giảng Đạo (Teaching)
- **Small:** `ngo_tinh` (comprehension) +1 · `than_thuc_pct` +0.5
- **Stat notables:** Lecture Hall (Giảng Đường): comprehension ×2 · Clear Words (Lời Rõ): thần thức ×2 · To Teach Is to
  Learn (Dạy Là Học): comprehension +1, thần thức +1 · Chalk and Slate (Phấn Bảng): heart-demon resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Teaching Is Learning (Giáo Học Tương Trưởng).** A season spent teaching (instead of seclusion) gives 60 % of
    seclusion's progress and +1 % technique mastery for every pupil (cap +5 %).
  - Minor: Explain It (Giảng Giải). Technique mastery +5 %. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Question from a Pupil (Câu Hỏi Của Trò). Once a year, a pupil's question gives you a Clarity (Tâm Đạo's key).

### 3. Đạo Thống (the Lineage of Arts)
- **Small:** technique mastery +1 · `attack_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Orthodox Line (Chính Thống): mastery ×2 · Handed Down Art (Chiêu Truyền): attack ×2 · Lineage Seal
  (Ấn Dòng): mastery +1, attack +1 · Founder's Shadow (Bóng Tổ Sư): renown +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Lineage of Arts (Đạo Thống).** Each technique you have taught to a pupil gives its arts +8 % damage in your
    hands.
  - Minor: Faithful Copy (Bản Chép Trung Thành). Techniques you teach cost no manual. *(starter)*
  - Minor: School Name (Danh Môn). Your lineage is a World Database fact; NPCs of your lineage are friendly.

### 4. Chỉ Điểm (Guidance)
- **Small:** renown +1 · `tam_ma_resist_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Pointing Finger (Ngón Chỉ): renown ×2 · Calm Guide (Người Dẫn Lặng): heart-demon resist ×2 · Wise
  Word (Lời Khôn): renown +1, heart-demon resist +1 · Mentor's Eye (Mắt Người Dẫn): comprehension +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Guide the Breakthrough (Chỉ Điểm Đột Phá).** When you help an NPC break through (an event), its chance
    rises, and its success gives you 2 skill points once per realm. `Needs key` + Meridian Map points.
  - Minor: A Word in Time (Lời Đúng Lúc). Helping NPCs removes 3 heart demon. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Grateful Student (Trò Biết Ơn). Guided NPCs owe you a debt (Thệ's key).

### 5. Di Sản (Legacy)
- **Small:** renown +1 · `attack_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Lasting Name (Danh Lưu): renown ×2 · Heirloom (Gia Bảo): attack ×2 · Written Legacy (Di Thư): renown
  +1, attack +1 · Lamp Passed (Đèn Truyền): comprehension +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Pass the Lamp (Truyền Đăng).** When you die, one pupil inherits a chosen technique and one artifact; if the
    game continues with a successor, the successor starts with them. `Needs rule` (succession, a big question).
  - Minor: Will (Di Chúc). You may write a will to direct your goods. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Remembered Master (Thầy Được Nhớ). Your renown does not decay after 50.
