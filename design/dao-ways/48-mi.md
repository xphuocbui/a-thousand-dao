# 48 · Mị Đạo · *Khuynh Thành* (City-Toppling Beauty: Charm)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none. The ◇ Ngưỡng Mộ (Admirers: followers) facet is archived;
> replaced here by **Liếc Mắt (the Glance)**.
> **Theme stats:** `buy_price_pct`, `sell_price_pct`, renown, `status_duration_pct`, `attack_pct` (fire, soul), `evade_pct`,
> `co_duyen_pct`.
> **Road:** `status_duration_pct` +2.

## Build directions

1. **No One Will Strike You.** Enthral: the foe's arts hesitate, and an Enthralled foe deals less (Mê Hoặc). A
   control build that wins by the foe acting less.
2. **Fox Fire.** A Fire and Soul hybrid: fox fire hits ward and soul at once (Hồ Hỏa).
3. **The Silver Tongue.** Prices, standing and event persuasion: the social life build (Thuyết Phục).
4. **The Glance.** A first-moment charm: the foe's first art is delayed and its first hit softened (Liếc Mắt).

## Facets

### 1. Mị Lực (Allure)
- **Small:** renown +1 · `evade_pct` +0.4
- **Stat notables:** Grace of Face (Nhan Sắc): renown ×2 · Elusive (Khó Nắm): evade ×2 · Bewitching (Mê Hồn): renown +1,
  evade +1 · Fragrance (Hương Thơm Tỏa): soul resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Allure (Mị Lực).** Foes whose realm is not above yours deal 10 % less to you, and their crits against you
    deal 20 % less.
  - Minor: Soft Gaze (Ánh Mắt Dịu). The foe's first hit each fight is dampened 30 % (rule 6a). *(starter)*
  - Minor: Captivating (Quyến Rũ). Events that offer persuasion choices always offer one to you.

### 2. Thuyết Phục (Persuasion)
- **Small:** `buy_price_pct` −1 · `sell_price_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Honeyed Words (Lời Ngọt): buy price ×2 · Fair Trade (Buôn Khéo): sell price ×2 · Silver Tongue (Lưỡi
  Bạc): buy −1, sell +1 · Many Friends (Nhiều Bạn): fortune +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Turn Enemies (Hóa Địch Thành Bạn).** Once a season, a hostile NPC encounter may be talked down (no fight),
    and the NPC becomes neutral. `Needs key` (Event Engine).
  - Minor: Discount (Bớt Giá). −5 % prices at every shop. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Favour Owed (Nợ Ân Tình). Talked-down NPCs owe you a favour (an event later).

### 3. Mê Hoặc (Enthral)
- **Small:** `status_duration_pct` +2 · `attack_pct` soul +1
- **Stat notables:** Spellbound (Mê Mẩn): status duration ×2 · Siren (Giọng Ma Mị): soul ×2 · Trance (Thôi Miên): status
  duration +1, soul +1 · Sweet Poison (Độc Ngọt): poison +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Enthral (Mê Hoặc).** Your soul hits apply Heavy Breath (1 stack) and Sealed Meridian on the foe's L2, as one Enthral application (6 s reapply lockout).
  - Minor: Lingering Charm (Mê Lâu). Enthralled lasts 1 pulse longer. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Charmed Wound (Vết Thương Ngọt). Your hits on an Enthralled foe leech 3 % health.

### 4. Hồ Hỏa (Fox Fire)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` fire +1 · `attack_pct` soul +1
- **Stat notables:** Nine Tails (Cửu Vĩ): fire +1, soul +1 ×2 · Blue Fox Flame (Lửa Cáo Xanh): soul ×2 · Will-o'-Fox (Lửa
  Ma Cáo): fire stack effect +2 · Fox's Cunning (Cáo Khôn): evade +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Fox Fire (Hồ Hỏa).** Your Fire arts gain a Soul part equal to 15 % of their damage (meets only soul
    resistance). A conversion to Soul is forbidden, so this is an added part. `Needs rule` (an added Soul part on a
    non-soul art).
  - Minor: Fox Light (Ánh Cáo). Your Fire hits apply 1 Slackened Soul stack every 4th hit. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Nine Lives (Chín Mạng). Once per fight, at the Down step's refusal, refuse it (health 1).

### 5. Liếc Mắt (the Glance) — replaces ◇ Ngưỡng Mộ
- **Small:** `evade_pct` +0.4 · `status_duration_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Sidelong Look (Liếc Ngang): evade ×2 · Lowered Lashes (Mi Cụp): status duration ×2 · One Glance
  (Một Cái Nhìn): evade +1, status duration +1 · City-Toppling (Khuynh Thành): renown +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: One Glance Topples a City (Nhất Cố Khuynh Thành).** At fight start, the foe is Enthralled for 3 s (no
    lockout on this one) and its first art waits 1 s.
  - Minor: Second Glance (Tái Cố). The opening Enthral lasts 1 s longer. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Look Away (Quay Đi). While Enthralled, the foe cannot crit.
