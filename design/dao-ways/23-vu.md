# 23 · Vụ Đạo · *Yên Vũ* (Misty Rain)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** Water ≥ 30. Holds the seed Mist (†).
> **Theme stats:** `evade_pct`, `accuracy_pct` (negative to foe via Effects only), `escape_pct`, `travel_speed_pct`,
> `health_regen_pct`, `linh_luc_regen_pct`, `attack_pct` (force, qi_disruption, cold), `stack_effect_pct` (ice),
> `status_duration_pct`.
> **Road:** `evade_pct` +0.4.

## Build directions

1. **The Veil.** Start every fight hidden (a new state, Veiled): the foe's hits are likelier to miss until you
   strike, and your first strike out of the veil is a guaranteed crit. Re-veil on a timer. Ambush burst.
2. **The Drowning.** Qi Disruption and a new linh lực tick, Drowned, empty the foe's pool through every shield;
   pairs with Băng's cost lock. A starve build.
3. **The Morning Dew.** Regeneration of both pools from a mist batch that grows while you are untouched; a
   sustain build for long fights.
4. **The Cloud Walker.** Escape and travel: the NPC who always gets away.

## Facets

### 1. Màn Sương (the Veil) †
- **Small:** `evade_pct` +0.4 · `escape_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Thick Fog (Sương Dày): evade ×2 · Grey Curtain (Màn Xám): evade +1, escape +1 · Ghost Shape
  (Bóng Mờ): evade +1, crit chance +0.5 · Hidden Road (Lối Ẩn): escape ×2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Into the Mist (Nhập Vụ).** You start each fight Veiled: foe hits on you have −25 pp accuracy. Your
    first hit breaks the veil and crits for certain. You re-veil after 8 s without being hit.
    `Needs key` (Veiled, a self-buff state).
  - Minor: Mist Step (Vụ Bộ). When you evade, gain +10 pp evade for 1 pulse (no stacking).
  - Minor: Shroud (Che Phủ). Your first art each fight cannot be evaded. *(starter)*

### 2. Hàn Vụ (Cold Mist)
- **Small:** `stack_effect_pct` ice +1.5 · `attack_pct` cold +1
- **Stat notables:** Freezing Fog (Sương Buốt): ice stack effect ×2 · Wet Chill (Lạnh Ẩm): cold ×2 · Icy Breath
  (Hơi Băng): ice +1, cold +1 · Damp Bones (Xương Ẩm): ice duration ×2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Mist in the Lungs (Vụ Phế).** Each pulse, apply 1 Ice stack and −1 pp accuracy (a new debuff,
    Fogged) to the foe for 6 pulses; Fogged stacks have no cap.
  - Minor: Dampened (Ẩm Ướt). Your Ice stacks on the foe also cut its Fire damage by 0.5 % each (cap 25 %).
  - Minor: Grey Cold (Hàn Xám). Your Cold hits on a Fogged foe deal more (L5 +10). *(starter)*

### 3. Mây (Clouds)
- **Small:** `travel_speed_pct` +2 · `escape_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Cloud Sea (Biển Mây): travel ×2 · Drifting Cloud (Mây Trôi): escape ×2 · High Road (Đường
  Cao): travel +1, evade +1 · Cloud Gate (Cổng Mây): escape +1, linh lực regen +0.05
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Walk on Clouds (Đạp Vân).** Your escape art's chance is not lowered by the pursuer's realm, and a
    failed escape veils you (as Into the Mist) for 4 s.
  - Minor: Lost in Cloud (Lạc Mây). Hostile travel encounters find you 20 % less often. `Needs key` (Travel).
  - Minor: Cloud Cushion (Đệm Mây). The first hit each fight that would take you below 30 % health is halved.

### 4. Sương Mai (Dew)
- **Small:** `health_regen_pct` +0.05 · `linh_luc_regen_pct` +0.075
- **Stat notables:** Morning Dew (Sương Sớm): health regen ×2 · Dew Drink (Uống Sương): linh lực regen ×2 · Damp
  Earth (Đất Ẩm): both regens +0.05/+0.04 · Dawn Mist (Sương Bình Minh): regen increased +4
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Gathered Dew (Tụ Lộ).** For each full second you are not hit, gain 1 Dew stack (cap 30): +0.05 pp
    regeneration of both pools each. A hit removes a third of them.
  - Minor: Dew on the Blade (Lộ Nhận). When your pool is full, 50 % of the regeneration you lose heals health.
  - Minor: Soft Mist (Vụ Nhu). Your regeneration is not dampened by the first 20 Stagnation stacks. *(starter)*

### 5. Chìm (Drowning)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` force +1.5 · `status_duration_pct` +2
- **Stat notables:** Undertow (Cuốn Xuống): force ×2 · Heavy Water (Nước Nặng): qi disruption damage ×2 · Lungful
  (Ngộp): status duration ×2 · Sinking (Chìm Dần): force +1, status duration +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Drown (Nhấn Chìm).** Your hits on a target below 50 % linh lực apply Drowned: a linh lực tick of 0.6 %
    of its max linh lực per pulse for 4 pulses, stacking in batches (no cap). It passes every shield.
    `Needs key` (Drowned, an element-free qi tick).
  - Minor: Waterlogged (Sũng Nước). A target with 0 linh lực takes more from your hits (L5 +15). *Shared minor*
    (with Phệ, Thâu).
  - Minor: Pressure (Áp Lực). Your Qi Disruption may stack to 4. *(starter)*

- Escape art outside the slot pool: `Needs rule` (Combat rule 13).

first-strike crit; Vụ re-veils and fogs). Băng Đạo (cost lock). Vụ is **the veil, the fog and the drowning**.
