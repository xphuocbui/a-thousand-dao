# 26 · Ám Đạo · *Dạ Hành* (Night Walker)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct` (chaos, darkness), `crit_chance_pct`, `crit_damage_pct`, `evade_pct`, `escape_pct`,
> `accuracy_pct`, `resist_pen_pct` (chaos), `status_duration_pct` (blind).
> **Road:** `crit_damage_pct` +1.5.

## Build directions

1. **The Assassin.** Everything into the hits that land before the foe has hit you (Thích Khách): first-strike
   crits, execution under 30 %. A front-loaded build with a real plan for when the first strike fails.
2. **The Long Night.** Power that grows with fight time (Đêm Sâu): weak at 5 s, monstrous at 40 s; it must
   survive the opening, so it pairs with sustain.
3. **Blindfold.** A new curse, Blinded, cuts the foe's accuracy and crit chance; with evade, the foe stops landing
   hits at all. A defensive darkness build.
4. **The Shade.** Escape and re-entry: leave the fight's first exchange and return with an ambush (Ảnh Bộ).

## Facets

### 1. Bóng Tối (Darkness)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chaos +1.5 · `status_duration_pct` blind +2
- **Stat notables:** Ink Night (Đêm Mực): chaos ×2 · Long Dark (Tối Dài): blind duration ×2 · Moonless (Không
  Trăng): chaos +1, chaos penetration +1 · Shadow Weight (Bóng Nặng): chaos crit damage +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Blinding Dark (Mù Tối).** Your Chaos hits apply Blinded: −10 pp accuracy and −5 pp crit chance, 3
    pulses, stacks to 3. `Needs key` (new curse).
  - Minor: Lightless (Vô Quang). Exposed on you is halved in strength. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Dark Adapted (Quen Tối). Your accuracy is +10 pp against a foe that has evaded you this fight.

### 2. Ẩn (Hiding)
- **Small:** `evade_pct` +0.4 · `escape_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Still Shadow (Bóng Lặng): evade ×2 · Hidden Blade (Đao Ẩn): crit chance +1, evade +1 · Silent
  Breath (Tức Câm): escape ×2 · Behind the Screen (Sau Bình Phong): evade +1, crit damage +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Unseen Hand (Ám Thủ).** For the first 8 s of a fight, your art hits deal more (L5 +30) and crit with +15 pp chance.
  - Minor: Night Tithe (Dạ Thuế). Your first 3 art hits each fight restore 0.8 % of max linh lực each. *(starter; the by-root keystone, Way form)*
  - Minor: Slip Away (Lẩn). The first hit that lands on you each fight is dampened 40 % (rule 6a).

### 3. Thích Khách (the Assassin)
- **Small:** `crit_chance_pct` +0.4 · `crit_damage_pct` +1.5
- **Stat notables:** Needle Point (Mũi Kim): crit chance ×2 · Heart Strike (Kích Tâm): crit damage ×2 · Quiet Kill
  (Giết Lặng): crit chance +1, crit damage +1 · Vein Cut (Cắt Mạch): chaos penetration +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Mark for Death (Tử Ấn).** Your crits on a target below 30 % health deal more (L5 +40) and restore 3 % of max linh lực.
  - Minor: Opening (Sơ Hở). Your first art each fight crits for certain. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Bleed in the Dark (Huyết Ám). Your crits apply 1 Bleed stack.

### 4. Đêm Sâu (Deep Night)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` darkness +1.5 · `health_regen_pct` +0.05
- **Stat notables:** Midnight (Nửa Đêm): darkness ×2 · Night Owl (Cú Đêm): regen ×2 · Third Watch (Canh Ba): chaos
  +1, regen +0.05 · Night Chill (Lạnh Đêm): max health +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: The Night Deepens (Đêm Sâu Thêm).** For every 5 s of fight, your hits deal more (L5 +6, cap +60).
  - Minor: Dusk (Hoàng Hôn Xuống). After 30 s of fight, your evade is +5 pp. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Night Air (Khí Đêm). After 20 s of fight, your regeneration of both pools is 30 % higher.

### 5. Ảnh Bộ (Shade Step)
- **Small:** `escape_pct` +1 · `evade_pct` +0.4
- **Stat notables:** Shade Walk (Bước Bóng): escape ×2 · Flicker (Chập Chờn): evade ×2 · Long Shadow (Bóng Dài):
  escape +1, crit chance +0.5 · Gone (Biến): evade +1, escape +1
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Step into Shadow (Nhập Ảnh).** A new art-free action: once per fight, when your health falls below 40 %,
    you vanish for 3 s (untargetable: no hits land, ticks still tick) and return with Unseen Hand restored.
    `Needs rule` (a side cannot be untargetable today).
  - Minor: Night Run (Chạy Đêm). Your escape art chance +20 % after 20 s of fight.
  - Minor: Shadow Swap (Hoán Ảnh). When you evade a crit, gain 1 s on every art timer. *(starter)*
