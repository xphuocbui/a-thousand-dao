# 43 · Ma Đạo · *Thiên Ma* (the Heavenly Demon)

> Overnight draft 2026-11-11. Proposal. **Needs:** none.
> **Theme stats:** `attack_pct` (chaos), `resist_pen_pct` (chaos), `khi_huyet_pct`, `leech_health_pct`, `art_cost_health_pct`,
> `tam_ma_resist_pct` (negative), `crit_damage_pct`.
> **Road:** `attack_pct` chaos +2.

## Build directions

1. **Demon Power.** Heart demon is a **second pool**: arts can be paid in tâm ma instead of linh lực (Ma Lực), so a
   cultivator with a high heart demon has a second mana bar. Distinct from Si (heart demon as a damage multiplier).
2. **Blood Sacrifice.** Sacrifice before a fight (health, years, a captured creature) for a fight-long power (Huyết
   Tế). The pre-fight ritual build.
3. **Corruption.** Convert damage to Chaos and spread Chaos statuses; the corruption build that wears down
   resistance (Ma Hóa).
4. **Forbidden Arts.** Arts with a cost outside the fight (lifespan, heart demon, renown) and huge power (Cấm
   Thuật). The world remembers who uses them (Thiên Đạo punishes them).

## Facets

### 1. Ma Lực (Heart Demon Power)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chaos +1.5 · `tam_ma_resist_pct` −1 (a price)
- **Stat notables:** Demon Sea (Biển Ma): chaos ×2 · Black Heart (Tâm Đen): crit damage ×2 · Whispering Voice (Giọng
  Thì Thầm): chaos +1, crit damage +1 · Demon Vein (Mạch Ma): max health +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Demon Power (Ma Lực).** Your arts may be paid in heart demon instead of linh lực (1 heart demon per 1 % of
    max linh lực of cost; the heart demon is spent, lowering tâm ma). The heart demon pool is Combat rule 11 (R12); the in-fight readable is R13.
  - Minor: Feed the Demon (Nuôi Ma). Each fight you win adds 5 heart demon. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Demon's Breath (Hơi Ma). While paying in heart demon, your Chaos hits deal more (L5 +12).

### 2. Huyết Tế (Blood Sacrifice)
- **Small:** `khi_huyet_pct` +0.5 · `attack_pct` +1
- **Stat notables:** Altar of Blood (Huyết Đàn): max health ×2 · Offering (Tế Phẩm): attack ×2 · Red Rite (Nghi Lễ Đỏ):
  attack +1, max health +1 · Dark Pact (Khế Ước Đen): crit damage +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Blood Sacrifice (Huyết Tế).** Before a foreseen fight, sacrifice one: 20 % of max health (start at 80 %), 3 months of lifespan, or a captured creature. Each gives L5 +25 for the fight (only one per fight). A preparation choice (Effects rule 8, Combat rule 12, R8): foreseen fights only.
  - Minor: Small Rite (Tế Nhỏ). A 10 % health sacrifice gives L5 +12. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Blood Returns (Máu Trả). Win a fight after a sacrifice: 20 % health is restored after the fight (reported to the caller).

### 3. Ma Hóa (Corruption)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` chaos +1.5 · `resist_pen_pct` chaos +0.6
- **Stat notables:** Taint (Ô Nhiễm): chaos ×2 · Rot of Heart (Mục Tâm): chaos penetration ×2 · Black Spread (Lan Đen):
  chaos +1, penetration +1 · Corrupt Blood (Máu Hư): leech +0.5
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Corruption (Ma Hóa).** Your hits convert 30 % to Chaos, and your Chaos crits apply Sundered (Chaos) and Withering.
  - Minor: Dark Drink (Uống Ma). Your Chaos hits leech 2 % of the damage they deal as health. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Spread the Rot (Lan Mục). While the foe is Sundered (Chaos) by you, each of your Chaos hits applies 2 Poison stacks for 4 pulses.

### 4. Cấm Thuật (Forbidden Arts)
- **Small:** `attack_pct` +1.2 · `crit_damage_pct` +1.5
- **Stat notables:** Forbidden Scroll (Quyển Cấm): attack ×2 · Price Paid (Đã Trả Giá): crit damage ×2 · Dark Knowledge
  (Tri Thức Đen): attack +1, comprehension +1 · Sealed Chapter (Chương Phong Ấn): thần thức +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Forbidden Arts (Cấm Thuật).** Arts tagged `forbidden` (from forbidden techniques) cost 30 % less in their
    outside-the-fight price (lifespan, heart demon) and deal L5 +15. Each use is a World Database fact.
    `Needs key` (`forbidden` art tag).
  - Minor: Hidden Practice (Tu Lén). Forbidden-art use is recorded only if the foe survives. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Damned Anyway (Đằng Nào Cũng Đọa). With 3+ forbidden arts slotted, +10 % to all damage.

### 5. Ma Thân (Demon Body)
- **Small:** `khi_huyet_pct` +0.5 · `leech_health_pct` +0.3
- **Stat notables:** Demon Flesh (Thịt Ma): max health ×2 · Hungry Skin (Da Đói): leech ×2 · Horned (Mọc Sừng): max health
  +1, leech +0.3 · Black Blood (Máu Đen): chaos resist +2
- **Effects:**
  - **Major: Demon Body (Ma Thân).** Your health regeneration is dampened by 300 (a quarter), but your leech is tripled. Your leech also reads ticks. (Effects rule 16, R37)
  - Minor: Demon Hide (Da Ma). Chaos damage on you −20 %. *(starter)*
  - Minor: Monstrous (Quái Dị). Light damage on you +20 %, Chaos and Cold −15 %.

## Kept apart from

demon as a second mana pool, sacrifice before the fight, and corruption**.
