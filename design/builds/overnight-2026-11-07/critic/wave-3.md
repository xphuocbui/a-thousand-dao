# Critic: Wave 3

Basis: all 10 wave 3 sheets read in full, plus index, ledger, health, forge, wave 2 and 12 plug-ins. All five wave 2 orders were carried out, and all five first-pick fantasies now exist.
**Biggest finding:** the numbers are honest and the builds are bold, but the ledger holds almost nothing a player would stack every day. The user's top priority is the thinnest thing here.

## 5. Rule discoveries (for the user; none is a reason to shave a build)

1. **Nothing without a hit is priced.** Masks, curses, escape arts and talismans have no lint. A 0.06 mask stops any blow of any size, and 0.09 reverses the fight. Same family as Forum Build's finding: the lint sees too little.
2. **Rule 15 is being read as "make it a counter".** Three authors did so alone. A counted crit beats a rolled one on the same stats (Loud Sword: a loss on 44 % becomes a win on 31 %). One ruling, with a price, would serve all three.
3. **A ledger is a 20-second invulnerability.** Slow Wound turns any blow into 5 % a second, and nothing says what happens to it on flight, between waves or after Down. Three sheets ask.
4. **Anchors are loaded guns pointed both ways.** With no clip, a Core fool kills a Deity top-end in 6 s. Any self-hit needs a limit by rule, not by sheet.
5. **Who opens matters, and nobody chooses.** Rule 10 turns Understudy against Forum Build from a 2 s loss to a 32 s win. Best Plan's ambusher hits the same gap.
6. **Real faults in the GDDs.** Formulas K's reserve has no ceiling (2,000 Qi Refining beat a sect at R 5.44), and AC-CB-49 and AC-CB-50 do not match their formulas, per Ant General. Is Formulas F's burn cap per status or per pulse? Is a curse on `hit` meant to silence every hit art? Tribulation rule 7 leaves healing inside a wave open: Still Water alone is 0.117 to 1.000, and a monk with no damage survives 0.942.
7. **Best Plan's economy is not "ordinary".** A top-end's first blow passes the 2,500 % that caps the estate claim at 25 %, so the Nerve Table's greed binds only against equals. Each entry is +1 heart demon and each funeral −3, so fleeing the old is a net cure. Unmodelled, and it wants a ruling.
8. **`fight_unit` is the one uncapped number in Combat.** Not-Yet-Full shows the world's whole power budget fits under one Deity's. Is that the intended scale?
