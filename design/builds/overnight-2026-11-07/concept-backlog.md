# Concept Backlog

Starting seeds for the overnight campaign, grouped to force variety. Agents may follow a stronger idea.
The orchestrator assigns these in waves and adds new seeds from the critic. `[done]` marks a used seed.

## A. Neglected channels

1. The basic attack as the whole build: empty art slots are the resource, a fist saint with no arts.
2. The companion is the fighter and the cultivator is its support; the beast is the build, not an add-on.
3. Every equipped artifact fights on its own timer: an artifact swarm limited only by thần thức.
4. A pure soul assassin who never touches a layer and bends `soul_lockout`.
5. Win through the enemy's own ward: hits sized so their drain ruins them. A creditor, not a fighter.
6. Tiny hits that only exist to fire status triggers, because the trigger budget counts damage, not statuses.
7. Famine: pure qi burn, where the enemy's linh lực at zero is the win condition.
8. No offence at all: every point of damage comes from being struck.

## B. The life around the fight

9. A poisoner who keeps their own pill toxicity high on purpose and fights with it.
10. Failed breakthroughs as power: scar tissue on the soul, a build that wants to fail.
11. Stronger as lifespan runs out: the last decade of a life, with a known date.
12. Heart demon as a second resource pool: a bargain with what lives in you.
13. Spirit stones carried are power, and arts can be paid in money: the merchant immortal.
14. A luck thief: fortune (khí vận) stolen from the enemy, where chance is the build.
15. Decades of closed-door seclusion banked and spent in one fight.
16. A tally of cultivators defeated that grows forever, and what the world does about it.
17. Always wounded: low vitality as the operating state.
18. One drop of perfect purity: everything sacrificed for a single element's purity.
19. The worst root in the world: five weak elements, scaling per element present.
20. Never upgraded: a lowest-tier technique mastered past all reason.

## C. Time and rules

21. A stalemate counts as a win: the immovable saint who only has to last.
22. Wins double knockouts by rule: a build that plans to die with you.
23. A dying side acts until the instant ends: everything happens in the last instant.
24. One art that waits the entire fight: a single cut, 40 seconds in the drawing.
25. Acts only in follow-up instants: the pure riposte.
26. Slot order as a puzzle: firing in the generating cycle.
27. A build that needs the full 40 seconds and grows every second of it.
28. Everything in one breath: all power at the first moment, and nothing after.
29. Borrows cooldown from the future and pays it back later.

## D. Linh lực and wards

30. Runs on an empty pool deliberately: power from having nothing.
31. Never spends: power from a full sea.
32. Arts paid in blood, with a way to make that sustainable.
33. Costs are shares of what is left, so the pool never empties and never fills.

36. Two wards at once, and what that breaks.

## E. Preparation

40. Twenty talismans: a gambler's sleeve.
