# 4. The Builds

All 69 overnight builds in plain words. **Enabler** is the one piece that makes the build possible. **Key effect** is the modifier or rule that makes it work. "None" means the build is a plain stack of ordinary rows, with no special piece. Proposals only. Each name links to the full sheet.

## Builds with a special enabler

### The first ten

| Build | Concept | Enabler | Key effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Brush Fee](../sheets/brush-fee.md) | Copy the enemy's arts and make them pay for the copies. | **Stele-Rubbing Scripture**, a công pháp whose art slots copy whatever art the enemy fires that instant. | **Brush Fee:** a copied art's linh lực cost is charged to the original caster. |
| [Fortieth Breath](../sheets/fortieth-breath.md) | An executioner who never strikes until one huge cut at the end. | **Withheld Hand:** your basic attack never fires. | Each skipped basic attack adds damage to your slowest art, until that art fires. |
| [Full Price](../sheets/full-price.md) | Pay for arts in spirit stones, so money is power. | **Seal of Full Payment**, an artifact: arts and ward cost stones, not linh lực. | **Priced Above the Sea:** a stone-paid art may cost more than your whole pool, so a rich player buys one giant opening blow. |
| [Great Tonic](../sheets/great-tonic.md) | A healer who never strikes back. The enemy's own blows overfill them until they burst. | **Feeding-Back Ward:** damage it blocks is pushed into the attacker's linh lực. | Past their maximum, the excess becomes **Surfeit**, a new rupture damage. |
| [Incense Vigil](../sheets/incense-vigil.md) | Stand still, take everything, win by outlasting the time limit, then pay in years of life. | **Incense Vow:** a stalemate counts as your win if the enemy started the fight. | **Slow Wound:** damage goes on a ledger that is paid slowly. What is left at the end costs lifespan. |
| [Receiving Hand](../sheets/receiving-hand.md) | Fight with empty hands. Every empty art slot catches one enemy art. | **Vow of the Open Hand:** empty slots catch. A slot that ever holds an art is stained for life. | **Catching Hand:** a caught art still costs them its price but deals nothing. |
| [Remnant Host](../sheets/remnant-host.md) | Six scavenged needles fly for free, faster the more thần thức you leave unused. | **Recall Banner**, an artifact: artifacts fly on their own timers, with no slot and no linh lực. | **Spare Mind:** +3 % flight speed per 1 % of thần thức left free. |
| [Struck Name](../sheets/struck-name.md) | Scrape the names off enemy arts, so their specialist bonuses stop working. | **Struck Name**, a capability on a sealed cluster: arts can carry no tags. | **Borrowed Name:** while the enemy is stripped, you gain most of the bonus they lost. |
| [Taster's Fever](../sheets/tasters-fever.md) | Keep your body two-thirds poison, because the fever makes you fast. | **Bitter Tithe:** poison arts are paid in toxicity, not linh lực. Cleansing pills stop working on you. | **Fever:** art speed for every point of toxicity you carry. |
| [Turned-Away](../sheets/turned-away.md) | Every breakthrough fails, and every failure is one more time you can't die. | **The Copyist's Error**, on the Spent Kiln Sutra: every great breakthrough fails and leaves a Gate Scar. | **Turned Away:** at 0 health, open a scar and stand back up at 5 %. |

| [Understudy](../sheets/understudy.md) | A silk double takes 40 % of the hits, and every miss comes back as a slap. | **The Understudy:** evade is never rolled. It fills a meter, and a full meter sends the hit to the double. | Your thần thức against theirs sets how fast the meter fills. |

## Builds with no enabler

These need no special piece. The key effect is the one row or art that matters most.
