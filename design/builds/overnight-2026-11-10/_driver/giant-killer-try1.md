**What I found in the rules.** One realm up, suppression takes only 10 %, so pierce at its cap of 75 adds about 8 % damage there; it matters more two realms up. Three other gaps decide the fight:
- **Fight unit:** ×2.512 a realm on both damage and health, so ×6.31 in fight power.
- **Thần thức:** ×3 to ×3.3 a realm, so the higher realm's soul hits take the full 30 % cap of your health and linh lực each.
- **The Effects layer (L5):** top-end builds already hold it at its cap.

**How the build answers them.** Each piece answers one gap:
- **Half a Realm (Bán Cảnh), the enabler and identity piece.** Against a foe one or two realms above, both sides strike as if the gap were half a realm (yours ×1.585, theirs ÷1.585). That turns ×6.31 into ×2.51. Against equals and lower, your hits drop a quarter realm (÷1.26).
- **Pierce does three jobs.**
  - **Suppression:** up to the cap it lifts damage from ×0.90 to ×0.975.
  - **Stiff Neck (Cường Hạng):** the same capped pierce becomes soul resistance against a higher foe. With the Soul-Guarding Lamp and +40 % thần thức, their soul hit falls from 30 % to about 6.5 %.
  - **Through the Ceiling (Xuyên Đỉnh):** pierce past 75 becomes resistance penetration, up to +120.
- **Against the Heavens (Nghịch Thiên):** L5 +100 against a higher foe, and no other L5 counts. This makes "weak against equals" a rule: about 0.3 of a top-end build's damage against an equal.
- **Golden Bell:** a flat fee per hit, so heavy hits from above don't drain your linh lực faster.
- **Mountain Notches (Sơn Ngân):** the needle gains pierce for each higher-realm cultivator you have knocked out in this life.

At Core Formation against a Nascent Soul, the multiplier works out to about 243, against about 200 for a generic top-end build. These are first guesses, not simulated.
