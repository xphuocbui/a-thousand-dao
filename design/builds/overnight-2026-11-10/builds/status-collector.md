# The Hundred-Ailment Ledger (Bách Bệnh Lục)

Put a dozen different afflictions on one enemy with cheap arts, then land a single hit that is paid for every one of them.

## Why it works

Most builds stack one affliction deep. This one stacks many different ones, and the game pays by the **kind**, not the depth.

- **Cheap brands.** Six arts, each costing 2 % of your linh lực and firing every 3 s, each lays one element's debuff (Fire burn, Earth accuracy loss, Metal armour-break, Wood poison, Thunder crit-exposure, Ice art-cost). The brands barely hurt, on purpose.
- **The Tally Gourd** adds three more kinds for free (Qi Disruption, Bleed, Exposed). That makes nine; the curse slots can take it to twelve.
- **Two readers of the count.** Marked Prey gives +4 in layer L5 for each different status of yours on the target, normally stopping at +16 (four kinds). **Tally Ten** lifts that to twelve kinds, +48. Crowded Ledger gives +3 % more art damage per kind in L2, up to +36.
- **The cash-in.** Ledger Reckoning (3.1 coefficient, 4 s cooldown) is a physical hit that meets Metal's armour-break stacks and the whole count. At twelve kinds its L2 is 300 (the cap) and its L5 is 100 (the cap): about 86 % of the top-end multiplier of 325.
- **Fuel.** At 2 % per brand and 6 % for the Reckoning, the whole rotation costs about the pool's regeneration, so the build does not run dry.

It is a build for a five-element root, the one root most players treat as a handicap.

## What to look for

In the order you find them.

1. **Spiritual root (linh căn):** a five-element root (ngũ linh căn) that has reached Lôi (Fire + Metal) and Băng (Water). In the game (roots, Lôi and Băng variants).
2. **Talents:** Comprehension, for three techniques; Thần thức, for the Gourd and the Needle. In the game.
3. **Đạo and nodes:** Độc Đạo (Vạn Độc), the Ôn Dịch facet ("statuses spread"). Way in the game. The Tally Ten and Rolling Refresh nodes are not in the game yet (staged: tally-ten, rolling-refresh).
4. **Công pháp:** Hundred-Ailment Register Canon (Bách Bệnh Chân Kinh), a Thiên-tier main method, and the Two Variants Pamphlet (Nhị Biến Tiểu Sách), an auxiliary. Not in the game yet (staged: hundred-ailment-canon, two-variants-pamphlet).
5. **Arts:** the six Brands and Ledger Reckoning. Not in the game yet (staged: ember-brand, cracked-earth-brand, cold-iron-brand, bitter-sap-brand, thread-lightning-brand, rime-brand, ledger-reckoning).
6. **Items:** Registrar's Needle (a generated cham weapon with Liệt and element affinity: in the game as a generated item); Hospice Ward Robe (a generated giáp: in the game); Nine Rods Array (not in the game yet, staged: nine-rods-array); Hundred-Herb Pill (not in the game yet, staged: hundred-herb-pill).
7. **Enabler:** the Tally Gourd (Kết Sổ Hồ Lô). Not in the game yet (staged: tally-gourd).

## The enabling Effects

- **Marked Prey (Tiêu Ký).** Your hits deal more for each different status of yours on the target: L5 +4 each, up to +16. In the game (the Effects catalogue). The build needs it because it is the main payoff.
- **Tally Ten (Thập Kết).** Marked Prey counts up to twelve kinds, so its cap is +48. Not in the game yet (staged: tally-ten). Without it, the fifth to twelfth statuses pay nothing in L5.
- **Tally Gourd echo.** When one of your arts applies a status, a one-stack, 3-pulse batch of the next status the target lacks (Qi Disruption, then Bleed, then Exposed) is added. Cost: −30 % direct damage on your status-applying arts, and one curse slot. Not in the game yet (staged: tally-gourd).
- **Rolling Refresh (Lan Bệnh).** Applying a status refreshes one other of yours on the target by 1 pulse (2 per firing). It holds a wide count with few casts. Not in the game yet (staged: rolling-refresh).

## Modifiers to stack

1. **L2 more on status-applying arts** (Canon, Pamphlet, Way nodes, cluster, tags): up to the L2 cap of 300; the build uses 250 static and 50 on the count. Stop at 300.
2. **Crowded Ledger** (+3 % per kind, cap +36): L2, uses the count; do not buy past the cap.
3. **Status chance and status duration:** every Brand must land, and 10 s batches must last. Status duration falls off once the batches outlast the 3 s cooldown; stop near 12 %.
4. **Thrifty Brand** (−8 % cost on status-applying arts): fuel. Stop when the rotation costs less than regeneration.
5. **Art speed** (+40 %): rate; returns fall, stops at the 0.5 s floor.
6. **L1 attack and element nodes:** 400 points, but L1 past +200 counts half; stop near +300.
7. **L3 gear:** three artifacts to 250 (cap).
8. **L4 preparation:** array and pill to 150 (cap).
9. **L5:** Kindled Wound and Steady Breath for 52 static, plus Marked Prey and Tally Ten for 48. Cap 100.
10. **Extra Curse Slot** (+1): a third curse beyond the Gourd's Exposed; stop at the slot count that your kinds can use.

## Realm by realm

- **Kết Đan:** five slots: four Brands and the Reckoning. Four kinds, +16 L5. The simulator has it winning 20-0 against a same-realm foe in 5.8 s and 8-12 against a Nguyên Anh foe.
- **Nguyên Anh:** the sixth slot takes Thunder, the Gourd arrives, and the count reaches eight.
- **Hóa Thần:** seven slots, six Brands, nine kinds in hand; twelve with Tally Ten, Exposed and two more curses.

## How it plays

Art order: Ember, Earth, Iron, Sap, Thunder, Ice, then Ledger Reckoning. Every Brand's first cast comes after 3 s divided by your art speed; Reckoning at 4 s. Load the Hospice Ward Robe, the Needle and the Gourd; keep a reserved drain ward. The signature moment: the target's status bar scrolls and the Breakdown shows "+48 for twelve kinds" and "+36 more for twelve kinds" on one hit.

A warning from the simulator: at Hóa Thần a typical foe dies in 1.6 s, before any Brand lands, so the count only matters against top-end foes (14-5-1, 9.5 s). Removing the Reckoning made the fight slower (12.6 s) but safer, because its 6 % cost dries the pool: cast it only when the count is full.

## What beats it

- **A cleanser** (an enemy that cleanses every pulse): the stacks are wiped. Keep Rolling Refresh up and hold the Reckoning until the count is back.
- **A body cultivator with high status resistance** (the thể tu type): the Brands do not land. Take Status chance and Extra Curse Slot; the Reckoning's physical hit still lands.
- **A top-end burst foe:** kills before the count is up. Open with the ward and the first Brands early.
- **Healers' guilds' Withering:** not a counter, but they remove curses with cleanse tags; Exposed goes first.

## Changes the game would need

- **Raising Marked Prey's ceiling through a second Effect** (effects.md, Starter Effects, Marked Prey "Limits: Cap +16"): a second Effect may raise another Effect's cap. Without this, the count pays only to four kinds. Trade-off: a count-reader at twelve kinds becomes a top-end layer filler.
- **A live-count Per in L2** (combat.md Formulas B and modifiers.md): L2 is read at compile, while Crowded Ledger reads the target in the fight. Trade-off: a modifier that reads fight state gets harder to show in a Breakdown.
