# The Quiet Vine (Tĩnh Đằng Độc Kinh)

Lay poison in a few big, slow batches, keep yourself topped up with Wood regeneration, and let the enemy lose the race you refuse to run.

## Why it works

Poison (Trúc Độc, Wood's debuff) is a tick: damage every second that skips evade and block and passes shields. Each Poison stack deals 0.019 of an art's worth per pulse, and **there is no cap on stacks, on duration or on tick damage any more** (tick caps are retired). Each batch of stacks has its own timer, so batches pile up: lay 10 stacks every 3 s and 24 every 6 s and, by second 15 or 20, around thirty stacks tick every pulse.

That is slow, so the build leans on three things:

- **Long batches.** Rot Bloom lays 24 stacks for 20 s. Remove it and a 38 s win at Foundation (Trúc Cơ) becomes a 63 s grind.
- **Stacking multipliers that ticks can use.** Layers L1 to L4 count (about 60 / 45 / 25 / 15 points at Trúc Cơ); L5 and crit never touch a tick, so we do not buy them.
- **A safety net.** Weeping Sap heals and grants Wood stacks, Watched Rot refunds linh lực while the target is rotting, and a Hot Spring Array under your feet adds regeneration. In the test fights the build never ran dry and finished with about 76 % health.

The price: a cleanser (an enemy that rinses debuffs) beats it outright, and nothing happens fast.

## What to look for

In the order a player finds them:

1. **Spiritual root (linh căn): Wood, middling purity.** In the game.
2. **Talents:** Comprehension (ngộ tính) for the technique's layers, vitality for the safety net. In the game.
3. **Đạo (the Way): Wood (Mộc), the Quiet Vine branch.** Patient Venom (more tick damage per 4 stacks, up to +25 %) and Watched Rot (regeneration while the target has 15+ stacks). Not in the game yet (staged: `moc-tinh-dang-lines`, `patient-venom`, `watched-rot`). Later: the Venom Facet (Nọc) for Spillover. Not in the game yet (staged: `noc-spillover`).
4. **Main technique (công pháp): Thanh Mộc Địa Quyết.** In the game.
5. **Secret technique: Quiet Vine Sutra (Tĩnh Đằng Kinh).** Not in the game yet (staged: `quiet-vine-sutra`).
6. **Arts:** Thorn Seed, Weeping Sap, Rot Bloom, Venom Tithe. Not in the game yet (staged: `thorn-seed`, `weeping-sap`, `rot-bloom`, `venom-tithe`).
7. **Items:** a needle-type weapon and a Mossback Vest (both in the game as generated items); Bitterroot Pill (not in the game yet, staged: `bitterroot-pill`); Corpse-Wax Coating and a Hot Spring Array (not in the game yet, staged: `corpse-wax-coating`, `hot-spring-array`).
8. **Enabler:** none. This is a starter.

## The enabling Effects

- **Seeds Left Behind (Di Chủng).** When an enemy cleanses your Poison, 30 % of the removed stacks regrow as a new 6 s batch (once per 4 s). It is the build's answer to cleansing, and it is how a new player learns what a cleanse does: it removes stacks, so make the stacks worth removing. Not in the game yet (staged: `seeds-left-behind`).
- **Root Drinks the Rot (Căn Ẩm Hủ).** 3.7 % of the damage you deal to a poisoned target returns as linh lực. Needed so the pool survives a long wait. Not in the game yet (staged earlier: `root-drinks-the-rot`).
- **Sap Rises (Nhựa Thăng).** Each Wood buff stack adds +1 % healing. It turns Weeping Sap into more than a heal. Not in the game yet (staged earlier: `sap-rises`).
- **Spillover (Dật Độc), upgrade.** Your ticks also burn linh lực. It makes a poison build threaten an enemy's ward. In the tree catalogue.

## Modifiers to stack

1. **Poison damage and Damage over time** (L1, up to about 60 points at Trúc Cơ). Cheap, plentiful. Past +200 in L1, points count half, so stop there.
2. **Rot Deepens** (L2, tick, +20). The first "more" you can get; L2 caps at 300.
3. **Status duration** (+12 % notable). Longer batches mean more stacks alive at once; the first one is the best.
4. **Gear** (L3): a weapon and a vest; Bitter-Thorn Needle first.
5. **Prepared poison** (L4): Bitterroot Pill, then Corpse-Wax Coating. Only counts in a fight you saw coming.
6. **Regeneration**: linh lực regeneration and a Hot Spring Array. It is a log curve with no cap, but each extra point is worth less.
7. **Patient Venom** (L2, up to +25), from Kết Đan.
8. **Stop:** L5 and crit do nothing for a tick. Do not buy them.

## Realm by realm

- **Qi Refining (Luyện Khí):** two arts. Plant a seed, drink sap, wait. This teaches ticks. Not simulated.
- **Foundation (Trúc Cơ):** Rot Bloom arrives; the plan is 20 s long. The first cleanser teaches the downside. About 38 s per same-realm fight.
- **Core Formation (Kết Đan):** Venom Tithe repays linh lực; Patient Venom rewards long fights; one free slot for Withering. About 24 s per fight, faster than before.

## How it plays

Order: Thorn Seed, Rot Bloom, Weeping Sap, then Venom Tithe. Loadout: three or four arts, a Common Ward, a vest. Cast the big batch early and do not cast it twice before it lands. **Signature moment:** four silent seconds in which the enemy's health bar falls on its own and your linh lực rises.

## What beats it

- **Cleanser** (enemy that rinses debuffs: a Water-Light healer or a ward cleansing a quarter of each batch a pulse): in the test it won 19 of 20. Adapt: bait the cleanse with Thorn Seed and drop Rot Bloom after; take Seeds Left Behind.
- **Healer:** use Withering.
- **Chaos-resistant body (thể tu):** resistance cuts Poison. Add Spillover and a Qi hit or two.
- **Any enemy a realm above, or a top-end rush:** dead before the plan starts. Avoid.

## Not in the game yet

Quiet Vine Sutra; Thorn Seed, Weeping Sap, Rot Bloom, Venom Tithe; Seeds Left Behind; Patient Venom; Watched Rot; Bitterroot Pill; Bitter-Thorn Needle and Mossback Vest as named items (the bases exist); the Bitter Sap Row cluster; Root Drinks the Rot, Sap Rises, Hot Spring Array, Corpse-Wax Coating, Spillover (all staged earlier or in the catalogue).

## Changes the game would need

- **Stack-count Per on ticks** (Modifier System, Per form; Effects System rule 9a): Patient Venom needs a modifier to read the target's Poison stack count at each tick.
- **Cleanser telegraph** (Combat rule 12, preparation; the Guidance Layer): so the player sees "this enemy cleanses" before a foreseen fight, and the lesson in cleansing is learnt in the prepare screen rather than by losing.
