# The Red Hearth Ladder (Xích Lô Môn Giai Thê)

Keep a small fire alive for a long time: the plain Fire-sect disciple who wins by Burn that never stops and a pool that never empties.

## Why it works

Two cheap Fire arts lay Burn stacks (fire damage that ticks every second, ignores dodge and block, never needs a crit and has no cap). Each tick gives a little linh lực back (Embers Feed the Hearth), so the pool that usually runs dry first stays full. The disciple trades away burst: direct Fire hits deal 15 % less.

Key numbers (first guesses, not final):
- Kindle Palm: coefficient 1.4, costs 2 % of your pool, every 2 s, 2 Burn stacks for 8 s.
- Red Lotus Ring: coefficient 2.9, costs 4 %, every 5 s, 3 Burn stacks for 10 s.
- Each Burn stack ticks for 0.019 of the realm's base per pulse; stacks add up, with no cap.
- Against a same-realm ordinary cultivator the simulator gives about 11 wins in 20 at Foundation (29 s) and 12 in 20 at Core Formation (21 s). Without Embers Feed the Hearth it falls to 4 and 6.

5. **Arts:** Kindle Palm, Red Lotus Ring, Cinder Lance, Hearth Banner: not in the game yet (staged: kindle-palm, red-lotus-ring, cinder-lance, hearth-banner).

7. **Enabler:** none. The identity piece is the keystone Embers Feed the Hearth: not in the game yet (staged: embers-feed-the-hearth).

## The enabling Effects

- **Embers Feed the Hearth** (Tàn Hỏa Dưỡng Lô): when you deal a Burn tick, restore linh lực equal to a share of that tick (25 % to start); your direct Fire hits deal 15 % less. Why: it lets two cheap arts and a thin tree last 30 s. Not in the game yet.
- **Kindled Wound** (Trì Nhiệt): your Fire parts deal more against a target bearing your Burn (+15 in layer 5). Why: the only layer-5 source this build uses, and the stacks are always there. In the game (Effects Starter).

## Modifiers to stack

Most important first.

1. **Linh lực regeneration and Fire art cost:** fills the fuel rung. Stop when the pool never dips under half (about +1 pp regeneration at Foundation); further points do little.
2. **Damage over time and Fire damage (layer 1):** the everyday stack. Stop around +200 points; past that layer 1 counts half.
3. **Deep Heat (layer 2):** +0.4 % more Burn per stack, to 20 stacks (+8 %). Stops at its own cap.
4. **Technique "more" on Fire arts (layer 2):** 20 points from the Canon; the layer caps at 300.
5. **Ember Rod and robe (layer 3):** gear; limited by thần thức.
6. **Pill and array (layer 4):** preparation; the array works only at home or in prepared fights.
7. **Fire art speed:** small steps; falling returns, floor 0.5 s on cooldowns.
8. Skip crit and pierce: Burn ticks never crit and have no use for pierce.

## Realm by realm

- **Qi Refining (Luyện Khí):** primer, Kindle Palm, Red Lotus Ring. A spark: two arts, three slots, one empty.
- **Foundation (Trúc Cơ):** Ember Rod, Hearth-Warming Pill, Đạo nodes, and for seniors Hearth Banner. Same loop, deeper pool.
- **Core Formation (Kết Đan):** the Canon, Cinder Lance, the sect array. Three arts and a stronger squeeze. Past it the sect has nothing left to teach, and the Nascent Soul build must come from somewhere else.

## How it plays

Art order: Kindle Palm, Red Lotus Ring, then Cinder Lance. Cooldowns of 2, 5 and 7 s let the stacks overlap. The loadout leaves one slot empty for a talisman. The signature moment is the last stretch of a long fight: the enemy at a fifth of its pool, the disciple above half, and one more Ring on a target already deep in Burn.

## What beats it

- **Cleansers and healers:** they strip stacks. Adapt: lay stacks faster than they cleanse, or put the first hits on their ward.
- **Stagnation curses:** halve regeneration, which turns off the Embers loop. Adapt: stock a cleanse pill.
- **A realm above:** suppression ends the fight in 4 to 8 s. Adapt: do not take the fight.
- **Thể tu with a big body shield:** Burn wears it down slowly. Adapt: pair with an earth-breaking art or avoid.
- **Burst builds:** the disciple's stacks never matter if it is dead by 10 s.
