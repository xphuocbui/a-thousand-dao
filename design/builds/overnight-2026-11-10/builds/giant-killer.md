# The Needle Under the Mountain (Sơn Hạ Châm)

Against anyone one realm above you, the gap is only half a realm, and your pierce does three jobs at once.
Against your equals you are ordinary, and you know it.

## Why it works

Fighting one major realm up is hard for seven reasons, all in the combat rules. This build has one answer for each.

| What the realm above has | How big it is (from the GDD) | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| A bigger fight unit: more health, bigger hits | ×2.512 a realm on both, so ×6.31 in fight power (Combat Formulas A: 6.31 / 15.85 / 39.81 / 100 / 251.19) | **Half a Realm**: both sides strike as if the gap were half a realm, ×1.585 for you and ÷1.585 for them. ×6.31 becomes **×2.51** |
| Suppression of your damage | ×0.90 one realm up, ×0.75 two up (Formulas H) | **Pierce to the cap (75):** ×0.975 one up, ×0.9375 two up |
| More tree points, better techniques (the rest of the "×10 a realm") | Typical M 5.48 at Kết Đan against 7.37 at Nguyên Anh | **Against the Heavens:** your Effects layer (L5) is full, +100, but only against a higher foe |
| A soul three times yours | Thần thức ×3 to ×3.3 a realm (Character Stats), so their soul hits take the 30 % cap of your health and linh lực each | **Stiff Neck:** your pierce share becomes soul resistance (+75). With the Soul-Guarding Lamp and +40 % thần thức, a soul hit falls from **30 % to about 6.5 %** |
| Extra evade against lower realms | +2 pp a realm (Formulas I) | Accuracy +10 |
| Heavier hits that empty a drain ward | A drain ward bills what it stops, so bigger hits cost more | **Golden Bell**: a flat 3.5 % of your pool per hit, however hard it lands |
| More resistance | Top-end rises from about 900 to 1,900 points | **Through the Ceiling:** pierce past 75 becomes penetration, 4 points a point, up to +120 |

Put together at Core Formation (Kết Đan) against a Nascent Soul (Nguyên Anh), your multiplier is about 157 (the top-end
target is 210), times 1.585 from Half a Realm, times 0.975 suppression: about **243**. A generic top-end build in the same
fight gets about 200. The bigger win is defence: their hits arrive ÷1.585 and their soul hits at a fifth.

Against an equal, Against the Heavens leaves your L5 empty and Half a Realm drops your hits a quarter realm (÷1.26).
You deal about **0.3** of a top-end build's damage. That is the price.

At three realms up every one of these Effects switches off, so the game's rule that no one wins three realms up still
holds. Run.

## What to look for

In the order you meet them:

   Metal debuffs make the enemy take more Physical damage; Earth buffs make you take less. Purity strengthens both.
   Metal also earns bounty money in daily life. *In the game.*
2. **Talents:** high soul (thần hồn) for thần thức, half of your soul defence. Some physique (căn cốt) for health.
   Comprehension to learn the scripture. *In the game.*
3. **Đạo and nodes:** Nghịch Đạo, Defy Heaven, Change Fate (Nghịch Thiên Cải Mệnh). The Way is in the catalogue *(in
   the game as a catalogue entry; its graph is not drawn)*. The nodes you want are *not in the game yet*:
   - the Fighting Upward (Vượt Cấp) facet's Stiff Neck, Through the Ceiling, two Needle's Eye notables, and pierce
     and soul resistance lines (staged: nghich-vuot-cap-facet);
   - the capstone line, Against the Heavens or a second Half a Realm (staged: against-the-heavens, half-a-realm).
4. **Công pháp (technique):** the Iron Pestle Scripture (Thiết Xử Quyết), Huyền giai, up to Kết Đan. Then the Iron
   Pestle True Scripture (Thiết Xử Chân Kinh), Thiên giai, up to Hóa Thần. *Not in the game yet (staged:
   iron-pestle-scripture).* Take the Ore-Vein Scripture as your secret art. *Not in the game yet (staged:
   ore-vein-scripture).*
5. **Arts:** Threaded Needle, Through the Mountain, Bearing the Mountain, Pestle Grind, Last Stitch and the escape art

   Tráo), the Soul-Guarding Lamp (Hộ Hồn Đăng), the Common Ward and the Clear Heart Ward. *In the game (Combat

   forty-year-needle).* Any armour (giáp) *(in the game)*, and a bell (chuông) that rolled the Realm-Piercing (Thấu

   half-a-realm).*

## The enabling Effects

- **Half a Realm (Bán Cảnh), enabler.** Against a foe one or two major realms above you, every hit and tick between
  you is struck as if the gap were half a realm narrower: yours ×1.585, theirs ÷1.585. In exchange, against a foe of
  your realm or below, your hits are struck a quarter realm down (÷1.26). It does nothing at three or more realms.
  *Why the build needs it:* the fight unit is the biggest part of the realm gap. Nothing else in the game touches it.
- **Against the Heavens (Nghịch Thiên), keystone.** Against a foe one or two realms above you, your hits gain +100 in
  the Effects layer (L5), its whole cap. No other L5 source counts for you, ever. *Why:* it fills a whole layer
  upward, and it makes "weak against equals" a rule, not an accident.
- **Stiff Neck (Cường Hạng), major.** Against a foe above you, your soul resistance rises by your pierce share: +1 per
  point of pierce, up to +75. *Why:* a higher realm's soul is about three times yours, which is the thing that
  really kills climbers. This turns your main stat into the answer.
- **Through the Ceiling (Xuyên Đỉnh), minor.** Against a foe above you, the pierce the cap cuts off becomes
  resistance penetration, 4 points for each point, up to +120. *Why:* it gives you a reason to keep stacking pierce
  past 75.
- **Mountain Notches (Sơn Ngân), on the needle.** For each cultivator of a higher realm you have knocked out in this
  life: +1 pierce and +3 Physical penetration, up to +20 and +60. *Why:* the needle grows with your story, and past
  the cap that pierce feeds Through the Ceiling.
- **Beneath Notice (Bất Túc Đạo), minor, existing.** +15 escape against a higher realm. *Why:* fleeing a higher
  pursuer costs 10 points a realm, and three realms up you must flee.

## Modifiers to stack

1. **Realm pierce** (`pierce_pct`, reach). The core. Fill to 75 first; that is the cap for suppression and for Stiff
   Neck. Then keep going to 105: each point past 75 is 4 penetration through Through the Ceiling, which stops at +120.
2. **"More" damage on piercing, Physical and Metal arts** (L2). Needle's Eye (+60 technique, +40 per Way notable),
   Ore Weight (+45), Cold Iron Order (+60 on the Thiên copy). Stop at 300, L2's cap.
3. **Increased damage** (`attack_pct`, Physical, Metal; L1). Up to +200. Past that each point counts half.
4. **Thần thức and soul resistance.** +40 % thần thức and the Soul-Guarding Lamp, on top of Stiff Neck. Stop when a
   higher realm's soul hit takes under 8 %. More is only for known soul-piercers.
5. **Crit** chance to 50 % (the cap) and damage to ×2.0 or more.
6. **Physical penetration** (`resist_pen_pct` [physical]). +60 from the needle and +100 from a notable. Notches add
   more.
7. **Art speed** (+40 to +60) and **art cost** (−20 %). Only enough that Through the Mountain fires every 5 to 6
   seconds and your pool holds. Fights one realm up end before you run dry.
8. **Preparation** (L4): the Nine Rods Array and the Hand Bath, 130 of 150. Tribulations and challenges are
   foreseen, so they count when you most need them.
9. **Accuracy** +10. Enough to cancel the higher realm's evade step.
10. **Health** (`khi_huyet_pct`) and **regeneration**. The ordinary rest.

## Realm by realm

- **Qi Refining (Luyện Khí):** three slots. Threaded Needle, Through the Mountain and the Soul-Guarding Lamp in an art
  slot; the Golden Bell in your one ward slot. You can already beat Foundation Establishment thugs.
- **Foundation Establishment (Trúc Cơ):** Bearing the Mountain arrives, and with it Earth stacks. The first pierce
  notables. Your first notch on the needle.
- **Core Formation (Kết Đan):** the defining realm. Pestle Grind, Stiff Neck and the Forty-Year Needle. A Kết Đan who
  kills Nguyên Anh: your own sect may put a bounty on you.
- **Nascent Soul (Nguyên Anh):** the Thiên giai copy, Last Stitch and Through the Ceiling. The tribulation to Hóa Thần
  always puts you one realm below the boss (Thiên Kiếp rule 6). It is the best fight of your life.
- **Deity Transformation (Hóa Thần):** there is no realm above. You are a middling caster among a handful of peers,
  and the next needle comes from below. If the game adds a "target is a boss" readable, bosses count as above, and
  you hunt them instead.

## How it plays

- **Art order:** Bearing the Mountain first (a defence that must be up before their heavy hits), then Threaded Needle,
  Through the Mountain, Pestle Grind, Last Stitch (guarded: enemy below 35 % health) and Sweeper's Exit (guarded: you
  below 30 % health). Nothing fires at 0 s, so Bearing at 6 s and Threaded at 3 s set up the 8 s Through the Mountain.
- **Loadout:** Golden Bell always. Soul-Guarding Lamp from the first fight against a higher realm. At Nascent Soul add
  the Common Ward; at Hóa Thần the Clear Heart Ward.
- **The signature moment:** the Breakdown reads "Half a Realm: struck at ×1.585", "suppression 0.975", "Against the
  Heavens: L5 +100", and a Nguyên Anh goes Down to your Through the Mountain while their soul lance takes 6 % instead
  of 30 %.

## What beats it

- **A soul-piercer one realm up.** Soul ignores Half a Realm, and Slackened Soul (−15 soul resistance a stack, no
  floor) eats Stiff Neck. *Adapt:* more thần thức, a second soul ward at Nascent Soul, a Clear Heart Ward against the
  curse. Against a known soul-piercer, take the fight only with preparation.
- **A top-end equal.** You are at 0.3 of their damage. *Adapt:* don't. Pick fights upward, or flee with Sweeper's
  Exit.
- **Anything three realms up.** Every gap-reader is off and suppression still takes 12.5 %. *Adapt:* Beneath Notice
  and Sweeper's Exit; you are faster to run than most.
- **A higher-realm healer.** A long fight drains your pool (about −1 to −2 % a second with five arts and the Bell).
  *Adapt:* art cost, regeneration, and Last Stitch to close.
- **A needle one realm below you.** At Hóa Thần, your own kind. This is how the founder died.

## Changes the game would need

1. **Strike at a shifted fight unit.** design/gdd/combat.md Formulas C (`base = U_att · stage_att · c_art`), F
   (`raw = U_src · …`) and G (`free_hit = U · …`) read the striker's own fight unit with no hook. Add: "an Effect
   may shift the fight unit a side's hits and ticks are struck at, by at most half a realm (×2.512^±0.5), read at
   compile". Add that action to design/gdd/effects.md rule 6's closed action list.
2. **Readable: higher-realm knockouts this life.** design/gdd/effects.md rule 5 (sheet reads at compile). Realms
   already reports knockouts for capability #9 (combat.md Interactions). Mountain Notches needs the count of those
   whose realm was above yours.
3. **Readable: the target is a boss.** design/gdd/effects.md rule 5 (in a fight). True when the target's
   `boss_pool_mult` is above 1 (combat.md rule 18). It gives the build a life at Hóa Thần.
4. **Gap readers stop at two.** design/gdd/effects.md rule 5 (the realm gap readable): any Effect that reads the realm
   gap pays out at gaps 1 and 2 only. This keeps combat.md Formulas H's "never three realms up" target true however
   such Effects stack.
