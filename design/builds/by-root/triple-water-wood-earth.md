# Triple root: Water, Wood and Earth (Thủy, Mộc, Thổ)

**What the root brings.** One generating pair (Water → Wood, +3 %); Earth with Water can converge into Darkness;
Wood breaks Earth. The mana buff, Poison and regeneration, Physical resistance and accuracy debuff. The marsh,
the forest and the field: the most patient root.

---

## 1. The Paddy Field (Đạo Điền)

**Concept.** Rice in flooded fields: Water, Wood and Earth all feed one harvest. An economy build that farms
everything and fights only on home ground, where it cannot lose.

**How it fights.** Prepared ground with two arrays; regeneration and resistance; Poison chips.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Water, Wood, Earth.
- **Công pháp:** Green Wood Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Spirit Farming.
- **Keystone:** Portable Cave Abode (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Field Walls (exists).
- **Modifiers:** herb growth; field plots; formation power.
- **Items:** Green Wood Array, Mountain Guard Array.
- **Element stacks:** Wood buff, Earth buff, Water buff.

**Weakness.** Ambush away from home.

**New pieces.** None.

## 2. The Bog Witch (Đầm Vu)

**Concept.** Mud that blinds, water that sustains, rot that kills. A slow control build whose foe misses, starves
and sickens.

**How it fights.** Earth debuff (−accuracy) and Poison from mud arts; Water stacks for the pool; evade from Iron
Shirt.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Water, Wood, Earth.
- **Công pháp:** Dark Water Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Poison.
- **Keystone:** Root Drinks the Rot (Wood, New).
- **Effects:** Mire (New, `dual-earth-water.md`); Spillover (exists).
- **Modifiers:** evade; damage [poison].
- **Items:** Iron Shirt; Gourd.
- **Element stacks:** Earth debuff, Wood debuff, Water buff.

**Weakness.** Cleansing; soul attacks.

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.

## 3. The Banyan Over the Pool (Dung Thụ Thủy Biên)

**Concept.** A healer-tank that lives longest of all: deep pool, regenerating body, resistant bark.

**How it fights.** Heal arts refund their cost; Water, Wood and Earth buffs; it wins by fatigue.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Water, Wood, Earth.
- **Công pháp:** Green Wood Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Life.
- **Keystone:** The Spring Never Fails (Water, New).
- **Effects:** Still Water (exists), Gorged (exists).
- **Modifiers:** healing; health regeneration; resistance.
- **Items:** Mountain Screen; Green Wood Array.
- **Element stacks:** Water buff, Wood buff, Earth buff.

**Weakness.** Low damage; a foe with its own sustain draws it to fatigue.

**New pieces.** None.

## 4. The Rain Caller (Hô Vũ Sư)

**Concept.** A weather cultivator who brings the rain. Rain is ground: it lifts Water and Wood and turns the field to
mud for everyone else.

**How it fights.** Before a foreseen fight it calls rain, a ground tag (Combat rule 12). Under rain its Water and
Wood stacks last longer and the foe takes Earth debuff (mud) each pulse.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Water, Wood, Earth.
- **Công pháp:** Dark Water Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Mist, the weather branch (New nodes).
- **Keystone:** The Spring Never Fails (Water, New).
- **Effects:** Rain (New).
- **Modifiers:** `stack_duration_pct` [thuy, buff] and [moc, buff].
- **Items:** Rain Banner (New kind, a Flag variant).
- **Element stacks:** Water buff, Wood buff, Earth debuff.

**Weakness.** Fire foes in dry places; blind fights.

**New pieces.**
- **Rain (Vũ)**, a ground tag and Effect: "under rain, Water and Wood stack batches last 50 % longer, and each pulse
  every non-Water foe gains 1 Earth debuff stack". How to create: a ground tag in Combat rule 12's preparation
  list, read by an Effect.
