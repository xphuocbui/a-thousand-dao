# Variant root: Wind (Phong)

**What Wind brings.** Innate (born with it, any root). Force or Physical damage. Buff stacks: +1 % art speed. Debuff
stacks: −1 pp evade on the foe. Role: travel speed. Công pháp: Wind Walk Art (Phong Hành Quyết). The Wind start
on the map sits between Water and Wood, and the Thunder island joins it.

**Wind's linh lực keystone (New, every Wind build):** **Breath Between Blows (Gian Tức)**: each art you fire
restores 1.2 % of maximum linh lực (starting number, `notes.md` § 9). How to create: Meridian Map keystone in the Wind land; an Effect on the
art-fired event, action "restore linh lực"; closing cost "your arts with an authored cooldown above 6 s cannot be
slotted".

---

## 1. The Gale Dancer (Cuồng Phong Vũ)

**Concept.** Faster and faster: Wind buff stacks raise art speed until arts hit the 0.5 s floor, and Overheat turns
the speed past the floor into damage.

**How it fights.** Quick arts; Wind buff stacks per firing; by 20 s arts fire at the floor; Breath Between Blows
pays for the frenzy.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wind (any base root).
- **Công pháp:** Wind Walk Art.
- **Đạo:** Space (Không Gian), the swift branch.
- **Keystone:** Breath Between Blows (New).
- **Effects:** Overheat (exists), Flowing Water (exists).
- **Modifiers:** art speed; art cost [quick]; `stack_effect_pct` [phong, buff].
- **Items:** Fan (wind); Flying Swords.
- **Element stacks:** Wind buff.

**Weakness.** Block (many small hits); Ice stacks (many arts, each costlier).

**New pieces.** Breath Between Blows.

## 2. The Untouchable (Bất Xúc)

**Concept.** Wind as evasion and escape: hard to hit, quick to leave. A travelling cultivator who survives fights it
should lose and runs from the ones it cannot.

**How it fights.** High evade; Cold Feet (escape art at `cooldown_min`); Beneath Notice against higher realms.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wind.
- **Công pháp:** Wind Walk Art.
- **Đạo:** Space.
- **Keystone:** Blink Mark (capability, exists: travel instantly once a year).
- **Effects:** Cold Feet, Beneath Notice (exist).
- **Modifiers:** evade; escape chance; travel speed.
- **Items:** Swift Walk talisman (Thần Hành Phù).
- **Element stacks:** Wind buff.

**Weakness.** Ticks and soul attacks never meet evade; locking pursuers.

**New pieces.** None.

## 3. The Storm Front (Bão Tuyến), Wind with Thunder

**Concept.** The map joins Thunder to Wind; this build joins them too: Wind's speed and Thunder's crits, Wind
debuff cutting the foe's evade so the crits land.

**How it fights.** Wind debuff (−evade) on the foe; Thunder buff (accuracy, crit); fast crits restore linh lực through
both keystones.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wind plus Thunder.
- **Công pháp:** Thunder Emperor True Canon.
- **Đạo:** Heavenly Thunder.
- **Keystone:** Breath Between Blows and Storm Battery (both New).
- **Effects:** Called Blow (exists).
- **Modifiers:** art speed; crit chance.
- **Items:** Bell; Fan.
- **Element stacks:** Wind buff and debuff, Thunder buff and debuff.

**Weakness.** Block; cleansing.

**New pieces.** None beyond the keystones.

## 4. The Wind Merchant (Phong Thương)

**Concept.** A life build. Wind's role is travel: the fastest trader in the realm, buying where things are cheap and
selling where they are dear, with Blink Mark for the one urgent delivery a year.

**How it plays.** Travel speed makes every mission and trade run shorter; Dao in Dreams turns travelling seasons into
cultivation. It fights like an Untouchable when it must.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wind (any base root; Metal helps with mission income).
- **Công pháp:** Wind Walk Art.
- **Đạo:** Space.
- **Keystone:** Blink Mark, Dao in Dreams (capabilities, exist).
- **Effects:** Cold Feet (exists) for the road.
- **Modifiers:** travel speed; mission income.
- **Items:** Swift Walk talismans.
- **Element stacks:** Wind buff in fights.

**Weakness.** Bandits on the road; it is a trader, not a fighter.

**New pieces.** None.
