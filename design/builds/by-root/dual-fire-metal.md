# Dual root: Fire and Metal (Hỏa, Kim)

**What the pair brings.** An overcoming pair (Fire melts Metal): the forge. Fire damage and Burn; Metal's Physical
damage and Physical-taken debuff. Together they converge into **Thunder (Lôi)**. Crafts: alchemy and refining, the
best artifacts. Linh lực keystones: Embers Feed the Hearth (Fire, New), Edge Returns the Breath (Metal, New).

---

## 1. The Red-Hot Blade (Hồng Nhận)

**Concept.** A sword fresh from the forge: half its blow is steel, half is heat. Both halves pile their own debuff,
so the foe burns and bleeds at once.

**How it fights.** Sword arts convert 40 % Physical to Fire. The Physical part builds Metal debuff, the Fire part
lays Burn; Burn ticks feed linh lực.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire and Metal.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Heaven Canon.
- **Đạo:** Sword, or Refined Metal's forge branch.
- **Keystone:** Embers Feed the Hearth (New).
- **Effects:** Opening Cut (exists), Kindled Wound (exists).
- **Modifiers:** `convert_pct` Physical → Fire; damage [sword]; damage [fire].
- **Items:** Flying Sword with Fire affinity; Fan.
- **Element stacks:** Metal debuff, Fire debuff.

**Weakness.** Split damage meets two resistances; a foe resistant to either halves it.

**New pieces.** None; conversion and both stacks exist.

## 2. The Master Smith (Đại Tượng)

**Concept.** The best refiner in the realm, with a furnace (Fire) and an anvil (Metal). Fights with gear far above
its realm.

**How it fights.** Ordinary arts; Thượng/Cực phẩm artifacts it forged itself carry it.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire and Metal (refining, alchemy).
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Refined Metal.
- **Keystone:** Twin Arms in Harmony (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Open Sky (exists).
- **Modifiers:** artifact power; refining success; element affinity.
- **Items:** self-forged Flying Sword pair; Bell.
- **Element stacks:** Metal buff.

**Weakness.** Its edge is items; a soul attack on low thần thức.

**New pieces.** None.

## 3. Before the Storm (Lôi Tiền)

**Concept.** A forge root waiting for lightning. Builds crit and Metal debuff now, and becomes a Thunder cultivator
when the convergence forms (see `variant-thunder.md`).

**How it fights.** Crit sword build (like the Thousand Cuts) with Fire's damage buff.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire and Metal at high purity (Thunder's chance is the product of both).
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Heaven Canon; Thunder Emperor Canon (Lôi Đế Chân Kinh) once Thunder forms.
- **Đạo:** Heavenly Thunder (Thiên Lôi) when glimpsed.
- **Keystone:** Edge Returns the Breath (New).
- **Effects:** Called Blow (exists), Flowing Water (exists).
- **Modifiers:** crit chance; crit damage; damage [sword].
- **Items:** Flying Swords.
- **Element stacks:** Metal debuff, Fire buff.

**Weakness.** Block; cleansing.

**New pieces.** None.

## 4. The Living Sword (Kiếm Linh)

**Concept.** A sword that woke up in the forge. The sword spirit is a companion: it fights beside its smith, and
the smith's Metal stacks make it sharper.

**How it fights.** The sword spirit is a companion whose hits are Physical with the Metal tag; it lays Metal
debuff. The smith fires few arts (Bare Rack) and keeps its pool for the ward.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire and Metal (forge-born spirit).
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Sword, the spirit branch (New nodes: companion share for sword spirits).
- **Keystone:** Beast Soul Union (exists); Twin Arms in Harmony (capability, exists) for a second sword.
- **Effects:** Pack Hunt (exists), Bare Rack (exists).
- **Modifiers:** companion power share; damage [companion].
- **Items:** an awakened Flying Sword (New: an artifact that becomes a companion).
- **Element stacks:** Metal debuff (from the spirit, element-tagged companions, New).

**Weakness.** Break the sword (an artifact-destroying Effect would be its nemesis).

**New pieces.**
- **Awakened Artifact (Linh Khí Thức Tỉnh)**: an artifact refined past Cực phẩm becomes a companion with its
  element tag. How to create: Artifact Refining rule plus a creature record generated from the artifact.
