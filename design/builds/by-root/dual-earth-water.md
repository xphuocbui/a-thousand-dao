# Dual root: Earth and Water (Thổ, Thủy)

**What the pair brings.** An overcoming pair (Earth dams Water). Earth's resistance and accuracy debuff; Water's
mana buff. Together they can converge into **Darkness (Ám)**, a variant with Chaos damage and stealth. Crafts:
fields and toxicity fading. Linh lực keystones: The Stone Remembers (Earth, New), The Spring Never Fails (Water,
New).

---

## 1. The Levee (Đê Điều)

**Concept.** A dam holds the flood. Earth resistance guards the body, Water deepens the pool behind it, and the
ward never breaks because the reservoir is so deep.

**How it fights.** A reserved aura (Still Lake, now 71 % of the pool) is affordable because Water stacks keep
raising the pool. Whatever is left pays cheap Physical arts.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Water.
- **Công pháp:** Deep Earth Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Gravity.
- **Keystone:** The Spring Never Fails (New).
- **Effects:** Field Walls (exists).
- **Modifiers:** reservation efficiency; maximum linh lực; resistance.
- **Items:** Still Lake Aura; Shield (Thuẫn).
- **Element stacks:** Water buff, Earth buff.

**Weakness.** Reserved wards leave little for arts; it wins slowly or not at all against another wall.

**New pieces.**
- **Reservoir (Hồ Chứa)**, an Effect: "reservations are taken from your maximum before Water stacks, so Water
  stacks add only usable linh lực". How to create: a rule change on Combat Defence & Healing rule 10's order.

## 2. The Mudslide (Bùn Trôi)

**Concept.** Mud in the eyes and around the ankles: Earth debuff blinds, a slowing Water effect drags, and the foe
fights at half speed.

**How it fights.** Mud arts apply Earth debuff (−accuracy) and a New slow (a short art-speed curse). The foe misses
and acts late.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Water.
- **Công pháp:** Dark Water Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Mist.
- **Keystone:** The Stone Remembers is wrong (closes evade); take Beneath Notice.
- **Effects:** Rime Lock's delay (exists) needs Ice; the New Mire curse instead.
- **Modifiers:** evade; `stack_duration_pct` [tho, debuff].
- **Items:** Iron Shirt; Flag.
- **Element stacks:** Earth debuff.

**Weakness.** Cleansing; Wind foes (art speed buff).

**New pieces.**
- **Mire (Lầy)**, a curse: "the target's art speed −20 % for 3 pulses". How to create: a curse row in the Effects
  status catalogue (rule changed: art speed), with a curse cap like Heavy Breath's.

## 3. The Hidden Spring (Ám Tuyền), toward Darkness

**Concept.** A cultivator waiting for the convergence: an Earth-and-Water root that becomes Darkness, and a build
that leans on Chaos from rot and still water until then.

**How it fights.** Before Darkness: a Water mana build with a Wood-free Poison (Chaos via a stagnant-water art).
After Darkness forms: see `variant-darkness.md`.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Water, aiming for Darkness.
- **Công pháp:** Shadow Secret Art (Ám Ảnh Bí Thuật) once Darkness forms.
- **Đạo:** Yin (Âm Đạo).
- **Keystone:** The Spring Never Fails (New).
- **Effects:** Spillover (exists).
- **Modifiers:** damage [chaos]; maximum linh lực.
- **Items:** Needle; Clear Heart Ward.
- **Element stacks:** Water buff.

**Weakness.** Before Darkness forms it is an ordinary mana build.

**New pieces.**
- **Stagnant Water (Tử Thủy)**, an art: Water-tagged, Chaos damage, a small tick. How to create: art record in Dark
  Water Earth Scripture with a Chaos part (Combat Damage Types allows any type on an art).

## 4. The Quicksand Runner (Lưu Sa Độn)

**Concept.** A cultivator who never fights a battle it might lose. Quicksand under the foe, an escape art at the
first sign of trouble, and the patience to come back with a better plan.

**How it fights.** A mud art lays Earth debuff and a lock on pursuit; the escape art fires as soon as the guard
"own health below 50 %" is true. Against a higher realm, Beneath Notice raises the escape chance.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Water.
- **Công pháp:** Dark Water Earth Scripture, with an Earth escape art (Earth Escape, New).
- **Đạo:** Space (Không Gian), the step branch.
- **Keystone:** Blink Mark (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Cold Feet (exists), Beneath Notice (exists).
- **Modifiers:** escape chance; evade.
- **Items:** Swift Walk talisman.
- **Element stacks:** Earth debuff.

**Weakness.** Escape locks; a foe that kills in the first seconds.

**New pieces.**
- **Earth Escape (Thổ Độn)**, an escape art that also leaves a Mire on the pursuer. How to create: escape art record
  (Combat Formulas I) with the Mire curse (New, this file) as a payload.
