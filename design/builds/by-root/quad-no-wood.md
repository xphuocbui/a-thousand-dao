# Quad root without Wood: Fire, Earth, Metal, Water (Hỏa, Thổ, Kim, Thủy)

**What the root brings.** Three generating pairs (Fire → Earth → Metal → Water, +9 %). Thunder (Fire + Metal) and
Darkness (Earth + Water) can both converge. Burn, Physical resistance and accuracy debuff, Physical damage and
debuff, the mana buff. The craftsman's root: alchemy, fields, refining and toxicity, every craft but herbs.

---

## 1. The Forge-and-Furnace Master (Lô Châm Đại Sư)

**Concept.** The finest crafter alive: pills, artifacts, formations and fields all from one root. It fights
prepared, armed and drugged, with gear and pills above its realm.

**How it fights.** Foreseen fights with self-made Cực phẩm artifacts, combat pills and a home formation.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire, Earth, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Refined Metal, then Pill Fire, then Array.
- **Keystone:** Divided Furnace Flame and Twin Arms in Harmony (capabilities, exist).
- **Effects:** Open Sky (exists).
- **Modifiers:** artifact power; alchemy and refining success; formation power.
- **Items:** its own best artifacts, pills and array.
- **Element stacks:** depends on the arts it carries.

**Weakness.** Ambushes; any fight where preparation is not allowed.

**New pieces.** None.

## 2. The Two Storms (Song Bão), toward Thunder and Darkness

**Concept.** A root that can hold two variants: Thunder from the forge pair and Darkness from the dam pair. Crit
lightning by day, Chaos shadow by night.

**How it fights.** After both variants form, a dual kit: Thunder crits apply crit exposure (Thunder debuff), then a
Chaos shadow art lands the big crit.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire, Earth, Metal, Water with Thunder and Darkness formed.
- **Công pháp:** Thunder Emperor Canon and Shadow Secret Art.
- **Đạo:** Heavenly Thunder, Yin.
- **Keystone:** Storm Battery (Thunder, New).
- **Effects:** Called Blow (exists).
- **Modifiers:** crit chance; crit damage; damage [chaos].
- **Items:** Flying Sword; Needle.
- **Element stacks:** Thunder debuff and buff.

**Weakness.** Needs both variants; slow before them.

**New pieces.** Storm Battery (see `variant-thunder.md`).

## 3. The Iron Reservoir Fortress (Thiết Hồ Thành)

**Concept.** Every defensive element but Wood: Earth resistance, Metal block, Water pool, Fire to punish. A
fortress that cannot be starved.

**How it fights.** Reserved aura, Golden Bell, Water stacks; Fire arts lay Burn.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire, Earth, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Deep Earth Heaven Canon.
- **Đạo:** Gravity.
- **Keystone:** The Stone Remembers (Earth, New).
- **Effects:** Answering Bell (New, `triple-earth-metal-water.md`).
- **Modifiers:** reservation efficiency; ward efficiency; block.
- **Items:** Still Lake Aura; Golden Bell.
- **Element stacks:** Water buff, Earth buff, Fire debuff.

**Weakness.** Chaos and soul.

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.

## 4. The Treasure Pavilion (Bảo Các)

**Concept.** A merchant-cultivator: refining, alchemy and fields make goods, Metal's missions bring stones. It buys
the right answer to every foe: a Fire ward against Fire, a cleansing ward against ticks, an Anchor against runners.

**How it fights.** Foreseen fights only: it reads the foe and slots the counter-ward and counter-talisman for that
foe. Its arts are ordinary; its preparation is perfect.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Fire, Earth, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Refined Metal.
- **Keystone:** Divided Divine Sense (capability, exists) for one more treasure.
- **Effects:** Talisman Sleeve (exists).
- **Modifiers:** mission income; artifact power; ward slots (rare).
- **Items:** a wardrobe of wards and talismans.
- **Element stacks:** whatever its arts grant.

**Weakness.** Blind fights and ambushes; an unknown foe.

**New pieces.**
- **Scouting report**: before a foreseen fight, the foe's main element and ward kinds are shown. How to create: an
  Event Engine field on a foreseen combat request, shown by the Guidance Layer.
