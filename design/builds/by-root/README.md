# Builds by spiritual root

Plain build concepts, grouped by spiritual root (linh căn) combination. Written overnight 2026-11-09/10 at the
user's request: "keep it plain and simple ... describing the concepts and explaining what kind of items,
modifiers, effects, keystone, dao, linh can this would take and stack. If that modifier, effects, items, dao do
not exist, suggest how they should be created."

These are **concepts**, not priced or simulated builds. Nothing here changes a GDD. Pieces marked **New** do not
exist yet; each says how it could be made, and `missing-pieces.md` collects them all in one list.

## How to read a build

Each build has:
- **Concept**: the fantasy in two or three sentences.
- **How it fights**: what happens in a fight, second by second where it matters.
- **Stacks**: what the build piles up, from which pieces:
  - **Linh căn**: the root and why it fits.
  - **Công pháp**: the cultivation technique.
  - **Đạo**: the Way it takes, and which branch.
  - **Keystone**: Meridian Map keystones or capabilities (thần thông). The map is a placeholder (user,
    2026-11-09), so keystones are named by what they do.
  - **Effects**: rule pieces, from the Effects System's starter catalogue or new.
  - **Modifiers**: the plain numbers it stacks, from the Modifier System's General Catalogue.
  - **Items**: artifacts (pháp bảo), wards, talismans, pills, formations.
  - **Element stacks**: which buff and debuff stacks it builds (Effects System rule 9a).
- **Weakness**: what beats it.
- **New pieces**: what is missing, and how to create it.

## The rules these builds are written against (after the Combat economy revision #44)

- **Linh lực is the real life bar.** Wards are paid in linh lực; a typical ward stops 82 % of a hit, so a side
  whose pool runs dry takes about 5.5 times the damage and falls within seconds. Sustain is king.
- **Health is the cheap pool.** Health is ×4 and worth little in a fight, so paying costs in health (Blood
  Casting, `art_cost_health_pct`) and leeching health are cheap ways to protect linh lực.
- **Fights last 30–60 s**; art cooldowns are 0.5–12 s; **fatigue** starts at 60 s (1 % of health, +1 % a second).
- **Element stacks** (Effects rule 9a): every element has a buff on you and a debuff on the foe, uncapped,
  answered by cleansing:

  Numbers re-fitted 2026-11-10 so every buff, and every debuff, is worth about the same (Effects rule 9a).

  | Element | Buff (on you) | Debuff (on the foe) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Fire | +1.2 % damage | Burn: Fire ticks, the applier's damage |
  | Water | +0.5 % max linh lực, +0.025 pp linh lực regen | — |
  | Earth | +8 pp Physical resistance | −1.2 pp accuracy |
  | Metal | +1.2 % Physical damage | +1.8 % Physical damage taken |
  | Wood | +0.1 pp health regen | Poison: Chaos ticks, passes shields |
  | Ice | — | +3.9 % art cost |
  | Thunder | +0.7 pp accuracy, +0.35 pp crit | +1.2 pp crit chance and +5 % crit damage taken |
  | Wind | +7.5 % art speed | −1.2 pp evade |

- **Each element's tree will hold a keystone that generates linh lực** (user, 2026-11-09). Builds below name the
  one they would take.
- **Công pháp and some items will get their own small trees**, in a separate tab (thoughts only,
  `design/research/side-trees-thoughts.md`). Builds that lean on one say so.

## Root combinations

The five elements form 31 combinations. Generating pairs (tương sinh) add +3 % cultivation speed each:
Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood. Overcoming pairs (tương khắc) have no bonus, but make good fight
stories: Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood.
