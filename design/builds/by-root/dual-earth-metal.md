# Dual root: Earth and Metal (Thổ, Kim)

**What the pair brings.** A generating pair (Earth bears Metal, +3 %). All Physical: Earth's buff resists it,
Metal's buff deals it, Metal's debuff makes the foe take more of it. The natural body cultivator (thể tu) root.
Crafts: fields, refining, mission income. Linh lực keystones: The Stone Remembers (Earth, New), Edge Returns the
Breath (Metal, New).

> **Note for the user.** After #44, maximum health is worth about 0.21 W per % in fights (modifiers.md Open
> Question 9). Body builds that rely on health are weak until that is settled. The builds below lean on
> resistance, block and the pool instead.

---

## 1. The Iron Mountain (Thiết Sơn)

**Concept.** A thể tu who cannot be cut: Physical damage dampened by Earth stacks, block from body gear, and the
Stone Remembers turning every blocked blow into linh lực.

**How it fights.** Against Physical attackers the ward almost pays for itself. Fists (Physical) pile Metal debuff
on the foe; the fight is a slow crush.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Metal.
- **Công pháp:** Dragon-Elephant (Long Tượng Công).
- **Đạo:** Refined Metal (Luyện Kim), the tempered-body branch.
- **Keystone:** The Stone Remembers (New).
- **Effects:** Iron Skin Renewed (exists).
- **Modifiers:** block; resistance [physical]; body shield; `stack_effect_pct` [tho, buff].
- **Items:** Armour (Giáp) body gear; Iron Body Art (body piece); Iron Shirt ward.
- **Element stacks:** Earth buff, Metal debuff.

**Weakness.** Chaos (passes the body shield, ignores Physical resistance); Force arts.

**New pieces.** None beyond the keystone.

## 2. The Ore Vein (Khoáng Mạch)

**Concept.** A miner-refiner: the Mother Lode keystone drawn on the map's Earth–Metal border, made real. Every
artifact this cultivator forges carries an extra affix, and it fights with many artifacts at once.

**How it fights.** Three artifacts (Flying Sword, Needle, Bell) on a high artifact-power build; the arts are
ordinary, the gear is not.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Metal (refining role).
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Refined Metal, the forge branch.
- **Keystone:** Mother Lode (Mẫu Khoáng, placeholder on the map): content New.
- **Effects:** Open Sky (exists: ward strength per free thần thức) to balance gear load.
- **Modifiers:** artifact power; artifact attack; element affinity; refining success.
- **Items:** self-forged artifacts; Twin Arms in Harmony (capability, exists: two treasures of a kind as a pair).
- **Element stacks:** Metal buff.

**Weakness.** Thần thức: every artifact binds it; a soul attacker punishes low free thần thức.

**New pieces.**
- **Mother Lode content (Mẫu Khoáng)**: "artifacts you refine roll one extra affix; your thần thức cost per artifact
  is +20 %". How to create: Meridian Map content pass, a passive on Artifact Refining.

## 3. The Avalanche (Tuyết Lở Thạch)

**Concept.** Stone and blade fall together: Earth slows the foe's aim, Metal sharpens every hit, and a single
falling-mountain seal ends it.

**How it fights.** Like Hammer and Anvil (single Metal) but with Earth debuff to protect itself while building
Metal debuff on the foe.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Metal.
- **Công pháp:** Deep Earth Heaven Canon.
- **Đạo:** Gravity (Trọng Lực Đạo).
- **Keystone:** Edge Returns the Breath (New).
- **Effects:** Measured Stroke (exists).
- **Modifiers:** damage [seal], [slow]; `stack_effect_pct` [kim, debuff].
- **Items:** Seal (Ấn); Black Tortoise Shell.
- **Element stacks:** Metal debuff, Earth debuff.

**Weakness.** Cleansing before the seal.

**New pieces.** Anvil Guard (see `single-metal.md`).

## 4. The Hundred Weapons Rack (Bách Binh Giá)

**Concept.** A refiner who carries an armoury: six cheap artifacts, each used once or twice a fight. Earth keeps
the body standing while Metal's artifacts take turns.

**How it fights.** Every art is an artifact art with a long cooldown; six of them in sequence. Metal buff stacks
from each firing raise the Physical damage of the next weapon.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth and Metal (refining, so the artifacts are cheap to make).
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Refined Metal, the armoury branch (New nodes: thần thức cost down).
- **Keystone:** Divided Divine Sense (capability, exists: +1 magic treasure slot, +20 % divine sense).
- **Effects:** Open Sky (exists).
- **Modifiers:** artifact power; thần thức; `than_thuc_cost_pct`.
- **Items:** Flying Sword, Needle, Seal, Bell, Fan, Flag.
- **Element stacks:** Metal buff.

**Weakness.** Thần thức: six artifacts bind a lot of it, so a soul attacker hits a thin soul.

**New pieces.** None.
