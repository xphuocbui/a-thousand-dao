# Quad root without Fire: Wood, Earth, Metal, Water (Mộc, Thổ, Kim, Thủy)

**What the root brings.** Three generating pairs (Earth → Metal → Water → Wood, +9 %); Earth with Water can converge
into Darkness. The mana buff, regeneration, Physical resistance, Physical damage; Poison, accuracy and Metal
debuffs. No Fire: no Burn and no damage buff, so this root wins by outlasting.

---

## 1. The Unending Grove (Vô Tận Lâm)

**Concept.** The most sustained root in the game: Water pool, Wood body, Earth bark, Metal thorns. It cannot be
starved, cannot be bled out, and wins every fight that reaches fatigue.

**How it fights.** Heal arts that refund their cost; Water and Wood buffs; Thorns (New) on being hit. Its foe's
pool runs dry first; then fatigue finishes what thorns began.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Earth, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Green Wood Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Life.
- **Keystone:** The Spring Never Fails (Water, New).
- **Effects:** Thorns (New, `dual-metal-wood.md`); Still Water (exists).
- **Modifiers:** healing; health and linh lực regeneration; block.
- **Items:** Mountain Screen; Green Wood Array.
- **Element stacks:** Water, Wood, Earth buffs.

**Weakness.** Withering and Stagnation together.

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.

## 2. The Glacier (Băng Hà), toward Ice

**Concept.** Earth, Metal and Water turned to ice: a slow, cold, crushing build. With Ice transmuted, every hit
raises the foe's art costs.

**How it fights.** Ice debuff from Cold Water arts; Earth buff to stand; Metal debuff for the Physical crush.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Earth, Metal, Water with Ice.
- **Công pháp:** Dark Water Heaven Canon.
- **Đạo:** Mist, the frozen branch (New).
- **Keystone:** Frost Tithe (Ice, New).
- **Effects:** Rime Lock (exists).
- **Modifiers:** `convert_pct` Physical → Cold; `stack_duration_pct` [bang, debuff].
- **Items:** Seal; Black Tortoise Shell.
- **Element stacks:** Ice debuff, Metal debuff, Earth buff.

**Weakness.** Cleansing; cheap-kit foes.

**New pieces.** Frost Tithe (see `variant-ice.md`).

## 3. The Jade Physician (Ngọc Y)

**Concept.** A doctor with steel needles (Metal), herbs (Wood), mineral cures (Earth) and clean water (Water): the
best healer and the best poisoner.

**How it fights.** Needles that apply Poison or heal; heal arts refund cost.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Earth, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Green Wood Heaven Canon.
- **Đạo:** Life, then Poison.
- **Keystone:** Root Drinks the Rot (Wood, New).
- **Effects:** Marked Prey (exists).
- **Modifiers:** healing; damage [poison].
- **Items:** Needle; Gourd.
- **Element stacks:** Wood debuff and buff.

**Weakness.** Cleansing.

**New pieces.** None.

## 4. The Stone Buddha (Thạch Phật)

**Concept.** A cultivator who never strikes first and barely strikes at all. It sits; blows land on stone and thorns;
the foe's own strength wears it down.

**How it fights.** Thorns (New) return damage and Poison; Answering Bell (New) rings back on per-hit fees; block and
regeneration hold. It fires one cheap art and wins on the foe's own offence.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Earth, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Diamond Body (Kim Cang Thể).
- **Đạo:** Life or Gravity.
- **Keystone:** The Stone Remembers (Earth, New).
- **Effects:** Thorns (New), Answering Bell (New).
- **Modifiers:** block; health regeneration; ward efficiency.
- **Items:** Golden Bell; Armour.
- **Element stacks:** Earth buff, Wood buff.

**Weakness.** Soul attacks and ticks (no hit to answer).

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.
