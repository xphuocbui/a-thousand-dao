# Variant root: Darkness (Ám)

**What Darkness brings.** Converges from Earth and Water. Chaos damage, which passes body shields and meets only Chaos
and all resistance. Role: stealth and ambush. **No element stacks** (user decision). Công pháp: Shadow Secret Art
(Ám Ảnh Bí Thuật). Đạo: Yin (Âm), Death (Tử). The Darkness island and the Shadow Shrine on the map belong here.

**Darkness's linh lực.** Darkness has no stacks, so its mana comes from its Earth and Water parents (The Spring
Never Fails, The Stone Remembers) or from a Darkness-island keystone (New): **Night Tithe (Dạ Thuế)**: "when you
hit a foe that has not yet hit you, restore linh lực". How to create: Meridian Map keystone on the Darkness island;
reads "hits taken this fight".

---

## 1. The First Strike (Tiên Sát)

**Concept.** An assassin. Stealth means the fight starts before the foe knows it: the opening blow is free and
huge, and the rest is cleanup.

**How it fights.** Ambush fights: First Move (first art free) plus a New opener that fires every art at t = 0 for
the first instant. Chaos ignores the thể tu's body shield.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Darkness (with Earth and Water).
- **Công pháp:** Shadow Secret Art.
- **Đạo:** Yin.
- **Keystone:** Night Tithe (New).
- **Effects:** First Move (exists); Ambush (New).
- **Modifiers:** damage [chaos]; stealth.
- **Items:** Needle; Concealed Breath (capability, exists: seen two stages lower).
- **Element stacks:** none.

**Weakness.** Foreseen fights where the foe is ready; Chaos resistance (rare).

**New pieces.**
- **Ambush (Phục Kích)**, an Effect: "in a fight you opened by stealth, your arts start ready at t = 0". How to
  create: reads the fight situation (Event Engine's ambush tag); changes the start rule (Combat rule 10).

## 2. The Night Plague (Dạ Dịch)

**Concept.** Shadow rot: Chaos ticks that pass every shield, from a root that has no Wood. The Corpse Rot standing
status, carried by a Darkness cultivator.

**How it fights.** Corpse Rot from t = 0 (Grave-Silk Shroud), Chaos arts, Spillover.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Darkness.
- **Công pháp:** Shadow Secret Art.
- **Đạo:** Death.
- **Keystone:** Night Tithe (New).
- **Effects:** Grave-Silk Shroud (exists), Spillover (exists).
- **Modifiers:** damage [chaos], [over_time].
- **Items:** Gourd of grave water.
- **Element stacks:** none (Corpse Rot is a status, not a stack).

**Weakness.** Cleansing; Clear Heart Ward.

**New pieces.** None beyond the keystone.

## 3. The Shadow Bargain (Ám Khế)

**Concept.** A cultivator who has made a deal with death: refusals at the Down step, a last act that strikes as they
fall, and Soul Leaves the Body for the events that kill.

**How it fights.** Unyielding Breath (exists: one refusal), Final Clause (exists: a last free art as you go Down).

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Darkness.
- **Công pháp:** Shadow Secret Art.
- **Đạo:** Death, or Reincarnation.
- **Keystone:** Soul Leaves the Body (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Unyielding Breath, Final Clause (exist).
- **Modifiers:** damage [chaos]; crit damage.
- **Items:** Seal.
- **Element stacks:** none.

**Weakness.** Fatigue (refusals do not stop it for long).

**New pieces.** None.

## 4. The Shadow Puppeteer (Ảnh Ngẫu Sư)

**Concept.** Its shadow fights for it. A Darkness companion made of the cultivator's own shadow: it strikes with
Chaos and cannot be cleansed away, but it dies when its owner's linh lực runs dry.

**How it fights.** The shadow is a companion paid by a reservation of linh lực; its Chaos hits pass body shields. The
cultivator stays back behind its ward.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Darkness.
- **Công pháp:** Shadow Secret Art.
- **Đạo:** Yin.
- **Keystone:** Beast Soul Union (exists).
- **Effects:** Pack Hunt (exists), Bare Rack (exists).
- **Modifiers:** companion power share; reservation efficiency.
- **Items:** none; the shadow is the weapon.
- **Element stacks:** none.

**Weakness.** Light (Exposed) and anything that drains the pool, since the shadow lives on the reservation.

**New pieces.**
- **Shadow Self (Ảnh Thân)**, a companion summoned by reserving linh lực: its power share scales with the reserved
  share. How to create: a reserved-ward-like record that creates a companion (Combat Defence & Healing rule 10 plus
  creature records).
