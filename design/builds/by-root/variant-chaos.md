# Variant root: Primordial Chaos (Hỗn Độn)

**What Chaos brings.** Converges only from all five elements. Role: cultivation speed and breakthrough chance.
**No element stacks** of its own; its arts use whatever damage type is authored. The rarest root in the game; the map
puts the Chaos start at the centre.

**Chaos's linh lực.** Through its five parents' keystones, or a centre keystone (New): **Primordial Breath (Hỗn
Nguyên Khí)**: "you regenerate linh lực +0.1 pp for each element whose buff stacks you hold". How to create: Meridian
Map keystone at the Chaos start; reads element stack counts.

---

## 1. The Hundred Arts Sage (Bách Pháp Thánh)

**Concept.** With every element, every art is open. A cultivator who slots one art of each element and holds every
buff stack at once.

**How it fights.** Five arts, five buffs, four debuffs; Primordial Breath makes the pool grow with breadth; Generating
Cycle (New) ties it together.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** five elements plus Chaos.
- **Công pháp:** Five Phases Canon (New).
- **Đạo:** Taiji, then Reincarnation, Space, Time.
- **Keystone:** Primordial Breath (New); Five Elements Cycle.
- **Effects:** Generating Cycle (New).
- **Modifiers:** universal stack effect and duration (New).
- **Items:** Five-Phase Flag (New).
- **Element stacks:** all.

**Weakness.** The slowest early life; Silence.

**New pieces.** Primordial Breath.

## 2. The Breakthrough Prodigy (Phá Cảnh Kỳ Tài)

**Concept.** Chaos's role is cultivation, not fighting. This build reaches realms early and wins fights by being a
realm above its peers (a realm gap decides fights fast after #44).

**How it fights.** Like a typical build, one realm higher than its age suggests.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Chaos.
- **Công pháp:** the best available, at Chaos's speed.
- **Đạo:** Time (Thời Gian).
- **Keystone:** Mutation Awakening (capability, exists), Last Radiance (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** none special.
- **Modifiers:** cultivation speed; breakthrough chance.
- **Items:** Breakthrough talisman (Phá Cảnh Phù).
- **Element stacks:** whatever its arts grant.

**Weakness.** Fights before it has used its speed.

**New pieces.** None.

## 3. The Unmaker (Hủy Nguyên Giả)

**Concept.** Chaos as dissolution: a cleanse-everything build that strips the foe's buff stacks and statuses, and
answers stack builds of every element.

**How it fights.** A New Chaos art cleanses the foe's buff stacks (an "unmake" action) and its own debuffs.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Chaos.
- **Công pháp:** Five Phases Canon (New).
- **Đạo:** Destruction.
- **Keystone:** Primordial Breath (New).
- **Effects:** Unmake (New).
- **Modifiers:** status resistance; damage [chaos].
- **Items:** Clear Heart Ward.
- **Element stacks:** none of its own; it removes others'.

**Weakness.** Foes with no stacks (plain damage builds).

**New pieces.**
- **Unmake (Hủy Nguyên)**, an Effect action: "remove a share of the target's buff stacks". How to create: the
  cleanse action (#44) extended to buff stacks on a foe; rare, on Chaos only.

## 4. The Reincarnator (Luân Hồi Giả)

**Concept.** A life build across lives. Chaos's speed and the Reincarnation Đạo aim at the one thing no other root
can: carrying something into the next life.

**How it plays.** It cultivates fast, survives one death per realm (Soul Leaves the Body), and at its end leaves a
legacy for the next character it plays (New: a Reincarnation Đạo keystone).

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Chaos.
- **Công pháp:** Five Phases Canon (New).
- **Đạo:** Reincarnation (Luân Hồi).
- **Keystone:** Soul Leaves the Body (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** none in fights.
- **Modifiers:** cultivation speed; breakthrough chance; lifespan.
- **Items:** a Soul Jade (New) that keeps the legacy.
- **Element stacks:** none needed.

**Weakness.** None in a fight that matters; everything rides on the legacy rule existing.

**New pieces.**
- **Legacy (Di Sản)**, a Reincarnation Đạo keystone: "when this life ends, the next life you play starts with one
  Đạo glimpse already open". How to create: a meta rule across lives (Meta Achievements system), owned by the user's
  game-concept decisions; it touches Pillar 4's one-life weight, so it needs a ruling.
- **Soul Jade (Hồn Ngọc)**, an item that holds the legacy. How to create: Item DB treasure, never tradable.
