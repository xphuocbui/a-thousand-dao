# Triple root: Earth, Metal and Wood (Thổ, Kim, Mộc)

**What the root brings.** One generating pair (Earth → Metal, +3 %); Metal cuts Wood; Wood breaks Earth.
Physical resistance and accuracy debuff, Physical damage and debuff, Poison and regeneration. A rugged,
mountain-forest root.

---

## 1. The Mountain Hermit (Sơn Ẩn)

**Concept.** A recluse among pines and rocks: hard to hit (Earth debuff), hard to hurt (Earth buff), slow to tire
(Wood regeneration), and one sharp pine-needle art.

**How it fights.** Defensive control: Earth debuff blinds, Wood regenerates, a Metal needle art finishes.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth, Metal, Wood.
- **Công pháp:** Deep Earth Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Illusion or Dust (New, `single-earth.md`).
- **Keystone:** The Stone Remembers (Earth, New), unless it takes evade.
- **Effects:** Beneath Notice (exists).
- **Modifiers:** evade; health regeneration; damage [physical].
- **Items:** Needle; Iron Shirt.
- **Element stacks:** Earth debuff, Wood buff.

**Weakness.** Soul attacks; Thunder accuracy.

**New pieces.** None.

## 2. The Bonsai Blade (Bồn Cảnh Kiếm)

**Concept.** A master who shapes a tree with wire and knife: patient Metal cuts and Wood growth, pruning the foe's
statuses away while growing its own.

**How it fights.** Metal sword arts that also cleanse 1 debuff stack from self per hit (pruning); Wood buff for
regeneration.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth, Metal, Wood.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Sword.
- **Keystone:** Edge Returns the Breath (Metal, New).
- **Effects:** Pruning Cut (New).
- **Modifiers:** damage [sword]; status resistance.
- **Items:** Flying Sword.
- **Element stacks:** Metal debuff, Wood buff.

**Weakness.** Few.

**New pieces.**
- **Pruning Cut (Tiễn Chi)**, an Effect: "when your sword hit lands, remove 1 debuff stack from yourself, oldest
  first". How to create: trigger on hit, action "cleanse element stacks" (new action, #44).

## 3. The Ironwood Golem (Thiết Mộc Nhân)

**Concept.** A thể tu of ironwood, combining all three Physical-defence elements: Earth resistance, Metal block,
Wood regeneration.

**How it fights.** Blood Casting with Wood regeneration; Earth and Metal buffs; a slow Physical fist kit.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth, Metal, Wood.
- **Công pháp:** Diamond Body.
- **Đạo:** Life.
- **Keystone:** Blood Casting (exists).
- **Effects:** Iron Skin Renewed (exists).
- **Modifiers:** block; health regeneration; `art_cost_health_pct`.
- **Items:** Armour; Iron Body Art.
- **Element stacks:** Earth buff, Wood buff, Metal buff.

**Weakness.** Chaos and Fire.

**New pieces.** None.

## 4. The Forest Ambusher (Lâm Phục)

**Concept.** A mountain bandit who waits among the trees. The fight begins with a volley from cover, and the foe is
blinded before it can answer.

**How it fights.** Ambush fights only: Ambush (New) makes every art ready at t = 0; the volley lays Earth debuff and
Metal debuff together; then a retreat into cover (an escape art) if the volley was not enough.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Earth, Metal, Wood.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Illusion.
- **Keystone:** Concealed Breath (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Ambush (New, `variant-darkness.md`), First Move (exists), Cold Feet (exists).
- **Modifiers:** stealth; damage [physical].
- **Items:** Needles; Swift Walk talisman.
- **Element stacks:** Earth debuff, Metal debuff.

**Weakness.** Foreseen fights in the open.

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.
