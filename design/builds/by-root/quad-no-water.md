# Quad root without Water: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal (Mộc, Hỏa, Thổ, Kim)

**What the root brings.** Three generating pairs (Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal, +9 %); Fire with Metal can converge
into Thunder. Every debuff (Poison, Burn, accuracy, Metal) and every buff but Water's. **No mana element**: this
root must find linh lực in keystones, leech and health payment.

---

## 1. The Thirsty Blade (Khát Nhận)

**Concept.** A root with no water must drink from its foes. Every hit leeches linh lực; Poison adds a second
leech (Root Drinks the Rot); Blood Casting covers the gaps.

**How it fights.** Leech on every hit; Poison on the foe; arts paid partly in health.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Destruction or Poison.
- **Keystone:** Root Drinks the Rot (Wood, New); Blood Casting (exists).
- **Effects:** Water Table (exists: gain 30 % of what the foe's drain wards pay against your hits).
- **Modifiers:** `leech_linh_luc_pct`; `art_cost_health_pct`; damage.
- **Items:** Needle; Gourd.
- **Element stacks:** Wood debuff, Metal debuff.

**Weakness.** Evasive foes (no hit, no leech); cleansing (no Poison, half leech).

**New pieces.** None.

## 2. The Four Wounds (Tứ Thương)

**Concept.** Every debuff the game has but Ice's: Poison, Burn, blindness and broken armour. A pure debuff build:
Marked Prey at full and every stack pressing.

**How it fights.** Four cheap arts, each laying one element's debuff; Marked Prey; Spillover.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal.
- **Công pháp:** a four-element technique (New).
- **Đạo:** Destruction.
- **Keystone:** Embers Feed the Hearth (Fire, New).
- **Effects:** Marked Prey (exists), Spillover (exists).
- **Modifiers:** universal stack duration (New); damage [over_time].
- **Items:** Fan, Gourd, Needle.
- **Element stacks:** four debuffs.

**Weakness.** A share-based cleansing ward thins them all at once.

**New pieces.** Universal stack duration (see `quad-no-metal.md`).

## 3. The Bonfire Forge Army (Hỏa Lô Quân)

**Concept.** A sect founder: fields (Earth), herbs (Wood), pills (Fire), weapons (Metal). Builds an army of
companions and disciples rather than a sword.

**How it fights.** A companion build with element-tagged beasts (New) carrying the four elements.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Array.
- **Keystone:** Ten Thousand Beasts, One Heart (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Pack Hunt (exists), Beast Soul Union (exists).
- **Modifiers:** companion power share.
- **Items:** beasts; Flag.
- **Element stacks:** from element-tagged companions (New).

**Weakness.** Kill the beasts.

**New pieces.** Element-tagged companions (see `triple-fire-earth-metal.md`).

## 4. The Drought Bringer (Đại Hạn Sứ)

**Concept.** A root with no water makes the world dry. If it cannot fill its own pool, it empties the foe's: Qi
Disruption, Spillover and Ice-free cost pressure.

**How it fights.** Qi Disruption ticks burn the foe's linh lực (no burn cap since #44); Spillover adds 25 % of every
tick to the burn; Poison and Burn give the ticks. The foe's ward falls first.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal.
- **Công pháp:** Crimson Flame Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Poison.
- **Keystone:** Root Drinks the Rot (Wood, New).
- **Effects:** Spillover (exists), Water Table (exists).
- **Modifiers:** damage [over_time]; status chance.
- **Items:** Gourd; Fan.
- **Element stacks:** Wood debuff, Fire debuff.

**Weakness.** Cleansing; a foe with a reserved ward (reservations do not drain).

**New pieces.** None.
