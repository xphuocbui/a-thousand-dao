# Quad root without Earth: Wood, Fire, Metal, Water (Mộc, Hỏa, Kim, Thủy)

**What the root brings.** Three generating pairs (Metal → Water → Wood → Fire, +9 %); Fire with Metal can converge
into Thunder. Mana, regeneration and damage buffs; Poison, Burn and Metal debuffs. No Earth: no Physical dampening buff, so
this root is fragile and fierce.

---

## 1. The Glass Cannon Grove (Lưu Ly Lâm)

**Concept.** Everything into offence: three tick streams and a deep pool, with Glass Meridians (arts far faster on a
fragile body). It kills fast or dies.

**How it fights.** Glass Meridians (exists); Burn, Poison, Metal debuff; Water stacks to keep up the fast casting.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Crimson Flame Heaven Canon.
- **Đạo:** Destruction.
- **Keystone:** Glass Meridians (Effect keystone, exists).
- **Effects:** Marked Prey (exists), Overheat (exists: clipped art speed becomes damage).
- **Modifiers:** art speed; damage [over_time]; maximum linh lực.
- **Items:** Fan, Flying Sword, Gourd.
- **Element stacks:** Fire, Wood, Metal debuffs; Water buff.

**Weakness.** Burst; anything that reaches its body.

**New pieces.** None.

## 2. The River of Lanterns (Hà Đăng)

**Concept.** A festival of floating lanterns: Water carries the Fire downstream. A support-heal build whose Fire
burns attackers and whose Water heals allies.

**How it fights.** Lantern Burn (New) on being hit; heal arts refund cost; Water stacks.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Green Wood Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Yang.
- **Keystone:** The Spring Never Fails (New).
- **Effects:** Lantern Burn (New, `triple-water-wood-fire.md`).
- **Modifiers:** healing.
- **Items:** Lantern (New kind).
- **Element stacks:** Water buff, Fire debuff.

**Weakness.** Withering.

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.

## 3. The Storm Over the Sea (Hải Lôi), toward Thunder

**Concept.** Thunder over water: crits that apply crit exposure, a deep pool to keep casting.

**How it fights.** After Thunder forms, see `variant-thunder.md`; before, a Water-backed crit sword build.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Canh Kim Heaven Canon → Thunder Emperor Canon.
- **Đạo:** Heavenly Thunder.
- **Keystone:** Storm Battery (Thunder, New).
- **Effects:** Called Blow (exists), Flowing Water (exists).
- **Modifiers:** crit; maximum linh lực.
- **Items:** Flying Swords.
- **Element stacks:** Thunder debuff, Water buff.

**Weakness.** Block.

**New pieces.** None.

## 4. The Drifting Cloud (Phù Vân)

**Concept.** No earth beneath it: a wanderer who never stands still. Evade, escape and travel; it fights where it
chooses and leaves when it likes.

**How it fights.** High evade from a cast ward; Cold Feet's fast escape; Beneath Notice against stronger foes. Its
damage comes from Burn laid while it dances away.

**Stacks.**
- **Linh căn:** Wood, Fire, Metal, Water.
- **Công pháp:** Dark Water Earth Scripture.
- **Đạo:** Space (Không Gian).
- **Keystone:** Blink Mark (capability, exists).
- **Effects:** Cold Feet (exists), Beneath Notice (exists).
- **Modifiers:** evade; escape chance; travel speed.
- **Items:** Steam Veil (New ward, `dual-water-fire.md`); Swift Walk talisman.
- **Element stacks:** Water buff, Fire debuff.

**Weakness.** Ticks and soul attacks; locking pursuers (the Anchor).

**New pieces.** None beyond those named.
