  cul [seasons]                          tu luyện (default 1 season)

  mission                                sect mission at your sect HQ, or bounty in a town
  explore                                search this place (events, lost treasures)
  go [place id]                          travel along a road (no id: pick from a list)
  craft [recipe id]                      luyện đan, one season (no id: pick)

Actions (free)
  plant [herb id|item id]                buy a seed / transplant a herb (at home)

  use [item id]                          eat a pill
  equip|unequip [item id]                pháp bảo

  talk [person id] [what you say]        conversation (LLM or template)

  event / choose <n>                     read / answer the pending event

  formation                              trận pháp at your động phủ: levels, next cost, upkeep
  formation build [id]                   raise a formation one level, one season (no id: pick); 'explain upkeep'

  beasts / release <id>                  linh thú: your beasts (tame them by beating them in events), feeding, loyalty
World
